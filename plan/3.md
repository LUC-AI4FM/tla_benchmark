1. **Counterexample-guided training (CEGIS-style).** Feed the model checker's *counterexample trace* back as structured feedback — "your spec allows this exact bad behavior" — not a binary reward. CEGIS (counterexample-guided inductive synthesis) is a celebrated formal-methods paradigm; **"counterexample-guided LLM fine-tuning/repair" is a fresh, defensible novelty.** Your current `preference_generator` throws the counterexample away and keeps only pass/fail — that's the missed opportunity.
2. **Temporal/liveness as the hard target.** Most verifier-feedback work checks *functional* correctness (one answer). Model checking checks *temporal* properties over infinite behaviors — a genuinely different and harder signal. Training specifically for liveness/fairness (where models fail most, per your construct map) is a sharper, more novel claim than "DPO helps."
