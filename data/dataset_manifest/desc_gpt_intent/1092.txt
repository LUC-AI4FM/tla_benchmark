Specify a TLA+ specification for a simplified PCR amplification process that tracks material counts at a high resolution. The model should start with a finite amount of double-stranded source DNA and finite primers, then repeatedly pass through temperature-controlled phases for denaturation, primer annealing, and extension. It should distinguish single-stranded templates of different lengths, primer-template hybrids, intermediate double strands, and the final short double-stranded product. Counts should be natural numbers, and all material transformations should conserve the relevant DNA/primer accounting: heating separates hybrids and double strands into templates and frees bound primers; annealing nondeterministically binds available primers to available templates; extension converts hybrids into the next double-stranded form and advances the completed cycle count.

The specification should allow nondeterministic choices of how many primers anneal and to which template classes, but require weak fairness so enabled phase transitions eventually occur. It must guarantee type correctness, consistency with a coarser abstract PCR stage model, eventual primer depletion for the finite initial stock, and the safety property that no final product exists before three cycles have completed.
