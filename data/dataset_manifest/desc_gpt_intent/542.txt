Specify a small TLA+ specification for a demonstration of concurrency: two independent processes share a single integer counter initially zero, and each process is intended to perform exactly one atomic increment and then finish. The model should capture all possible interleavings of these two atomic operations, including the possibility of stuttering, and should allow the completed system to remain unchanged forever rather than deadlocking.

The key safety requirements are that the counter never decreases, never exceeds two, and always equals the number of processes that have completed their increment. No process may increment more than once, and once a process has finished, it remains finished. If both processes have finished, the counter must be exactly two. The specification should be deadlock-free by permitting stable behavior after termination. Do not require fairness or guaranteed progress as part of the main behavior; termination may be stated as a separate property that can be checked under appropriate assumptions.
