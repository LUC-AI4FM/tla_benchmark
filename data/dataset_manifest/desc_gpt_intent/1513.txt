Specify a small TLA+ demonstration model of an unconstrained state machine with a fixed finite universe. The model should have three ordinary nodes numbered 0 through 2, two possible colors, white and black, and a token that is always located at one of the ordinary nodes. Each ordinary node may independently have a color. Separately, maintain a Boolean activity/passivity assignment over the three identifiers 23, 42, and 56 rather than over the ordinary node set.

The initial state may choose the activity assignment, ordinary-node colors, and token location arbitrarily within those domains, but the token’s color must initially be black. Every subsequent step may again choose the activity assignment, ordinary-node colors, and token location arbitrarily, with no dependence on the previous state, while preserving that the token color is black.

The key safety property is domain/type correctness for these components and invariance of the token color being black. No progress, fairness, or algorithmic liveness requirement is intended.
