Specify a small TLA+ specification for a deterministic countdown counter. The model should be parameterized by a natural-number constant giving the initial counter value. The system starts with the counter equal to that value. On each transition, if the counter is positive, it must decrease by exactly one; once the counter reaches zero, it must remain zero forever.

The specification should capture the intended behavior of a simple finite countdown rather than an unbounded arithmetic process. The key safety property is that the counter value is always a natural number and never becomes negative. It should also never increase, and the only permitted positive-state change is decrementing by one. The terminal state is zero, which is absorbing.

The key progress property is that, from any natural initial value, repeated transitions eventually reach zero after finitely many decrement steps, and all subsequent states keep the counter at zero.
