Specify a TLA+ specification for a simple transaction commit protocol among a fixed set of participating resource managers. Each participant begins in a working state, may become prepared, and may eventually decide either to commit or abort according to the global transaction status. A participant may commit only when every participant is already prepared or committed. A participant may abort only while no participant has committed. Once a participant has reached a final decision, that decision is permanent.

The model should allow nondeterministic ordering of participant moves and should permit stuttering; it should not require that participants eventually prepare or decide. The essential safety properties are that every participant is always in one of the valid protocol states, and that the system never reaches an inconsistent outcome in which one participant has committed while another has aborted. No stronger liveness guarantee, such as eventual commit or eventual abort, is required.
