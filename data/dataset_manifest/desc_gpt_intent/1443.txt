Model a single finite-state counter initialized to zero that advances deterministically by adding one modulo three, so the reachable behavior cycles through 0, 1, 2, and back to 0. The specification should allow ordinary stuttering as appropriate for a standard temporal spec.

The safety requirement is that the counter never leaves the three-value cycle and every non-stuttering transition follows the modulo-three increment rule. The test should finish with a TLC postcondition asserting that each of the three registered possible conditions was detected exactly once by TLC’s named possible-condition accounting.
