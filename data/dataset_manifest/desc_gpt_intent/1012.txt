Specify a TLA+ specification for a simplified voucher-cancellation protocol using two-phase commit. Model a set of vouchers, voucher holders, and voucher issuers, coordinated by a voucher transaction provider. Initially all vouchers are valid and in an active lifecycle state; holders are holding vouchers, issuers are waiting, and the coordinator has not decided. Holders and issuers may independently become prepared and send prepared notifications, or may independently abandon the operation. The coordinator records prepared notifications and may cancel a voucher only after all holders and issuers have prepared; otherwise it may choose to abort. A coordinator cancellation marks the selected voucher as cancelled and completes its lifecycle, while an abort leaves vouchers valid and unfinished. Coordinator decisions are broadcast and may be received by eligible holders and issuers.

The specification must guarantee type correctness of all modeled states and messages. It must also guarantee agreement: no holder and issuer may reach conflicting final outcomes, specifically one cancelled while the other aborted. The voucher behavior should conform to the abstract voucher lifecycle for cancellation.
