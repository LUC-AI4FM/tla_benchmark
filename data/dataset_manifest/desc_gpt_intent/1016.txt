Specify a TLA+ specification for a simplified voucher redemption protocol based on two-phase commit. Model a set of vouchers, voucher holders, voucher collectors, and a voucher transaction provider acting as coordinator. Vouchers initially are valid and in an active lifecycle state; holders initially hold them and collectors wait to receive them. Holders and collectors may independently announce that they are prepared to redeem, and may also independently choose to abort before completion. The coordinator records prepared announcements and may either abort the transaction while it is still pending, or redeem a voucher only after every holder and collector has prepared. A coordinator decision is broadcast as either redeem or abort; messages may be modeled abstractly and need not be consumed.

The specification should guarantee type correctness of all states and messages. Its main safety property is agreement: no holder and collector may reach conflicting final decisions, i.e. one redeemed while the other aborted. Redemption must update the voucher lifecycle consistently, from valid/working to redeemed/done. No fairness or progress guarantee is required.
