Specify a TLA+ specification for a classic bounded FIFO producer-consumer buffer. There is a nonempty finite set of producers, a nonempty finite set of consumers, the two sets are disjoint, and the capacity is a positive natural number. Initially the buffer is empty and no process is waiting. A producer may try to add an item identifying itself; if space is available, the item is appended and one waiting consumer, if any, may be unblocked. If the buffer is full, the producer becomes waiting. A consumer may try to remove the oldest item; if an item is available, it is removed and one waiting producer, if any, may be unblocked. If the buffer is empty, the consumer becomes waiting. Waiting processes of the relevant kind cannot take their corresponding action until unblocked. The model must preserve bounded capacity and FIFO discipline. Its key safety property is that it is never possible for every producer and every consumer to be waiting simultaneously. No fairness or eventual-progress guarantee is required.
