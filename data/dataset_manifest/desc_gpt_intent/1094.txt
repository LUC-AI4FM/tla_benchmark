Specify a TLA+ specification for an abstract PCR thermal-cycling process with finite natural-number stocks of initial double-stranded DNA and primers. Model the temperature as one of three qualitative levels and track the quantities of double-stranded DNA, single-stranded templates, free primers, and primer-template hybrids, plus the number of legitimate completed PCR cycles. The process must enforce the biologically meaningful order of phases: initial or completed-extension material may be denatured by overheating; the system then cools to a ready state; annealing may occur only when primer and template are both available and must bind some positive number up to the smaller available stock; extension then converts all hybrids into double-stranded DNA and increments the completed-cycle count.

All molecular quantities and the cycle count must always remain natural numbers, and the temperature and phase must remain within their finite domains. Under weak fairness for each phase transition, the model should not allow indefinite stuttering when a phase is continuously enabled. For the configured finite initial stocks, primers should eventually be depleted.
