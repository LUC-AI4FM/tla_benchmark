Specify a small TLA+ demonstration module for a degenerate state machine with two state components. The purpose is to model an initialization-only behavior: in every valid initial state, both components must have the value zero, and the two components must also be equal. After initialization, the system must never change either component; the only permitted behavior is infinite stuttering in the same state.

The specification should make clear that the initial constraints are simultaneous consistency requirements, not a sequence of updates. A behavior is valid exactly when it starts in the unique state where both components are zero and then remains there forever.

The key safety properties are that both components always remain zero and always remain equal. The liveness/progress expectation is intentionally minimal: the behavior may stutter forever, and no nontrivial transition is required or allowed.
