Specify a small TLA+ demonstration model of a bounded counter component whose state is supplied by an enclosing system. The counter starts at zero and has one meaningful transition: while its value is less than three, it may increase by exactly one. When the value has reached three, no further meaningful updates are possible, though the overall behavior may still stutter.

The specification should capture the component’s weak fairness requirement: if the increment transition remains continuously enabled, it cannot be ignored forever. The enclosing system should use the component’s transition and fairness condition for its own state.

Required safety properties are that the counter never decreases, never skips values, and never exceeds three; every non-stuttering transition is exactly a single increment from a value below three. The required liveness property is that every behavior satisfying the weak fairness condition eventually reaches the terminal value three.
