Write a TLA+ specification for a small TLC test module demonstrating postconditions and named possibility checks. Model a simple finite counter system that starts at zero and may either stutter or increment by one while below three; once it reaches three, only stuttering remains possible. The safety behavior should ensure the counter never leaves the range zero through three, never decreases, and never changes by more than one in a non-stuttering step. Do not require fairness or eventual progress, since stuttering should be allowed at any point.

Configure the model so TLC evaluates postconditions after state-space exploration. Include postconditions that record TLC’s generated-state count and distinct-state count into TLC values, and include a postcondition that checks named possibility statistics. The model should declare two named possible conditions: one identifying the reachable state where the counter is two, and one identifying the transition from two to three. The postcondition should assert that each of these named possible conditions is counted exactly once.
