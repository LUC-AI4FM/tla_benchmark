Specify a TLA+ specification for Safra’s EWD998 distributed termination-detection algorithm on a finite nonempty ring of nodes, modeling communication channels as FIFO-like sequences of messages. Nodes may be active or passive, may send ordinary computation messages while active, become active and black when receiving such a message, and may later become passive. A single circulating control token carries a color and an integer balance; passive nodes forward it around the ring, contributing their local message-count balance and propagating blackness, then resetting themselves to white. The distinguished initiator starts a new probe when the returning token or its own state shows that termination has not yet been established.

The specification should allow nondeterministic application behavior for sending, receiving, and deactivation, while preserving well-typed states and exactly one token in the whole system. It should maintain the accounting relationship between sent, received, and in-flight ordinary messages. It should guarantee that any state in which the protocol can regard termination as detected is safe: all nodes are passive and no ordinary messages are in transit. Include weak fairness for the token/probe protocol and show refinement to the abstract EWD998 model with unordered pending messages.
