Specify a TLA+ specification for the translation phase of a PlusCal compiler, taking as input a parsed and renamed abstract syntax tree and a selected fairness mode. The spec should precisely define the accepted AST language for uniprocess and multiprocess algorithms, including declarations, definitions, procedures, processes, labels, control constructs, assignments, calls, returns, gotos, and simple statements. It should validate the tree, reject malformed inputs, detect duplicate labels within a control context, and reject gotos to nonexistent labels except the reserved termination/error targets. It should produce a tokenized TLA+ specification fragment, not a pretty-printer, containing variable declarations, initialization, labeled atomic actions, the next-state relation, the complete temporal specification, and a termination formula. The generated behavior must preserve PlusCal atomic-step semantics, leave unassigned variables unchanged, handle process-local state and procedure call stacks correctly, and report errors for illegal assignments or bad procedure calls. Liveness must reflect the requested mode: none, weak fairness of actions, weak fairness of the whole next action, or strong fairness of actions.
