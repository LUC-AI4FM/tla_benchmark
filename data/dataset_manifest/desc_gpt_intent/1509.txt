Specify a small TLA+ demonstration module for invariant checking with a recursively defined finite function. The model should have a single state value, initially chosen nondeterministically from the integers 1 through 5. After initialization, every behavior should leave that value unchanged forever; the next-state relation may be expressed through a finite, indexed family of no-op actions, but no action should ever modify the state.

The specification should also define a finite recursive function over the domain 1 through 5, where the first result is 1 and each later result is one greater than the previous result. Thus the function’s range is exactly the integers 1 through 5.

The key safety property is that, in every reachable state, the chosen state value is equal to the result of the recursive function at some point in its finite domain. There is no additional progress or fairness requirement; infinite stuttering behavior is acceptable.
