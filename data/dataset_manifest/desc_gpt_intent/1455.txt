Write a small TLA+ specification that demonstrates and checks the behavior of subset inclusion between powersets. The model should be a simple finite-state test harness with a trivial Boolean state that remains true, while the transition relation asserts a collection of expected facts about when one powerset is included in another.

The specification should cover representative positive and negative cases: the powerset of a smaller integer interval is included in the powerset of a larger containing interval; the reverse inclusion is false; overlapping but non-contained intervals do not imply powerset inclusion; a shifted sub-interval contained in a larger interval does imply inclusion; finite enumerated sets behave analogously; the powerset of the empty set is included in itself and in the powerset of any nonempty set; the reverse is not true; and the powerset of a singleton natural/integer set is included in the powersets of the natural numbers and integers.

The key correctness property is that the Boolean state remains true and Boolean throughout all behaviors.
