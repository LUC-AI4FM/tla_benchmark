Specify a TLA+ specification for a resource allocator serving a fixed set of clients and a finite set of resources via asynchronous message passing. Clients may issue a nonempty request only when they have no outstanding request and hold no resources. The allocator may satisfy requests incrementally, granting any subset of available requested resources, and clients may return held resources even before their whole request has been granted; once a client’s request is fully satisfied, it is required eventually to return what it holds.

The allocator should use a repeatedly extended scheduling discipline for pending clients: grants may be made out of schedule order only when they do not take resources requested by earlier scheduled clients. Model request, grant, and return messages as delayed but eventually delivered, and assume the allocator eventually schedules and grants resources when enabled.

Prove safety: no resource is ever held by two distinct clients, and allocator/client state remains consistent with in-transit messages. Prove liveness: every requested resource is eventually obtained, satisfied clients eventually release resources, and every client is infinitely often request-free.
