Specify a small TLA+ specification demonstrating a nondeterministic integer state update constrained only by the next value. The model should have one mutable integer state, initially equal to 0. On every step, the system may either leave the state unchanged or assign it nondeterministically to any even integer digit, namely one of 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8. The choice of the next value should not depend on the previous value except that ordinary stuttering is always allowed.

The main safety requirement is that the state is always an even integer in the range 0 through 9; in particular, it must never become odd, negative, greater than 9, or non-integer. The specification should permit infinite behaviors, including behaviors that stutter forever, and it should not impose any progress or fairness requirement. There is no liveness obligation beyond maintaining the invariant on all reachable states.
