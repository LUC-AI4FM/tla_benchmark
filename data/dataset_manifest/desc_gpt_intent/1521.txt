Specify the coffee-can bean replacement puzzle in TLA+. A can initially contains a positive, bounded number of black and white beans, with the initial counts chosen nondeterministically within a configurable maximum. While at least two beans remain, the system nondeterministically selects a legal pair: two black beans are replaced by one black bean, two white beans are replaced by one black bean, and one black plus one white results in the black being discarded and the white remaining. When only one bean remains, the process may stutter to represent termination.

The specification should guarantee that all reachable states keep bean counts natural and within the configured bound, and that every non-stuttering replacement step reduces the total number of beans by exactly one. It should express the inductive invariant that the parity of the number of white beans is preserved. With weak fairness preventing indefinite avoidance of enabled moves, the process must eventually reach a one-bean terminal state. The final bean must be black iff the initial number of white beans was even, and white iff it was odd.
