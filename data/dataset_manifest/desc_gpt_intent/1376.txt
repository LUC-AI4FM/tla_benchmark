Specify a very small TLA+ model of an accumulating collection over a fixed universe of two data items. The system should start with no items recorded. On each transition, it nondeterministically chooses one item from the fixed universe and records it; recording an item that is already present leaves the collection unchanged. No item outside the fixed universe may ever appear.

The key safety properties are that the recorded collection is always a subset of the fixed universe, it only grows monotonically, and a single transition can add at most one new item. Once an item has been recorded, it is never removed. The model should allow all possible choices of which item is selected at each step.

Do not impose fairness or eventual-completion requirements: the specification should not require that both items are eventually recorded. Infinite behavior that keeps selecting an already recorded item, and therefore makes no further progress, should be permitted.
