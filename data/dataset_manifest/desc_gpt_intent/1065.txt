Specify Dijkstra’s EWD 840 distributed termination detection algorithm for a nonempty ring of processes. Each process may be active or passive; active processes may send messages that make other processes active, and may later become passive. A single circulating token is used by the detection algorithm, moving around the ring in one fixed direction, with white/black coloring of processes and token to record whether activity or messages may have invalidated the current detection round. A distinguished process initiates probes and declares termination only when it receives a clean token under the required local clean/passive conditions. The environment may freely perform computation steps and message sends as allowed, while the token-passing detection mechanism must be weakly fair.

The specification should guarantee type correctness and the standard EWD 840 safety property: whenever termination is detected, every process is passive. It should also guarantee liveness: whenever the system reaches a state in which all processes are passive, termination is eventually detected. It should satisfy an appropriate inductive invariant capturing Dijkstra’s proof argument.
