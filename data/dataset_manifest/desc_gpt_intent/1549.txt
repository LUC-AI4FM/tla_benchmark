Specify a TLA+ specification for an eventually perfect failure detector in a partially synchronous message-passing system with crash faults. The model should have a fixed finite set of processes, with at most a configured number crashing. Correct processes periodically send “alive” heartbeats to other non-crashed processes, periodically update their failure suspicions using per-peer timeouts, and otherwise receive pending messages; sending and prediction must not occur in the same local step. Messages should be modeled with bounded in-transit age, nondeterministic delivery, and removal from the channel on delivery.

The environment should capture partial synchrony without unbounded clocks: message delays are constrained by a configured delay bound, and every correct process must take a step within a configured bound on relative speed. Crashed processes take no further meaningful local steps.

Require type and boundedness invariants for all process states, clocks, timeouts, message ages, failure counts, and suspicion sets. The main liveness properties are strong completeness and eventual strong accuracy: every crashed process is eventually permanently suspected by every correct process, and eventually no correct process is suspected by any correct process.
