Specify a TLA+ specification for Peterson’s two-process mutual exclusion algorithm. Model two identical processes that repeatedly execute a noncritical phase, attempt to acquire exclusive access using per-process intent indicators and a shared tie-breaker, enter a critical section only when Peterson’s waiting condition permits it, and then release their intent before repeating forever. The specification should allow arbitrary interleavings and stuttering, without assuming fairness or proving starvation freedom.

Include basic type-correctness requirements for the process control states, Boolean intent indicators, and the tie-breaker value. State and check an inductive safety invariant capturing that a process’s intent is set exactly during its entry, critical, and release phases; that a process holding or releasing the lock is justified by the tie-breaker or by the other process not yet competing; and, most importantly, that the two processes are never simultaneously in the critical/lock-held region. Also specify that the algorithm refines a simple abstract lock with acquire, critical-section, and release behavior.
