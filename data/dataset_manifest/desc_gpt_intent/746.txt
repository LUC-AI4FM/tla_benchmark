Specify a TLA+ specification for the Chameneos concurrency game with a positive number of creatures and a positive target number of meetings. Each creature starts with one of three colors and an individual meeting count of zero. There is a single rendezvous place that can either be empty or hold one waiting creature. A non-faded creature may try to participate: before the target number of meetings is reached it may wait if the place is empty, or, if another creature is already waiting, the two meet, both increment their individual counts, and both change to the color determined by the Chameneos complement rule: same colors remain unchanged, different colors become the third color. A creature must never meet itself.

After the target number of meetings has occurred, no further meetings occur; remaining active creatures may fade. The model should preserve type correctness for colors, faded state, identifiers, and bounded counts. The key safety property is that whenever the target number of meetings has been reached, the sum of all individual meeting counts is exactly twice the target. No requirement should imply that every creature necessarily participates.
