Specify Lamport’s bakery mutual-exclusion algorithm for a fixed finite set of processes. Each process repeatedly tries to enter a critical section by announcing that it is selecting a ticket, reading the other processes’ tickets to choose one larger than those observed, then waiting until every other process has either no ticket or has lower priority according to ticket value with process id as the tie breaker. After leaving the critical section, the process releases its ticket and may try again forever.

The specification should model asynchronous interleaving of process steps and allow stuttering, with ticket values optionally bounded by a constant for finite TLC checking. The main safety requirement is mutual exclusion: at no reachable state may two distinct processes be in the critical section simultaneously. Include standard type/range constraints needed for model checking. Do not require starvation freedom or eventual entry in the base specification unless additional fairness assumptions are explicitly added. For the small two-process bounded configuration, include coverage checks for critical-section reachability and ticket-selection transitions.
