Specify a small TLA+ / TLC demonstration model whose purpose is to confirm that exploration of a simple nondeterministic next-state relation produces exactly three distinct reachable states. The model should begin in a single initial state with two simple state components fixed to their initial values. On each step, it may either leave the state unchanged or choose nondeterministically among three possible values for one component while keeping the other component fixed. Diagnostic printing may be included to announce the test and show generated states, but it must not affect the modeled state.

The key safety requirement is that every reachable state has the fixed component unchanged and the varying component equal to one of the three allowed values, so TLC’s reachable-state graph contains precisely those three states. There is no substantive invariant beyond successful state generation, and no progress or fairness requirement is intended; infinite stuttering behavior is allowed.
