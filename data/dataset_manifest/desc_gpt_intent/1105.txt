Specify a parameterized TLA+ specification for Guerraoui’s asynchronous non-blocking atomic commitment protocol with a fixed finite set of processes and unreliable processes that may crash. Each process initially votes YES or NO; optionally support restricted scenarios where all vote YES or all vote NO. Non-suspected processes may broadcast their vote, crashed processes stop participating, and a failure detector marks processes as suspected. A suspected process that has not yet broadcast should be allowed to abort. Processes receive vote messages asynchronously, may abort after learning of any NO vote, and may commit only after learning enough YES votes to know that all processes voted YES and no NO vote is known.

The model should preserve basic typing and counting bounds for sent and received messages, including possible messages from processes that crash before broadcasting. It must guarantee agreement: no execution may contain both a commit decision and an abort decision. If any initial vote is NO, commit is forever impossible. If all initially vote YES and no process is suspected, abort is impossible except for crashed processes. If no process ever crashes or is suspected, all processes eventually decide.
