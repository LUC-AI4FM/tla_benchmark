Specify a TLA+ specification for the classic prisoners-and-switches puzzle using the Car Guys strategy. Model a finite set of more than one prisoner, one designated as the counter, and a room containing two switches whose initial positions are arbitrary. On each visit, exactly one selected prisoner must flip exactly one switch, and no prisoners can communicate after the strategy begins. The scheduling assumption should express that every prisoner is brought to the room infinitely often.

The strategy should use one switch as a signal and the other as a harmless switch to satisfy the required flip when no useful signal action is possible. Each non-counter prisoner should contribute a bounded number of signal events, while the counter observes and counts those events. The counter declares success only when enough signal events have been counted to overcome uncertainty about the initial switch position.

Prove safety: whenever the declaration condition is reached, every prisoner has visited the room at least once. Prove liveness: under the fairness assumption, the declaration condition is eventually reached.
