Specify a TLA+ specification for the Chandy-Misra solution to the Dining Philosophers problem for a positive, configurable number of philosophers arranged in a ring. Each pair of neighboring philosophers shares one fork, and philosophers may communicate by handing forks directly to neighbors. Model philosophers as fair, continually running participants that alternate between being hungry, eating, and thinking. A philosopher may eat only when it possesses both adjacent forks and those forks are clean; eating makes the used forks dirty. Dirty forks held by a philosopher should be cleaned and passed to the neighboring philosopher that shares them, allowing ownership to circulate and preventing permanent retention.

The specification should include an initial asymmetric fork ownership arrangement with all forks dirty, sufficient to avoid the symmetric deadlock of the classic problem. It must guarantee type correctness of all modeled state, mutual exclusion for shared forks so that adjacent philosophers never eat at the same time, and starvation freedom: under the fairness assumptions, every philosopher eventually eats again, repeatedly.
