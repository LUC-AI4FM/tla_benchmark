Specify a TLA+ specification for abstract asynchronous termination detection in a nonempty finite network of processes, described as a ring but modeled at the level of process activity and in-transit messages. Initially, processes may be either active or passive, with no messages in transit; if the system is already globally terminated, detection may or may not have occurred.

The model should allow active processes to generate messages for other processes, processes with pending messages to receive them and become active, and active processes to become passive. Global termination means every process is passive and there are no pending messages anywhere. Once this condition holds, it must be stable: no future behavior can make the system non-terminated again.

The detection indication must be safe: it may be true only when the system is globally terminated. Under a fairness assumption that termination detection is eventually performed when continuously enabled, the specification must guarantee liveness: whenever global termination is reached, it is eventually detected.
