Specify a small TLA+ specification for an abstract mutual-exclusion lock used by exactly two identical processes. Each process repeatedly spends time outside the critical section, attempts to acquire the shared lock, enters the critical section only after a successful acquisition, and then releases the lock before returning outside. The lock should be modeled abstractly as either free or held, initially free, with both processes initially outside the critical section. Acquiring the lock must be atomic and possible only when the lock is free; releasing it makes the lock free again.

The key safety requirement is mutual exclusion: the two processes must never both be in the critical section, nor may one be in the critical section while the other is still considered to hold the lock during its release step. Also, whenever any process is in or just leaving the critical section, the lock must be held. Include basic type-correctness invariants. No fairness, starvation-freedom, or eventual-entry liveness guarantee is required.
