Specify a small TLA+ demonstration model of a single Boolean flag. The flag should start in the set/true state, and every subsequent step may either leave it unchanged or flip it to the opposite Boolean value. The model should allow arbitrary stuttering, including executions that never change after the initial state, and should impose no fairness or progress requirement.

The purpose is to demonstrate basic temporal-property checking rather than model a real protocol. Include a property asserting that the flag is true as a temporal formula, which should hold because the initial state satisfies it, even though the flag is not required to remain true forever. The specification should also make clear that “the flag is always true” is not a valid invariant, since a legal behavior can flip it to false. A trivial always-true property may also be included as a sanity check. Ensure all reachable states keep the flag Boolean.
