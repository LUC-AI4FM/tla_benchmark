Specify a TLA+ specification for the classic Dining Philosophers problem with a fixed ring of N philosophers and N shared forks, where each fork can be held by at most one philosopher at a time. Philosophers repeatedly try to acquire both adjacent forks, eat once both are held, and then release them before trying again. Model a simple deadlock-avoidance strategy in which all philosophers except one acquire their two forks in the same order around the ring, while one distinguished philosopher acquires them in the opposite order.

The specification should include blocking behavior when a requested fork is unavailable and should assume fair scheduling of philosopher progress. It must guarantee the safety property that no two neighboring philosophers are eating simultaneously. It should also state the liveness property that the solution is starvation-free: every philosopher eventually eats, and indeed eats infinitely often, in every fair execution.
