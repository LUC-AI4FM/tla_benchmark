Specify a TLA+ specification for Lamport’s fast mutual exclusion algorithm for a finite set of competing processes, with a small model-checking instance of two processes. Each process repeatedly attempts to enter a critical section, may back off and retry when it detects interference, and releases any claim it successfully acquired after leaving the critical section. The model should capture the algorithm’s shared “doorway” state and per-process intent information abstractly enough to represent the known fast path and retry behavior.

The required safety property is mutual exclusion: at no reachable state may two distinct processes be genuinely in the critical section at the same time. Use weak fairness for each process’s execution so that continuously enabled process behavior is eventually taken. The required liveness property is system-wide progress: along every fair behavior, infinitely often at least one process is in the critical section. For the two-process configuration, include simple reachability/sanity checks showing that critical-section states and contention states are reachable.
