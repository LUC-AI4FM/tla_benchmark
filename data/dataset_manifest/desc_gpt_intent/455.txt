Specify a small TLA+ demonstration model of a 12-hour clock. The system should represent the currently displayed hour as a state value that is initially any valid hour from 1 through 12. Its non-stuttering behavior should model a clock tick: the displayed hour advances to the next hour, and after 12 it wraps around to 1. The specification should also permit stuttering steps, so it makes no progress or fairness guarantee that time must eventually advance.

The key safety requirement is that the displayed hour is always a valid 12-hour clock value, regardless of the initial valid hour and regardless of how many ticks or stuttering steps occur. Another safety requirement is that whenever the display changes, it changes only according to the 12-hour successor relation, including the wrap from 12 to 1. No stronger liveness property is required.
