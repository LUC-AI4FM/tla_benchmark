Specify a TLA+ specification for the classic two-jug water-measuring puzzle using a 3-gallon jug and a 5-gallon jug. The model should start with both jugs empty and allow exactly the usual legal operations: fill either jug to capacity, empty either jug, or pour from one jug into the other until the source is empty or the destination is full. The specification should explore all reachable configurations nondeterministically, without imposing fairness or requiring termination.

The safety requirement is that the amount of water in each jug always remains within its capacity. The model should demonstrate that it is possible to reach a configuration where the 5-gallon jug contains exactly 4 gallons. As a TLC test/demonstration, include checks that count the reachable configurations satisfying that 4-gallon condition and the possible pour transitions that change both jug levels, expecting 8 such configurations and 14 such transitions.
