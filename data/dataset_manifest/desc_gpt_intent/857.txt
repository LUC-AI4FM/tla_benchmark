Specify a TLA+ specification for the prisoner light-bulb puzzle. Model a finite nonempty set of prisoners, one designated beforehand as the counter, and a special cell containing a single lamp. The initial lamp state is either known to be off or, in a configurable harder variant, unknown. The warden repeatedly chooses one prisoner to enter the cell; after the game begins, prisoners cannot communicate and must follow the agreed strategy.

Ordinary prisoners signal by turning the lamp on when it is off, doing so at most once in the known-initial-state case and at most twice in the unknown-initial-state case. The counter turns the lamp off whenever seeing it on, counts such signals, and announces victory only after the appropriate threshold: all prisoners in the known case, or the doubled-count threshold for the unknown case.

The specification should assume fair selection so progress is possible, prove that an announcement eventually occurs, and guarantee safety: whenever victory is announced, every prisoner has actually visited the cell.
