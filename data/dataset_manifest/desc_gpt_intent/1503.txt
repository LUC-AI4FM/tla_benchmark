Specify a small TLA+ demonstration module for choosing a constant value from a filtered finite set. The model should have one state component whose initial value is selected nondeterministically from the numbers 1 through 5, after applying two predicate-style filters: the value must be greater than 1, and it must also be odd. The purpose is to illustrate that a reusable filtering construct can accept predicate parameters, including a predicate of two arguments where one argument is supplied by the filter itself.

After initialization, the system should not change state; every transition is a stuttering transition that preserves the chosen value. The key safety property is that the state value is always one of the allowed filtered results, namely 3 or 5. The key liveness expectation is simply stability: once an initial value is chosen, it remains the same forever, with no later evolution or additional choices.
