Specify a TLA+ specification for a small demonstration of Lamport’s fast mutual exclusion protocol with a finite set of processes, split into two syntactically distinct process groups that nevertheless run the same entry/exit protocol. Each process should repeatedly try to enter a critical section, using shared doorway state and per-process intent flags to coordinate. A process may back off and wait if another process appears to be ahead, may scan the other intent flags before proceeding, and may abandon a failed attempt and retry without being considered inside the critical section.

The model should be parameterized by the total number of processes and a split point between the two process groups, with the checked instance having two processes split one-and-one. Assume weak fairness for each process’s overall behavior so enabled progress is not ignored forever. The key safety property is mutual exclusion: no two distinct processes are ever simultaneously in the successful critical section. The key liveness property is that the system always eventually has some process successfully in the critical section, repeatedly over time.
