Specify a small TLA+ teaching example for asynchronous concurrency: a positive finite number of processes arranged in a ring, with each process having a binary flag and a binary recorded value. Initially all flags and recorded values are 0. Each process may run independently under arbitrary interleaving; during its execution it eventually reaches a completed state only after publishing its own flag as 1 and recording the current flag value of its immediate predecessor in the ring, using wraparound indexing.

The specification should allow arbitrary scheduling and stuttering, including stuttering after all processes have completed, rather than requiring fairness or guaranteed termination. It should maintain basic type safety: process identities remain within the finite ring, control states remain valid, and all stored values remain binary.

The main safety property to prove is the conditional postcondition: in every reachable state, if all processes have completed, then at least one process has recorded the value 1 from its predecessor.
