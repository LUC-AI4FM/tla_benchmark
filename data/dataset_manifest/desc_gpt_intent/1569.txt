Specify Lamport’s bakery mutual exclusion algorithm for a fixed finite set of processes. Each process repeatedly tries to enter a critical section, obtains a logical ticket based on the currently observed tickets, announces while it is choosing, waits for every other process either to be idle or to have lower priority, enters the critical section, and then releases its ticket. Priority is determined by smaller ticket number, with process identity used as the tie breaker.

The model should allow arbitrary interleavings of process steps and nondeterministic order of reading other processes. It should be suitable for finite-state TLC checking by allowing a configurable bound on ticket values as a state constraint.

The primary safety requirement is mutual exclusion: at no reachable state may two distinct processes be in the critical section simultaneously. No starvation-freedom or eventual-entry liveness property is required unless separate fairness assumptions are added.
