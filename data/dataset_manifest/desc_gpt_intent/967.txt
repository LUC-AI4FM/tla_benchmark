Specify a TLA+ specification for a finite search version of the balance-scale stone puzzle. The model should be parameterized by a total stone weight and a required number of pieces. It should consider ways of cutting the stone into that many positive integer-weight pieces whose weights sum to the total. A candidate cut is successful exactly when every integer target weight from 1 up to the total can be measured on a balance scale by placing each piece either on the target’s side, on the opposite side, or not using it.

The specification should let TLC exhaustively determine whether such a cut exists for the chosen parameters, reporting a valid cut if one exists and reporting that there is no solution otherwise. The key correctness requirements are soundness and completeness: any reported cut must have the right number of positive pieces, sum to the total weight, and weigh every required target; “no solution” may be reported only when no such cut exists.
