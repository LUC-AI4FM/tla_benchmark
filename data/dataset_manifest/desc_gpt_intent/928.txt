Specify a simple twelve-hour clock as a demonstration of equivalent TLA+ formulations. The model should have a single observable state: the displayed hour. It starts at a valid clock hour, 1 through 12. At each step the clock may either remain unchanged, to allow ordinary TLA+ stuttering, or perform a tick. A tick advances to the next hour in the usual cycle: 1 to 2, continuing through 11 to 12, and 12 back to 1. Present the behavior in a way that can be compared with an alternative formulation using arithmetic wraparound modulo 12, and assert that the two formulations describe exactly the same behaviors. The essential safety properties are that the display is always a valid hour and every non-stuttering change is precisely the cyclic successor. There is no progress requirement: the clock is not required to tick eventually, so infinite stuttering is permitted.
