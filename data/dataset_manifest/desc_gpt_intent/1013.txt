Specify a TLA+ specification for issuing vouchers using a two-phase-commit-style protocol. The model should include a finite set of vouchers, voucher holders, voucher issuers, and a voucher transaction provider acting as coordinator. Vouchers start unissued in a phantom lifecycle state, and holders and issuers start waiting. Holders and issuers may independently become prepared, or may independently abandon the transaction while it is still pending. The coordinator records prepared notifications and may issue a voucher only after every holder and issuer has prepared; alternatively, it may abort the transaction while it is still pending.

The specification should model asynchronous broadcast-style decision messages for issue and abort, with repeated receipt being harmless. On issue, the selected voucher becomes valid and enters the active lifecycle; holders may become holding and issuers may become issued. On abort, participants may become aborted and vouchers remain unissued. Prove type correctness, consistency between holder and issuer outcomes, and refinement of the basic voucher lifecycle. Do not require eventual issue, abort, or message receipt.
