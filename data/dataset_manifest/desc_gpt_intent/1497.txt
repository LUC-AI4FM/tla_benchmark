Specify a small TLA+ demonstration module for reasoning about finite sets, bounded integer intervals, and selecting a maximum element from a finite range. The system should have a single numeric state component initialized to zero, with initialization also including a sanity condition that every subset of the interval 1 through 17 has nonnegative cardinality. On each transition, the system nondeterministically chooses between two cases: compute the maximum element of the interval 1 through 10, or compute the maximum element of the interval 1 through 3, and store that result as the next state value.

The key safety requirements are that initialization establishes the state as zero, that every transition updates the state only to a value that is truly the maximum of the selected finite interval, and therefore every post-transition state value is either 10 or 3. The specification should not impose fairness, alternation, or eventual occurrence of either choice; infinite executions may repeatedly choose either case.
