Specify a small TLA+ model of processors issuing memory requests into per-processor queues. There should be finite sets of processors, registers, addresses, and values. A request is either a read or write over an address/value pair, and issuing a request associates it with a chosen register. Initially every processor has an empty queue and there is no ordering information. Each step may choose any processor, register, and request and append a corresponding queue entry for that processor.

The model should also maintain an abstract ordering relation over the identifiers of all issued queue entries, where an identifier consists of the issuing processor and the entry’s queue position. This relation may be extended as new entries are added, but existing ordering facts must never be removed. It must always be a strict transitive relation, and it must order entries from the same processor according to their queue positions. The key safety property is type correctness of the queues and ordering relation, together with preservation of this well-formed per-processor order. No additional liveness or fairness requirement is needed.
