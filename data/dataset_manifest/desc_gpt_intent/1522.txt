Specify Huang’s distributed termination detection algorithm for a finite set of processes with a distinguished leader. Model an asynchronous fully connected message-passing system in which computation is represented by processes becoming active, sending work messages that carry fractional credit, receiving messages in arbitrary order, and eventually becoming idle. Initially only the leader is active and owns the entire unit of credit; all other processes are idle and no messages are in transit. Credit must be represented exactly and conserved: at every reachable state, the sum of all credit held by processes plus all credit carried by in-transit messages is exactly one.

Non-leader processes that become idle must return their remaining credit to the leader. The leader may become idle and may collect returned credit. Termination is detected precisely when the leader is idle and has recovered the full unit credit. The specification should prove that detection is safe: once termination is detected, all processes are idle and no messages remain in transit forever. It should also prove the corresponding liveness condition that if the system has globally terminated, detection eventually occurs under appropriate weak fairness assumptions.
