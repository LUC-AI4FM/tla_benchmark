Specify a TLA+ specification for Lamport’s fast mutual exclusion protocol for a fixed natural number of processes. Each process repeatedly spends arbitrary time in a noncritical section, executes the entry protocol, enters the critical section, performs the exit protocol, and returns to the noncritical section. Model the protocol using shared registers that record a competing process’s identity and per-process intent indicators, including the restart and waiting behavior used when a process observes contention. The noncritical section should not be forced to make progress, but once a process is participating in the entry/exit protocol, its enabled internal protocol steps should be weakly fair.

The main safety requirement is mutual exclusion: no two distinct processes may ever be in the critical section at the same time. The progress requirement is conditional: if the system has infinitely many moments where at least one process is outside its noncritical section, then infinitely often some process must be in the critical section. Do not require starvation freedom for each individual process.
