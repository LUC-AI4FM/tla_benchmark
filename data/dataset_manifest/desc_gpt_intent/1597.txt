Specify a TLA+ specification for a parameterized fast mutual exclusion protocol for N concurrent processes that repeatedly try to enter a critical section. Each process should cycle forever through an entry protocol, either successfully entering the critical section or abandoning a failed attempt and retrying later. The model should include shared coordination state and per-process intent state sufficient to represent a “fast mutex” style algorithm, including withdrawal and waiting behavior when a process detects contention.

The specification should treat one process as a distinguished instance and the remaining processes as another process class, to demonstrate that the same protocol can be modeled with two different process definitions. Assume weak fairness for each process’s overall behavior.

The main safety property is mutual exclusion: at no reachable state may two distinct processes be successfully in the critical section at the same time. Attempts that have been marked failed and are being skipped must not count as critical-section occupancy. The liveness property to check is system-wide progress: always eventually, some process successfully reaches the critical section.
