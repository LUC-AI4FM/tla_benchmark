Specify a TLA+ specification for a shared-memory fast mutual exclusion algorithm for a finite set of competing processes. Each process repeatedly tries to enter a critical section, using shared coordination registers and per-process intent flags to take a fast path when uncontended and to back off and wait when contention is detected. A process that loses a race should withdraw its intent, wait until it is safe to retry or until other contenders have cleared their intent, and then either enter the critical section if it still owns the lock state or restart its attempt.

The model should capture arbitrary interleavings of the processes, with atomic program steps and weak fairness for overall progress. Initially no process is in the critical section and no process has announced intent. The key safety property is mutual exclusion: no two distinct processes may ever be in the critical section simultaneously. The liveness property required is global progress: along every fair behavior, infinitely often at least one process reaches the critical section. Individual starvation freedom is not required.
