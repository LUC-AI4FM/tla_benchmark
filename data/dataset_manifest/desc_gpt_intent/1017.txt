Specify a TLA+ specification for a simplified voucher transfer protocol based on two-phase commit. Model a fixed set of valid, working vouchers, a set of source holders initially holding vouchers, a set of destination holders initially waiting to receive them, and a single transaction provider coordinating the transfer. Holders may spontaneously announce that they are prepared, or may independently abort before participating further. The provider records prepared announcements, may commit the transfer only after every source and destination holder has prepared, and may instead abort the transaction before any final decision. Provider decisions are broadcast as persistent messages that holders can observe.

The specification should guarantee type correctness and basic agreement safety: the provider reaches at most one final decision; transfer is decided only after all participants have prepared; and no reachable state has a source holder considering the voucher transferred while any destination holder has aborted, or a source holder aborted while any destination holder considers itself holding the voucher. No fairness or progress guarantee is required; stuttering and nontermination are allowed.
