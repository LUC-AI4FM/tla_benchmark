Specify a TLA+ specification for a bounded single-producer, multi-consumer Disruptor-style ring buffer. The producer publishes events whose value is their monotonically increasing sequence number, up to a fixed maximum bound for model checking. The ring buffer has a fixed positive size and reuses slots by sequence-number indexing, but the producer must not overwrite any slot until every consumer has advanced far enough that the previous value in that slot is no longer needed.

Each consumer independently reads the published sequence in multicast fashion: every published event must be available to every consumer, and consumers may progress at different rates. The model should allow interleavings of producer and consumer access to buffer slots and should represent slot access sufficiently to detect overlapping unsafe reads and writes.

The safety requirements are that all state remains well typed, consumers only observe the correct sequence-number values in order, and no producer/consumer data races occur on ring-buffer slots. The liveness requirement is that, assuming fair consumer progress, every consumer eventually reads every value that has been published within the bounded run.
