Specify a TLA+ specification for a reusable barrier synchronization mechanism among a fixed set of N processes. Each process is always in one of two abstract states: before entering the barrier for the current round, or already arrived and waiting at the barrier. Initially, all processes are before the barrier. The model should allow any individual process that has not yet arrived to enter the barrier nondeterministically. Once every process has arrived, the barrier may release the whole group and begin a new round, returning all processes to the initial pre-barrier state.

The key safety requirement is that no process that has arrived at the barrier may be released or reset while any other process has not yet arrived. Thus, release is only permitted when all processes are waiting, and it happens for the group as a whole. Include a basic type/state invariant. Do not require fairness or progress: stuttering, delayed arrivals, and indefinite waiting are allowed.
