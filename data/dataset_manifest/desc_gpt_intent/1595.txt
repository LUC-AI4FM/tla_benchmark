Specify a small TLA+ demonstration model of a fair sequential counting loop. The system consists of one process with an integer counter initially at zero. While the counter is below ten, the process may perform a work step that increases it by exactly one. Once the counter has reached ten, the process is considered finished and may stutter forever so that terminated executions are represented as infinite behaviors.

The specification should require weak fairness for the process’s work/termination behavior, so an execution cannot remain forever before completion merely by stuttering while progress is continuously possible. Safety properties should ensure the counter never decreases, never skips values, never exceeds ten, and no behavior other than the intended counting and final stuttering is allowed. The key liveness property is that every fair behavior eventually reaches the finished state. Include simple reachability/coverage checks demonstrating that the state where the counter equals five occurs, and that the final increment from nine to ten occurs.
