Specify a small TLA+ model of a fair, terminating counter-like process. The system has a single numeric state component initialized to 1. Its intended progress behavior is to advance by exactly one on each real transition while the value is below 5. The model may allow stuttering between real transitions, but once the value reaches 5 it must remain there forever and never advance further.

The specification should include an appropriate weak fairness assumption ensuring that the process cannot stutter forever while an incrementing transition remains continuously possible. Under this fairness condition, every behavior starting from the initial value must eventually reach 5 and then stay at 5 permanently.

The key safety requirements are that the value starts at 1, changes only by single-step increments before termination, never skips values, and is stable after reaching 5. The key liveness requirement is eventual permanent convergence to 5.
