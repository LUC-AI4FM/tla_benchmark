Specify a TLA+ specification for a nondeterministic algorithm that computes a rooted spanning tree of an undirected, unweighted graph. The graph is finite, has no self-loops, represents edges as unordered pairs of distinct nodes, and includes a designated root. A natural-number bound at least as large as the number of nodes should be used as the “infinite” distance value.

The algorithm should maintain, for every node, a tentative distance from the root and a chosen predecessor. Initially, the root has distance zero and points to itself; all other nodes have infinite distance and point to themselves. Repeatedly, any node may improve its tentative distance using information from an adjacent node whose distance shows a shorter path; the new distance may be any smaller value that is at least one more than that neighbor’s distance, and the predecessor becomes that neighbor.

Require type correctness, eventual termination under a suitable fairness assumption, and the safety property that whenever the algorithm terminates, the root is correct, every reachable non-root node points to a neighbor exactly one level closer to the root, and unreachable nodes remain at infinite distance with no reachable neighbor.
