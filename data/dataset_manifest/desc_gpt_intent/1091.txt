Specify a TLA+ specification for a deliberately simplified PCR amplification cycle. Model a reaction mixture with a finite initial stock of double-stranded DNA and primers, no free single-stranded templates or primer-template hybrids, and a temperature that moves among three regimes: extension-ready, denaturing, and annealing. The model should allow repeated cycling: denaturing separates all double strands and hybrids into single templates, returning primers from hybrids; cooling changes only the temperature; annealing consumes some positive number of available primers and templates to form hybrids, bounded by what is available; extension converts all existing hybrids into double-stranded DNA.

The specification should track only these coarse molecule counts and ignore nucleotides, enzyme limits, failed reactions, and DNA reannealing. All molecule counts must always remain natural numbers and the temperature must always be one of the three regimes. The key safety property is conservation of material: free primers plus single templates plus twice the number of double-stranded molecules and hybrids remains equal to the initial weighted total. Do not require primer depletion or any progress/fairness guarantee.
