Specify a small TLA+ demonstration module for a one-bit toggling state machine, intended to exercise TLC’s treatment of bounded recursive predicates in initialization, invariants, and constraints. The model should have one state component whose legal values are Boolean. The initial condition should establish that the component begins as the negation of false, expressed indirectly through a recursive helper rather than as a direct equality. Each transition should replace the current Boolean value with its negation, so executions alternate between the two Boolean states and always have a next state.

Include a type-correctness invariant, also expressed through a simple recursive predicate, stating that the state component is always Boolean. Add a state constraint expressed via another recursive predicate that is true for all reachable legal states and therefore does not restrict the intended behavior. The key safety properties are Boolean type preservation and correct alternation on every transition. No additional fairness or liveness property is required beyond deadlock freedom of the toggling transition system.
