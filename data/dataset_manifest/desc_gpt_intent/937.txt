Specify a TLA+ specification for a simple live 12-hour clock. The modeled clock should have a single visible hour reading, initially any valid hour from 1 through 12. A real clock tick advances the reading by exactly one hour, wrapping from 12 back to 1. The behavior may allow stuttering steps that leave the displayed hour unchanged, but no other state changes are permitted.

Add a liveness requirement ensuring the clock cannot remain forever idle: whenever ticking is continuously possible, ticking must eventually occur. Since ticking is always possible in this model, the specification should guarantee infinitely many real tick steps.

The key safety property is type correctness: at all times the displayed hour remains in the set 1 through 12. The key liveness properties are that the clock ticks infinitely often and that every hour value from 1 through 12 is displayed infinitely often over any complete behavior.
