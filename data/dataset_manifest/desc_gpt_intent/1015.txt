Specify a TLA+ specification for the lifecycle of a finite set of vouchers. Each voucher begins as not-yet-issued, represented by a “phantom” state, with its lifecycle machine in an initial phase. A voucher may be issued exactly once, after which it becomes “valid” and its lifecycle is active/working. While valid, it may be transferred without changing its voucher state or lifecycle phase. A valid voucher may then be either redeemed or cancelled, and either outcome ends its lifecycle. Redeemed and cancelled vouchers are final and must not later become valid, phantom, or transition to the other final state.

The model should allow vouchers to evolve independently, one voucher action at a time, and should permit stuttering or indefinite inactivity; it need not require that vouchers are eventually issued, redeemed, or cancelled. The key safety properties are type correctness of all voucher and lifecycle states, and consistency between them: initial implies phantom, working implies valid, and done implies redeemed or cancelled.
