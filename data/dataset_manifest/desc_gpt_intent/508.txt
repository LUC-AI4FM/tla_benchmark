Specify a small TLA+ demonstration model for checking a simple arithmetic assertion over a nondeterministically chosen integer. The system should begin with one integer value selected from the inclusive range 1 through 10. Its only substantive requirement is that every allowed initial choice satisfies the safety condition that the square of the chosen value is at most 100. The model should include an assertion representing this condition, so that model checking confirms the arithmetic invariant for the entire initial range.

After the assertion has been evaluated, the computation may be considered complete and should not change the chosen value. The completed state may stutter indefinitely, as is customary for a terminated behavior in TLA+. Do not require any stronger liveness guarantee unless an explicit fairness or progress assumption is added; the essential purpose is to demonstrate initialization over a finite range and verification of a simple safety assertion.
