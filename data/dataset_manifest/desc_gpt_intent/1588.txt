Specify a TLA+ specification for the subtraction-based Euclidean algorithm computing the greatest common divisor of 24 and a nondeterministically chosen positive integer in the range 1 through a constant bound N. The model should remember the originally chosen input, repeatedly transform the working pair according to Euclid’s subtraction method, and stop when the computation has found the gcd, at which point the final value is reported as the gcd of 24 and the remembered input.

The specification should capture all possible initial choices in the allowed range. Its safety requirements should ensure the remembered input never changes, the computation never leaves the natural-number domain or performs an invalid subtraction, and the gcd of the working pair is preserved throughout the run. On termination, the reported result must equal the mathematical greatest common divisor of 24 and the original input. The liveness requirement is that every behavior eventually reaches the terminated state, with harmless stuttering allowed afterward.
