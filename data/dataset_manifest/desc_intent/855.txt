Specify a solution to the following puzzle: a warden places a group of prisoners in a situation where they must collectively determine when all of them have visited a certain room at least once. The room contains two switches, each up or down. The warden selects prisoners one at a time, in any order, and each selected prisoner must flip exactly one switch. The prisoners agree on a strategy in advance but cannot communicate afterward. At any point a prisoner may declare that everyone has visited the room; if correct they go free, if wrong they are all executed. The switches start in an arbitrary unknown position.

Model the agreed-upon counting strategy, in which one designated prisoner acts as counter and the rest are non-counters. The initial switch states are nondeterministic. Fairness must guarantee every prisoner is selected infinitely often. The specification must satisfy two properties: safety, meaning that whenever the counter declares completion every non-counter has indeed flipped the designated switch at least once; and liveness, meaning that the counter eventually reaches the completion condition and the prisoners are ultimately freed.
