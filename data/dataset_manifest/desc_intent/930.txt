Write a TLA+ specification for a simple 12-hour clock. The clock maintains a single hour counter that always holds a value between 1 and 12 inclusive. Each tick of the clock advances the hour by one, wrapping from 12 back to 1. The system begins in any valid hour state and transitions by this cyclic increment rule, with stuttering steps permitted. The key correctness property to verify is that the hour value remains within the range 1 through 12 at all times — that is, the valid-hour condition is an invariant of the specification. The theorem to establish is that the overall clock specification implies this invariant holds forever. Configure the model checker to treat the clock formula as the specification under check and to verify the valid-hour range condition as an invariant.
