Write a TLA+ specification that demonstrates a simple concurrent increment scenario with two independent processes sharing a single integer variable. Both processes start with the variable at zero and each atomically increments it by one. The two processes run concurrently, so their increments may occur in either order. The specification should model all possible interleavings of the two atomic increment steps. The key liveness requirement is termination: both processes must eventually complete their respective increments. No additional safety properties beyond what the model checker would verify by default need to be stated, making this a small illustrative example suitable for teaching basic concepts of concurrency and process interleaving in TLA+.
