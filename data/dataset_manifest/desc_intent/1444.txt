Write a TLA+ specification that demonstrates TLC's post-condition and "possible" counting features. Model a simple counter that starts at zero and increments by one each step until it reaches three. The system has no meaningful safety or liveness properties of its own — the point is to exercise TLC evaluation hooks that run after model checking completes. Specifically, configure two post-conditions that record the total number of generated states and the number of distinct states into TLC state slots, and a third post-condition that verifies expected counts for two named "possible" predicates. The possible predicates are: one that holds when the counter equals two, and one that identifies the transition where the counter moves from two to three. After checking, the post-condition should assert that each of those predicates was witnessed exactly once during the model check. Include a deliberately failing post-condition variant (not used in the main run) that checks an impossible state count, serving as a negative test.
