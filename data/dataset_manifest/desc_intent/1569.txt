Specify a concurrent bakery mutual exclusion algorithm for a configurable number of processes. Each process repeatedly tries to enter a critical section, using a two-phase ticket-based protocol: first it reads all other processes' current ticket numbers (while they are not in the process of choosing) to determine a maximum, then assigns itself a ticket one higher than that maximum, and finally waits until no other process with a lower ticket (breaking ties by process ID) is ahead of it. A boolean "choosing" flag per process signals when a ticket is being selected, allowing waiting processes to defer reading until the choice is complete. After leaving the critical section the process resets its ticket to zero and repeats. The ticket-reading and waiting phases each scan all other processes one at a time in nondeterministic order, making the concurrency granular. The key correctness property is mutual exclusion: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time. A state-space constraint bounding ticket values to a configurable maximum should be included to make model checking with TLC feasible for small configurations.
