Specify a Multi-Producer, Multi-Consumer (MPMC) Disruptor ring buffer. The system has a fixed number of producer threads and consumer threads sharing a bounded ring buffer. Producers atomically claim monotonically increasing sequence numbers, write their claimed sequence number as a value into the corresponding ring buffer slot, and then publish the slot. Multiple producers may claim sequences concurrently, so a slot is only considered available to consumers once all preceding slots have also been published. Consumers operate in multicast fashion: every consumer must independently read every published event in sequence order, and a producer may not overwrite a slot until all consumers have advanced past it.

The specification must guarantee two safety properties: no data races occur on ring buffer slots (no concurrent reads and writes or concurrent writes to the same slot), and the ring buffer type invariant holds throughout execution. For liveness, the specification must guarantee that under weak fairness of consumer steps, every consumer eventually reads all published values, and the value read at each position equals the sequence number claimed by whichever producer wrote it. The model should be bounded by a maximum number of published events and a configurable ring buffer size, with the number of producers and consumers left as parameters.
