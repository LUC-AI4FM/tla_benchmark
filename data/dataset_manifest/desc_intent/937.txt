Write a TLA+ specification for a simple 12-hour clock that ticks forward continuously. The clock has a single variable representing the current hour, which ranges over 1 through 12. Each tick advances the hour by one, wrapping from 12 back to 1. The specification must include a fairness condition ensuring that the clock never stops ticking. Three properties must hold: first, ticking actions occur infinitely often; second, every hour value from 1 to 12 is visited infinitely often; and third, the hour variable always remains within the valid range 1 through 12. This module should extend a base hour-clock module that defines the state predicate and next-state relation, adding weak fairness on the tick action to produce the live specification, then state and verify the theorem that the live specification implies all three properties.
