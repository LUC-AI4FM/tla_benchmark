Write a TLA+ specification for a real-time clock that tracks hours and demonstrates a bug fix for handling stuttering steps (of the form [A]_v) within next-state relations. The system models a hybrid clock combining a discrete hour-hand component (imported from a separate module) with a continuously advancing real-valued "now" timestamp and a timer variable that measures elapsed time between hour-change events. The clock starts with the hour component initialized, the timer at zero, and "now" at some real value. On each step, "now" may advance to any larger real value, the hour hand may tick according to the imported hour-clock definition, and the timer resets to zero on each hour-change and otherwise accumulates the elapsed real time. The key correctness properties are: the system satisfies the imported hour-clock specification as a refinement, a type invariant holds (now stays within the real range, hour stays in 1–12), and the real-time accuracy constraint is satisfied — specifically, every hour-hand transition occurs no sooner than SecondsPerHour minus Rho seconds and no later than SecondsPerHour plus Rho seconds after the previous one, where Rho is a positive tolerance constant.
