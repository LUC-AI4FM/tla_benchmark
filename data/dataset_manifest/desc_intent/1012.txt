Specify a voucher cancellation protocol modeled on Two-Phase Commit, involving three kinds of participants: a Voucher Transaction Provider (VTP) coordinating a set of Voucher Holders and a set of Voucher Issuers, over a fixed set of vouchers. Initially all vouchers are valid and in a working lifecycle state, holders are in a holding state, and issuers are in a waiting state. The protocol proceeds as follows: holders and issuers may spontaneously prepare for cancellation by sending Prepared messages to the VTP, or may spontaneously decide to abort without sending any message. The VTP collects Prepared messages and, once every holder and issuer has prepared, may broadcast a Cancel decision, marking the voucher cancelled and the lifecycle done. At any point before that, the VTP may spontaneously broadcast an Abort. Holders and issuers transition accordingly upon receiving Cancel or Abort broadcasts. The key safety invariant is consistency: no holder must end up cancelled while any issuer is aborted, and vice versa. The specification must also maintain type correctness for all state components throughout execution.
