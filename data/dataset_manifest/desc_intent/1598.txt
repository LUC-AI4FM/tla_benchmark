Specify a mutual exclusion algorithm for N processes (where N is a given natural number constant) that uses three shared variables: an integer x, an integer y (both initially 0), and a boolean array b indexed by process identifiers (initially all false). Each process repeatedly attempts to enter a critical section by executing a fast-path mutex protocol loosely based on Lamport's fast mutex algorithm. A process signals its intent by setting its flag in b and writing its identity to x and y, then checks for contention by examining y and x in sequence. If contention is detected, the process backs off, clears its flag, waits until y is clear, and retries. If it survives the contention checks, it enters the critical section, then clears y and its flag before looping. The specification should use weak fairness over all process steps. The key safety property is mutual exclusion: no two distinct processes may be in the critical section simultaneously. The liveness property is that some process enters the critical section infinitely often.
