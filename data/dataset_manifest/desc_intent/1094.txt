Specify a TLA+ model of the Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) process that enforces a strict stage-based sequencing of thermal steps. The system tracks double-stranded DNA, single-stranded template, primer-template hybrids, free primers, the current temperature, a stage variable encoding which phase was last completed, and a cycle counter. Starting from an initial stock of double-stranded DNA and primers, the process must cycle through denaturation (heating to separate strands), cooling to a ready state, annealing (primers bind to templates at a warm temperature), and extension (hybrids become new double strands at a hot temperature, incrementing the cycle count). The stage variable must constrain transitions so that only the next meaningful thermal step is permitted, preventing arbitrary temperature changes. The system must satisfy a type invariant over all variables. Liveness must guarantee that, given weak fairness on every step, primers are eventually depleted and the process continues cycling as long as primers and templates remain available. The model should be checked against a previously established clean-PCR specification as a refinement property, confirming that the stage-constrained model is consistent with the simpler version.
