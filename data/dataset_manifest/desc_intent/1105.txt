Specify a parameterized TLA+ model of the asynchronous Non-Blocking Atomic Commitment (NBAC) algorithm with failure detectors, for N processes. Each process initially votes either YES or NO. Correct processes broadcast their vote; faulty processes may crash at any time, potentially before or after sending their vote, and the failure detector reports (possibly prematurely) which processes are suspected. A process that has sent its vote then receives votes from others and decides to either COMMIT or ABORT. A process aborts immediately if it suspects any failure or receives any NO vote; it commits only if it receives YES from all N processes and no NO vote.

The specification must satisfy four correctness properties. Agreement: no two correct processes decide differently (one COMMIT and one ABORT must never coexist). Abort validity: if any process votes NO, no process ever commits. Commit validity: if all processes vote YES and none is suspected, no process aborts. Termination: if no process ever crashes and no process is ever suspected, every process eventually reaches a decision. Consider three initialization scenarios — arbitrary votes, all YES, all NO — and check all properties under weak fairness of non-crash actions.
