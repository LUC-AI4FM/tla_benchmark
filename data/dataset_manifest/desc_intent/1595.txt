Write a TLA+ specification demonstrating a simple fairly-scheduled counter that increments from 0 up to 10. The system starts with a counter at zero and repeatedly increments it by one as long as it remains below 10, then halts. The key correctness property to verify is termination: the system must eventually finish, meaning the counter must eventually reach 10 and the process must complete. The specification should use weak fairness to ensure the loop is not allowed to stall indefinitely — the incrementing step must eventually execute whenever it is continuously enabled. Additionally, the model checker should confirm that exactly one state is reachable where the counter equals 5, and exactly one state is reachable where the counter equals 9 and is about to transition to 10, verifying that the execution path through the loop is deterministic and unique with no branching.
