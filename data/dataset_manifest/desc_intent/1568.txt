Specify Lamport's Bakery mutual exclusion algorithm for a fixed number of concurrent processes. Each process repeatedly tries to enter a critical section, uses a ticket-based mechanism to establish a priority ordering, and then exits before trying again. To obtain a ticket, a process first signals that it is choosing a number, scans all other processes' current numbers to find the maximum, sets its own number to one more than that maximum, then clears its choosing flag. Before entering the critical section, a process waits for every other process to finish choosing, then waits until it has priority over each other process — priority determined by ticket number, with process ID breaking ties when numbers are equal. The critical section itself is a no-op placeholder.

The key safety property is mutual exclusion: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time. The model should support a configurable number of processes and a bounded maximum ticket value for finite-state checking. Verify the mutual exclusion invariant holds across all reachable states.
