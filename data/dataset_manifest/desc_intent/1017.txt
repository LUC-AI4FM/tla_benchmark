Specify a voucher transfer protocol based on the Two-Phase Commit protocol, as described in RFC 3506. The system involves a set of vouchers, a set of source voucher holders (who currently hold the vouchers and wish to transfer them), a set of destination voucher holders (who are waiting to receive the vouchers), and a single Voucher Transaction Provider (VTP) that coordinates the transfer. Source holders begin in a holding state and destination holders begin in a waiting state. Each holder can independently decide to prepare for the transfer or spontaneously abort; prepared holders send a Prepared message to the VTP. Once the VTP has received Prepared messages from all source and destination holders, it may broadcast a Transfer decision; at any earlier point it may spontaneously broadcast an Abort decision. Upon receiving a Transfer message, a source holder moves to transferred and a destination holder moves to holding; upon receiving an Abort message, both move to aborted. The key safety property is consistency: no source holder must reach the transferred state while any destination holder is aborted, and no source holder must be aborted while any destination holder reaches holding. The specification should also refine a voucher lifecycle specification in which vouchers remain valid and working throughout the transfer process.
