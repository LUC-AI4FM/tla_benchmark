Write a TLA+ specification that models a single integer variable constrained to evolve only through valid transitions. The variable must start at zero and, in every step, the next value must satisfy a specific predicate: it must be an even number in the range 0 through 9 (inclusive). Stuttering steps are permitted. The specification should express this purely as a constraint on the next-state value, with no explicit action structure beyond what is needed to enforce the predicate on every transition. There are no additional safety or liveness properties to check beyond what is implied by the state constraint itself — the goal is simply to demonstrate how a next-state predicate can restrict all possible transitions of a variable throughout an execution.
