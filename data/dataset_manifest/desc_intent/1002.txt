Specify a barrier synchronization primitive for N processes. Each process repeatedly cycles through two phases: first it signals that it has arrived at the barrier, then it waits until all N processes have arrived before proceeding. The barrier reset must be atomic — all processes are released together only once every process has checked in. The key safety property is that no process may leave the barrier (return to its pre-arrival state) while any other process has not yet arrived; in other words, a process that has already reached the barrier cannot be released as long as even one other process is still approaching it. The system should be modeled with N as a parameter (configured to 6 for checking), and the specification should verify a type invariant confirming each process is always in one of its two valid states, as well as the barrier property expressed as a stuttering-tolerant action property ensuring no premature releases occur.
