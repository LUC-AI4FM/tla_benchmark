Write a TLA+ specification for a simple countdown system that starts at a given non-negative integer constant N and decrements by one on each step until it reaches zero, where it remains. The system should guarantee that the counter never goes negative. The specification should assume N is a natural number, initialize the counter to N, and allow either a decrement step when the counter is positive or a stuttering step when it has reached zero. The key safety property to verify is that the counter is always greater than or equal to zero. Use a concrete value of N (such as 4) for model checking.
