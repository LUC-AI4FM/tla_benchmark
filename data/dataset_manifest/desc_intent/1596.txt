Write a TLA+ specification for Lamport's fast mutual exclusion algorithm running across a fixed set of N processes split into two syntactically distinct groups: processes numbered 1 through M, and processes numbered M+1 through N. Both groups execute identical mutual exclusion logic but are modeled as separate process types to exercise PlusCal's translation when two different process declarations are present. Each process repeatedly tries to enter a critical section using shared registers: a flag array indicating interest, and two shared variables used for fast-path coordination. A process that detects contention backs off and waits until conditions are clear before retrying. The specification should assume weak fairness for all processes. The key safety property is mutual exclusion: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time, regardless of which group it belongs to. The key liveness property is that some process always eventually enters the critical section. The model should be checkable with small concrete values of N and M.
