The VoucherCancel module specifies a voucher cancellation protocol modeled as a Two-Phase Commit (2PC) interaction among three kinds of participants: a set of Vouchers (V), Voucher Holders (H), and Voucher Issuers (I), coordinated by a single Voucher Transaction Provider (VTP). The protocol governs how vouchers transition from "valid" to "cancelled" (or how the cancellation is aborted), tracking each voucher's state, each holder's state (holding, prepared, cancelled, aborted), each issuer's state (waiting, prepared, cancelled, aborted), the VTP's state (init or done), the set of participants that have sent Prepared messages to the VTP, and the accumulated message set.

Initially all vouchers are valid and working, all holders are holding, all issuers are waiting, and no messages have been sent. In each step, holders and issuers may spontaneously prepare (sending a Prepared message) or choose to abort silently; the VTP collects Prepared messages from both holders and issuers and, once all of H ∪ I have prepared, may broadcast a Cancel message and mark the voucher cancelled, or it may spontaneously broadcast an Abort message at any time while still in its initial state. Holders and issuers react to Cancel or Abort broadcasts by moving to the corresponding terminal state. The safety invariant VTPConsistent asserts that no holder reaches "cancelled" while any issuer reaches "aborted," and vice versa. The specification also asserts refinement of a VoucherLifeCycle module. The model-checking configuration instantiates three vouchers, three holders, and three issuers, checks VTPTypeOK and VTPConsistent as invariants, and disables deadlock checking.
