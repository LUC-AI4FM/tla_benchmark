The module models the Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) at high resolution, tracking the molecular species that appear across successive thermal cycles: long-long double-stranded DNA, short-long and tiny-short intermediate doubles, primer-template hybrids of three lengths, free single-stranded templates, free primers, and the fully defined "tiny-tiny" product double strand. Constants `DNA` and `PRIMER` set the initial counts of double-stranded template and primer molecules respectively.

The system cycles through four temperature-driven actions. `heat` raises temperature from `Hot` to `TooHot`, denaturing all doubles and hybrids back into single-stranded templates and recovering primers. `cool` drops temperature back to `Hot`, marking the system ready for annealing. `anneal` lowers temperature to `Warm` and nondeterministically binds some number of primers across the three template types, forming hybrids. `extend` returns temperature to `Hot`, converting each hybrid into its corresponding longer double-stranded form and incrementing the cycle counter; notably, only `tinyHybrid` extension directly produces the desired `product`. The initial state has all DNA as `longLongDouble` with no templates, hybrids, or product yet formed.

The specification enforces weak fairness on all four actions, ensuring cycles proceed indefinitely. Two invariants are checked: `TypeOK` (all variables are natural numbers or valid enumerated strings) and `thirdCycleProduct` (no product can exist before cycle 3, reflecting the known PCR property that discrete product requires at least three full cycles). The module also instantiates a coarser `stages` module, verifying that the detailed model refines the abstract staged behavior and that the `primerDepleted` liveness property from that abstraction is preserved.
