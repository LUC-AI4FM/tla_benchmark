The module FastMutex2 is a model-checking test for a fast mutual exclusion algorithm, structured specifically to exercise the PlusCal-to-TLA+ translation when two syntactically distinct process types share the same algorithmic logic. Constants N and M partition the process set: Proc1 processes are indexed 1..M and Proc2 processes are indexed (M+1)..N, with the model-checking configuration fixing N=2 and M=1, yielding one process of each type.

All processes implement Lamport's fast mutex protocol. Each process repeatedly attempts to enter a critical section by setting a boolean flag b[self], writing its identity to shared variable x, and then checking shared variable y. If y is nonzero, the process clears its flag and waits until y returns to zero before retrying. Otherwise, it writes self to y and then checks whether x still equals self; if not, it clears its flag, waits for all b[j] flags to be false, and then checks whether y still equals self, setting a local failure flag if y has changed and waiting for y to become zero. A process enters the critical section only when the failure flag is not set, after which it clears y and its own b flag. The global variables x, y, and b are shared; each process family has its own local loop counter and failure flag. The temporal specification is the conjunction of the initial-state predicate, the always-enabled next-state relation, and weak fairness for every process. The safety invariant asserts mutual exclusion — no two distinct processes are simultaneously in their respective critical-section labels with failure flags clear — and the liveness property asserts that some process is infinitely often in the critical section.
