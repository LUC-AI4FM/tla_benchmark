The module specifies the Yo-Yo leader-election algorithm with pruning, verified over all undirected, loop-free, connected graphs on a fixed number of nodes `N`. Unlike a companion module that fixes a particular graph, the `Init` predicate existentially quantifies over all strongly connected subsets of the complete edge set, so model checking explores every possible initial topology.

Each node maintains Boolean `active` status, a current `phase` ("down" or "up"), sets of `incoming` and `outgoing` neighbors (establishing an initial orientation by node identity: lower-numbered nodes point to higher-numbered ones), and a `mailbox` of pending messages. The algorithm alternates between a down phase, in which nodes propagate the minimum identity received from upstream, and an up phase, in which nodes send "yes"/"no" replies back to their incoming neighbors and selectively prune or reverse edges. Sources initiate the down phase immediately; internal nodes and sinks wait until all expected down messages have arrived. During the up phase, edges carrying "no" replies are reversed, pruned edges are removed, and lone sinks deactivate themselves. The next-state relation allows any enabled node to execute either its down or up action, and weak fairness ensures every continuously enabled action is eventually taken.

The specification checks four properties: `TypeOK` (structural well-formedness of all variables), `NeighborInv` (consistency of incoming/outgoing sets across nodes, accounting for in-flight reversal or pruning messages), `FinishIffTerminated` (deadlock occurs exactly when the termination condition holds), and the liveness property `Liveness`, asserting that the algorithm eventually reaches a state where the minimum-identity node is the sole leader, all other nodes are inactive, and all mailboxes are empty.
