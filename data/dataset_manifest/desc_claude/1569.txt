The module CBakery specifies Lamport's Bakery mutual exclusion algorithm for a configurable number of processes, translated from a PlusCal algorithm into TLA+. The system models `NumProcs` processes (identified by integers 1 through `NumProcs`) competing for access to a critical section using a ticket-based priority scheme.

Each process has shared variables `num` (its bakery ticket number, initially 0) and `choosing` (a boolean flag indicating it is currently selecting a number), plus per-process local variables `read`, `max`, and `nxt`. A process begins each iteration by setting `choosing` to TRUE and scanning all other processes' ticket numbers (labels `d1`–`d2`) to pick a number one greater than the current maximum. It then clears `choosing` (label `d3`) and enters a waiting loop (`w1`–`w2`) that checks each other process: it waits until that process is not choosing, then waits until the other process either holds no ticket, holds a strictly higher ticket, or holds the same ticket but has a higher process ID. Once all processes have been checked, the process enters the critical section (`cs`) and upon exit resets its ticket to zero before looping again.

The temporal specification is `Init /\ [][Next]_vars` (safety only, no fairness). The model-checking configuration sets `NumProcs = 2` and `MaxNum = 2`, enforces a state constraint bounding all ticket numbers to at most `MaxNum`, and checks the mutual exclusion invariant asserting that no two distinct processes are simultaneously at label `cs`.
