This module is a small regression test that checks whether built-in TLA+ operators can be passed as arguments to user-defined operators. It defines a higher-order helper `Sum(F(_, _), x, y)` that applies its operator argument to two values, and then uses it with integer addition and set union. It also defines a simple membership helper and verifies Boolean-set membership, while `Init` and `Next` are trivial `TRUE` predicates and no temporal behavior, fairness, fault model, or proofs are present.
