This specification models a client, server, and message-sequencer interaction in which responses and events may be sent, duplicated, dropped, and received out of order. The server emits response messages with sequence and index metadata and emits event messages that refer to prior response indices; the client either accepts messages immediately when their ordering constraints are satisfied or queues them in pending sets otherwise. The spec includes a type invariant asserting that the ordered `responses` sequence respects the intended relationship between responses and events, and its temporal specification is a simple initialization plus next-state relation with no explicit fairness assumptions.
