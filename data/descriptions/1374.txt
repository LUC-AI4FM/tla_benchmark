This specification models a very small module used to exercise name overloading between a module's own declarations and names imported through an INSTANCE. The module declares a constant and a variable, instantiates module Github362B with explicit substitutions for x and C, and uses Print calls in Init to demonstrate that the local variable overloadedName and constant overloadedConst are distinct from Abstract!overloadedName and Abstract!overloadedConst inside the instantiated module. The behavior itself is trivial: the single variable never changes, and the checked property is the instantiated module's Spec operator.
