This module is a small regression-style TLA+ specification that tests Cartesian product behavior, tuple membership, and function construction over tuple domains. It models a trivial one-variable system where x is always 1 and the next-state relation is stuttering, while the main logical content is an invariant composed of a sequence of IF-THEN-ELSE checks that call TLC's Assert and Print operators. The specification checks properties involving \X, SUBSET, tuple membership in products of sets, intersections with function sets, and functions defined on Cartesian-product domains; it does not model faults or distributed behavior, and it is primarily intended as a TLC test of language semantics.
