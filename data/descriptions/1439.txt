This specification models Dijkstra's EWD 840 distributed termination detection algorithm on a ring of N nodes. The system tracks whether nodes are active or passive, the color of each node, and the position and color of a circulating token; the environment can activate other nodes by sending messages or deactivate active nodes, while the termination-detection mechanism controls token passing. The spec defines safety and liveness properties about detecting global termination, includes Dijkstra's inductive invariant, and uses weak fairness for the token-control subsystem in the main specification, with an alternate stronger fairness variant for comparison.
