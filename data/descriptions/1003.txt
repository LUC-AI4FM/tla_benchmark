This module specifies and proves properties of a reusable barrier synchronization algorithm for N concurrent processes using two gates and a lock. The PlusCal algorithm models a two-chamber barrier in which processes first accumulate in a first waiting chamber, then pass through a second waiting chamber, preventing any process from re-entering the barrier too early and overtaking others. The translated TLA+ specification defines the operational steps a0 through a12, proves type correctness, mutual exclusion in the lock-protected critical section, several barrier invariants and a flush invariant, and finally proves that the detailed barrier implementation refines an abstract instantiated Barrier specification through a translated program counter view.
