This module defines real-time temporal constraints over actions by introducing an explicit real-valued time variable `now` and auxiliary timer behavior. The operator `RTBound(A, v, D, E)` specifies that there exists a timer `t` which is reset when action `A` occurs or when `A` becomes disabled in the next state, otherwise advances by the elapsed real time `now' - now`, while also enforcing an upper bound `E` and a lower bound `D` relative to occurrences of `A`. The operator `RTnow(v)` constrains `now` to range over the reals, to increase monotonically through a stuttering-insensitive next-state action `NowNext`, and adds a weak fairness condition ensuring time can always progress past any real threshold.
