This specification models a channel-based version of Dijkstra-Scholten style distributed termination detection derived from EWD998, but using arbitrary node identifiers arranged in a ring instead of natural-numbered nodes. A distinguished initiator circulates a token around the ring while nodes may send and receive payload messages; local counters, node colors, and a vector clock are maintained, and termination detection is based on the token returning white with zero accumulated deficit while the initiator is passive and white. The spec defines initialization, token-passing and environment actions, asserts a weak fairness condition for token-system actions, proves a bounded-round property stating that detection occurs within at most three token rounds after termination, and also includes a refinement mapping to an instantiated EWD998Chan specification plus safety and liveness refinement theorems.
