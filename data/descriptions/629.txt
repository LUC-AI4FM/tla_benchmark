This specification models a high-level abstraction of the Tendermint blockchain, focusing on the light client perspective. It assumes all validators have equal voting power and models the blockchain as a sequence of block headers, each containing validator sets, commit sets, and timestamps. The model includes mechanisms for tracking faulty nodes, enforcing a trusting period, and ensuring that blocks are committed with sufficient validator agreement (more than two-thirds). The specification defines initial conditions for the blockchain, constraints on validator sets and faulty nodes, and an action for advancing the global clock. The design is simplified for analysis, omitting some implementation details such as block hashes and focusing on validator set transitions and commit rules.
