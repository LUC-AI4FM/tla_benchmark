This specification is a minimal working example designed to reproduce issue #1126. It models a single variable `seq` that stores a sequence, starts from the empty sequence, and then nondeterministically assigns `seq` to some sequence drawn from `Seq({1, 2, 3})`. The file is very small, has no fault model, no temporal `Spec` operator, and no proof content; it primarily exercises sequence typing and existential nondeterministic next-state assignment.
