This module provides a TLAPS proof for the InternalMemory specification, specifically proving that the type invariant (TypeInvariant) is preserved by the system under the ISpec specification. The proof addresses the fact that TypeInvariant alone is not inductive due to the next-state expression in Do(p) accessing fields of buf[p] that are only valid when buf[p] is in MReq. To resolve this, the invariant is strengthened with BufConsistent, which ensures that buf[p] has the correct type depending on the value of ctl[p]. The proof is structured hierarchically and demonstrates that the strengthened invariant (Inv) is inductive, and thus TypeInvariant is always preserved.
