This module specifies Lamport's Bakery mutual exclusion algorithm for a fixed set of processes. It includes a PlusCal algorithm in comments together with its generated TLA+ translation, modeling each process choosing a ticket number, waiting according to the bakery ordering, entering a critical section, and then releasing its ticket. The specification checks safety through a mutual exclusion invariant, includes a state constraint bounding ticket values for TLC exploration, and defines auxiliary predicates for whether some process is in the critical section and whether a choose-number step occurs.
