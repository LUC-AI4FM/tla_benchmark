This module defines a collection of state invariants over an external transaction history, for a system modeled in another module named ExternalHistory. The history contains read-write and read-only requests and responses, transaction IDs, observed transaction sequences, and transaction status events such as committed or invalid. The invariants characterize request/response ordering, uniqueness of transactions and transaction IDs, constraints on committed and invalid statuses, observation consistency, serializability, linearizability, and ordered speculative linearizability, along with a few debugging invariants that intentionally characterize special reachable states such as having no committed or invalid transactions.
