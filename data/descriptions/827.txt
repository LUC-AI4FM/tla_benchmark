This specification models the classic missionaries and cannibals river-crossing puzzle. The state records which bank the boat is on and, for each bank, which people are present; a step consists of moving a nonempty set of one or two people across the river in the boat while preserving safety on both banks. The spec uses set and cardinality reasoning to express the safety condition that missionaries are never outnumbered by cannibals on a bank when missionaries are present, and it defines a state predicate intended to be checked as an invariant so TLC can produce a counterexample trace that serves as a solution to the puzzle.
