This specification models traffic over a single-lane bridge where cars may travel in two opposite directions, represented by the disjoint sets CarsRight and CarsLeft. The bridge admits concurrent movement only for cars traveling in the same direction, and the specification enforces safety conditions preventing cars going in opposite directions from occupying the bridge simultaneously or colliding within bridge positions. The model defines movement, waiting behavior before entering the bridge, weak fairness assumptions for movement and entry actions, and verifies safety invariants, typing constraints, and liveness properties stating that cars eventually enter and eventually exit the bridge.
