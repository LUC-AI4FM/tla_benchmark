This specification models a one-bit mutual exclusion protocol for N processes, originally written in PlusCal and translated to TLA+. Each process repeatedly cycles through a noncritical section, raises its own flag, scans other processes, may back off if it sees a competing flag before its own turn, and eventually enters and exits a critical section. The spec assumes a nonempty process set Procs = 0..(N-1), uses weak fairness for each process when it is outside the noncritical section, and defines the overall behavior with Init, Next, and a temporal Spec formula.
