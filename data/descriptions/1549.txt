This specification models a parameterized, partially synchronous distributed system with eventually perfect failure detectors, as described in Chandra and Toueg's classic paper. It composes two other modules: Age_Channel for the communication system (modeling message delivery and aging) and EPFailureDetector for the behavior of correct processes. The system tracks process crashes, message delivery, local and global clocks, and the evolution of process suspicions about failures. The model encodes environmental and local clocks in a way that ensures finiteness when parameters are fixed, and enforces constraints on message delay (DELTAConstraint) and process activity (PHIConstraint) to capture partial synchrony. The specification verifies two key properties: Strong Completeness (crashed processes are eventually permanently suspected by all correct processes) and Eventually Strong Accuracy (correct processes are eventually never suspected by other correct processes). Notable design decisions include the explicit modeling of process transitions, message aging, and the use of TLC for model checking small instances, with comments on the challenges of state explosion for certain constraints.
