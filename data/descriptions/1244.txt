This module, PlusCal, specifies the translation of a PlusCal (+cal) algorithm into a TLA+ specification. It assumes the existence of an abstract syntax tree (AST) for the algorithm and a fairness condition, both provided by the AST module. The specification is divided into three main parts: (1) a precise definition of the AST structure for +cal algorithms, including all syntactic constructs and their representation as records and sequences; (2) a mechanism for checking the correctness of labels and goto statements in the AST, ensuring there are no duplicate labels and all gotos refer to valid labels; and (3) the translation process itself, which recursively traverses the AST to produce a TLA+ specification, handling variable declarations, control flow, procedures, processes, and fairness. The translation introduces variables such as pc and stack to model control state and procedure calls, and includes detailed handling of assignments, calls, returns, and the addition of subscripts and primes to variables as needed. The module is intended to be a faithful and complete specification of the +cal-to-TLA+ translation as of its last modification date, with extensive comments explaining design decisions and translation subtleties.
