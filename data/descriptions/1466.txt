This specification models a very small TLC-executed state machine with two variables, x and y, whose next-state relation chooses new values for both variables from 1..10 while also updating TLC's internal hyperproperty-style aggregate store via TLCSet and TLCGet. The comments explain that the purpose is to test that basic hyperproperty aggregation works correctly even when TLC uses multiple workers, and that users can aggregate per-worker results afterward. The postcondition checks TLC-maintained metadata and accumulated values, including that the collected map has keys {R, 42}, that the per-worker tuple for R has length equal to the configured worker count, and that the sum of those per-worker counts equals TLC's reported number of distinct states.
