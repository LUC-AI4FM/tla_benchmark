This module defines a smoke-testing wrapper around the EWD998 specification. It does not define the full algorithm itself in this file; instead, it redefines the initial predicate through SmokeInit to randomly sample from the space of initial states, while also providing one concrete initial state to ensure at least one initial state always exists. The module also defines a StopAfter helper that uses TLC runtime configuration and statistics to terminate simulation-oriented smoke tests after a short time budget or a bounded number of generated traces, and it includes CHOOSE-based definitions BF and PN derived from environment values.
