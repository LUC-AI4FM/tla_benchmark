This module contains TLAPS proofs for safety-related theorems of a voting/Paxos-style specification imported from the module Voting. It proves helper facts about quorums and choosability, and then proves a central safety theorem showing that if the invariant Inv holds and a quorum shows safety at ballot b for value v, then SafeAt(b, v) holds. The file is proof-oriented rather than operational: it introduces no variables or actions of its own, and focuses on structured proofs using assumptions, quorum intersection arguments, ballot arithmetic, and invariant expansion.
