This module defines a collection of operators for reasoning about functions in TLA+, including restriction, range, pointwise operations, inverses, and properties such as injectivity, surjectivity, and bijectivity. It provides definitions for restricting a function to a subset of its domain or to values satisfying a predicate, checking if one function is a restriction of another, and constructing the range of a function. The module also defines operators for constructing sets of injections, surjections, and bijections between sets, as well as checking for their existence. Additional operators include pointwise combination of functions and folding over function values. The module is utility-focused and does not specify a distributed algorithm or system, nor does it include temporal properties or fault models.
