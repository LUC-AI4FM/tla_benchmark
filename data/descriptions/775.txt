This module contains TLAPS proofs for a CRDT specification imported from the module CRDT. It proves safety-style results such as type correctness, an invariant called Safety, and a monotonicity property, and then develops a natural-number-valued termination measure based on the disagreement between nodes' replicated counters. Using that measure together with fairness assumptions for the Gossip action, it proves convergence/liveness results showing that under fair gossip all nodes eventually agree on the counter values.
