This module specifies, at the TLA+ level, the translation from the abstract syntax tree of a global-naming PlusCal (+CAL) algorithm into a TLA+ specification. It defines the grammar of legal abstract syntax trees for algorithms, procedures, processes, labeled statements, and related constructs, and then defines operators that translate such trees into lexeme sequences representing TLA+ output, including Init, Next, Spec, and a Termination property. The file also models fairness options for the generated specification, supporting no fairness, weak fairness of process actions, weak fairness of Next, and strong fairness of process actions; it is designed as an executable specification for TLC, with numerous comments about representation hacks, limitations, formatting omissions, and error cases in translation.
