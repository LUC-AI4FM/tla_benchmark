This module defines the mathematical structure and operations for bags (multisets), where a bag is a set that can contain multiple copies of the same element, with each element's multiplicity being a positive integer. The bag is represented as a function from its domain to the positive integers, and the module provides operators for bag membership, union, difference, subset, subbag enumeration, mapping, and cardinality. The specification is purely functional and declarative, with no temporal or action-based properties, and is intended for use as a standard mathematical library; most operators are overridden by TLC for model checking, except for SubBag, which is defined in a way that TLC can evaluate. The design is focused on mathematical correctness and TLC compatibility, with careful attention to finite support and computability.
