This module provides a TLAPS proof that the implementation of a two-phase protocol (as specified in the TwoPhase module) refines another specification (A!Spec) under a refinement mapping. The proof focuses on the safety part of the specification, showing that the implementation maintains the invariant and that the refinement mapping preserves the required properties. The proof is structured hierarchically, with temporal and non-temporal leaf proofs, and uses backend solvers such as Z3 and PTL for certain steps. The design follows a standard approach for refinement proofs, with the temporal leaf proofs being reusable across similar specifications.
