This specification models the classic coffee can bean problem, where a can initially contains some number of black and white beans and one repeatedly removes two beans according to color-dependent replacement rules. The state is a single record variable representing the current numbers of black and white beans, bounded by a constant maximum bean count for finite-state model checking. The spec defines actions for removing two black beans, two white beans, or one of each, includes a termination stuttering action when exactly one bean remains, and verifies a type invariant, a monotonic decrease property on total bean count, eventual termination under weak fairness, a parity-based loop invariant on the number of white beans, and a hypothesis relating the parity of the initial white-bean count to the color of the final remaining bean.
