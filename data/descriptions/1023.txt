This specification models the one-communication-step consensus algorithm with crash faults from the cited paper, focusing only on the algorithm itself and not the underlying consensus mechanism. Processes can propose values, receive broadcast messages, crash subject to a bound, decide based on thresholds over received proposed messages, and relay decision messages. The spec defines the transition system, a weak fairness condition for message receipt and progress actions, and asserts safety and liveness-style properties such as type correctness, validity, agreement, weak agreement, termination, and a stronger inductive strengthening predicate.
