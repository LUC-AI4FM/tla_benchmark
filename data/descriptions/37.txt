This module defines Apalache-compatible helper operators for bag (multiset) manipulation, intended to rewire or replace corresponding operators from the CommunityModules BagsExt module. It provides bag element addition, single removal, complete removal, and aggregate computations such as sum and product over bag contents, while explicitly marking unsupported fold-style bag operators as not supported by the model checker. The specification is purely definitional: it introduces no state variables, no transition system, and no temporal correctness properties; instead it focuses on functional operator definitions over bags represented as functions from elements to integer multiplicities.
