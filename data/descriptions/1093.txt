This module provides a TLAPS proof of the type-correctness invariant for the system specified in stages.tla. The main property proved is that the type-correctness invariant TypeOK is preserved by the system's specification Spec, i.e., Spec => []TypeOK. The proof is structured hierarchically and checks that all state transitions maintain the Nat-typedness of the relevant variables, using assumptions about the constants and properties of the natMin function. The proof leverages definitions and actions from the extended stages module and uses TLAPS proof commands and structure.
