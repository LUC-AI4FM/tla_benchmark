This specification models a very small state machine over a single variable `msgs`, which maps round numbers to sets of strings. The initial condition constrains `msgs` to be a function from the round set `0..2` to subsets of the fixed message set `{"a", "b", "c"}`. The next-state action nondeterministically chooses a subset `X` of `msgs[2]` having cardinality at least 2 and updates only entry `2` of `msgs` to `X`, so the behavior repeatedly shrinks or preserves the round-2 message set while maintaining at least two elements there; no temporal properties or fairness conditions are asserted in the file.
