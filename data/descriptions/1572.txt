This specification models a small PlusCal algorithm with an either-or branch. Starting from x = 0, y = 0, and pc = "a", the system nondeterministically chooses one of two paths: either it sets x to 1 and then increments x, or it sets y to 1 and then increments y. After the branch completes, it checks an assertion that x + y = 2, then terminates in a Done state; the temporal specification requires standard step evolution with weak fairness on Next and includes an eventual termination property.
