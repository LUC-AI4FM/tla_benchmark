This module defines a collection of operators for reasoning about functions in TLA+, including restriction, range, pointwise operations, inverses, and properties such as injectivity, surjectivity, and bijectivity. It provides operators to restrict a function to a subset of its domain or to values satisfying a predicate, to check if one function is a restriction of another, and to compute the range of a function. The module also defines operators for constructing sets of injections, surjections, and bijections between sets, and for checking their existence. Additionally, it includes fold operations over functions, leveraging a local instance of the Folds module. The specification is utility-focused and does not model a distributed algorithm or system, nor does it specify temporal properties or fault models.
