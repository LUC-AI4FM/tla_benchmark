This module specifies a light client detector algorithm for Tendermint-style light client verification, based on the referenced English detection specification. The detector compares a target light block obtained from a primary peer with one obtained from a secondary peer and, if they disagree, iteratively searches for evidence by verifying intermediate heights and recording a conflicting block together with a common trusted height. The model includes a faulty validator set bounded by a parameterized faulty ratio, distinguishes correct versus faulty primary and secondary peers, and explicitly models local and reference clocks with drift, including the possibility that clock assumptions are violated. The specification defines initialization, fetching and comparison of light blocks, evidence construction, switching from secondary to primary evidence search, and several invariants about evidence soundness, accuracy, and precision under trusting-period assumptions.
