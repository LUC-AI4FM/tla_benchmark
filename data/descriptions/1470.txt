This specification models a simple one-variable counter system intended to exercise TLC-specific initialization and introspection operators rather than a distributed algorithm or fault-tolerant protocol. The state variable x starts at 1, advances by incrementing while x < 10, and uses TLCSet, TLCGet, and PrintT to store and inspect named information during model checking. The spec also defines predicates AtFive and Step and checks, via a LET-bound expression in PossibleCounts, that TLC's recorded possible-state/action counts for those named formulas have the expected values.
