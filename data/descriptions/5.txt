This module is a small TLA+ syntax-highlighting test specification rather than a realistic distributed algorithm. It defines a variety of constants, functions, operators, tuples, sets, ranges, lambdas, CHOOSE expressions, operator declarations, and comments to exercise parser and editor support, and it also includes a tiny state machine over variables foo, bar, and pc with an initial state, one step action, a terminating stuttering action, a standard safety-style specification, and an eventual termination property. There is no fault model, no PlusCal algorithm, and no proof content; the temporal behavior is given by Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars and Termination == <>(pc = "Done").
