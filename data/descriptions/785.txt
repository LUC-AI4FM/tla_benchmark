This module specifies Huang's distributed termination detection algorithm on a fully meshed network of processes with a distinguished leader. Each process has an active flag, a dyadic rational weight, and a queue of in-transit messages; the total weight across processes and messages is conserved at 1, and idle processes return their weight toward the leader. The specification defines initialization, message sending, receiving, and idling actions, adds weak fairness for receive and idle actions, and verifies both a safety property that detected termination implies permanent global termination and a liveness property that global termination eventually leads to the leader detecting termination.
