This module specifies the Dining Philosophers problem for N philosophers arranged in a ring, using a simple asymmetric fork-acquisition strategy to avoid deadlock. Philosophers 1 through N-1 pick up their right fork and then their left fork, while philosopher 0 picks up the forks in the opposite order; the translated TLA+ model represents control flow with the variable pc and fork availability with the function sem. The specification defines the initial state, individual philosopher actions, the global next-state relation, and a temporal specification with strong fairness for every process; it also states a mutual-exclusion style safety invariant saying adjacent philosophers do not eat simultaneously, and a starvation-freedom property requiring each philosopher to eat infinitely often.
