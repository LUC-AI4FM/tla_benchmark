This module models safe arithmetic operations over 256-bit unsigned integers in the style of Solidity's SafeMath library. It defines helper operators for checked addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and modulo, and then nondeterministically tests selected operations by choosing in-range integer arguments and storing the result together with an error flag. The specification is expected to deadlock after one test step, and it checks arithmetic correctness conditions as invariants relating the stored result and error flag to the mathematically exact integer result.
