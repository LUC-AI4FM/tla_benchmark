This specification models a simple counter system with a single variable x that starts at 0 and increments by 1 on each step. The system is parameterized by a constant UpperBound, and both an invariant and a constraint ensure that x remains less than UpperBound. The temporal property Spec asserts that the system starts in the initial state and evolves according to the Next action, with stuttering steps allowed. The specification is minimal and does not include any fault model or advanced features.
