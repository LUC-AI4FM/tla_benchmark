This specification models a composite FIFO-style communication system built by connecting a sender, an inner buffer, and a receiver through channel interfaces. The top-level module instantiates a Channel module twice, once for the input side and once for the output side, and also defines an inner module that represents a queue-based buffer with receive and send actions over those channels. The specification asserts structural channel constraints on the records in and out, constrains acknowledgement and ready fields to match, and composes the Sender, existentially quantified Buffer, and Receiver behaviors into the overall Spec; it does not include explicit fault modeling or proof scripts.
