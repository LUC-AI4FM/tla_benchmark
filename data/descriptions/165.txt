This specification, MegaSpec1, is designed to exercise a wide range of TLA+ constructs for the purposes of type checking and expression tagging. It includes examples of literals, logical operators, set operations, function and record constructors, sequence operations, arithmetic, control flow (IF/CASE), and actions. The spec is not modeling a particular algorithm or distributed system, but rather serves as a comprehensive testbed for TLA+ syntax and semantics, including constructs from the Integers, Sequences, FiniteSets, Reals, and Apalache modules. There are no temporal properties or fairness conditions beyond trivial Init/Next actions, and the only assumption is that the constant N is greater than 42.
