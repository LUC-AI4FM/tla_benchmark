This module specifies a two-procedure factorial algorithm translated from a PlusCal program. The algorithm uses mutually recursive procedures FactProc and FactProc2, together with an explicit call stack, to compute the factorial of 5 in the shared variable result, and then prints either a success or error message depending on whether the result is 120. The specification includes an initial predicate, action definitions for each control location, a next-state relation, a temporal specification with weak fairness on Next, a termination property asserting eventual arrival at pc = "Done", and a simple invariant that result always remains a natural number.
