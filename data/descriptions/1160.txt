This module specifies the Two-Phase Commit (2PC) protocol for distributed transactions, focusing on the behavior of resource managers (RMs). The protocol is modeled by tracking the state of each RM, which can be 'working', 'prepared', 'committed', or 'aborted'. The specification defines the possible actions (Prepare and Decide) that RMs can take, the initial state, and the next-state relation. It asserts type correctness and consistency invariants, ensuring that no two RMs reach conflicting decisions (i.e., one aborts while another commits). The main temporal property proved is that the type correctness and consistency invariants always hold under the protocol's specification.
