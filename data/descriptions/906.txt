This specification models a refinement relationship between two channel instances, a low-level bit channel and a high-level channel carrying values in 1..12. The inner module introduces a state variable bitsSent and accumulates up to three transmitted bits, converting a completed bit sequence into a natural number with BitSeqToNat and forwarding it on the high-level channel. It also models an error behavior in which the low-level channel changes unexpectedly while no send or receive is occurring, forcing the high-level channel state to ErrorVal; the temporal properties define the inner behavior and then existentially hide bitsSent to obtain the overall refinement formula IR.
