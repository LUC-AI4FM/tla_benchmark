This module specifies a parameterized asynchronous non-blocking atomic commitment algorithm with failure detectors, based on Guerraoui's 2001 paper and encoded in TLA+. Processes initially vote YES or NO, may crash and become marked as "BYZ", may send their votes, receive YES/NO messages, abort when suspicion or NO evidence appears, and commit only when they know all proposals are YES. The specification includes general, all-YES, and all-NO initializations, a weak fairness condition over the non-stuttering process actions, a type invariant, and temporal correctness properties for agreement, abort validity, commit validity, termination, and eventual commit-per-process.
