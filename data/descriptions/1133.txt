This module models heaps, objects, and promise-like objects in an object-capability style setting, including resolved and unresolved promises and actions that resolve or garbage-collect them. It defines the shape of objects, heaps, eventuals, promises, and heap well-formedness conditions such as absence of dangling pointers and acyclicity of promise chains, and it assumes semantic properties of an abstract method evaluator Eval. The file contains state-level structure definitions and three heap-updating actions for resolving when-promises, resolving non-when-promises, and garbage collecting resolved promises, but it does not define a full temporal specification or liveness/fairness properties.
