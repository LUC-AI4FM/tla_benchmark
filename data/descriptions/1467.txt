This module models a small TLC-specific test that inspects the special TLC value returned by TLCGet("level") across initial states, next-state steps, constraints, invariants, and temporal properties. The state consists of variables x, y, yb, and z, and the action Next checks that the TLC level increases across a step while relating the state variables to current and next values of TLCGet("level"). The specification includes a state invariant Inv, an action constraint and state constraint, a temporal property Prop combining eventual-always and boxed action-style formulas, and a leads-to tautology Tautology. A large PostCondition operator also checks the structure of TLCGet("spec") by comparing it to a nested record that describes discovered inits, actions, invariants, temporals, and constraints.
