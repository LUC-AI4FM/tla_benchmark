This module is a small model-checking wrapper around another specification, Session9alt. It defines concrete model values for the number of processes and the process set, and adds two temporal properties over the inherited state variable pc: a mutual exclusion safety property stating that no two distinct processes are simultaneously in the critical section, and a naive liveness property stating that every process visits the critical section infinitely often. The file itself does not define the underlying algorithm or transition relation; it only adds these constants and property definitions on top of the extended module.
