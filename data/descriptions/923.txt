This module provides a TLAPS proof for the FIFO queue specification defined in the InnerFIFO module. The main theorem proved is that the specification Spec implies that the type invariant TypeInvariant is always maintained, i.e., Spec => []TypeInvariant. The proof is structured hierarchically and relies on the definitions and invariants from the InnerFIFO module, as well as proof automation from TLAPS. The proof demonstrates that the initial state satisfies the invariant and that the invariant is preserved by all possible transitions, including Send, Receive, and stuttering steps.
