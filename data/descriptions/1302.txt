This specification models a one-variable state machine whose next-state update is determined by an operator-valued constant F. The state starts with x = 0, and each step sets x' to F(x), leaving the behavior of the transition relation parameterized by the interpretation of F supplied externally. The spec also defines a simple invariant candidate Inv asserting that x /= 2, but it does not define a temporal Spec formula, fairness condition, or proof.
