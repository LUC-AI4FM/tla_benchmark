This module models a parallel bounded model checking algorithm that extends a sequential bounded search by using a pool of workers that alternate between exploring transitions and proving invariants on discovered prefixes. Explorers borrow transitions, check whether they are enabled, and deposit newly extended prefixes for invariant checking, while provers use an abstract SMT oracle to determine whether a bad execution exists within the overapproximated execution space induced by a prefix. The specification tracks exploration depth, enabled-transition prefixes, postponed slow prefixes, worker roles, and pending unsafe prefixes, and it defines safety-style properties stating that workers should never reach the terminal states "noerror", "error", or "deadlock", as well as a property expressing that some worker remains idle.
