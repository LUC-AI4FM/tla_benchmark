This specification models a simple state machine over a single variable S, where S is a set of strings. The initial state sets S to {"a", "b", "c"}, and each next-state step nondeterministically chooses a subset X of the current S whose cardinality is at least 2, then updates S to that subset. The spec appears to illustrate existential choice over subsets together with a finite-set cardinality constraint, rather than a full protocol or fault-tolerant algorithm, and it does not include temporal correctness properties, fairness, or proofs.
