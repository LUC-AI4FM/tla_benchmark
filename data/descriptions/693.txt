This module defines structural predicates and set constructors for directed and undirected graphs, along with path-based connectivity and a rooted tree predicate. It models graphs as records with fields `node` and `edge`, where edges are pairs of nodes, and uses sequence-valued paths to express reachability and strong connectivity. The specification is not a transition system and contains no state variables, actions, or temporal properties; instead it provides static mathematical definitions for graph classes, subgraphs, paths, connectivity, and rooted trees.
