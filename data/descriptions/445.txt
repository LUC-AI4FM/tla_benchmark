This specification is a small test module illustrating subtle INSTANCE or enabling-related semantics in TLA+, as indicated by the file comment. It declares two state variables, u and v, and defines an action A that leaves u unchanged while incrementing v by 1. The module also defines helper operators around ENABLED, comparing the direct use of ENABLED on A with passing A as an argument to another operator B, but it does not include an initial predicate, a full temporal specification, or any verified safety or liveness theorem.
