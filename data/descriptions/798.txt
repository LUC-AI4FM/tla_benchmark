This module specifies and proves correctness of a very simple algorithm for finding the largest value in a sequence of natural numbers. The algorithm iterates through a sequence variable f, maintains a current highest value h, and advances an index i until termination, after which h is shown to be greater than or equal to every element of the sequence. The specification includes the PlusCal algorithm and its TLA+ translation, defines type and inductive invariants, proves a helper property about the final index value, and then proves overall correctness with TLAPS proof steps. The design explicitly allows infinite stuttering after termination to avoid deadlock.
