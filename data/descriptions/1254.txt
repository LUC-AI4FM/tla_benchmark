This module defines standard operators for manipulating finite sequences in TLA+. A sequence of length n is represented as a function with domain 1..n, aligning with TLA+'s definition of tuples. The module provides constructors for sequences, length calculation, concatenation, appending elements, extracting the head and tail, subsequence extraction, and selection of elements based on a predicate. All operators are overridden by TLC for execution, but the definitions here serve as the mathematical basis for reasoning and proof. The module does not introduce any temporal properties or fault models, and is intended as a foundational library for use in other specifications.
