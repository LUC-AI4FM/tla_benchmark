This module defines a small model-checking wrapper around another specification, ReachabilityTest, and the Sequences module. It introduces a constant RandomSuccCount, defines RandomSuccSet as the result of applying SuccSet2 to that constant, and defines LimitedSeq(S) as the union of all functions from 1..len to S for lengths ranging from 0 to Cardinality(Nodes). The file also contains an assumption asserting that every element of Test evaluates to TRUE over its entire domain; there are no state variables, actions, temporal specifications, fairness conditions, or proofs in this module.
