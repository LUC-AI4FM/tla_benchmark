This module specifies a very abstract model of the PCR cycle, tracking temperature and counts of primers, double-stranded DNA, single-stranded templates, and template-primer hybrids. The system alternates among heating, cooling, annealing, and extension actions, with annealing nondeterministically consuming some number of available primers and templates and extension converting hybrids into DNA. The specification checks basic safety-style properties such as typing, nonnegativity, and a count-preservation invariant, and it also states a liveness property claiming eventual primer depletion, which the comments note does not hold. The model intentionally omits many biochemical details and treats PCR as a coarse state-transition system.
