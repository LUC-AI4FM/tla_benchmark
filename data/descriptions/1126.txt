This module provides a TLAPS proof for the TCommit protocol, specifically for the theorem that TCSpec implies that always (TCTypeOK and TCConsistent) hold. The proof is structured hierarchically and refers to definitions and operators from the TCommit module, as well as using the TLAPS proof system. The proof demonstrates that the invariant Inv (which is TCTypeOK and TCConsistent) is preserved by the protocol's initialization and next-state actions, and that the temporal property holds globally.
