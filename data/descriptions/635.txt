This specification models a small supervisory control process with two state variables, state and output. The system starts in an initial state, may either initialize successfully or fail, then cycles through a verification phase and a detector phase, and may eventually produce output or terminate in several terminal states. The file defines the initial predicate, several next-state action operators, a combined Next relation, and a simple invariant asserting that the system has not exhausted peers in either of two failure modes; there are no temporal properties, fairness conditions, proofs, or PlusCal constructs in this draft.
