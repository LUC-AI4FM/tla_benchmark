This module is a small model-checking wrapper around the DieHardest specification that compares two water-jug configurations. One configuration has jugs of capacities 5 and 3, while the other has capacities 5, 3, and an additional duplicate 3-gallon jug; the comments explain that the first requires 6 steps to reach 4 gallons and the second requires 5 steps. The module defines the goal value and the two capacity mappings, packages them into a tuple, and is intended for TLC to show by counterexample that the second configuration reaches the goal faster.
