This specification models a PlusCal algorithm that mutually recursively computes whether a number is even or odd using two procedures, Even and Odd, with an explicit call stack introduced by the PlusCal translation. The translated TLA+ system tracks control flow in pc, local procedure parameters xEven and xOdd, the global result variable, and a stack of activation records represented as records in a sequence. The specification initializes a call to Even(2), allows normal execution plus infinite stuttering after reaching termination, and verifies termination with the temporal property <>(pc = "Done"); it also imposes weak fairness on Next.
