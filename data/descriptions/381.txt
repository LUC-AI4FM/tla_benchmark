This module is a parser-oriented test specification for temporal-proof restrictions in TLA+. It models almost no algorithmic behavior beyond a single state variable x, and instead checks that temporal formulas are rejected in syntactically invalid proof contexts, such as non-temporal proof steps, certain CASE or HAVE steps, and constant instantiations in INSTANCE substitutions. The file includes instance declarations with substitutions involving temporal and primed expressions, plus two THEOREM blocks designed to trigger parsing or proof-checking errors about mixing temporal and non-temporal reasoning.
