This module specifies Safra's distributed termination detection algorithm on a ring, following EWD 998. Nodes can be active or passive, exchange asynchronous messages tracked via per-node counters and pending-message counts, and circulate a colored token carrying position, counter summary, and color information to detect global termination. The specification includes the core system and environment actions, a safety theorem relating local termination detection to actual termination, a liveness property stating that termination is eventually detected, and additional Büchi-style auxiliary variables for loop and accepting-state tracking used to encode liveness-related reasoning.
