This TLA+ specification is a test suite designed to exercise and validate the behavior of the UNCHANGED operator in various contexts. The system models two variables, x and y, where x is initialized as a function mapping 1..3 to their respective values, and y is initialized to 1. The Next action consists of a disjunction of several cases, each testing UNCHANGED on different expressions, including tuples, function values, set comprehensions, CHOOSE expressions, and record constructors. Each case uses Print or Assert statements to indicate success or failure, and the invariant Inv is trivially set to TRUE. The specification does not model a distributed algorithm or fault tolerance, but rather focuses on the semantics of UNCHANGED in TLA+ and its interaction with TLC. No temporal properties or fairness conditions are asserted.
