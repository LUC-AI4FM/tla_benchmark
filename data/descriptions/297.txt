This module models a parameterized one-round asynchronous reliable broadcast algorithm with Byzantine faults, based on the Srikanth-Toueg broadcast algorithm. The system tracks correct and faulty processes, local control states, received ECHO messages, and messages sent by correct processes; Byzantine messages are modeled abstractly as any pair from Faulty \X {"ECHO"}. The specification includes an initial condition, transition relation, a fairness-augmented liveness specification, a no-broadcast safety specification, typing and cardinality invariants, and temporal properties for correctness, relay, unforgeability, and a leads-to acceptance property. It also contains a TLAPS-oriented inductive invariant for proving unforgeability when no correct process initially broadcasts, along with a TLC-reordered variant for model checking.
