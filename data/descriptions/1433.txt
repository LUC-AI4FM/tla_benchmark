This module is a small TLC-oriented specification that explores how initial states are generated from different syntactic shapes of disjunctive initialization predicates. It defines several candidate initial predicates over a single variable x, with comments explaining when TLC should or should not generate duplicate initial states depending on conjunct ordering, disjunction placement, and the use of double negation. The behavior relation is trivial, with Next leaving x unchanged, so the specification focuses on initialization structure rather than system evolution or temporal correctness properties.
