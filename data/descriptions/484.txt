This module specifies a very small transition system over two variables, a and b. The initial condition constrains only a to be 0, while the next-state action is a conjunction between a negated action formula and a concrete assignment action on primed variables. The temporal specification asserts that Next is the step relation in a standard stuttering form over the state vector << a >>, so the model focuses on a simple temporal behavior and illustrates use of negation over primed equalities.
