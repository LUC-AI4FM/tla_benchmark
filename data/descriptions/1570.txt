This module specifies a small call-and-return control-flow example generated from a PlusCal algorithm with three procedures, Proc1, Proc2, and Proc3, plus a top-level call into Proc1. The specification models procedure invocation and return explicitly using a program counter pc and a stack of records that save return locations and local variables across calls. It checks assertion-based safety conditions about argument values and local variables during nested calls, and it also specifies liveness through weak fairness of Next together with an eventual termination property requiring that execution can reach pc = "Done".
