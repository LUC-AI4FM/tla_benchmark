This module specifies part of a light client verification interface for blockchain headers, focusing on when an untrusted header can be considered valid relative to a trusted header and what invariants should hold of the light store. It models timing assumptions explicitly through a local clock, trusted and actual clock drift bounds, a trusting period, and validator-set overlap checks based on cardinalities and intersections. The specification defines preconditions and result-valued verification functions, plus store and correctness invariants, and a postcondition for verifying to a target height; it is a state-level specification with no transition system, no temporal Spec operator, and no fault actions, though it refers to peer correctness and faulty validator ratio as part of the intended security model.
