This module defines typed helper operators for finite-set computations, adapted from the CommunityModules FiniteSetsExt module and annotated with types. It provides set reduction with a commutative operator, derived numeric aggregations such as sum and product, counting elements satisfying a predicate, enumerating k-element subsets, selecting extrema of finite non-empty integer sets, constructing choice sets from a family of sets, and choosing the unique element satisfying a predicate. The module is a pure library module with no state variables, no transition system, no temporal specification, and no fault model; it focuses entirely on mathematical operators over sets and functions.
