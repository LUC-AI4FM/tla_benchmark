This module models a bounded multi-producer, multi-consumer Disruptor-style ring buffer. Producers claim sequence numbers, write their claimed sequence into a shared ring buffer, and publish availability; consumers read published entries in multicast fashion so that every consumer should eventually observe every published value. The specification checks absence of producer-consumer data races via an instantiated RingBuffer module, basic typing and bounded-state constraints, and a liveness property stating that for every reader and every publishable index below the bound, eventually always: if that index is available, then the reader has that value recorded at the corresponding position in its consumption history.
