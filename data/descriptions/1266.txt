This module specifies and model checks a merge algorithm that combines two sorted input sequences into a sorted output sequence while preserving exactly the elements that appeared in the inputs. The specification includes helper operators for computing maxima, images of functions, converting sequences to sets, checking whether a sequence is ordered, and generating ordered sequences over a set. A PlusCal algorithm is present in comments and a generated TLA+ translation defines the state machine, invariant, and termination property, with weak fairness on Next to ensure progress toward the Done state.
