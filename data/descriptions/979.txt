This module is a very small wrapper around another specification, declaring three variables p, c, and x, defining two simple helper operators, and instantiating the module TwoPhase. The operator XInit specifies an initial-state condition requiring its argument to equal 0, and XAct specifies a trivial next-state relation equating xNext with xInit. The file itself contains no temporal specification, no explicit safety or liveness theorem, and no fault model; it appears to provide parameterized initialization and action helpers for use with the instantiated module.
