This module specifies the Yo-Yo leader-election algorithm with pruning on undirected, loop-free, connected graphs over a fixed set of numbered nodes. The initial state nondeterministically chooses a connected graph over Nodes, orients each edge from the smaller endpoint to the larger one, and then models the algorithm as alternating down and up phases in which nodes exchange messages, reverse edges, prune edges, and eventually deactivate non-leader nodes. The specification includes typing and structural invariants, a liveness property stating eventual termination, and a characterization of termination as exactly the deadlock states under weak fairness of Next.
