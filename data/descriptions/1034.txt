This module models a small bounded configuration of the Sailfish protocol with three nodes, one of which is Byzantine. The file defines concrete node sets, quorum and blocking-set predicates, a leader schedule, a global stabilization time constant, and a state constraint that bounds the explored rounds for non-faulty nodes to control state-space explosion. It introduces a terminating variant of the behavior specification and several deliberately false temporal/state predicates intended to help TLC produce interesting counterexample executions, including conditions about all correct nodes finishing and about committed log entries.
