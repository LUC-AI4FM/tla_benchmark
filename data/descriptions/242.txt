This module defines a very small TLA+ specification composed entirely of state-level formulas over empty-set quantification and a CHOOSE expression. It introduces trivial initial and next-state predicates, several invariant-like predicates Inv1 through Inv6 using universal and existential quantification over the empty set, and two summary predicates NoFail and Fail. The specification is primarily about evaluating logical formulas with vacuous truth and empty-domain choice, and it does not define variables, transitions over state, temporal behavior, fairness, or proofs.
