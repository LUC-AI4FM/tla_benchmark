This module is a small test specification for TLC-specific operators and values. It defines a collection of top-level operators whose bodies invoke TLC built-ins such as Print, PrintT, Assert, JavaTime, TLCGet, TLCSet, Permutations, SortSeq, RandomElement, Any, ToString, and TLCEval, and it also includes examples of TLC function-construction operators :> and @@. The file does not define any state variables, actions, temporal behavior, fairness conditions, or proofs; it is focused purely on exercising TLC operator typing and expression forms.
