This specification models a Percolator-style transactional storage protocol with primary and secondary keys, explicit locks, committed writes, rollback records, and cleanup of stale locks. Transactions progress through init, working, prewriting, committing, committed, or aborted states; they may read keys, prewrite locks concurrently, commit a primary key, or abort, while stale-lock cleanup may either roll back data or complete secondary-key commit based on the primary key's outcome. The spec verifies safety-oriented invariants including typing, write-order consistency, lock consistency, committed/aborted consistency, rollback consistency, and a snapshot-isolation monitor encoded with auxiliary variables.
