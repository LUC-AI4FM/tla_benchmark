This module defines debugging-oriented helper definitions for a Tendermint accountability specification, rather than the core protocol itself. It models special initial states with either no injected Byzantine messages or a bounded number of faulty proposals, prevotes, and precommits, and it also defines a transition relation that augments the inherited protocol step relation with extra faulty broadcasts. The module includes simple state predicates used as reachability checks, such as whether no correct process has reached PREVOTE or PRECOMMIT, whether no valid precommit exists, whether all rounds stay below 1, and whether no decision has been made.
