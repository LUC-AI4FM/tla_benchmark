This module defines an Apalache-compatible version of standard bag (multiset) operators in TLA+. A bag is modeled as a function from elements to positive integers, and the module provides predicates and constructors for testing bags, converting between sets and bags, bag membership, empty bags, bag union, bag subtraction, folding a set of bags with union, subbag comparison, image construction over bags, cardinality, and copy counting. The design explicitly avoids inherited standard Bags definitions and reimplements them within the Apalache-supported fragment, with SubBag left unsupported through a designated placeholder operator.
