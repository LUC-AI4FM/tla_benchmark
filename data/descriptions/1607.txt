This module specifies a PlusCal-generated concurrent factorial program with three processes sharing a result array indexed by process id. Two processes in 1..2 execute the same Main process body, and a third process executes Minor; each invokes a recursive factorial procedure emulated by explicit stack manipulation in the generated TLA+ translation. The specification initializes per-process control state and procedure-local variables, models recursive calls by pushing records onto a stack, uses weak fairness for the process and procedure actions, and asserts eventual termination together with a simple invariant that result remains a natural number.
