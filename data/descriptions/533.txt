This module is a very small extension module over Session4 that defines two constant-valued operators. It appears to provide model-checking helper values: a distinguished minimum integer value and a union of function-set expressions representing tuples from 1..l to shifted ranges (l+1)..(2*l) for l from 0 to 5. The file contains no variables, no actions, no temporal specification, and no explicit safety or liveness properties; it is purely a collection of top-level mathematical definitions.
