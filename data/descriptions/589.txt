This specification models a single-variable cyclic counter together with TLC-managed side effects through the TLCSet, TLCGet, and PrintT operators. The state variable x is initialized to 0 and the next-state action increments it modulo 5, while also updating a TLC tool value stored under key 42. The temporal specification requires the initialized behavior, all steps to satisfy the stuttering-closed next-state relation, and weak fairness of Next; it also states a liveness property that x repeatedly reaches 1, plus postconditions about the TLC tool state.
