This module axiomatizes the Peano natural numbers using three parameters: a carrier set N, a distinguished zero element Z, and a successor function Sc. The main operator PeanoAxioms states that zero is in N, successor maps N to N, every non-zero element is the successor of some element, and every subset of N containing zero and closed under successor is all of N. The module then assumes existence of such a structure and defines Succ, Nat, and Zero by CHOOSE expressions to extract a particular successor function, its domain as the naturals, and a corresponding zero element.
