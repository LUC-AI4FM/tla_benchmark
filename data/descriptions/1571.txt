This module specifies a dining philosophers system with N philosophers arranged in a ring and forks represented by a semaphore-like array. Philosophers 1 through N-1 acquire their right fork first and then their left fork, while philosopher 0 acquires forks in the opposite order to avoid the symmetric deadlock pattern. The translated PlusCal model defines the control locations and semaphore updates as actions, specifies temporal behavior with a standard initialization-and-next-state formula plus strong fairness for every philosopher process, and states safety and liveness properties in the form of a mutual-exclusion-style eating invariant and a starvation-freedom property.
