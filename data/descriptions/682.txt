This module defines basic operations over dyadic rationals, represented as records with integer numerator and denominator fields. It introduces helper operators for divisibility, divisors, greatest common divisor, reduction to lowest terms, recognition of dyadic rationals, addition, halving, and pretty-printing. The specification is purely functional and state-free: it declares no variables, no transition relation, no temporal specification, no fairness conditions, and no proofs; it mainly models arithmetic structure and normalized record-based representations of rationals whose denominators are powers of 2.
