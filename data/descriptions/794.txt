This specification models a Knuth-Yao style stochastic state machine for generating one of the outcomes 1 through 6 by repeatedly tossing a fair coin and following a transition graph. The variables track the current dyadic probability mass p associated with the current node, the current control state, and the most recent coin flip. The specification initializes at state "s0" with probability One, evolves by halving p on each step and updating state through the Transition table, and asserts a standard temporal behavior consisting of initialization, next-state steps, and weak fairness of Next.
