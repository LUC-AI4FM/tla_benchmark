This module models a self-composition of two jug-filling systems in order to analyze a 2-hyperproperty about which of two Die Hard configurations reaches a target amount of water in fewer steps. It compares several composition styles: lock-step parallel composition, per-behavior freezing, TLC-specific global BFS-level freezing, and finally an interleaved composition that gives a clean TLA+ solution. The specification assumes both configurations are solvable, assumes TLC is running in single-worker BFS mode so counterexamples are shortest, and uses the final interleaved specification to obtain shortest step counts s1 and s2 for the two independent instances.
