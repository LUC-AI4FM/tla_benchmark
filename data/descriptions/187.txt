This module is a small regression-test specification for issue #1084 concerning polymorphic definitions and their correct instantiation. It defines a trivial behavior with Init and Next both equal to TRUE, and then introduces a polymorphic helper operator X(f) == DOMAIN f to extract the domain of a function. The specification checks, through three invariant-style state predicates, that X instantiates correctly on functions over integers and strings, including inside nested LET bindings.
