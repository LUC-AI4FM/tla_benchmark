This specification models consistency of an abstract transactional service with histories of client-visible request/response events and a set of ledger branches representing views. Read-write transactions can be requested, executed onto a branch, responded to with an observed prefix, and later receive committed or invalid status messages; read-only transactions observe a branch and receive a response carrying the observed sequence and transaction identifier. The model includes view changes via ledger truncation and branch extension, checks typing and committed-event structure, and asserts a basic temporal specification `Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars` together with state and temporal helper properties such as ledger monotonicity and a consistency invariant relating committed read-write responses to later committed read-only observations.
