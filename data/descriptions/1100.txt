This module specifies an abstract mutual-exclusion lock for two processes. The system is written as a PlusCal algorithm and includes its generated TLA+ translation, where each process cycles through a non-critical location, acquires the lock when it is free, enters the critical section, and then releases the lock. The specification checks basic safety structure through type correctness and an invariant asserting mutual exclusion, and it also states a liveness property that process 1 being at the waiting location "l1" leads to eventually reaching the critical-section location "cs". No fault model is present.
