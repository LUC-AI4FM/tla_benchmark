This module is a small regression-style TLA+ specification that tests usability of several standard-module operator aliases. It checks that bag addition and subtraction aliases from Bags, sequence concatenation aliases from Sequences, and comparison aliases from Naturals behave equivalently to their corresponding canonical operators. The spec declares a single variable x with a trivial initial predicate and stuttering next-state action, while the main interesting predicate Inv consists of a conjunction of IF-THEN-ELSE checks that call TLC's Print and Assert operators to validate the expected equalities.
