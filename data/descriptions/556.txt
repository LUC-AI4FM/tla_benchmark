This specification models a simple semaphore-based mutual exclusion algorithm generated from a PlusCal algorithm named SemMutex. A set of processes repeatedly cycles through a non-critical section, waits until a binary semaphore has value 1, enters the critical section by setting the semaphore to 0, and then exits by restoring the semaphore to 1. The spec assumes a positive number of processes indexed by Procs = 0..(N-1), defines the translated control-state variable pc, and verifies behavior under a temporal specification with both weak fairness for non-ncs process steps and strong fairness for the enter action of each process.
