This module specifies a very small transition system over a single variable x, with two separate pairs of actions that move x around finite ranges. The first part defines actions A and B over the range 0..2 and studies specifications and properties involving weak fairness and liveness of A; the second part defines actions C and D over the range 0..3 and similarly studies weak fairness and liveness of D. The file also defines negations of some fairness and liveness formulas, a leads-to property saying that when x is 1 it eventually changes value, a recurrence property saying x is always eventually 0 again, and a tautological leads-to formula. There is no fault model, no PlusCal algorithm, and no proof content; the module is a compact temporal-logic example focused on fairness and liveness relationships.
