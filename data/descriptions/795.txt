This module specifies a simple Markov-style probabilistic transition system encoded in TLA+ using rational weights represented as numerator-denominator tuples. The state starts at an initial node and evolves according to a transition function, while the variable p accumulates path probability mass by multiplying the current probability tuple by the chosen transition weight. The specification defines initialization, a next-state relation with weak fairness, and proves that every behavior satisfying Spec eventually reaches an accepting state or a zero-normalized probability.
