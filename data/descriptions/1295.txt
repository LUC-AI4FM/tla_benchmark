This specification models Dijkstra's token-based distributed termination detection algorithm (EWD 840) for a ring of N processes. The system tracks which nodes are active or passive, their colors (white or black), the token's position and color, and models both the algorithm's token-passing logic and the environment's ability to activate or deactivate nodes. The main safety property asserts that if a white token is at node 0, then all nodes are inactive, and the liveness property ensures that termination is eventually detected. The spec includes several variants of liveness properties, counterexamples, and an inductive invariant for validation, as well as fairness conditions on the system and environment actions.
