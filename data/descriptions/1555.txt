This module specifies a distributed termination-detection algorithm in the style of EWD998 over a ring of nodes identified by abstract identifiers rather than naturals. Nodes exchange payload messages and a circulating token, maintain local color and counters, and additionally carry vector clocks in messages and the token; the vector clock information is explicitly noted as irrelevant to correctness and is used for tracing/refinement purposes. The specification initializes a token at an arbitrary ring position, models system and environment actions, assumes a finite nonempty node set, asserts weak fairness for token-system actions at every node, proves a bound on token rounds after termination, and also proves refinement/safety/liveness obligations against an instantiated lower-level specification.
