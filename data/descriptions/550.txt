This specification models a simple round-robin mutual exclusion protocol generated from a PlusCal algorithm named Alternate. A shared variable turn determines which process in Procs is allowed to enter the critical section, and each process cycles through control locations "ncs", "enter", "cs", and "exit". The temporal specification requires initialization, all behavior steps to satisfy Next modulo stuttering, and weak fairness for each process action procs(self), ensuring that every process that remains enabled eventually gets scheduled.
