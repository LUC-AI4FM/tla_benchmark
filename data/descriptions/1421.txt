This module specifies a very small one-variable transition system over the integer variable x. It defines two initial predicates, a basic action NextAB that nondeterministically assigns x' to 0 or 1, and a larger action NextC that extends that next-state relation with the additional possibility x' = 2. The specification includes safety-style temporal formulas AtMostOnce and AtMostOnceC, a full behavior specification Spec with weak fairness WF_x(NextAB), and two leads-to properties, one intended as a liveness property and one tautological.
