This module states theorem properties of the basic set-folding operator MapThenFoldSet imported from another module. It does not define the folding algorithm itself; instead, it records reference theorems about the defining equation, empty and non-empty cases, invariance under bijections, equality under pointwise-equal mapping functions, typing, and behavior under associative-commutative operators and disjoint union. The module is proof-oriented and non-temporal: it contains theorem statements with ASSUME/PROVE structure, relies on finiteness assumptions via IsFiniteSet, and leaves actual proofs to a separate proofs module mentioned in the comments.
