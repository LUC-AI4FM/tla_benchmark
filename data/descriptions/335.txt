This specification models a simple year counter for a driver's license registration office that suffers from a Y2K-style bug. The current year is stored modulo 100, so after 99 it wraps back to 0, while a person's birth year is also represented with two digits; age is computed as year - BIRTH_YEAR, which becomes incorrect across the century boundary. The spec initializes the year to the birth year, allows either advancing to a new year or issuing a license once the computed age reaches a required threshold, and defines a safety property asserting that a license is held only when the computed age is at least the minimum licensing age. It also includes a type invariant constraining year to 0..99 and hasLicense to BOOLEAN.
