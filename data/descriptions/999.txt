This module contains TLAPS proofs for two safety properties of the SimpleAllocator specification: that SimpleAllocator implies the invariants TypeInvariant and ResourceMutex. TypeInvariant is shown to be directly inductive, while ResourceMutex requires the combined invariant Inv (TypeInvariant and ResourceMutex) and an argument about the disjointness of allocated resources. The proofs are fully written in hierarchical style, with detailed reasoning about each action (Request, Allocate, Return) and the preservation of invariants. The module does not define the allocator algorithm itself, but proves properties about it using definitions and actions from the imported SimpleAllocator module.
