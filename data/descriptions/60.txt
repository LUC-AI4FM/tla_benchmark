This module defines a very small TLA+ specification with two helper operators F and G, an initial predicate Init, a next-state relation Next, and a simple invariant Inv. The modeled system is trivial: both Init and Next are TRUE, so every behavior is allowed and there are no state variables or transition constraints. The property of interest is the state-level predicate Inv, which evaluates G(1,2) and therefore checks inequality through a helper-operator call chain; there is no fault model, no temporal specification, and no fairness or proof content.
