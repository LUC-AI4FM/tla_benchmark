This specification models a simple spanning-tree construction protocol over a fixed directed neighbor relation on a set of processes. Initially every process has no parent, no process has reported, and no sent-message flag is set; processes can send discovery messages along edges, adopt a parent when they receive a message while parentless and not the root, and later mark themselves as reported. The spec defines a temporal behavior with weak fairness on the main next-state action, and it asserts typing, an eventual spanning-tree property, an invariant-like one-parent stability property, and a predicate constraining when parentless non-root processes may have sent messages.
