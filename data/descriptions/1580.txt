This specification models a dining philosophers system in which philosophers 1 through N-1 acquire forks in one order while philosopher 0 acquires them in the opposite order. The file contains a PlusCal algorithm and its generated TLA+ translation, with semaphore-like fork ownership stored in sem, control state in pc, and a call stack used for translated procedures. The temporal specification asserts initialization, the next-state relation, and multiple weak and strong fairness conditions for translated processes and subactions. It also defines a mutual-exclusion invariant stating adjacent philosophers are not eating simultaneously, and a starvation-freedom property requiring each philosopher to eat infinitely often.
