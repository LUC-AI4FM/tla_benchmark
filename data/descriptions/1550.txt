This specification models the Dijkstra-Scholten tree algorithm for detecting termination of a diffusing distributed computation over an arbitrary directed network. Processes can be active or idle, send computation messages along graph edges, and send acknowledgment control messages in the reverse direction; the algorithm dynamically maintains an overlay tree of non-neutral processes rooted at the leader. The spec assumes asynchronous unordered delivery without message loss, and proves safety properties about counter consistency, overlay-tree structure, and correct termination detection, as well as a liveness property stating that once the computation has terminated, the leader eventually becomes neutral and thus detects termination.
