This specification models a simple infinite-state transition system with two state variables, x and y. The initial condition constrains x to be 0 and y to lie in the finite range 1..1000, and the temporal behavior requires every step to increment x by 1 while leaving y unchanged. The spec does not include an explicit fault model, fairness condition, or proof structure; it consists of a single temporal specification that defines an unbounded evolution of x from a bounded initial choice of y.
