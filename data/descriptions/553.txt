This module is a model-checking wrapper for a Peterson-style mutual exclusion specification defined in the extended module Session9peterson. In this file, it introduces concrete constants for the number of processes and the process identifier range, and states two correctness properties: mutual exclusion and starvation freedom. The module itself does not define the algorithm steps directly, but it expresses that no two distinct processes are simultaneously in the critical section and that any process at location "enter" eventually reaches location "cs".
