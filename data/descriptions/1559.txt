This module specifies a lock protocol with auxiliary variables to facilitate refinement to the Peterson mutual exclusion algorithm. It introduces a history variable 'h_turn' to track the assignment of the turn variable, and a stuttering variable 's' to mimic the three-step entry protocol of Peterson's algorithm. The specification is designed to allow a formal equivalence between the Lock and Peterson specifications, using the Stuttering module (with modifications) to model stuttering steps and history tracking. The module defines the initial state, transition actions, and invariants, and instantiates the Peterson specification with appropriate substitutions to relate the two systems.
