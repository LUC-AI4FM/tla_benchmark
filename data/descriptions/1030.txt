This module is a test harness for BlockDag operators using small concrete directed acyclic graphs. It defines a tiny node set, round range, and round leader function, instantiates the BlockDag module with those concrete parameters, and then states a sequence of ASSUME-based test assertions about derived operators such as Node, Round, LeaderVertex, OrderSet, PreviousLeader, and Linearize. The specification is not a transition-system model with variables or temporal behavior; instead, it is a collection of concrete structural checks over finite sets, sequences, tuples, and a sample DAG.
