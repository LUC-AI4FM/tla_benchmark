This specification models the classic missionaries and cannibals river-crossing problem using a typed TLA+ description. The system state records which bank the boat is on and, for each bank, the set of people currently there; a step consists of moving a set of one or two people in the boat from the current bank to the opposite bank while preserving safety on both banks. The safety condition is that whenever missionaries are present on a bank, they are not outnumbered by cannibals, and the comments explain that TLC can be used to find a solution by checking an invariant such as who_is_on_bank["E"] /= {} and obtaining a counterexample trace when everyone reaches the west bank.
