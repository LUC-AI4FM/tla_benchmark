This module provides a TLAPS proof that the DieHard water jug puzzle specification maintains the TypeOK invariant throughout all behaviors. The proof shows that the initial state satisfies TypeOK and that every step of the Next action preserves TypeOK, using case analysis on each possible action (FillSmallJug, FillBigJug, EmptySmallJug, EmptyBigJug, SmallToBig, BigToSmall, and stuttering steps). The proof leverages the Min operator and its properties, as well as arithmetic reasoning about the jug capacities. The NotSolved invariant is intentionally not proved, as it is meant to be violated to find a solution to the puzzle.
