This specification models Dijkstra's first published mutual exclusion algorithm, as described in his 1965 CACM paper. The algorithm ensures mutual exclusion among a set of processes (Proc), each of which cycles through non-critical and critical sections, using shared variables b, c, and k. The PlusCal translation is provided, and the TLA+ translation defines the system's state, actions, and temporal properties. The model checks mutual exclusion, deadlock freedom (under weak fairness), and discusses starvation freedom, with explicit invariants and liveness properties. The design allows for arbitrary process names and includes detailed commentary on the translation and fairness requirements.
