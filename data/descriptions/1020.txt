This module specifies and proves properties of a variant of Paxos consensus in which leaders may send phase 1c messages to assert that values are safe at a ballot before sending phase 2a proposals. The system models message-passing among ballot-indexed leaders and acceptors using a history variable msgs together with per-acceptor state maxBal, maxVBal, and maxVVal, and it refines an instantiated voting specification from module VoteProof. The file contains a PlusCal algorithm and its translated TLA+ form, proves equivalence between the translated Next relation and a manually structured TLA+ next-state relation, states invariants used for refinement and safety, and states a liveness theorem under fairness assumptions; the fault model is omission faults, represented by enabled actions and message receipts that may simply never occur.
