This module is a synthetic TLA+ specification intended to exercise a wide variety of TLA+ constructs rather than model a concrete distributed algorithm. It defines examples of literals, logic, sets, functions, records, tuples, sequences, arithmetic, actions, and temporal operators, including fairness, leads-to, and guarantees. The only state variable is msgs, and the temporal/action portion demonstrates priming, stuttering, non-stuttering, UNCHANGED, action composition, and standard temporal modalities; no fault model is specified, and the design is clearly geared toward type-checking and construct coverage.
