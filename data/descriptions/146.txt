This specification models a very small two-variable state machine over integers. The initial state sets x to 0 and y to 1, and each Next step swaps the values of x and y by assigning x' = y and y' = x. It also defines two temporal properties using LET-IN expressions and a local helper operator Plus, one asserting that a derived sum is always eventually equal to 2 and another asserting that the simpler sum eventually stays equal to 2 forever.
