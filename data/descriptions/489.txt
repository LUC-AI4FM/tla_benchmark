This specification models a simple system with four variables (a, b, c, d) and three additional variables (x, y, z) that are left unchanged by the main action. The Next action updates a, b, c, and d deterministically or nondeterministically, while leaving x, y, and z unchanged. The temporal property Spec asserts that the Next action is always enabled over the tuple <<a, b>>. The model is basic, with no fairness, no PlusCal, and no proofs.
