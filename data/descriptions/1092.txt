This specification models a PCR-like product generation process across repeated temperature cycles. It tracks temperature, stage, cycle count, primer supply, several classes of single-stranded templates, hybridized intermediates, double-stranded forms, and the final product, with actions for heating, cooling, annealing, and extension. The spec verifies a typed state invariant, imposes weak fairness on each main action in the temporal specification, and includes a safety property stating that no final product exists before the third cycle.
