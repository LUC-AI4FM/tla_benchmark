This module specifies an erroneous non-blocking atomic commitment protocol variant built on top of definitions imported from ACP_SB. The comments explain that the mistake is that a participant delivers a broadcast decision locally before forwarding it to other participants, which can violate the atomic commitment consistency property AC1. The module adds a forwarding field to participants, defines the corresponding type invariant and initial condition, specifies forwarding, deciding on a forwarded decision, and aborting on timeout, and then gives a temporal specification with weak fairness and a leads-to property stating that if one participant decides commit, then every participant eventually decides commit.
