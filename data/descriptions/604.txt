This specification models a simple scenario for the Percolator system, focusing on two concurrent clients each associated with a unique primary key. The model is intended for TLC model checking, as indicated by the comments and the extension of the TLC module. The constants and definitions set up a minimal environment with two keys and two clients, mapping each client to its primary key. No actions, temporal properties, or proofs are defined in this file; it serves as a configuration or parameterization layer for the Percolator module.
