This specification models Euclid's algorithm for computing the greatest common divisor, starting from a fixed value 24 and a nondeterministically chosen initial value v in 1..N. The file contains a PlusCal algorithm and its generated TLA+ translation, with control-state variable pc and state variables u, v, and v_ini. The temporal specification requires initialization, stepwise execution with stuttering, and weak fairness of Next, and it asserts eventual termination as well as a final assertion that the result equals GCD(24, v_ini).
