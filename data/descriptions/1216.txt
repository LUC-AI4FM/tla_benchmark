This specification is a test module designed to explore the subtleties of priming and variable confusion in TLA+. It defines several operators that either prime or do not prime their arguments, and then uses them in various IF expressions within the Next action to test how priming interacts with operator application and LET bindings. The spec uses simple variables x and y, and includes a variety of IF-THEN-ELSE constructs, LET-IN expressions, and primed/unprimed operator calls to exercise the TLA+ semantics. The temporal property is minimal, with only an invariant Inv == TRUE, and the module is intended for testing and demonstration rather than modeling a real system.
