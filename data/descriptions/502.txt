This specification models Multi-decree Paxos with an aggregated prepare phase, where each replica acts as proposer, acceptor, and learner. The model incorporates practical details to closely resemble real-world implementations, such as explicit ballot management, message passing, and slot-based consensus. The system assumes finite sets of replicas, values, slots, and ballots, with constraints ensuring non-triviality (e.g., at least three replicas, at least two values, non-empty slots and ballots). The algorithm is specified in PlusCal and translated to TLA+, with actions for sending and handling Prepare, PrepareReply, Accept, AcceptReply, and Learn messages. The main temporal property asserts that the system starts in Init and always evolves according to Next, with all state transitions captured by the Next action. The design ensures safety by requiring majority quorums and tracks learned values per slot.
