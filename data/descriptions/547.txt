This module specifies a one-bit mutual exclusion protocol for N processes, originally written in PlusCal and translated into TLA+. Each process repeatedly cycles through noncritical section, entry, critical section, and exit, using a shared boolean flag array and a local index j to scan other processes and wait when contention is detected. The specification includes the initial state, per-process actions, a global Next action, and the temporal specification Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars.
