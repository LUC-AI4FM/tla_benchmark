This specification models the BOSCO one-step Byzantine asynchronous consensus algorithm for a fixed set of processes, split into correct and faulty processes. Correct processes propose 0 or 1, broadcast ECHO messages, receive sets of messages that may also include Byzantine messages from faulty processes, and then either decide immediately or move to an underlying consensus with a chosen value. The spec assumes numeric constraints on N, T, and F, defines safety-oriented lemmas about incompatible decisions and underlying-consensus outcomes, includes a basic temporal specification Spec with Init and stuttering-enabled Next, and states two always-properties about executions starting from unanimous initial proposals.
