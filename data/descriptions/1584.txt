This module specifies a small PlusCal algorithm named Either in which control first makes one of three choices: assign and increment x, assign and increment y, or assign z to 100. It then executes a second either statement whose enabled branch depends on the value of x, updating z accordingly, and finally checks two assertions about the relationship among x, y, and z. The translated TLA+ model includes the standard initial predicate, next-state relation, a terminating stuttering action, a temporal specification with weak fairness on Next, and an eventual termination property.
