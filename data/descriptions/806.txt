This module is a small model-checking wrapper around a Quicksort specification. It introduces a constant bound MaxSeqLen and defines LimitedSeq(S) as the union of all sequences over S whose lengths range from 1 to MaxSeqLen, thereby restricting the input space to bounded nonempty sequences. The file does not define an algorithm step relation, variables, temporal specification, or proofs; it only adds a bounded-sequence helper on top of an extended module and assumes the bound is a natural number.
