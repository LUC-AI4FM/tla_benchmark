This specification models the Apache BookKeeper protocol for the lifetime of a single ledger, including ledger creation, normal add-entry writes, ensemble changes after write failures, ledger closing, and ledger recovery by other clients. It models metadata state, per-bookie storage and fencing state, and per-client local state, with asynchronous message-passing actions for requests and responses. The spec checks typing and safety invariants about truncation, readability, dirty reads, quorum replication, and ordering, and it also includes liveness assertions that under weak fairness of Next the ledger is eventually closed.
