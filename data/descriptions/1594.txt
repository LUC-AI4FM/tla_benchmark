This specification models a very small fair sequential PlusCal algorithm translated into TLA+. It has a single integer variable x and a control variable pc; the algorithm repeatedly increments x while x < 10 and then moves to a terminal control state "Done". The temporal specification requires the usual initialization and next-state relation together with weak fairness on Next, and it asserts termination as the eventual reachability of pc = "Done" while permitting infinite stuttering after termination to avoid deadlock.
