This specification models the Towers of Hanoi puzzle with a parameterized number of disks D and towers N. Towers are encoded as natural numbers whose bit patterns represent which power-of-two disk values are present on each tower, and the actions move a smallest available disk from one tower to another when the destination permits it. The spec defines an initial state, a next-state relation over legal moves, a safety-style invariant about preservation of the total disk sum, a type condition over tower values, and a temporal specification Spec asserting Init /\ [][Next]_vars; it also defines NotSolved so TLC can search for a counterexample trace reaching the solved configuration.
