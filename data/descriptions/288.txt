This module specifies a simple transition system with three variables: two integer state variables x and y, and a string-valued action variable recording whether the most recent step was Init, A, or B. The initial state sets x and y to 0 and marks the action as "Init"; transition A nondeterministically adds an integer d to x while leaving y unchanged, and transition B nondeterministically adds an integer d to y while leaving x unchanged. The specification also defines a state invariant x + y < 10, two view operators, and three history-based trace invariants over sequences of record states, using sequence indexing, Len, record field access, tuple construction, and LET-bound last-state extraction.
