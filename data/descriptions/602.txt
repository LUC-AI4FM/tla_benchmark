This specification models Google's Percolator-style distributed transaction protocol with primary and secondary keys, timestamp assignment, prewrite locking, primary-key commit, stale-lock cleanup, and client aborts. It tracks clients, per-key data versions, locks, committed writes, and auxiliary variables used to check a snapshot isolation condition by comparing commit timestamps against the last observed read timestamp. The spec verifies safety properties including type correctness, write ordering and non-overlap, lock consistency, consistency of committed and aborted transactions, and preservation of snapshot isolation, expressed by a theorem that PercolatorSpec implies these invariants always hold.
