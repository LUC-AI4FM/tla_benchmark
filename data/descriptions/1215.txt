This module is a test specification designed to check the syntactic and semantic constraints on the use of temporal operators in TLA+. The comments and operator definitions illustrate which uses of primed variables and temporal operators are permitted and which are considered errors. The module does not model a real algorithm or system, but rather serves as a suite of test cases for the TLA+ parser or semantic checker, especially regarding the correct placement and usage of primed variables, temporal formulas, and quantification. No fault model or liveness/safety property is being verified; the focus is on language-level correctness and error detection.
