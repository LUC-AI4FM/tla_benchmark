This module provides a TLAPS proof that the CoffeeCan specification maintains the TypeInvariant property throughout execution. The proof strengthens the invariant by adding a bound on the total number of beans (BeanCount) to ensure that the bean count never exceeds MaxBeanCount, which is necessary because certain actions could otherwise violate this bound. The proof is structured hierarchically and covers all possible actions, showing that each action preserves the invariant and that the system is type-correct under the specified dynamics.
