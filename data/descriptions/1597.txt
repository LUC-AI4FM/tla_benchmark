This specification models Lamport's Fast Mutual Exclusion algorithm for two classes of processes: a distinguished process 1 and a family of processes in 2..N. The file contains a PlusCal algorithm and its generated TLA+ translation, using shared variables x, y, and b together with per-process control state and failure flags to coordinate entry into a critical section. It specifies initialization, the step relation, weak fairness for both process classes, a mutual exclusion invariant stating that no two distinct processes are simultaneously in the critical section, and a liveness property asserting that infinitely often some process is in the critical section.
