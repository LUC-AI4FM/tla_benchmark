This specification models a state-graph exploration algorithm that searches a directed graph represented as a record with components `states`, `initials`, and `actions`. The explorer does not enforce BFS ordering: the frontier `S` is a set, and the next state to explore is chosen non-deterministically, while it still records predecessor edges and level information to reconstruct a counterexample path. It checks for safety violations and deadlocks, reconstructs a path once termination is detected, and asserts temporal properties for overall termination and for eventual discovery of a violation state together with a valid counterexample path, under weak fairness of `Next`.
