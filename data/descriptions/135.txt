This module defines helper operators for reasoning about typed mathematical functions, specifically restriction, range, inverse, injectivity, surjections, and bijections between sets. It is a library-style specification rather than a transition-system model: it declares no variables, no actions, and no temporal behavior, and instead gives pure state-level definitions over functions and sets. The file also notes that IsInjective is overridden by TLC in Java, but this module itself only contains the mathematical definitions and existence predicates based on nonemptiness of the corresponding sets.
