This module is a comprehensive test of TLA+ subexpression labels and their use in subexpression selectors. It defines a variety of operators, including parametric and higher-arity operators, and exercises TLA+'s subexpression referencing and labeling features. The module includes numerous ASSUME and THEOREM statements to test the evaluation of labeled subexpressions, LET-in constructs, CHOOSE expressions, record and tuple constructors, and function overrides. There are no temporal properties or state variables; the focus is on the syntactic and semantic manipulation of expressions, labels, and selectors, making this a testbed for TLA+ parsing and subexpression referencing rather than a model of a distributed algorithm or protocol.
