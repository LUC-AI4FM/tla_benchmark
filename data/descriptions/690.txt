This module states and proves a large collection of theorems about functions and cardinality-style reasoning in TLA+. It covers restriction, range, inverse, injections, surjections, bijections, existence predicates for these notions, transitivity properties, Cantor-Bernstein-Schroeder style results, and facts about finite sets indexed by intervals such as 1..n. It also proves algebraic and typing properties of folding and summing functions over finite sets, including behavior under EXCEPT updates, disjoint union, monotonicity, emptiness, singleton characterization, and constant-valued functions; the module is proof-oriented and contains only theorems, with no state variables, actions, or temporal behavior.
