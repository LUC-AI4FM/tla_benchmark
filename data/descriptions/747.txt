This specification models checkpoint coordination in a replicated Paxos/RSL-style cluster with node failures, network failures, leader election, replicated requests, and lease-based authorization for checkpointing. It separates environment state, Paxos-related state, and checkpoint-specific state, and uses replicated log entries plus checkpoint leases to ensure that at most one eligible node checkpoints at a time while preserving log consistency. The spec verifies a type invariant, a safety invariant about lease ownership and non-conflicting logs, and temporal expectations that checkpoint opportunities and completions eventually occur under stated fairness assumptions.
