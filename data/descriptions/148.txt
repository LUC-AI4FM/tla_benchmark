This module specifies a small TLA+ model used to exercise nested local operator bindings together with a left fold over a sequence. It defines a constant sequence and a family of local operators inside LET expressions, where the innermost operator adds its arguments together with an outer LET-bound parameter, and then applies ApaFoldSeqLeft over the sequence. The specification has one variable x with a trivial behavior consisting of initialization to 0 and stuttering thereafter, and it defines an invariant characterizing the value computed by the nested fold construction.
