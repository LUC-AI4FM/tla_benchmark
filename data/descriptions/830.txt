This module contains TLAPS-checked proofs for the multi-car elevator specification, focusing on safety properties. It proves that the system maintains type correctness and safety invariants, as specified in the main Elevator module, by strengthening the state invariant and deriving the required invariants as corollaries. The liveness (temporal) properties are not addressed here. The proofs handle the limitations of TLAPS regarding multi-argument function application by explicitly stating unfolding lemmas, and they make explicit the disjointness between Elevator and Floor, which is implicit in TLC models. The proof strategy involves mutual induction over several auxiliary invariants, with detailed case analysis for each action, but the full inductive step for the strengthened safety invariant is omitted as it is complex and left for future work.
