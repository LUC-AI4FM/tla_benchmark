This specification models a TCP-like three-way handshake protocol between an initiator and a receiver. The state consists of a set of exchanged messages, sequence numbers for each side, and control states for the initiator and receiver; the messages are represented as records with syn, ack, seqno, and ackno fields. The spec defines the initialization and four actions for sending SYN, sending SYN-ACK, sending ACK, and receiving the final ACK, and it states an invariant asserting that whenever the receiver is established, the initiator is also established.
