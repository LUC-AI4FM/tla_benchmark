This specification models an optimized transactional commit protocol in the style of Percolator with primary and secondary keys, per-key locks, versioned writes, and transaction timestamps. Clients move through init, working, prewriting, committing, committed, or aborted states; they can read keys, clean up stale locks, prewrite locks on keys, commit the primary key, or abort. The model checks type correctness and several safety properties including write ordering, lock consistency, committed/aborted consistency, rollback consistency, and preservation of a snapshot-isolation indicator tracked explicitly in the state.
