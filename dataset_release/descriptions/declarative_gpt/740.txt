This specification models the Car Talk puzzle from the 22 October 2011 program, in which a farmer must break a 40-pound stone into 4 pieces so that any integer weight from 1 to 40 pounds can be measured using a balance scale and those pieces. The spec generalizes the problem to breaking an N-pound stone into P pieces, with N and P as constants. It defines a 'break' as a function from 1..P to 1..N (the weights of the pieces), constrained to be non-decreasing and sum to N. The main property verified is that for every weight w in 1..N, there exist two disjoint subsets of the pieces such that w plus the sum of one subset equals the sum of the other, i.e., the weight can be balanced. The specification computes all such solutions and provides an expanded form showing, for each solution and each weight, how to arrange the pieces on the scale. The design avoids ambiguity from identical weights by using functions rather than sets for breaks, and includes comments explaining all nonstandard TLA+ notation.
