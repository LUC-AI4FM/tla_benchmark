This module specifies a simple existence property over two constant sets A and B and a constant binary operator AreGood. The specification models no stateful algorithm or transition system; instead, it defines a single logical predicate asserting that there exists a pair of elements, one from A and one from B, such that AreGood holds for that pair. The comments indicate intended informal types: A and B are sets of integers, and AreGood maps two integers to a Boolean result.
