This module is a model-checking wrapper for a TCP specification imported from the `tcp` module. It introduces two peers and constrains the initial state so that one node starts in `"SYN-SENT"` after an active open while the other starts in `"LISTEN"` after a passive open, with an initial `"SYN"` segment in the network toward the listening side. It also defines additional state constraints that forbid retransmission by requiring adjacent network messages to differ, and forbid any further active opens, passive opens, or sends after the initial setup, reflecting a finite-state design choice suitable for TLC exploration.
