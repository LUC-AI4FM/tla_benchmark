This module is a test-oriented TLA+ specification for bitwise operators provided by the extended Bitwise-related modules. It defines pure recursive arithmetic characterizations of bitwise AND, OR, XOR, and NOT, and then uses many ASSUME assertions to check that the built-in or imported bitwise operators agree with those definitions and with expected algebraic identities on bounded natural-number ranges. The file has no state variables, no actions, and no temporal behavior; it is essentially a collection of logical and executable consistency checks over natural numbers, including explicit example tables and quantified properties such as commutativity, associativity, self-inverse behavior, and right-shift correspondence with division by 2.
