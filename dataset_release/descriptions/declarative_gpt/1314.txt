This specification models the classic Die Hard water-jug problem using two buckets of capacities 5 and 3. The PlusCal algorithm nondeterministically chooses among filling, emptying, and pouring operations between the two buckets, and the translated TLA+ action `FixWater` captures those state transitions over the bucket contents and the last chosen action. The temporal specification defines an initial state and allows arbitrary repetitions of `Next` with stuttering, and it includes a simple invariant candidate `Inv == bigBucket # 4`, expressing that the big bucket is not equal to 4.
