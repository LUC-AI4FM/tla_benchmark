This specification models a single-decree Paxos protocol with a fixed leader, generated from a PlusCal algorithm and then translated into TLA+. It represents proposers and acceptors communicating through a set of message records, with acceptors tracking their highest prepared ballot, highest accepted ballot, and accepted value. The model assumes finite acceptor and value sets, quorum intersection, and ballots drawn from natural numbers, and it verifies the basic state-machine specification consisting of an initial condition and all behaviors generated by the Next action under stuttering.
