This specification models single-decree Paxos with a fixed leader and without leader election. It represents communication as a growing set of sent messages and models acceptor state with the variables maxPrepared, maxAccepted, and valAccepted. The spec includes assumptions about finite acceptor and value sets, quorum intersection, and ballots being natural numbers, and it defines the standard Paxos phases 1a, 1b, 2a, and 2b together with a temporal specification stating that execution starts in Init and thereafter always follows Next up to stuttering.
