This specification models a simple PlusCal algorithm with two processes, ProcA and ProcB, that both increment a shared variable x once. The translated TLA+ spec uses a program-counter variable pc to track each process's control location, initializes both processes at their single statement, and then allows either process action to execute until both reach "Done". The specification checks the standard safety-style behavior through the transition relation and includes a temporal termination property asserting that eventually all processes are done; it also permits infinite stuttering after termination via an explicit Terminating action to avoid deadlock.
