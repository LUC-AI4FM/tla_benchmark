This specification models the classic moving-cat puzzle with a line of numbered boxes. A cat occupies one box and must move every step to an adjacent box without wrap-around, while the observer checks boxes according to a back-and-forth search pattern over boxes 2 through N - 1, reversing direction at the ends. The spec defines an initial state, a cat movement action, an observation action, a combined next-state relation, a temporal specification with weak fairness for the cat's movement, and an eventual victory property stating that the observed box will eventually coincide with the cat's box.
