This module specifies a small state machine that tracks a packet-like record `p` and a Boolean error flag `error`. The packet has a `sender` field drawn from a small set of account identifiers and a nested `data` record with an `amount` field in the range `0..3`; a packet is considered well formed when its sender is not the empty string and its amount is greater than 0. The next-state relation nondeterministically chooses a new packet and sets `error'` to FALSE when the chosen packet satisfies the precondition and to TRUE otherwise, and the invariant asserts that whenever `error = TRUE`, the current packet must have either a nonpositive amount or an empty sender.
