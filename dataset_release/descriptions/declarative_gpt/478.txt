This module specifies a parameterized asynchronous non-blocking atomic commitment algorithm with failure detectors, following the Guerraoui 2001 paper cited in the comments. The model tracks how processes initially vote YES or NO, may crash and become Byzantine-marked via the "BYZ" status, send messages, receive YES/NO messages, abort, or commit, while a failure detector variable records suspicion information. The specification defines three initializations (arbitrary votes, all YES, all NO), a next-state relation with weak fairness for message-processing and decision actions, and temporal properties for agreement, abort validity, commit validity, and termination.
