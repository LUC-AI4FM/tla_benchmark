This module specifies and proves properties of a Byzantine Paxos-style consensus algorithm called BPCon, an abstraction/generalization of Castro-Liskov Byzantine fault tolerance. The system models good acceptors, malicious fake acceptors, arbitrary leaders, message sets for phases 1a/1b/1c/2av/2b, and an auxiliary knowledge variable knowsSent that represents which 1b messages a good acceptor knows were sent. The specification includes the PlusCal algorithm, its generated TLA+ translation, a refinement mapping to an instantiated non-Byzantine Paxos module PConProof, an inductive invariant proof showing safety, and a theorem that the BPCon spec implements the abstract PCon spec under the refinement mapping.
