This module specifies a solution to the classic prisoners-and-switches puzzle. One designated prisoner acts as a counter, while every other prisoner is allowed to move switch A up at most twice; the counter increments a count whenever he finds switch A up and turns it down, and everyone otherwise flips switch B. The specification models random repeated selection of prisoners under weak fairness assumptions that guarantee the counter and every non-counter prisoner are brought into the room infinitely often, and it states both a safety property saying that when the protocol declares completion then every prisoner has visited the room, and a liveness property saying that the declaration condition is eventually reached.
