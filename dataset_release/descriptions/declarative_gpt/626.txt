This specification models attacker isolation at a full node after it receives evidence from a light client about conflicting blocks. It assumes a Tendermint-like setting with unit voting power per validator, a trusted common height, a conflicting height, and a violated security model in which Byzantine validators may have signed a conflicting block. The spec initializes an arbitrary blockchain and conflicting evidence, then classifies the attack into Lunatic, Equivocation, or Amnesia cases and computes a set of attackers; it checks invariants asserting that the detected attackers have more than one third of the voting power and that all detected attackers are faulty.
