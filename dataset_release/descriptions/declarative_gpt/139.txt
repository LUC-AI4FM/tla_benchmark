This specification models a simple 12-hour clock with a single state variable hr representing the current hour. The initial condition requires hr to be in the range 1 through 12, and the next-state relation increments the hour unless it is 12, in which case it wraps around to 1. The temporal specification HC states that the system starts in a valid clock state and always evolves according to HCnxt, and the theorem asserts that this implies the invariant that HCini holds in all states.
