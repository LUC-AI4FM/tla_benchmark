This specification models a simple multi-car elevator system with a set of people, a set of elevators, and a bounded set of floors. It defines how people choose destinations, call elevators, enter and exit cars, how elevators open and close doors, move, stop, and are dispatched to calls using a simple nearest-eligible-elevator policy rather than a globally optimized scheduling algorithm. The specification includes type and safety invariants, temporal liveness expectations that calls are eventually serviced and waiting people eventually reach their destinations, and fairness assumptions over the main actions; it culminates in a theorem asserting that under Spec, the type, safety, and temporal invariants always hold.
