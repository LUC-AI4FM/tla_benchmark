This specification models a very small transition system with two state variables, a and b. The initial state sets both variables to 0, and the main action disj increments a while also constraining the next-state value a' to lie in the range 1..10; Next executes disj and leaves b unchanged. The temporal specification consists of Init, the standard stuttering-closed step formula [][Next]_vars, and a weak fairness condition WF_vars(disj), with a comment highlighting that disj does not mention b even though vars includes b. An invariant-style operator Inv checks whether disj is enabled, and the property Prop is <>FALSE, apparently included to force TLC to perform liveness checking.
