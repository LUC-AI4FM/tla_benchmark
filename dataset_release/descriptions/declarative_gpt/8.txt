This module specifies the classic two-phase commit resource-manager state machine. It models a set RM of participating resource managers, each of which starts in the "working" state, may move to "prepared", and may then decide either "committed" or "aborted" subject to global conditions captured by canCommit and notCommitted. The specification defines the initial state, a next-state relation over rmState, a complete temporal specification TCSpec using [][TCNext]_rmState, and proves that the protocol preserves type correctness and consistency, namely that no two resource managers decide conflicting outcomes.
