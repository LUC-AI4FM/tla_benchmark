This module axiomatizes and then selects a model of the real numbers on top of a Peano-style natural-number foundation imported from the Peano module. It defines a predicate IsModelOfReals(R, Plus, Times, Leq) expressing algebraic, order, and completeness properties of a real-number structure, proves existentially that some such model exists, then chooses one witness RM and introduces derived operators for Real numbers, infinities, arithmetic, ordering, subtraction, division, integers, and exponentiation. The specification is not a transition-system or fault-tolerant protocol model; instead it is a mathematical TLA+ theory module with no variables, no actions, and no temporal behavior, focused on abstract construction and characterization of reals and extended-real-like values.
