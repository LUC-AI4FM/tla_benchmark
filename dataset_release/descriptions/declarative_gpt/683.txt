This module is a TLAPS proof development for theorem properties of finite-set folding operators provided by imported modules, especially FoldSet and derived operators such as SumSet, ProductSet, and MapThenSumSet, as well as Max and Min over integer or natural-number sets. It does not define a transition system or algorithm with variables; instead it proves algebraic, typing, monotonicity, subset, empty-set, nonempty-set, disjoint-union, and extremum properties for these set operators over finite sets. The design relies on proof decomposition, finite-set induction, CHOOSE witnesses for nonempty finite subsets, and previously proved lemmas from imported modules such as FiniteSetsExt, FiniteSetTheorems, and FoldsTheorems.
