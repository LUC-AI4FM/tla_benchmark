This specification models a rolling deployment of software across a small set of servers behind a load balancer. One process updates individual servers, while a separate coordinator process removes servers from the load balancer, triggers updates, waits for completion, and then restores service, with the goal of preserving availability during deployment. The translated PlusCal model checks a temporal specification consisting of initialization, step transitions, and weak fairness for both the per-server update process and the coordinator, and it defines termination as eventual completion of all processes.
