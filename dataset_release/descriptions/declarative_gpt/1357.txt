This specification models a tiny two-party message-passing system with parties "A" and "B". Each party starts in a "Start" control state, may take a step that sends a single hello message to the other party by updating the network, and then moves to "Done"; once all parties are done, the system stutters in a terminating state. The temporal specification requires initialization and all Next steps, together with weak fairness for each party's Start action, and it checks the liveness property that eventually every party reaches "Done".
