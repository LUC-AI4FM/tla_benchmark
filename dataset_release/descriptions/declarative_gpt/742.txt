This specification models the Car Talk puzzle from the 22 October 2011 program, in which a farmer must break a 40-pound stone into 4 pieces so that any integer weight from 1 to 40 pounds can be measured using a balance scale and those pieces. The spec generalizes the problem to breaking an N-pound stone into P pieces, with N and P as constants. It defines a 'break' as a function from 1..P to 1..N (the weights of the pieces), constrained so the pieces are in non-decreasing order and sum to N. The main property verified is that for every weight w in 1..N, there exist two disjoint subsets of pieces that can be placed on opposite sides of the scale to balance w. The specification computes all such solutions and provides an operator to expand and display them, illustrating how each weight can be measured. The design uses recursive operators, set comprehensions, and Hilbert's CHOOSE to express the solution space.
