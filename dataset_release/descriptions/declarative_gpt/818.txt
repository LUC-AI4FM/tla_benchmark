This module contains TLAPS proofs for the safety properties of the parallel reachability algorithm specified in the imported module ParReach. It proves that the invariant Inv is preserved by Spec and also proves a refinement-style safety result showing that the concrete specification implies the initialization and step relation of an instantiated module R under the refinement mapping already defined in ParReach. The file is proof-oriented rather than algorithm-defining: it introduces no new state variables or transition operators of its own, and focuses on invariant preservation, stuttering-step reasoning, and refinement of safety behavior.
