This module specifies Peterson's mutual exclusion algorithm for two processes, as taught in a parallel programming course at ULi\u00e8ge. The specification models the algorithm's control flow, shared variables, and process-local variables, and includes a translation from PlusCal to TLA+. The invariant 'Inv' formalizes the mutual exclusion property and is augmented to ensure that at most one process is in the critical section at any time. The module also includes a refinement mapping to an abstract lock specification and proves that the invariant is inductive. Temporal properties such as liveness (waiting leads to critical section) are also specified.
