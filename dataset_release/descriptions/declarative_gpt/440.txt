This specification models a very small one-variable transition system over the variable z, initialized to 0 and updated by toggling between 0 and 1 via the action Next. It instantiates another module, test55a, by substituting both instance parameters x and y with z, and then defines properties in terms of instance-exported operators such as F, G, Init, SafeSpec, and LiveSpec. The temporal specification requires the initial condition, that every step satisfies Next up to stuttering, and weak fairness of Next; it also asserts an invariant combining instance properties and exposes imported safety and liveness properties through the instance.
