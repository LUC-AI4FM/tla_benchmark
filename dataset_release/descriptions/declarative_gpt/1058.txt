This specification models the Dijkstra-Scholten tree algorithm for detecting termination of a diffusing distributed computation over an arbitrary directed process graph. Processes can be active or idle, computation messages flow along graph edges, acknowledgments flow in the reverse direction, and an overlay parent relation is maintained dynamically to form a tree of non-neutral processes rooted at the leader. The spec assumes asynchronous unordered but lossless message delivery, no Byzantine failures, and verifies safety and liveness properties including counter consistency, an overlay-tree invariant, the fact that if the leader is neutral then all other processes are neutral, and the leads-to property that if the computation has terminated then the leader eventually becomes neutral.
