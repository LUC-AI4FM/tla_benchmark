This module specifies a resource allocator for a finite set of clients and resources. Clients issue resource requests only when they have no outstanding unsatisfied request and currently hold no resources; requests may be partially satisfied, resources may be returned early, and a schedule sequence constrains which client requests may be served before others. The specification includes weak fairness assumptions requiring clients to return resources once fully satisfied, requiring allocations to eventually happen when enabled, and requiring scheduling to eventually occur; it states and checks type, mutual exclusion, allocator invariants, and several liveness properties such as eventual return, eventual obtainment of requested resources, and infinitely often satisfied requests.
