This specification models a very small state machine with two variables, a boolean variable b and a natural-valued counter i. The initial state sets b to FALSE and i to 1, and each next-state step nondeterministically assigns b' to an element of BOOLEAN while incrementing i by 1. The temporal specification asserts that the system starts in Init and then always evolves according to Next, allowing stuttering through the standard TLA+ box-subscript form.
