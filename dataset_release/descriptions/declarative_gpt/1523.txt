This module specifies the classic prisoners-and-switches puzzle in which one designated prisoner acts as a counter and all other prisoners use one switch to signal visits. The state consists of two boolean switches, a function tracking how many times each non-counter prisoner has moved switch A up, and a count of how many times the counter has moved switch A down. The specification includes an initial condition, next-state actions for the counter and non-counter prisoners, weak fairness assumptions ensuring every prisoner is brought into the room infinitely often, and temporal properties asserting both safety and eventual completion.
