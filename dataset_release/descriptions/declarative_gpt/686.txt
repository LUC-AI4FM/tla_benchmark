This module contains TLAPS proofs of theorems about the set-folding operator MapThenFoldSet imported from the Folds module. It proves that the operator is well-defined on finite sets with a choice function for non-empty subsets, characterizes its behavior on the empty and non-empty cases, and establishes extensionality and bijection-based equivalence results. It also proves typing and algebraic properties for associative and commutative folding operators, including add-element and disjoint-union theorems, using induction over finite subsets and well-founded induction; the file is proof-oriented rather than an executable state-machine or temporal-system specification.
