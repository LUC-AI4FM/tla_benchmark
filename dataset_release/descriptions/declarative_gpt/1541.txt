This specification models an erroneous variant of the Non-blocking Atomic Commitment Protocol (ACP-NB), where the mistake is that a broadcast message is delivered locally before it has been forwarded to other participants. The protocol is intended to demonstrate a violation of the atomic commitment consistency property (AC1). The model introduces a 'forward' variable for each participant to track the forwarding of decisions, and defines actions for forwarding, deciding based on forwarded messages, and aborting on timeout. The specification includes temporal properties and fairness conditions to capture the protocol's liveness and safety requirements, and highlights the flaw that leads to inconsistency.
