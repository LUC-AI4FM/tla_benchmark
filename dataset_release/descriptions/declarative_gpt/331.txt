This module specifies a very small state machine over two variables: an integer-valued year and a boolean-valued hasLicense flag. The initialization sets year to 80 and hasLicense to FALSE, one transition increments the year modulo 100 while leaving hasLicense unchanged, and another transition sets hasLicense to TRUE once the age-like condition year - 80 >= 18 holds while leaving year unchanged. The file includes simple assumptions constraining the constants 80 and 18 to numeric ranges, uses assignment-style primed updates in action definitions, and contains no temporal specification, fairness, proofs, or PlusCal algorithm.
