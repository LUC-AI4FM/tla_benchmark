This module gives an abstract specification of a distributed consensus algorithm in terms of commit logs maintained by a finite set of servers. The model assumes a strong observation model in which any node can atomically inspect the state of all other nodes, and it focuses on append-only logs that never diverge: servers may copy a suffix from another server, extend a longest log, or copy one of the longest logs and extend it. The specification defines an initial-state condition, a next-state relation over the single variable cLogs, and temporal safety-style properties such as append-only growth, absence of conflicting logs, and equivalence between existential action definitions and axiom-style formulations used for efficient refinement checking in TLC.
