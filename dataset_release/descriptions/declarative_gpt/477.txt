This module specifies the NBAC (non-blocking atomic commitment) algorithm with crash failures and a per-process failure detector. Processes vote YES or NO, send their votes as messages, receive nondeterministically delivered messages, may crash, and then decide ABORT or COMMIT depending on received votes and the detector state. The specification defines an initial condition, a next-state relation over program counters, received and sent messages, and detector outputs, and checks typing and a validity property; it also includes a weak fairness condition in Spec to ensure fair progress of certain enabled receive/send/decision behavior.
