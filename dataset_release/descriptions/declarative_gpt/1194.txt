This specification models a simple system with three variables x, y, and z, each initialized to a large range of natural numbers. The initial predicate (Init) allows x, y, and z to take any value in their respective ranges, resulting in a large initial state space. The Next action is defined as a stuttering step, meaning the variables do not change in any transition. The invariant Inv is always TRUE, indicating no additional safety property is being checked. The spec is intended as a test of state space size rather than a model of a particular algorithm or protocol.
