This module gives a high-level TLA+ specification of the Paxos consensus algorithm, modeling leaders and acceptors that communicate by sending messages for phase 1 and phase 2 of Paxos. It represents sent messages as elements of a monotonically growing set, so message receipt is modeled by enabling actions when matching messages are present, and the specification focuses on safety rather than liveness or message loss. The module defines the state variables tracking highest seen ballots, highest voted ballots and values, and sent messages, then proves that the resulting behavior implements the imported Voting specification and preserves an inductive invariant connecting messages and votes.
