This specification models a fair solution to the classic readers-writers problem using a waiting queue of pending access requests. Processes can request either read or write access, queued requests are served from the head of the queue, readers may coexist, and writers require exclusive access with no current readers. The spec defines initialization, state transitions, weak fairness conditions for all request and service actions, safety invariants about mutual exclusion and writer cardinality, and liveness properties asserting that every actor eventually repeatedly reads, writes, and also eventually leaves those states.
