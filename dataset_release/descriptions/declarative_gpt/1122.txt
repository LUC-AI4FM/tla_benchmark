This specification models a modified two-phase commit protocol with a transaction manager, multiple resource managers, and a backup transaction manager that can take over if the primary transaction manager becomes hidden after deciding. The resource managers move among working, prepared, committed, aborted, and failed states; the transaction manager decides commit or abort based on global conditions, and optional failures are controlled by the constants RMMAYFAIL and TMMAYFAIL. The spec defines the initial state, the next-state relation, weak fairness for all processes, an eventual termination property, and invariants for type correctness, consistency of decisions, and absence of committed resource managers in NotCommitted.
