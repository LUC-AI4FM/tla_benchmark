This module provides TLAPS proofs for the Cigarette Smokers problem, focusing on two main properties: type correctness (TypeOK) and the AtMostOne invariant (at most one smoker is smoking). The specification assumes that the set Ingredients is finite, as required for the use of Cardinality. The proofs show that TypeOK and AtMostOne are inductive invariants under the system's actions, and that AtMostOne is equivalent to the smoking set having cardinality at most one. The module includes several supporting lemmas about the structure of the smoking set and the offers, and demonstrates that the invariants are preserved by all actions in the system.
