This module specifies the Paxos consensus algorithm in an abstract message-passing style without explicit leaders or learners. It models acceptors tracking their highest promised ballot and highest voted ballot/value, and models communication via a monotonically growing set of sent messages rather than explicit in-transit delivery, emphasizing safety over network behavior. The spec defines the standard Paxos phase 1 and phase 2 actions, a next-state relation and safety-oriented invariant, and includes a refinement-oriented reconstruction of voting information together with copied predicates from a Voting specification to express safe value selection.
