This module provides wrapper theorems that expose TypeOK and Inv as named invariants of the Spec for the SimpleRegular algorithm. The actual inductive content and algorithmic details are contained in the imported SimpleRegular module; this file focuses on stating and proving that TypeOK and Inv are invariants under the system's specification. The proofs are structured hierarchically and use TLAPS proof constructs, but do not introduce new algorithmic content or state variables. The design is intended for proof management and clarity, making the invariants explicit for use in further verification or as proof obligations.
