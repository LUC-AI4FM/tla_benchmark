This specification models a simple lossy message transmission system with three set-valued state variables: a sender set, a receiver set, and a transmission medium set. Initially, all values are in the sender, while the receiver and medium are empty; messages may be sent from sender to medium, dropped from the medium, or received into the receiver without being removed from the medium. The intended regression-test scenario checks behavior of set difference in the presence of existentially chosen elements, and the invariant asserts that not all three components are empty at the same time.
