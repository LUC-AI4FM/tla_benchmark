This module specifies a simplified one-shot Tendermint consensus protocol focused on safety and fork accountability. It models correct and Byzantine processes, message sets for proposals, prevotes, and precommits, and local process state such as rounds, locks, valid values, and decisions. The specification assumes non-deterministic timeouts, a non-deterministic proposer, pre-injected Byzantine messages in the initial state, unit voting power, and identity hashes, and it defines safety properties including agreement, validity, accountability, and several intentionally false invariants used to generate counterexamples demonstrating equivocation or amnesia by faulty processes.
