This module models a small transition system over two integer state variables, v1 and v2, intended to illustrate assignment reordering behavior in an SMT-based action analysis. The comments explain two action formulas, MayFail and MustFail, and discuss when reordering of primed-variable constraints may incorrectly succeed or must fail because of use-before-assignment dependencies across existentially quantified blocks. The file does not define a complete temporal specification such as Spec; instead it defines an initial state and two candidate next-state actions to study how ordering of assignments and constraints affects satisfiability.
