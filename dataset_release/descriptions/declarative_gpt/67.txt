This specification models a bounded buffer shared by a nonempty set of producer processes and a nonempty set of consumer processes. The state consists of a sequence-valued buffer together with sets of currently waiting producers and waiting consumers, and the main actions describe how a producer-like step or consumer-like step either waits when the buffer is full or empty, respectively, or updates the buffer and possibly wakes a waiting process. The spec assumes producers and consumers are disjoint and that the buffer capacity is at least one, defines an initial empty state, and specifies behaviors with the temporal formula `Init /\ [][Next]_vars`. It also defines an invariant asserting that not all producers and all consumers are simultaneously waiting.
