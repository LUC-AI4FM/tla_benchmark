This specification models the Alternating Bit Protocol (ABP) for reliable communication over unreliable channels. It defines the sender and receiver state variables, including the alternating bit, acknowledgment bit, and the data being sent and received. The model includes actions for sending new values, receiving messages, and receiving acknowledgments, and specifies the protocol's temporal properties, including fairness conditions for message and acknowledgment receipt. The specification also defines a type invariant and an example liveness property expressing that the sender and acknowledgment bits are infinitely often out of sync.
