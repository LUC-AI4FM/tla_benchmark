This module specifies a disk-based variant of Synod/Paxos in which processes interact through persistent disks and maintain local phase, block, and read/write bookkeeping state. The model assumes each process has a disjoint positive ballot set, a distinguished set of disks with a majority predicate, and requires that any two disk majorities intersect. The specification defines initialization, phase-0 recovery reads, phase-1 and phase-2 writes and reads, ballot restarts, and failures, then verifies that the overall behavior refinement-style implies the imported SynodSpec theorem target.
