This specification models a small consensus-style voting protocol over three fixed nodes and two possible decision values. Nodes send vote requests, send votes in response, collect received votes, may become leaders once they have a quorum of votes, and leaders may then decide a value if they have not yet decided one. The file defines the initial state, the next-state relation as a disjunction of protocol actions, and a safety property asserting agreement: if any two nodes have decided values, those values must be equal.
