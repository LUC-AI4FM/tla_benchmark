This specification models a simple recursive factorial operator over integers and uses it to initialize and update a single state variable. The recursive operator Fact computes factorial by returning 1 when the argument is at most 1 and otherwise multiplying n by Fact(n - 1). The behavior consists of an initial state setting f to Fact(4), a next-state action setting f' to Fact(7), and an invariant asserting that f is always at least 1. The file is a small test focused on recursive operator handling, as indicated by the comment about a missing annotation for unrolling recursion.
