This module provides TLAPS proofs for the theorems stated in EWD687a.tla, which models a distributed termination detection algorithm based on Dijkstra's EWD687. The system involves processes communicating over edges, with counters tracking sent, received, acknowledged, and in-flight messages. The proofs establish inductive invariants for counter consistency, tree structure, and acyclicity, and relate these to properties such as global termination and the existence of a spanning tree rooted at the leader. The module also interfaces with the community Graphs module to formalize tree properties, and includes detailed hierarchical proofs for safety properties, while some liveness and path-construction arguments are left as future work or omitted.
