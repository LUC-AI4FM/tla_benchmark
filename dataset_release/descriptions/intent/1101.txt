Specify Peterson's mutual-exclusion algorithm for two processes. Two processes compete to enter a critical section, coordinated by a shared turn variable and a pair of Boolean flags indicating each process's intent to enter. Each process repeatedly: signals its intent, yields the turn to the other process, waits until either the other process is not interested or the turn belongs to it, executes the critical section, then retracts its intent. The specification must satisfy two correctness properties. First, mutual exclusion: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time. Second, the algorithm must refine an abstract lock specification, meaning the concrete protocol correctly implements the higher-level notion of acquiring and releasing a lock. An inductive invariant should be provided and proved: it captures the relationship between each process's flag and its program location, and the condition that any process in the critical section either holds the turn or the other process has not yet signaled intent. Both the inductive invariant and the refinement mapping to the abstract lock must be formally verified using TLAPS.
