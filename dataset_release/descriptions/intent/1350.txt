Write a TLA+ specification that demonstrates a simple counter that increments from 1 up to 5 and then stops. The system starts with the counter at 1 and repeatedly increments it by 1 until it reaches 5, at which point it remains fixed forever. The specification should use weak fairness to ensure the counter always makes progress when it can. The key liveness property to verify is that if the counter starts at 1, it will eventually stabilize permanently at 5. This serves as a small demonstration or test case for verifying that TLA+ model checkers correctly handle liveness properties combining eventuality and stability (the "leads to stable" pattern), particularly the combination of a simple reachability argument with weak fairness ensuring no infinite stuttering occurs before the goal state is reached.
