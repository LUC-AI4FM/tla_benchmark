Write a TLA+ specification for a mapping algorithm that translates source regions from a TLA+ specification to corresponding regions in a PlusCal program. The system operates over a sequence of typed objects called a TPSpec, which interleaves TLA+ token regions, parenthesis markers (begin/end) that delimit syntactic units in the PCal code, and break markers that indicate non-adjacent PCal regions. Locations are line/column pairs ordered left-to-right and top-to-bottom; regions are pairs of ordered locations.

The algorithm takes a TPSpec and a highlighted region in the TLA+ source as inputs. It first identifies the leftmost and rightmost TLA+ tokens that intersect or are nearest to the given region, with tie-breaking based on distance when the region falls entirely in whitespace. It then scans between those two tokens, tracking the relative parenthesis depth, computing both the depth difference between the right and left tokens and the minimum depth encountered along the way.

The key correctness property, checked by assertion at termination, is that after the scan completes: the parenthesis depth at the right token equals the depth at the left token plus the accumulated depth delta, and the minimum depth computed incrementally equals the true minimum parenthesis depth over all positions between the two tokens. The specification must also verify that the algorithm terminates.
