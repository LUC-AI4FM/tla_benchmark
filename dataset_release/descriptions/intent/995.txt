Write a TLA+ specification for a resource allocator that manages a shared pool of resources among multiple clients, communicating via an asynchronous message-passing network. Clients may request a non-empty set of resources only when they hold nothing and have no pending requests; the allocator responds by allocating subsets of available resources, possibly incrementally; and clients may return resources they hold at any time, even before their full request is satisfied, though they are only obligated to return everything once fully satisfied. The allocator maintains a schedule of pending clients and allocates resources in schedule order, ensuring no client earlier in the schedule is denied a resource given to a later one. Messages of three kinds — request, allocate, return — travel through an unordered network and must eventually be delivered. The specification must guarantee mutual exclusion (no resource held by two clients simultaneously), that every client request is eventually fully satisfied, that clients who receive all requested resources eventually return them, and that every client is satisfied infinitely often. Liveness relies on fair message delivery, fair scheduling, fair allocation, and clients faithfully returning resources once their request is complete.
