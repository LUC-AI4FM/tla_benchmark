Specify the Chameneos concurrency game as described in the original benchmark paper. The system involves M creatures (chameneos), each starting with one of three colors (blue, red, yellow), that repeatedly attempt to meet at a shared meeting place (mall). The mall holds at most one waiting creature at a time. When a creature arrives at an empty mall and the total meeting count has not yet reached N, it waits; when a second creature arrives, the two meet, each adopting the complement color of the pair (if colors differ, both take the third color; if identical, both keep their color), and each increments its personal meeting count. Once N total meetings have occurred, any creature arriving at the mall instead fades permanently. The system terminates when all creatures have faded. The key safety property is that once exactly N total pairwise meetings have occurred, the sum of all individual creatures' personal meeting counts equals 2*N. Type correctness must also be maintained throughout. Deadlock need not be considered a failure condition.
