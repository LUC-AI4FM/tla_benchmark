Write a TLA+ specification that demonstrates and tests TLC's `_POSSIBLE` checking feature. Model a simple counter that cycles through the values 0, 1, and 2 (incrementing modulo 3), starting at 0. Define three predicates of interest: one that holds when the counter reaches 2, one that holds when it equals 1, and one that captures the wrap-around transition from 2 back to 0. Configure TLC to track whether each of these predicates is ever satisfied during model checking (using the `_POSSIBLE` directive), and include a postcondition that asserts TLC recorded exactly one witness for each of the three predicates. The purpose is to verify that TLC's possibility-tracking mechanism correctly identifies that all three conditions are reachable in this simple cyclic system.
