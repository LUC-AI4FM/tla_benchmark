Write a TLA+ specification for an augmented mutual-exclusion lock that serves as a refinement bridge between a simple two-process lock and Peterson's mutual-exclusion algorithm. The base lock has processes cycling through entry, critical section, and exit steps. To establish equivalence with Peterson's algorithm, the lock must be extended with two auxiliary constructs: a history variable that tracks the assignment of a turn value (recording which process was last assigned the turn during entry), and a stuttering variable that artificially expands a single lock entry step into three sub-steps, mirroring the three-step entry sequence in Peterson's specification. The stuttering mechanism should follow Lamport's auxiliary-variable methodology. The extended specification must satisfy type correctness for all variables, an invariant relating process program-counter positions and the auxiliary variables to the turn ownership, and must refine both the original lock specification and Peterson's specification — demonstrating that the two are equivalent modulo the auxiliary variables. The refinement to Peterson requires translating program-counter labels and stuttering state into Peterson's internal labels and boolean flags.
