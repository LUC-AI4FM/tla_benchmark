Specify a solution to the Dining Philosophers problem for N philosophers seated around a circular table, where each philosopher alternates between thinking and eating, and must hold both adjacent forks simultaneously in order to eat. Model the forks as binary semaphores. To break the deadlock that arises when all philosophers acquire forks in the same order, philosopher 0 picks up forks in the opposite order from all other philosophers: philosophers 1 through N−1 each acquire their right fork first, then their left, while philosopher 0 acquires its left fork first, then its right. Each philosopher repeatedly cycles through acquiring both forks, eating, and then releasing both forks. The system must satisfy strong fairness for every philosopher's process. The key safety property is mutual exclusion on forks: no two adjacent philosophers may eat simultaneously. The key liveness property is starvation freedom: every philosopher must eat infinitely often. Use N=4 as the model-checking constant.
