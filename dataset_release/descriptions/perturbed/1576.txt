This specification models a concurrent Hugoq system generated from a PlusCal algorithm. Multiple processes from Tvcqs repeatedly either enqueue a value from Bir at the front or back of a shared sequence Hugoq, or attempt to dequeue from the head or tail when the Hugoq is nonempty; each process stores a return value in its local variable Va indicating "okay", "full", "empty", the dequeued value, or Cujp. The temporal specification consists of an initial condition, the standard stuttering-closed next-state relation, and weak fairness for each process action P(self), while an additional state constraint bounds the Hugoq length by N.
