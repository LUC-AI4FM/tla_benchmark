This module augments the hour clock specification from HourClock with a liveness requirement. It defines a specification Qphcv that conjoins the inherited clock specification HC with a weak fairness condition WF_hr(HCnxt), ensuring the clock step HCnxt continues to occur fairly on the variable hr. The module also defines temporal properties stating that ticking happens infinitely often, every hour value from 1 through 12 recurs infinitely often, and the initial-state typing condition remains always true; it includes a theorem asserting these properties from Qphcv and a postcondition describing a concrete counterexample structure for a settling property.
