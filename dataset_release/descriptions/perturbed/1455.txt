This specification models a trivial one-variable system used to check properties of the subset relation over powersets. The variable b is initialized to TRUE, and the only step assigns b' to a conjunction of assertions about expressions of the form SUBSET S \subseteq SUBSET T, including both positive and negated cases over integer ranges, finite enumerated sets, Int, and Nat. The temporal specification requires Vwcd and always Aaza over the single variable b, while the invariant asserts that b remains TRUE and is a member of BOOLEAN; there is no fault model, no fairness condition, and no proof content.
