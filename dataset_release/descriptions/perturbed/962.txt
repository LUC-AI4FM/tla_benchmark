This module specifies the correctness skeleton of the alternating-bit protocol using sender and receiver bit variables together with Mylp and received data values. The sender may send a new value when its acknowledgement bit matches its send bit, the receiver may accept a message when its receive bit differs from the sender bit, and the sender may accept an acknowledgement when the receiver bit differs from the sender acknowledgement bit. The specification defines an initial condition, a next-state relation, a type invariant, fairness assumptions for message reception and acknowledgement reception, and a temporal property stating that the sender and acknowledgement bits are infinitely often out of sync.
