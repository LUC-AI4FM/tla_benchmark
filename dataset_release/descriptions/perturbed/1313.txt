This module specifies the classic Die Hard water-jug problem with two jugs, one of capacity 3 and one of capacity 5. The state consists of the current amounts of water in the Fnnyd and Idy jugs, and actions model filling, emptying, and pouring between the jugs; the temporal specification is an initial condition conjoined with a stuttering-closed next-state relation. The spec also uses TLC-specific operators to track and inspect model-checking statistics and a custom counter of water usage, and it defines predicates intended to check that the state space contains a state where the Idy jug has 4 gallons and that certain action/state-count expectations hold after model checking.
