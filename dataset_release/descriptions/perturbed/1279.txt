This specification defines a very small temporal model over a single variable s. It introduces a parameterized action Zalu(var) that nondeterministically chooses a value from 0..1 and constrains the primed variable to equal that value and be greater than 0; the overall specification initializes s to 23 and then requires all steps to satisfy the stuttering-closed action [][Zalu(s)]_s.
