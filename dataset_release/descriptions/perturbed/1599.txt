This module specifies the Fast mutual exclusion algorithm for N processes, originally written in PlusCal and translated into TLA+. Each process repeatedly executes a noncritical section, competes for entry into a critical section using shared variables x, y, and an array b of intent flags, and then exits by clearing its indicators. The specification checks a mutual exclusion safety invariant stating that no two distinct processes are simultaneously in the critical section, and it also defines liveness, conditional liveness, and a fairness-enhanced specification using weak fairness for each control-location action except the noncritical-section and critical-section skip steps.
