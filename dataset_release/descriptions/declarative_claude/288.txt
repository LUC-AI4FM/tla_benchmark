The View module specifies a simple two-variable integer system used to explore simulation views and trace invariants, likely serving as a test or demonstration module for model-checking techniques involving state views and history-based properties.

The system has three variables: two integers `x` and `y`, both initialized to zero, and a string `action` initialized to `"Init"`. Action `A` nondeterministically changes `x` by any integer delta while leaving `y` unchanged, and action `B` nondeterministically changes `y` by any integer delta while leaving `x` unchanged; both record their name in `action`. The next-state relation is the disjunction of `A` and `B`. The safety invariant `Inv` asserts that `x + y < 10` always holds.

The module also defines two simulation views: `View1` is a pair of Booleans indicating whether `x` and `y` are each non-negative, and `View2` is simply the last action name. Three trace invariants over history sequences are defined: `TraceInv1` accepts traces where either the first two states show a specific `x=10` jump or the final state satisfies `x + y < 10`; `TraceInv2` and `TraceInv3` similarly accept traces whose initial states satisfy non-negativity conditions under different view equivalences, or else require the final state to satisfy the sum bound. No temporal specification or fairness conditions are included.
