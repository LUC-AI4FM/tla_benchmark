A small TLA+ model used as a test or regression fixture (likely for a model-checker bug fix, as suggested by the name "Fix365"). It models a single variable `msgs`, which is a function mapping each element of `Rounds` (the integers 0, 1, and 2) to a subset of the alphabet `{"a", "b", "c"}`.

In the initial state, `msgs` is chosen nondeterministically as any total function from `Rounds` to subsets of `{"a", "b", "c"}`. The sole transition action selects a subset `X` of `msgs[2]` (the set currently stored at round 2) such that `X` has at least two elements, then updates `msgs[2]` to `X`, leaving all other rounds unchanged. Because `X` must be a subset of the current `msgs[2]` and must contain at least two elements, the set at index 2 can only stay the same or shrink, and only as long as it retains at least two members. No invariants, liveness properties, or fairness conditions are declared; the module exists purely to exercise the model checker's handling of existential quantification over subsets of a function-valued variable.
