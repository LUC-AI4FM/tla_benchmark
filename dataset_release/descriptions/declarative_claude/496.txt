The module specifies a Raft-based consensus protocol extended with client session management, modeling a cluster of servers that elect a leader and replicate a log of typed entries.

The state is organized around per-server variables: `currentTerm`, `state` (Follower, Candidate, or Leader), and `votedFor` capture election state; `log` and `commitIndex` track the replicated log; `lastApplied` and `session` track state-machine application and registered client sessions; `preVotesGranted` and `votesGranted` track pre-vote and vote tallies; and `nextIndex` and `matchIndex` are leader bookkeeping for follower replication progress. The system is initialized with one designated Leader (s1) and two Followers (s2, s3), all at term 1.

The protocol implements a two-phase election: followers send `PollRequest` (pre-vote) messages before transitioning to candidate, then candidates increment their term and send `VoteRequest` messages, becoming leader upon receiving a quorum of votes. Leaders replicate log entries one at a time via `AppendEntries` RPCs and advance `commitIndex` once a quorum acknowledges an entry from the current term. Any message carrying a higher term causes the recipient to immediately revert to follower. Applied log entries are typed as `OpenSessionEntry`, `CloseSessionEntry`, or `CommandEntry`; applying each entry respectively registers a session and sends an `OpenSessionResponse`, removes a session and sends a `CloseSessionResponse`, or dispatches a `CommandResponse` to the associated client. Leaders append client requests (`OpenSessionRequest`, `CloseSessionRequest`, `CommandRequest`) directly to the log upon receipt. The module defines no temporal specification or invariants itself; it serves as a behavioral component intended for use with a separate model-checking configuration.
