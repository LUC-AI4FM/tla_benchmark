The module specifies a simplified, single-node view of the Two-Phase Commit (2PC) protocol, modeling only the resource manager (RM) side without an explicit transaction manager. The single constant `RM` is the set of participating resource managers, and the single variable `rmState` maps each RM to one of four states: `"working"`, `"prepared"`, `"committed"`, or `"aborted"`.

Initially, every RM is in the `"working"` state. Each RM may take one of two actions: `Prepare` transitions it from `"working"` to `"prepared"`, and `Decide` either commits it (if it is `"prepared"` and every RM is already `"prepared"` or `"committed"`) or aborts it (if it is `"working"` or `"prepared"` and no RM has yet committed). The overall next-state relation `TCNext` is the existential choice of any RM performing either action. The temporal specification `TCSpec` is an initial condition conjoined with a stuttering-tolerant always-`TCNext` box action, but includes no fairness constraints.

The module states two invariants: `TCTypeOK`, asserting that `rmState` always stays within its four-value domain, and `TCConsistent`, asserting that no RM ever reaches `"aborted"` while another has reached `"committed"`. A theorem asserts that both invariants hold for all reachable states of `TCSpec`.
