The module models a simple message-passing system with two separate typed message queues — one holding integer-tagged records and one holding string-tagged records — along with detection flags for specific message values.

The state consists of `msgs`, a record with two set-valued fields (`int` holding records with an `x` field drawn from `{1,2,3}` and `str` holding records with a `y` field drawn from `{"a","b","c"}`), plus two Boolean flags `found3` and `foundC`, all initialized to empty sets and `FALSE`. The four actions model adding and consuming messages from each queue independently. `AddIntMsg` nondeterministically inserts a record `[x |-> v]` for some `v` in `{1,2,3}` into the integer queue; `CheckIntMsg` removes a message from that queue and sets `found3` to whether its `x` field equals `3`. Similarly, `AddStrMsg` inserts a string record and `CheckStrMsg` removes one, setting `foundC` to whether its `y` field equals `"c"`. No fairness conditions are specified. The only property stated is the type invariant `TypeOk`, which asserts that both message sets remain within their declared record types. No liveness or additional safety properties are included. The module appears intended primarily as a typing and model-checking test for the Apalache type-checker, given its Apalache-style type annotations.
