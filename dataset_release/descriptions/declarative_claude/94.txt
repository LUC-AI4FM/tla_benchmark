The module `ConfigUnsorted` is a minimal TLA+ utility or test module designed to expose preprocessing and override-resolution issues, particularly circular dependencies introduced through configuration overrides.

It declares a single integer variable `x`, initialized to zero by `Init`, with a trivial `Next` action that leaves `x` unchanged. Three simple constant-like operator definitions — `A`, `B`, and `C` — are assigned the values 1, 2, and 3 respectively. Alongside these, three override operators — `OVERRIDE_A`, `OVERRIDE_B`, and `OVERRIDE_C` — are defined such that each overrides one constant with the value of another: `A` is overridden by `B`, `B` by `C`, and `C` back by `A`. This creates a deliberate circular dependency cycle among the three overrides. The module does not define a temporal specification, fairness conditions, or any invariants or liveness properties. Its primary purpose is to serve as a test case for checking how a TLA+ tool or preprocessor handles unsorted or circularly dependent operator overrides rather than to model any real system or algorithm.
