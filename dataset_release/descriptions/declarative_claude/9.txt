Lamport's Bakery algorithm for mutual exclusion among N processes, specified in PlusCal and translated to TLA+. The constant N (assumed a natural number) determines the process set Procs = 1..N. Global variables num and flag are arrays indexed by Procs, representing ticket numbers and choosing flags respectively; each process also has local variables unchecked, max, and nxt, plus a program counter pc.

Each process cycles through labeled steps: ncs (non-critical section), e1–e4 (entry protocol), w1–w2 (waiting protocol), cs (critical section), and exit. In the entry protocol a process sets its flag, scans others' ticket numbers to find a maximum, then picks a strictly larger ticket number, and clears its flag. In the waiting protocol it checks each other process, blocking until that process is not choosing (flag false) and either has no ticket or has a lexicographically larger (number, id) pair. The \prec operator defines the lexicographic order on pairs used in this comparison. The specification Spec includes the Init/Next safety portion plus weak fairness for all non-ncs steps and additional weak fairness for the nondeterministic branching steps e1, e3, e4, and exit.

The module states and machine-checks MutualExclusion (no two processes simultaneously in cs) and provides a full TLAPS-verified proof via the inductive invariant Inv, which includes TypeOK and the Before(i,j) helper predicate. Liveness properties DeadlockFree and StarvationFree are also defined, asserting respectively that some trying process eventually enters cs, and that every individual trying process eventually enters cs.
