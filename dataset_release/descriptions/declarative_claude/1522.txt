The module specifies Huang's algorithm for distributed termination detection. The system consists of a fixed set of processes (`Procs`) with one designated `Leader`. Each process carries a dyadic-rational `weight`, and messages in transit also carry weights; the invariant maintained throughout is that the sum of all process weights plus all in-transit message weights equals one.

Initially, the Leader holds the entire weight of one and is the only active process; all others are idle with weight zero and empty message queues. A process can send a message by halving its own weight and delivering that half as a message to any other process. A non-leader process becomes active upon receiving a message, adding the message's weight to its own. A non-leader process goes idle by sending its entire weight back to the Leader and setting its own weight to zero. The Leader has separate `IdleLdr` and `RcvLdr` actions: it can independently become inactive, and it can absorb incoming weight messages without reactivating. The temporal specification `Spec` includes weak fairness for all receive and idle actions, ensuring that pending messages are eventually processed and active processes eventually idle.

The safety property `Safe` asserts that once termination is detected — meaning the Leader is inactive and holds weight one — the system is and remains truly terminated (all processes inactive, all queues empty). The liveness property `Live` asserts that actual termination is eventually detected. Both `Safe` and `Live` are stated as theorems. The `TypeOK` invariant enforces correct types and the global weight-conservation invariant. A `StateConstraint` bounds denominator growth to keep model checking finite.
