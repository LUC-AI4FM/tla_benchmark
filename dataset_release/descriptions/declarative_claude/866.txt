The module specifies a simplified version of Leslie Lamport's Fast Paxos protocol, extending a base Paxos module. It assumes a unique coordinator (eliminating Phase 1), full connectivity among agents, and stable storage for all replicas. The system is modeled over four replicas, three values, five ballots (with ballots 1 and 3 designated as fast), and classic quorums and fast quorums each consisting of all size-3 subsets of the four replicas.

The protocol distinguishes fast rounds from classic rounds. In a fast round, the coordinator broadcasts a special "any" P2a message, allowing acceptors to propose their own values directly in P2b replies. A fast decision is reached when an entire fast quorum agrees on a single value. If a collision occurs (multiple values proposed in a fast quorum), a subsequent classic round resolves it: the coordinator must choose the majority value among fast-quorum responses if one exists, or any proposed value otherwise, then proceeds with standard Paxos Phase 2a/2b. Classic decisions require a classic quorum to agree on the same ballot. The variable `cValue` tracks the coordinator's chosen value during collision recovery. The temporal specification `FastSpec` is a safety+liveness formula combining the initial condition, a stuttering-tolerant next-state relation, and strong fairness on both `FastDecide` and `ClassicDecide`. The checked invariants are `FastTypeOK` (type correctness) and `FastNontriviality` (only proposed values are decided), and the model also verifies `PaxosConsistency` (at most one value is ever decided).
