The module specifies Multi-Paxos (multi-decree Paxos with an aggregated prepare phase) with explicit proposer, acceptor, and learner roles. Constants include `Proposers`, `Acceptors`, `Quorums`, `Learners`, `Values`, `Slots`, and `Ballots`, with assumptions requiring at least three acceptors, pairwise-intersecting quorums, at least two values, and ballots drawn from natural numbers. Global variables track the shared message pool (`msgs`), each proposer's current ballot (`pBallot`), each acceptor's highest seen ballot (`aBallot`) and per-slot accepted vote (`aVoted`), as well as `proposed` and `learned` sets per slot for model-checking purposes.

Proposers loop indefinitely, either broadcasting a Phase 1a prepare message for a fresh ballot (ensuring no duplicate ballots), or sending a Phase 2a accept message for a chosen slot and value, provided a quorum of Phase 1b responses exists and the value selection rule is satisfied—either no acceptor voted in that slot, or the value is the highest-ballot previously accepted value. Acceptors respond to Phase 1a messages with Phase 1b promises (if the ballot exceeds their current ballot) and to Phase 2a messages with Phase 2b acknowledgments, updating their ballot and voted state. Learners observe Phase 2b messages and record a value for a slot once a full quorum has acknowledged it, learning at most one value per slot. The temporal specification is a standard safety-only `Init /\ [][Next]_vars` with no fairness constraints or explicit liveness or safety properties stated in the module itself.
