The module `RecordExcept987` is a small test or model-checking utility that exercises TLA+ record construction and the `EXCEPT` update syntax with a typed record structure.

The specification maintains two variables: `recs`, a set of records each carrying six fields (`id`, `name`, `oneMoreField`, `age`, `isShadow`, and `isAllow`), and `ok`, a Boolean. In the initial state, `ok` is `TRUE` and `recs` is empty. On each step, `ok'` is updated to reflect whether every record in the current `recs` has an age strictly less than 15. The next-state relation then nondeterministically chooses one of three actions: adding a fixed new record named `"foo"` with age 10 and specific field values; replacing an existing record `r` with a copy whose `age` field is incremented by one (only if the incremented record actually differs from the original); or leaving `recs` unchanged. The invariant `Inv` asserts that every record in `recs` has an age less than 15. Because the age-increment action can raise a record's age to 15 or beyond, and the add action introduces a record with age 10 that can subsequently be incremented, the invariant is eventually violated, making this a functional test of record `EXCEPT` expressions and multi-field record typing rather than a model of a real system.
