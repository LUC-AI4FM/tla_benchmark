The module models Euclid's algorithm for computing the greatest common divisor (GCD) of two integers using repeated subtraction. It is a PlusCal-to-TLA+ translation of a straightforward sequential algorithm.

The model has two global variables: `u`, initialized to 24, and `v`, chosen nondeterministically from the range 1 to 50, representing the two inputs. A program counter `pc` tracks control flow through three labeled steps plus a terminal `Done` state. The algorithm first prints the initial pair, then enters a loop that continues while `u` is nonzero: if `u` is less than `v`, the two values are swapped; then `v` is subtracted from `u`. When `u` reaches zero, `v` holds the GCD of the original inputs.

The actions `Lbl_1`, `Lbl_2`, and `Lbl_3` correspond to the print step, the loop guard and conditional swap, and the subtraction step, respectively. A stuttering disjunct in `Next` keeps the system enabled after termination. The temporal specification `Spec` is a standard safety specification (`Init /\ [][Next]_vars`) with no fairness constraints, no liveness properties, and no invariants defined beyond what the model checker verifies by default during state-space exploration with depth-first search.
