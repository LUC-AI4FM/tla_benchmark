The Chameneos module specifies the "Chameneos" concurrency game, in which a population of color-changing creatures (chameneoses) repeatedly meet at a shared meeting place and mutually adopt a new color determined by the complement of their two colors.

The model has two constants: `N`, the total number of meetings allowed before creatures fade, and `M`, the number of chameneoses. The three variables are `chameneoses`, a function mapping each creature's ID to its current color and personal meeting count; `meetingPlace`, recording which single creature (if any) is currently waiting; and `numMeetings`, the global count of completed meetings. Colors are drawn from `{"blue", "red", "yellow"}`, plus a special `Faded` value. The complement of two distinct colors is the third color; the complement of two identical colors is that same color.

In the `Meet` action, a non-faded chameneos either enters an empty meeting place (if the global count has not yet reached `N`), fades immediately (if `numMeetings = N` and the place is empty), or completes a meeting with the waiting creature (if the place is occupied by a different creature), in which case both creatures adopt the complement color, each increments its personal meeting count, and the global count advances. The system's next-state relation requires that at least one non-faded chameneos acts. The specification uses `Init /\ [][Next]_vars` with no fairness condition. The checked invariant `SumMet` asserts that once `numMeetings` reaches `N`, the sum of all individual meeting counts equals `2*N`.
