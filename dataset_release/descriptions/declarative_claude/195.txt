The module PrisonersTyped is a typed variant of the classic TLA+ Prisoners puzzle specification, adapted for the Apalache model checker by using `ApaFoldSet` instead of recursive functions and adding `@type` annotations. It models a strategy by which a group of prisoners can collectively determine that every prisoner has visited a switch room at least once.

The model has two Boolean variables `switchAUp` and `switchBUp` representing the two physical switches, an integer `count` tracking how many times the designated Counter prisoner has flipped switch A down, and a function `timesSwitched` mapping each non-Counter prisoner to how many times they have flipped switch A up (bounded at 2). The `Counter` constant is a distinguished member of the `Prisoner` set; `OtherPrisoner` is everyone else. Initially, the switches are unconstrained Booleans, `count` is 0, and `timesSwitched` is all zeros.

Each step is either `CounterStep`—the Counter flips switch A down and increments `count` if it is up, otherwise flips switch B—or `NonCounterStep(i)` for some non-Counter prisoner `i`, who flips switch A up if it is down and they have flipped it fewer than twice, otherwise flips switch B. The specification `Spec` combines `Init`, the always-enabled next-state relation `Next`, and a `Fairness` condition requiring weak fairness for every prisoner's action, ensuring each prisoner visits the room infinitely often. `Done` holds when `count` equals `2 * (|Prisoner| - 1)`. The module states a theorem that `Spec` implies both `Safety`—that `Done` entails every non-Counter prisoner has flipped switch A at least once—and `Liveness`, that `Done` is eventually reached. A `CountInvariant` relating `count`, `totalSwitched`, and `oneIfUp` is also provided to support the safety proof.
