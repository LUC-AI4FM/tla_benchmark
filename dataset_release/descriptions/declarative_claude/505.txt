The module specifies single-decree Paxos as a PlusCal algorithm translated to TLA+, with leader election omitted and a single fixed proposer assumed.

The constants are `Acceptors` (a finite set of at least three nodes), `Quorums` (subsets of acceptors satisfying the intersection property), `Values` (a finite set of at least two candidate values, not containing zero), and `Ballots` (a finite subset of the natural numbers). The variables are `msgs`, a global message pool that grows monotonically, and per-acceptor arrays `maxPrepared`, `maxAccepted`, and `valAccepted` tracking each acceptor's highest prepared ballot, highest accepted ballot, and the associated accepted value.

Two kinds of processes execute concurrently. A single `Proposer` process loops forever, nondeterministically choosing to send a Phase 1a prepare message for any ballot not yet prepared, or to send a Phase 2a accept message for any ballot for which a quorum of Phase 1b responses has arrived and the Paxos value-selection constraint is satisfied (either no acceptor has accepted anything, or the value is the one accepted at the highest ballot among the quorum). Each `Acceptor` process loops forever, nondeterministically responding to a Phase 1a message with a higher ballot than its current `maxPrepared` by sending a Phase 1b reply, or responding to a Phase 2a message with a ballot at least as high as `maxPrepared` by sending a Phase 2b acknowledgment and updating its local state. The temporal specification is `Init /\ [][Next]_vars` with no fairness constraints and no explicitly stated invariants or liveness properties in this module.
