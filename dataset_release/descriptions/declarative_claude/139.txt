The module specifies a simple 12-hour clock, drawn from the "Specifying Systems" textbook examples. It has a single integer variable `hr` representing the current hour.

The initial condition `HCini` requires `hr` to be an element of the set 1 through 12. The next-state action `HCnxt` advances the clock by one hour each step, wrapping from 12 back to 1. The overall specification `HC` is the conjunction of the initial condition and a stuttering-tolerant always-box of `HCnxt`, written `[][HCnxt]_hr`, meaning the clock either advances by one hour or leaves `hr` unchanged. The invariant `TypeOK` asserts that `hr` remains in the range 1 to 12 at all times. The module also states the theorem that `HC` implies `[]HCini`, i.e., if the clock starts correctly and transitions according to `HCnxt`, the hour variable remains in 1 through 12 forever. No fairness conditions are imposed, and no liveness properties are checked beyond the stated theorem. This module is a self-contained illustrative example of a trivially simple reactive system in TLA+.
