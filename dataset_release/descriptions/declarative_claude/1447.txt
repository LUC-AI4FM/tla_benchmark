The module models a simple two-variable system where both variables independently take on random Boolean-like values. The two variables `x` and `y` are initialized to zero, and in each step both are independently and non-deterministically reassigned using TLC's `RandomElement` operator, drawing from the set `{0, 1}`. The temporal specification is the standard safety formula requiring that every step either satisfies `Next` or leaves both variables unchanged (stuttering). The module checks the invariant `Inv`, which asserts that `x` and `y` are always equal. Because `x` and `y` are chosen independently from `{0, 1}`, this invariant will be violated whenever one variable takes the value `0` and the other takes `1`. The module thus serves primarily as a demonstration or test of TLC's `RandomElement` functionality, illustrating how random simulation can quickly surface invariant violations when two independently randomized variables are constrained to remain equal.
