Module D is a small TLA+ specification that models a simple single-variable system used primarily to exercise recursive operator definition in the context of model-checking coverage and cost analysis. It declares one variable, `x`, initialized to zero. The module defines a recursive factorial function `fact(n)`, which returns 1 when `n` equals 1 and otherwise returns `n * fact(n - 1)`. The next-state relation `Next` is the disjunction of two actions: action `A`, which sets `x` to `fact(3)` (i.e., 6), and action `B`, which sets `x` to `fact(9)` (i.e., 362880). The temporal specification `Spec` is the conjunction of the initial condition `x = 0` and the always-enabled stuttering-tolerant box of `Next` over the variable `x`. No invariants, liveness properties, or fairness conditions are declared beyond the basic specification. The module's stated purpose is to verify that the TLC model checker does not fall into endless recursion when computing coverage or cost metrics for operators defined with the `RECURSIVE` keyword.
