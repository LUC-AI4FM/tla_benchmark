This is a model-checking test module that exercises TLC's handling of subset constraints in next-state relations, particularly the `\subseteq` operator when used in primed (next-state) expressions.

The module declares two variables, `x` and `y`. The initial condition requires `x` to be any subset of `{1, 2}` and `y` to equal `{1, 2, 3}`. The `Next` action leaves `y` unchanged and constrains the next value of `x` to be any subset of `y'` (which, because `y` is unchanged, remains `{1, 2, 3}`). This means each step allows `x` to transition to any of the eight subsets of `{1, 2, 3}`. Two invariants are checked: `TypeOK` asserts that `x` remains a subset of `{1, 2, 3}`, and `Inv` asserts both that the action `x' \subseteq {1}` is enabled (i.e., TLC can take a step restricting `x` to a subset of `{1}`) and that `y` equals `{1, 2, 3}`. The module also defines two state predicates, `FullSet` and `GainThree`, tracked via TLC's `_POSSIBLE` directive. The postcondition `PossibleCounts` verifies that TLC found exactly 8 states satisfying `FullSet` and exactly 16 transitions satisfying `GainThree`, confirming that TLC correctly enumerates all subset-valued successor states.
