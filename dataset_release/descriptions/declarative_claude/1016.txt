The VoucherRedeem module specifies the redemption operation for electronic vouchers, as described in RFC 3506, implemented over a Two-Phase Commit protocol. Three constants parameterize the model: a set of Vouchers (V), Voucher Holders (H), and Voucher Collectors (C). The seven state variables track each voucher's validity state, its lifecycle machine state, the local state of each holder and collector, the Voucher Transaction Provider's (VTP) control state, the set of participants from which the VTP has received "Prepared" messages, and a global message bag.

Initially, all vouchers are valid and in the "working" lifecycle state, all holders are "holding," all collectors are "waiting," and the VTP is in "init." Holders and collectors spontaneously send Prepared messages or choose to abort silently. The VTP accumulates Prepared acknowledgments from both holders and collectors; once all members of H ∪ C have prepared, it may broadcast a Redeem message and mark the voucher redeemed and lifecycle done, or it may spontaneously broadcast an Abort message at any time while still in "init." Holders and collectors then transition to "redeemed" or "aborted" upon receiving the corresponding broadcast. The temporal specification is a safety-only stuttering-closed formula with no fairness requirements. The module asserts two invariants — VTPTypeOK (type correctness) and VTPConsistent (no holder reaches "redeemed" while any collector is "aborted," and vice versa) — and claims a refinement mapping to an abstract VoucherLifeCycle specification. The model-checking configuration instantiates three vouchers, three holders, and three collectors, checks both invariants, and disables deadlock checking.
