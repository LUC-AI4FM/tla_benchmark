The module specifies Lamport's Fast Mutual Exclusion algorithm for N processes (numbered 1 to N), rendered as a PlusCal algorithm translated to TLA+. The algorithm uses three shared variables: an integer `x` (last process to register interest), an integer `y` (a "winner" slot), and a boolean array `b` indexed over processes indicating whether each process is actively trying to enter the critical section. Each process also has a local variable `j` used as a loop counter.

The protocol proceeds in two phases. In the fast path, a process sets its flag `b[self]`, writes its identity to `x`, and checks whether `y` is zero; if so, it claims `y` and proceeds to the critical section if `x` still equals itself. In the slow path (when another process also wrote `x`), the process clears its flag, waits for all other processes' flags to be clear by scanning `b[1..N]`, and then checks whether it still owns `y`. Blocking await statements at labels `l4`, `l8`, and `l10` enforce synchronization. After the critical section, the process resets `y` and its flag before returning to the noncritical section.

The safety property `Invariant` asserts mutual exclusion: no two distinct processes can be in the critical section simultaneously. The liveness property `CondLiveness` states that if some process is perpetually outside the noncritical section, then some process enters the critical section infinitely often. The `FairSpec` combines the base specification with weak fairness on every protocol step for every process and is the specification checked against both the invariant and the conditional liveness property, with `N = 2` in the model-checking configuration.
