The module models a classic bounded-buffer (producer-consumer) concurrency problem with multiple producers and multiple consumers sharing a fixed-capacity buffer and explicit wait sets.

Three CONSTANTS parameterize the model: `Producers` and `Consumers` are disjoint, non-empty sets of process identifiers, and `BufCapacity` is a positive natural number giving the buffer's maximum size. The three VARIABLES are `buffer` (a sequence holding items up to capacity), `waitP` (the set of producers currently blocked), and `waitC` (the set of consumers currently blocked). In the model-checking configuration, two producers `{p1, p2}`, two consumers `{c1, c2}`, and a buffer capacity of 1 are used.

The `p(self)` action represents a producer step: if the producer is not already waiting and the buffer is full, it adds itself to a wait set; otherwise it appends itself to the buffer and optionally wakes one waiting consumer. The `c(self)` action mirrors this for consumers: if the buffer is empty the consumer waits; otherwise it removes the head of the buffer and optionally wakes one waiting producer. The `Next` relation is the disjunction of all producer and consumer steps. The temporal specification `Spec` is safety-only (`Init /\ [][Next]_vars`), with no fairness constraints. The sole checked property is the invariant `Inv`, asserting that it is never the case that all producers and all consumers are simultaneously blocked — a deadlock freedom condition.
