The Raft module is the top-level composition of a formal TLA+ specification of the Raft consensus algorithm, assembling components defined in separate Client, Server, and Messages modules. It introduces a single additional variable, `allStates`, which acts as a global step counter incremented on every transition, along with the full variable tuple `vars` spanning client, server, state-machine, follower, candidate, leader, and log sub-variables.

The initial state initializes all sub-components via their respective `Init` predicates and sets `allStates` to zero. The `Next` relation is a disjunction of all system actions: servers may restart, time out as followers or candidates, exchange pre-vote and vote request messages, transition to candidate or leader, append entries, advance the commit index, or apply log entries; clients may open sessions, close sessions, or submit requests; and the message layer may receive, duplicate, or drop messages. Every action preserves variables outside its concern via `UNCHANGED` clauses, and every step increments `allStates`. The temporal specification `Spec` is a standard safety formula requiring initialization and stuttering-closed transitions with no fairness conditions. The module also states an invariant `Inv` asserting a form of log consistency: any two committed entries at the same index across any two servers must be identical, capturing the core Raft safety property that the replicated log remains consistent across the cluster.
