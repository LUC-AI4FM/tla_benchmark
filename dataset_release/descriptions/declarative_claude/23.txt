The module models a parallel bounded model checking (BMC) algorithm that extends a sequential search by distributing work across a pool of worker threads. The system is parameterized by three constants: `NTRANS` (the number of symbolic transitions extracted from a `Next` relation), `MAX_DEPTH` (the maximum exploration depth), and `NWORKERS` (the number of worker threads).

The state is captured by six variables: `depth` (current exploration depth), `runningPrefix` (the sequence of enabled-transition sets explored so far), `slowPrefixes` (prefixes deferred due to SMT timeouts), `workerState` (each worker's current role and data), `runningTrans` (a per-transition status tracking whether each transition is new, borrowed, enabled, disabled, or timed out), and `unsafePrefixes` (prefixes awaiting invariant checking).

Workers play two roles. **Explorers** borrow individual transitions via `BorrowTransition`, check feasibility via `CheckOneTransition` (which nondeterministically yields enabled, disabled, or timeout), and deposit newly discovered prefixes into `unsafePrefixes`. **Provers** claim prefixes via `StartProving` and call `ProveInvariant`, which uses the abstract `SMT` operator to determine whether a bad execution exists in the prefix. Global actions `IncreaseDepth`, `SwitchSlowPrefix`, and `WhenAllDisabledButNotFinished` manage depth progression and slow-prefix rescheduling. `FinishNoError` terminates all workers with "noerror" when no violations remain and the depth bound is reached. A `BadExes` constant encodes the concrete counterexample sequences against which the abstract SMT oracle checks. The properties checked are `NeverError`, `NeverNoError`, `NeverDeadlock`, and `NeverParallel`, which assert that no worker ever reaches the respective terminal or non-idle states.
