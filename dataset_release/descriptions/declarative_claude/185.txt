The module PickPerf is a small exploratory or performance-testing specification rather than a model of a real-world system. It declares four variables: two integer sets `S` and `T`, a derived set `R`, and an integer `state`.

Initially, `S` contains only 1, `T` contains only 4, `R` is their union, and `state` is 0. Each step of the `Next` relation simultaneously advances a set-growth component and a state-transition component. The set component grows `S` through the cycle 1→2→3→1 (each element present in `S` enables adding the next) and grows `T` through the analogous cycle 4→5→6→4, with `R` always updated to `S ∪ T`. The state component allows transitions among values 0 through 4 following a fixed graph: from 0 to 1 or 2, from 1 to 3 or back to 0, and from 2 to 1 or 4; the state may also remain unchanged. The two sub-components of `Next` are conjoined, so both must move together in every step. The sole invariant `Inv` asserts that `S` and `T` are never equal as sets. There is no liveness or fairness condition specified.
