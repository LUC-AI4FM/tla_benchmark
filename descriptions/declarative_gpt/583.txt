This specification models a multi-node version of a CCF service by extending a single-node model with additional behavior for view changes and leader election. It introduces an action that simulates leader election by truncating uncommitted suffixes of a ledger branch and appending a new branch, and another action that sends invalid transaction status responses when a transaction can no longer be committed in the current or future views. The temporal property defines the system behavior as starting from Init and evolving according to NextMultiNodeAction under stuttering, with no explicit fairness or proof obligations in this module.
