This specification models a high-level abstraction of the Tendermint blockchain, focusing on the aspects relevant to a light client. It assumes validators have equal voting power and models the blockchain as a sequence of block headers, each containing information about height, time, validator sets, and commits. The model includes faulty nodes, whose set is non-decreasing, and enforces constraints on validator sets and commit quorums, such as the two-thirds majority and limits on faulty validators. The specification defines initialization to a maximal height, actions for advancing time, and predicates for checking trust periods and digital signature validity. The design is simplified for analysis, omitting some implementation details (e.g., hashes) and using sets and records to represent blocks and validators.
