This module is a proof-oriented TLA+ development of theorems about extended sequence operators provided by other modules, especially SequencesExt and related theorem libraries. It formalizes algebraic, structural, inductive, and well-founded properties of sequence constructors and transformers such as Cons, InsertAt, RemoveAt, Front, Last, Reverse, prefix and suffix relations, and fold operators. The file contains no state-machine variables or temporal system specification; instead, it consists of theorem statements and TLAPS proofs establishing correctness properties, induction principles, recursive-definition principles over well-founded prefix/suffix orders, and typing/recursion laws for FoldLeft, FoldRight, FoldLeftDomain, and FoldRightDomain.
