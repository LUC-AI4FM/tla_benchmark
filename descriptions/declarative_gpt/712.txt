This module is a small test specification for the DyadicRationals module. It does not define a transition system, variables, or temporal behavior; instead, it consists entirely of assumptions that check the behavior of the Half operator on dyadic rational record values. The file also includes an assumption with a LET-bound INSTANCE of TLC used to invoke T!PrintT("DyadicRationalsTests"), apparently as a test-side effect or marker, and then asserts a sequence of expected equalities for repeated halving.
