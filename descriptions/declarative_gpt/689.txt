This module contains TLAPS proofs of theorems about functions, injections, surjections, bijections, inverses, restrictions, existence of jections, the Cantor-Bernstein-Schroeder theorem, and folding or summing functions over finite sets. It is a proof-oriented library module rather than a state-machine specification: there are no variables, actions, or temporal behaviors, and the content consists of theorem statements, proof steps, and local proof definitions. The file proves structural facts about function spaces, cardinality-style arguments over finite sets and intervals 1..n, and algebraic properties of FoldFunctionOnSet, FoldFunction, SumFunctionOnSet, and SumFunction under finiteness and associativity/commutativity assumptions.
