This specification models Atomix's implementation of the SWIM membership and failure-detection protocol. Members have logical incarnation numbers and can be observed in the states Alive, Suspect, or Dead, with transitions constrained to progress monotonically within an incarnation and requiring a higher incarnation to return to Alive. The spec models probing, acknowledgments, gossip propagation of updates, message duplication and loss in the network, member addition and removal, and a history structure used to check an invariant that a member does not regress or repeat illegal state transitions within the same incarnation.
