This specification models a distributed mutual exclusion algorithm based on Lamport's bakery-style numbering, with separate mutex processes and message-handling processes for each node. Each mutex process chooses a ticket number, broadcasts it, waits for acknowledgments and priority conditions, enters its critical section, and then broadcasts a reset; message processes receive write messages, update remote number information, and send acknowledgments. The spec includes the translated PlusCal algorithm, a type invariant, a mutual exclusion safety property, and a starvation-freedom liveness property under weak fairness assumptions for both mutex and message actions.
