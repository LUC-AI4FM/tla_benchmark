This TLA+ specification models a parameterized version of the one-round asynchronous reliable broadcast algorithm with Byzantine faults, as described in Srikanth and Toueg (1987). The model captures the behavior of correct and faulty processes, the sending and receiving of ECHO messages, and the acceptance of messages under Byzantine conditions. The specification is parameterized by the number of processes (N), the threshold for Byzantine faults (T), and the number of faulty processes (F), and it encodes both safety (unforgeability) and liveness (eventual acceptance) properties. Notably, the model supports both model checking (with TLC) and deductive verification (with TLAPS), and includes a full TLAPS proof of the unforgeability property, which asserts that if no correct process broadcasts a message, then no correct process ever accepts it. The design uses variables for the sets of correct and faulty processes to allow exploration of all possible assignments, and models the control state and message sets explicitly. The specification includes detailed invariants, fairness conditions, and inductive invariants to support both model checking and proof.
