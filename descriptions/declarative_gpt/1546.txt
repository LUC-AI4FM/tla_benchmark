This module specifies a parameterized model of a reliable broadcast by message diffusion under crashed process failures, based on the Chandra-Toueg folklore broadcast algorithm. Processes may start either initialized with value "V1" or not, can receive ECHO messages, may accept and rebroadcast ECHO, and may crash up to a bound F; the model tracks correct processes, crash count, program counters, received messages, and sent messages. The specification defines an initial configuration, a no-broadcast variant, a transition relation with receive/accept/crash behavior, a temporal specification with weak fairness for receive-and-progress steps, and several safety/liveness-style properties such as no acceptance without broadcast, eventual acceptance, and reliable channel delivery.
