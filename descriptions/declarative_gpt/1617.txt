This module specifies a small PlusCal algorithm named NoLoop that executes three labeled steps without looping. It initializes variables x and y to 0, assigns x := 3 in step a, nondeterministically chooses j from { 1 , 2 } in step b to assign y and update x, and in step c conditionally increments x depending on whether y = 1 before printing the result and terminating. The translated TLA+ spec includes the standard Init/Next/Spec structure, allows infinite stuttering after termination through a Terminating action to avoid deadlock, asserts weak fairness of Next, and states a termination property that eventually pc = "Done". Additional operators inspect execution-state information and TLC named-state counts using TLCGet.
