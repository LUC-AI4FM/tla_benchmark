This module is a model-checking wrapper around another specification, Session9sem, and adds concrete constants and temporal properties for a mutual exclusion protocol. It fixes the number of processes to 3 and defines the process set as the range 0..(ConstN-1). The module asserts mutual exclusion as a safety property, and also states deadlock/livelock freedom and starvation freedom as leads-to liveness properties over the process control-state variable pc.
