This specification models the Paxos consensus algorithm, focusing on the core protocol without explicit leader or learner processes. The model abstracts message passing by tracking the set of all messages ever sent, rather than modeling messages in transit, loss, or duplication, as the primary concern is safety rather than liveness under unreliable communication. The specification defines the possible messages, the state variables for acceptors' ballots and votes, and the actions corresponding to the phases of the protocol. It includes a refinement mapping to the Voting module, and verifies both safety (via an inductive invariant) and liveness (via temporal properties), with a clear separation between the protocol's actions and the refinement mapping. The fault model assumes quorums with intersection properties, and the design omits explicit learners for abstraction.
