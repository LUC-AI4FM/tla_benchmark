This module is a SANY2 parser test rather than a conventional algorithm specification. It exercises a wide range of TLA+ syntax, especially proof language constructs, theorem forms, INSTANCE usage, record-field access with keyword-like field names, EXCEPT updates, tuple syntax, and hierarchical proof steps. The file contains no state variables, no transition system, and no temporal behavior being verified; instead, it checks that many reserved words and proof constructs are syntactically usable in different contexts.
