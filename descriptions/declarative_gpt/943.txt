This specification models a simple 12-hour clock. The state variable 'hr' represents the current hour, which ranges from 1 to 12. The initial predicate HCini ensures that hr starts within this range. The next-state action HCnxt increments hr by 1 unless it is 12, in which case it wraps around to 1. The temporal property HC asserts that the system starts in a valid state and always evolves according to HCnxt. The theorem proves that the invariant HCini is always maintained. The design is straightforward, with no fault model or concurrency, and focuses on demonstrating a cyclic counter with a simple temporal invariant.
