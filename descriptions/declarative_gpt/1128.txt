This module contains TLAPS proofs about a two-phase commit specification imported from the TwoPhase module. It proves that the temporal specification TPSpec implies the type correctness invariant TPTypeOK and also implies the safety property TC!TCConsistent, which states that no resource managers can end in conflicting committed and aborted decisions. To prove consistency, the module defines a strengthened inductive invariant Inv that tracks transaction manager state, prepared messages, decision messages on the wire, and the relationship between local RM states and messages, then proves the invariant is inductive and implies the target consistency theorem.
