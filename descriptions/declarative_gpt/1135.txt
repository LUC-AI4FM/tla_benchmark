This module specifies the set of possible proof-obligation states for a proof management or proof-coloring system used by the TLA+ Toolbox. It models prover status information from a constant sequence of sequences of strings, defines the space of obligation states including dummy states like "missing" and "omitted" as well as ordinary proof states, and assigns each obligation state a unique consecutive number. The specification also proves that this numbering is well-formed and injective over the set of obligation states, and then instantiates another module to use this state space and its enumeration for color predicates.
