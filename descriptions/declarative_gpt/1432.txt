This specification models a simple cyclic transition system with one state variable x that repeatedly moves through the values 0, 1, 2, 3, and then back to 0. The actions One, Two, Three, and Back define the step relation, Next is their disjunction, and Spec states that execution starts in Init and then always takes Next steps or stutters via the standard box-subscript form. The module also defines a weak fairness variant of the specification, a liveness property asserting that x is infinitely often equal to 3, its negation, an eventuality property stating that x eventually becomes 0, and a tautological leads-to property over vars.
