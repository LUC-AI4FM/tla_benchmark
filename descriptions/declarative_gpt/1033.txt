This module gives a high-level TLA+ specification of the Sailfish and Sailfish++ consensus algorithms, modeling a DAG-based protocol with correct and Byzantine nodes. The comments explain that Byzantine behavior is constrained because vertices are disseminated with reliable broadcast, so Byzantine nodes cannot equivocate and can only create protocol-conforming vertices subject to quorum checks. The specification defines initialization, the next-state relation for correct and Byzantine nodes, a basic type invariant, an agreement safety property over delivered logs, and a liveness property stating that after GST, leaders of synchronous rounds are eventually reflected in correct nodes' logs.
