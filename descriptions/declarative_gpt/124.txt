This specification is a regression test for using a set-folding operator in the initial predicate, the next-state relation, and an invariant. It models a single variable `values` ranging over sets of integers, with a helper operator `Sum` that uses `ApaFoldSet` to sum the elements of a set. The initial condition chooses a subset whose sum is exactly `N`, the next-state action allows moving to another subset with strictly smaller sum, and the invariant asserts an arithmetic upper bound on twice the sum of the current set.
