This module is a theorem-only reference specification for properties of finite-set operators defined elsewhere, especially in FiniteSetsExt. It states algebraic and typing theorems about FoldSet, SumSet, ProductSet, MapThenSumSet, Min, and Max over finite sets of naturals and integers, including empty-set cases, non-empty decomposition, disjoint-union laws, subset monotonicity, and interval extrema. The module contains no variables, actions, temporal behavior, or PlusCal algorithm; it is purely a collection of theorem statements intended to accompany proofs in a separate proof module.
