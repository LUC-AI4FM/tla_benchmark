This module models a TLC-like reachability model checker that performs breadth-first exploration over a graph of states, with a parameter K controlling whether exploration is strict BFS (K = 1) or degraded non-strict BFS (K > 1) to approximate multiple workers. The state graph is given as a record with states, initial states, and an action relation, and the model checker tracks explored states, a queue of pending states, predecessor information, and BFS levels in order to detect violations and reconstruct counterexamples. The specification verifies termination of the translated PlusCal algorithm and a liveness-style property stating that when violation states exist, the eventual counterexample belongs to a supplied set of known counterexamples, with an additional minimal-length requirement when K = 1; it also includes a refinement mapping to an instantiated MCReachability specification.
