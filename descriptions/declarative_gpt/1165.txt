This module specifies a TLA+ model of Docker SwarmKit at a high level, combining user actions, orchestrator behavior, allocation, scheduling, worker behavior, and reaping of tasks. It models services and tasks, including replicated and global services, task replacement, cleanup of terminated tasks, service removal, task scheduling to nodes, and convergence toward a desired running configuration. The spec uses weak fairness for core progress actions and verifies type correctness, a task-transition sanity property, and a liveness-style property asserting that unless disruptive events continue forever, the system eventually stabilizes in the desired state.
