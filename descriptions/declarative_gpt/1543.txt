This specification models a scheduling allocator that manages a set of resources among multiple clients. Clients can request sets of resources, and requests can be partially fulfilled; clients may return resources even before their full request is satisfied, but are only obligated to return resources after their entire request is granted. The allocator operates by repeatedly choosing a schedule (a sequence of clients) and allocating resources according to this schedule, ensuring that no client earlier in the schedule is denied resources they have requested. The specification includes liveness properties ensuring that clients eventually return resources, that allocations eventually occur when possible, and that scheduling progresses. Several invariants and temporal properties are specified, including mutual exclusion of resource allocation, progress guarantees, and symmetry considerations for model checking.
