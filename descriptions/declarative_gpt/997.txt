This module provides TLAPS proofs for the safety properties of the SchedulingAllocator specification. It proves that the Allocator specification implies the invariants TypeInvariant and ResourceMutex, ensuring type correctness and mutual exclusion of resources. The proofs rely on properties of sequences, finite sets, and permutation sequences, and use well-founded induction for reasoning about recursive definitions. The module also includes auxiliary lemmas about sequence operations and the structure of permutation sequences, and leaves the proof of AllocatorInvariant as future work due to its complexity.
