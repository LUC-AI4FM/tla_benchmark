This module specifies a simplified Tendermint consensus protocol with proposer-based timestamps and explicit modeling of local clocks, real time, clock precision, clock accuracy, bounded message delay, and optional clock drift. It models correct and Byzantine processes, tracks proposal, prevote, and precommit messages, and includes actions for sending, receiving, and processing protocol messages, as well as timeout and catch-up behavior. The specification focuses primarily on safety and timing validity properties, asserting agreement on decided values and timestamps and constraints relating decisions to proposal and reception times; it also defines a liveness-style property about timely proposal reception, while noting in comments that deadlock is possible and expected because the emphasis is on safety.
