This module contains TLAPS proofs for two safety theorems from AllocatorImplementation.tla: that the system's Specification implies both the TypeInvariant and ResourceMutex properties. The TypeInvariant here combines the SchedulingAllocator's TypeInvariant (via the Sched! instance) with additional client-side variables, ensuring type correctness of the allocator state. The ResourceMutex property asserts that the holding sets of any two clients are disjoint unless the clients are the same, capturing client-side mutual exclusion. The proofs rely on an internal Invariant that combines the Sched-level AllocatorInvariant with a consistency invariant relating alloc, holding, and in-transit messages. The module includes detailed proofs for TypeInvariant and sketches for ResourceMutex, using well-founded induction and properties of permutation sequences over finite sets.
