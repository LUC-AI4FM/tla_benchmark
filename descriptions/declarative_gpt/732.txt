This module specifies the Fast Paxos consensus algorithm, modeling coordinators, acceptors, proposals, learning, retransmission, message loss, leader changes, and failure/repair of agents. The fault model includes arbitrary message loss except for the most recent message of a good agent, and arbitrary changes to the set of good agents and to coordinator leadership; progress is then stated under fairness assumptions and an eventual stable-goodness condition LA(c, Q). The specification defines both safety and progress aspects: safety through invariants such as type correctness, nontriviality, and consistency, and liveness through fairness constraints on good coordinators, good acceptors, and learning actions, together with a theorem that eventual stability implies eventual learning.
