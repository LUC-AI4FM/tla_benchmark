This module is a test suite for predicate logic and the CHOOSE operator in TLA+. It defines a single variable x and checks its type and initialization. The main operator Inv consists of a sequence of IF-THEN-ELSE assertions that test the semantics of existential and universal quantification, the CHOOSE operator (both bounded and unbounded), and their interaction with empty and non-empty sets, tuples, and arithmetic. The tests also check the behavior of TLC with respect to CHOOSE, including a note that TLC does not preserve the semantics of CHOOSE in all cases. The module is intended for validation of logical constructs rather than modeling a distributed algorithm or system.
