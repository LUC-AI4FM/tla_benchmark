This module provides a TLAPS proof that the SpanTree specification guarantees the type invariant TypeOK is always maintained. The proof shows that if the initial state satisfies TypeOK and every step of the Next action preserves TypeOK, then the temporal property []TypeOK holds under the given specification Spec. The proof assumes certain properties about the constants, such as Root being a node, every edge being a subset of Nodes of cardinality 2, and MaxCardinality being a natural number at least as large as the number of nodes. The proof is structured hierarchically and uses standard TLAPS proof commands.
