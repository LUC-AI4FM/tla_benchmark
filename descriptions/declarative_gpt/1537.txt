This module is a model-checking companion specification for an alternating bit protocol model. It imports the main protocol module and adds auxiliary temporal properties, queue-length constraints, and a concrete postcondition describing a counterexample structure over actions and states. The specification checks liveness and stability-style properties such as sent data leading to receipt under bit disagreement, eventual permanent disagreement of protocol bits, and a persistence property for sent values, while also referring to the imported protocol behavior through ABSpec and ABCNext.
