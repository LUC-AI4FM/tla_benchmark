This module specifies a two-phase handshake protocol between a Producer and a Consumer, where the Producer acts when the two handshake bits are equal and the Consumer acts when they differ. The protocol uses state variables p and c as control bits and an abstract data state x, with abstract initialization and transition behavior supplied by the operator-valued constants XInit and XAct. The specification defines the initial condition and next-state relation, then proves with TLAPS that this concrete protocol implements the specification imported from module Alternate under the refinement mapping v <- (p + c) % 2.
