This module defines arithmetic characterizations of several bitwise operations on non-negative integers: bitwise AND, bitwise OR, bitwise XOR, bitwise NOT, and logical right shift. The specification is purely functional rather than state-based: it introduces recursive operators that compute results from integer arguments using division, modulus, exponentiation, and recursion over bit positions. There are no variables, actions, temporal properties, fairness conditions, or proofs; the notable design choice is that bitwise operations are encoded mathematically in TLA+ using recursive helper operators and infix top-level operators such as `x & y`, `x | y`, and `x ^^ y`.
