The module extends Naturals to provide natural number arithmetic, and it instantiates the HourClock module, presumably to inherit its definitions and structure. No operators, actions, or temporal properties are defined directly in this file; its purpose is to serve as a typed wrapper for model checking or analysis tools that require explicit type information.
