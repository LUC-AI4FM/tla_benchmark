This specification models a model-checkable version of MultiPaxos, focusing on linearizability and type correctness. It defines additional symmetry-breaking constructs for TLC model checking, such as conditional permutations over sets of replicas, writes, and reads, and sets a constant for the maximum ballot. The spec includes a type invariant (TypeOK) ensuring that messages, node states, pending and observed commands, and client events are well-typed and consistent. It also defines a linearizability constraint, with operators to extract request and acknowledgment positions, results, and orderings of commands, and proves that the main specification implies both type correctness and linearizability. The fault model is not explicitly described, but the focus is on verifying safety properties under the modeled system's assumptions.
