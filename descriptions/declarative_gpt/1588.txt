This specification models Euclid's algorithm, starting from a fixed value 24 and a second value chosen from the range 1..N, to compute their greatest common divisor by repeated subtraction and swapping. The file contains a PlusCal algorithm and its generated TLA+ translation, with control-state variable pc and state variables u, v, and v_ini. The temporal specification requires initialization, repeated Next steps with stuttering allowed, and weak fairness of Next; it also defines a termination property asserting that the control eventually reaches the "Done" state.
