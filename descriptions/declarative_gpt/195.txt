This specification models the classic prisoners-and-switches puzzle in which one designated prisoner acts as a counter and the others use a strategy involving two switches to determine when everyone has visited the room at least once. The model uses typed constants and variables, tracks how many times each non-counter prisoner has switched switch A up, and lets the counter increment a count whenever switch A is found up and turned down. The specification assumes weak fairness for the counter step and for each non-counter prisoner step, thereby capturing the puzzle's requirement that every prisoner is brought into the room infinitely often, and it asserts both a safety property and an eventuality property that together show the strategy works.
