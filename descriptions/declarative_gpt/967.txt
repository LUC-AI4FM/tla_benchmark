This module models the classic balance-scale stone-cutting problem: given a total weight W and N pieces, find an ordered partition of W into N natural-number weights such that every target weight from 1 to W can be balanced using those pieces on either side of the scale. The specification encodes weighing by allowing coefficients in {-1, 0, 1}, where a negative coefficient represents placing a piece on the same side as the object being weighed. It uses recursive operators to enumerate ordered partitions and to sum sequences, and then uses TLC-oriented ASSUME formulas with PrintT to search for and display a solution or report that no solution exists.
