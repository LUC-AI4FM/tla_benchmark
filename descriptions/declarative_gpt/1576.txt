This specification models a concurrent queue system generated from a PlusCal algorithm. Multiple processes from Procs repeatedly either enqueue a value from Val at the front or back of a shared sequence queue, or attempt to dequeue from the head or tail when the queue is nonempty; each process stores a return value in its local variable rV indicating "okay", "full", "empty", the dequeued value, or null. The temporal specification consists of an initial condition, the standard stuttering-closed next-state relation, and weak fairness for each process action P(self), while an additional state constraint bounds the queue length by N.
