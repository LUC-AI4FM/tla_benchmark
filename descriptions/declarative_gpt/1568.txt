This specification models Lamport's Bakery algorithm for mutual exclusion among a fixed number of processes. The algorithm is translated from a PlusCal description, with each process repeatedly attempting to enter a critical section while ensuring mutual exclusion using a ticketing mechanism. The model includes variables for process states, ticket numbers, and auxiliary variables to track the algorithm's progress. The specification verifies mutual exclusion (no two processes in the critical section simultaneously), and includes a constraint on the maximum ticket number for TLC model checking. The design is parameterized by the number of processes and the maximum ticket value, and uses explicit fairness and invariants to capture the algorithm's correctness properties.
