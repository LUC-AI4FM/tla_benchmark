This module specifies a simple hour clock whose state is the variable hr ranging over the hours 1 through 12. The initial condition requires hr to be in 1..12, and the next-state action increments hr by 1 unless it is 12, in which case it wraps around to 1. The specification defines the temporal behavior HC as HCini /\ [][HCnxt]_hr, includes a type correctness predicate TypeOK, and states a theorem that HC implies the invariant []HCini.
