This specification models the Die Hard 3 water jug problem, where the goal is to obtain exactly 4 gallons of water using a 5-gallon jug, a 3-gallon jug, and a water faucet. The system is modeled with two variables representing the amount of water in each jug, and actions correspond to filling, emptying, or pouring between the jugs. The specification defines all allowable behaviors, including type invariants, initial state, and the possible actions, and encodes the problem as a reachability property: finding a sequence of actions that results in exactly 4 gallons in the 5-gallon jug.
