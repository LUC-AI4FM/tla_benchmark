This module is a small test specification for definability of user-defined infix operators with aliases that resemble built-in TLA+ operators. It declares a single variable x, defines several infix operators such as (+), (-), (.), (/), (\X), \o, %, \leq, and \geq, and then uses them inside the Next action to confirm they can be parsed and referenced as ordinary user-defined operators. The specification does not define a temporal Spec operator or verification theorem; it is primarily a syntactic/operator-definition test over a one-variable state machine.
