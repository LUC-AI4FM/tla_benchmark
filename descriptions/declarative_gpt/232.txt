This module specifies and checks the behavior of the Apalache-specific operator SetAsFun by constructing several functions from sets of key-value tuples. It models three state variables: a string-to-integer function, a set-of-integers-to-integer function, and a function whose domain itself consists of functions. The specification initializes these values, allows only stuttering steps afterward, and states an invariant asserting the expected domains and lookup results, including the design choice that functions can appear as elements in domains.
