This specification models a very small state machine with a single variable x whose initial value is one of the three strings "A", "B", or "C". The next-state relation either assigns x' to one of those same three values, or disables further steps when the TLC debugger watch expression TLCGet("level") reaches at least 50. The temporal property Spec requires Init and then always [][Next]_x, so executions consist of Next steps or stuttering on x; there is no explicit fault model or fairness condition.
