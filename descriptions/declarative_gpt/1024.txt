This specification models a crash-prone conditional consensus protocol in which processes exchange two phases of broadcast messages and use the maximal proposed value to drive decisions. The fault model is crash failures: up to F processes may crash, and crashed processes stop taking protocol steps. The spec defines initialization, message reception, both broadcast phases, local decision steps, and a deterministic fallback choose step, and it verifies typing, validity, agreement, unconditional termination as a temporal property, and a stronger conditional termination result under Condition1.
