This module specifies voucher cancellation between a Voucher Transaction Provider (VTP), voucher holders, and voucher issuers using a simplified Two-Phase Commit protocol. The model represents messages as elements of a growing set, lets holders and issuers spontaneously prepare or abort, and lets the VTP either cancel once all parties are prepared or spontaneously abort the transaction. The specification checks a type-correctness invariant and a consistency invariant stating that a holder and an issuer never end in conflicting cancelled/aborted decisions, and it also states that this protocol specification implements an imported voucher life cycle specification.
