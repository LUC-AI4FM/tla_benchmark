This module specifies the Raft-based region merge algorithm used by TiKV for merging two regions, RegionA and RegionB, that are replicated on the same set of stores. It models Raft log replication, client requests, internal merge and rollback requests, message passing via AppendEntries RPCs, and application of committed logs to the state machine, including special admin logs LogPreMerge, LogMerge, and LogRollback. The specification assumes no leader switch, allows a configurable quorum size as a modeling simplification, and checks structural type correctness plus safety invariants about unique leaders, log-prefix consistency, apply/commit ordering, and consistency of merge outcomes across stores.
