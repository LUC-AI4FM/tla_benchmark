This module specifies the Dining Philosophers problem for N philosophers arranged in a ring, with a semaphore-like variable sem representing fork availability and a control-state variable pc representing each philosopher's program counter. The design intentionally breaks symmetry to avoid deadlock: philosophers 1 through N-1 acquire their right fork and then their left fork, while philosopher 0 acquires them in the opposite order. The translated PlusCal specification defines the initial state, individual process actions, the next-state relation, strong fairness for each process, a mutual-exclusion style invariant stating adjacent philosophers never eat simultaneously, and a starvation-freedom temporal property asserting that every philosopher eats infinitely often.
