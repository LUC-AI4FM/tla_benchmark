This module contains a PlusCal algorithm named transfer and two top-level TLA+ operator definitions. The PlusCal algorithm initializes alice_account = 10, bob_account = 10, and account_total = alice_account + bob_account, with process Transfer \in 1..2 and a local process variable money \in 1..20. The TLA+ definitions state that money is nonnegative and that alice_account + bob_account equals account_total.
