This module specifies a parameterized reliable broadcast algorithm by message diffusion under crash failures. Processes may start either with an initial value indicating they have received the broadcast or without it, may receive ECHO messages, may accept and rebroadcast ECHO, and up to F correct processes may crash. The specification includes initializations with and without an initial broadcast, a safety-oriented type invariant, temporal specifications with weak fairness for receive-and-accept behavior, and liveness/safety properties such as no forgery, correctness of propagation, relay, and reliable channel delivery.
