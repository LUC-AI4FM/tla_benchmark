This specification models a Software Defined Perimeter (SDP) architecture with a legitimate client, an SDP controller, a firewall, a target server, and an attacker. The client performs Single Packet Authorization (SPA), the controller authenticates SPA messages and installs firewall ACL rules, the firewall mediates data-plane TCP traffic, and the attacker can sniff, replay, spoof, and probe based on captured information under a Dolev-Yao style threat model that does not break the underlying crypto abstractions. The spec verifies both safety and liveness-style properties, including that attacker SPA attempts are detected, legitimate SPA sessions lead to corresponding ACL rules, legitimate user access can eventually succeed, firewall behavior is consistent, and the attacker should not successfully establish protected service access.
