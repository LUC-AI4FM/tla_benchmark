This specification models the classic two-jug water pouring problem with a small jug of capacity 3 and a big jug of capacity 5. The state consists of the current amounts in the two jugs, and the actions fill, empty, or pour water between them subject to the capacity constraints encoded in the transition formulas. The temporal property defines the overall behavior as starting from both jugs empty and then evolving by repeated Next steps under the standard stuttering-step box action formula; there are no fairness constraints, proofs, or fault assumptions in this file.
