This module provides a TLAPS proof of type correctness for the Alternating Bit protocol, as specified in AlternatingBit.tla. The main theorem proved is that the specification ABSpec implies that the type invariant ABTypeInv always holds. The proof is structured hierarchically, with supporting lemmas establishing the type preservation of sequence operations such as Append, Tail, and Head, as well as the correctness of the Lose operation. The proof uses standard TLAPS commands and relies on the AlternatingBit and TLAPS modules for definitions and proof infrastructure.
