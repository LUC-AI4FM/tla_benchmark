This specification models a simple ring counter system through a nested module `Ring` parameterized by a variable `cardinality`, with a shared variable `value` that ranges over ring members `0..(cardinality - 1)`. The ring supports increment and decrement transitions modulo the ring size, defines temporal specifications over those transitions, and then instantiates the module as `Z7(cardinality) == INSTANCE Ring` to refer to behaviors specialized at cardinality 7. The file also checks instance-referenced temporal formulas, weak and strong fairness, and always-eventually properties, and includes deliberately failing sanity checks and incorrect cycle-step properties intended for testing.
