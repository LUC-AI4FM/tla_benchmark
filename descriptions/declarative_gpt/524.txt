This module specifies an alternating-bit style communication system with a sender-side value AVar, a receiver-side value BVar, two message channels AtoB and BtoA, and a queue of pending new data values. The PlusCal algorithm models concurrent processes for sending data, receiving acknowledgments, sending acknowledgments, receiving data, nondeterministically losing messages from either channel, and injecting new data when the endpoints agree. The translated TLA+ specification initializes the channels and queue to empty, defines the process actions and their effects on state variables, and verifies a temporal specification consisting of initialization, the standard next-state relation under stuttering, and weak/strong fairness conditions for the communication actions.
