This module defines two operator-valued predicates, Equal and NotEqual, and instantiates another module, ConstantOperator, multiple times with different constant substitutions for sets A, B, and the operator constant AreGood. The purpose is to check, through the imported instance predicate GOOD_PAIR_EXISTS, that appropriate pairs exist when equality or inequality is used over the chosen sets, and that they do not exist in the mismatched cases. The module itself contains only trivial Init and Next definitions set to TRUE, and its main asserted condition is the state predicate Inv combining positive and negative checks over the four module instances.
