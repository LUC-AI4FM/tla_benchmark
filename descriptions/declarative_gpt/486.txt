This specification models a very small two-variable state machine over variables a and b. The initial state sets both variables to 0, and the Next action permits a transition that constrains the next-state values using either a swap-like relation between a' and b' or the predicate IsTwo applied to b', together with a' = 1. The temporal property defines Spec as the standard safety-style behavior formula [][Next]_<< a,b >>, meaning all behaviors consist of Next steps or stuttering over the tuple << a,b >>; there are no explicit liveness or fairness requirements.
