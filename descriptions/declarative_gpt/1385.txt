This module is a regression-style TLA+ specification that encodes a collection of ASSUME statements about inequality comparisons between tuples, functions, records, and Cartesian products of sets. Its purpose is to exercise TLC/TLCExt behavior on mixed-type comparisons, especially cases that historically caused errors such as comparing integers with non-integers or booleans with non-booleans. The file does not define a transition system, variables, actions, or temporal correctness properties; instead, it serves as a suite of static semantic/value-comparison checks, including one expected failure wrapped with AssertError.
