This specification models the Tower of Hanoi puzzle using three towers represented as sequences of natural numbers, where each number denotes a disk size. The initial contents of the three towers are provided by the constants A, B, and C, and the state variable towers stores the current configuration as a 3-tuple of sequences. The spec defines when a move is legal, how a move updates the towers, and a Next action that nondeterministically chooses a legal move. It also includes state invariants checking that the tower structure remains well-typed, that no new disk values are introduced, that the total number of disks remains constant, and a predicate NotSolved describing the negation of the solved configuration.
