This specification models the externally visible behaviour of a single-node CCF service. It tracks an abstract external history of requests and responses together with abstract per-view ledger branches, and it assumes there are no view changes because only a single node is considered. The spec checks type correctness of the history and ledgers, defines append-only ledger monotonicity as a temporal property, and gives actions for submitting, executing, and responding to read-write transactions as well as reporting committed transaction status and appending unrelated transactions.
