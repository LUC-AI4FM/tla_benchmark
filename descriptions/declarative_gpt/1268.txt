This specification models the behavior of Java's BufferedRandomAccessFile class, focusing on its core fields and operations such as seek, read, write, flush, and setLength. It defines both the internal invariants of the buffered file implementation and its refinement to a general RandomAccessFile, ensuring that the buffered version behaves correctly with respect to the abstract file semantics. The spec verifies that invariants are maintained, that each operation refines its abstract counterpart, and that the system as a whole refines the general RandomAccessFile specification. Notable design decisions include explicit modeling of undefined bytes with a special ArbitrarySymbol, careful handling of buffer boundaries, and the use of helper actions and invariants to capture the subtleties of the Java implementation.
