This module is a TLC-oriented wrapper around another specification of atomic commit. It extends ACP_NB, TLC, and TLCExt; defines a symmetry helper Perms; gives a TLC-friendly reformulation of AC4 as the temporal formula AC4_alt; states the liveness property AllMustCommit requiring every participant to eventually commit; declares constants p0 and p1; and defines PostCondition as a concrete record containing a counterexample trace and visited states.
