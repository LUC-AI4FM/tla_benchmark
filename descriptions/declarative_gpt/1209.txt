This module, test212, is a test specification for checking Leibnizness in TLA+ operator substitution. It explores the distinction between Leibniz and non-Leibniz operators, particularly in the context of substituting operators for constants in module instantiations. The file defines several operators that either are or are not Leibniz, and then attempts both legal and illegal instantiations of another module (test212a) with these operators, to illustrate which substitutions are permitted by the TLA+ semantics. The module does not specify a distributed algorithm or system, but rather serves as a meta-level test of the TLA+ language's operator substitution rules.
