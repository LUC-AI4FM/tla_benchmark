This module gives a high-level TLA+ specification of a Tendermint-style blockchain model tailored for reasoning about a light client. It models a bounded blockchain up to ULTIMATE_HEIGHT, block headers with timestamps, validator sets, next validator sets, and commit sets, together with a global time and a monotone set of faulty validators. The specification captures a fault assumption that for every still-trusted height, the correct validators have more than two-thirds of the voting power, and it defines helper predicates for supermajority voting, trusting period validity, and whether a light block could be justified by digital signatures. The dynamics in this file are limited to advancing time and adding one more faulty node, while initialization constructs an entire blockchain satisfying timestamp, commit, and validator-power constraints.
