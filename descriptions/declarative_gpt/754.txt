This specification models the classic Coffee Can problem, where a can contains black and white beans and repeatedly removes two beans according to their colors, possibly adding back a black bean. The model represents the can as a record with black and white counts bounded by a constant maximum, and it studies the effect of the three possible bean-selection cases plus a termination stuttering action when only one bean remains. The spec checks a type invariant, monotonic decrease in total bean count, eventual termination under weak fairness, preservation of the parity of the white-bean count, and a hypothesis that the final bean is black iff the initial number of white beans is even.
