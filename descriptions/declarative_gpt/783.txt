This specification models a replicated log in which nodes append transactions to a shared log and track, per node, how many log entries have been executed. The state consists of the sequence-valued log and a function executed mapping each node to a natural number, with actions for writing a new transaction and for executing pending transactions. The spec states type correctness and safety, and studies convergence under different fairness assumptions, including sufficient fairness conditions that imply all nodes eventually catch up to the log length and several fairness variants that are identified as insufficient or effectively false.
