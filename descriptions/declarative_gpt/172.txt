This specification defines a small TLA+ module that tests nested call-by-name behavior for locally defined operators passed to an Apalache sequence-fold operator. The top-level operator A(x, y) introduces a local operator F(old, new) == new and applies ApaFoldSeqLeft to the singleton tuple <<y>>, so A returns y for its given arguments. The module then defines trivial initialization and next-state predicates as TRUE, and states an invariant Inv asserting that folding A over the singleton sequence <<1>> from initial accumulator 0 yields 1.
