This specification models a single natural-valued state variable x that starts at 1 and increments by 1 on each step until it reaches 5. Once x = 5, the next-state relation stutters by leaving x unchanged. The spec defines a standard temporal specification with an initial condition, a boxed next-state relation, and a weak fairness condition on Next, and it asserts a liveness property that from the initial value x = 1 the system eventually reaches and thereafter always remains at x = 5.
