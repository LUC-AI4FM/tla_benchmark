This module specifies a parameterized asynchronous non-blocking atomic commitment algorithm with failure detectors, based on the algorithm discussed in Guerraoui's paper on failure-sensitive agreement problems. The model tracks how processes initially vote YES or NO, may crash, may become suspected by a failure detector, send messages, receive messages, and eventually either abort or commit. It defines a transition system with weak fairness for non-stuttering process actions, specialized initial conditions where all processes vote YES or all vote NO, a type invariant, and temporal properties for agreement, abort validity, commit validity, termination, and eventual commit.
