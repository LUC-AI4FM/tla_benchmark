This specification models a simple transition system over a single set-valued variable S. The initial states choose S as an arbitrary subset of the integer range 1..50 subject to the constraint that 5 is not a member, and the transition relation either removes some existing element from S or stutters. The file defines an invariant asserting that 5 is never in S; there is no explicit temporal specification, fairness condition, proof, or PlusCal algorithm.
