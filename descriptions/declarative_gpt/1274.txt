This module specifies a very small system with a single variable s whose initial value is constrained by an initialization operator. The specification is designed to highlight the cost of evaluating an expensive CHOOSE over SUBSET (1..18), as explained by the comments, and contrasts this with a commented-out parameterized variant that would trigger repeated reevaluation. The temporal behavior after initialization is pure stuttering, expressed by requiring that s remain unchanged forever via a boxed UNCHANGED action.
