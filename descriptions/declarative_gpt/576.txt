This module defines the externally observable client history for a transactional system in which clients can receive transaction responses before the transactions are committed. It models read-write and read-only transactions, transaction status notifications, and helper sets derived from the history to identify committed and invalid transactions and related request/response events. The specification focuses on typing and monotonicity properties of the shared history, using an append-only history variable and temporal assertion that the history always remains a prefix of its next-state value.
