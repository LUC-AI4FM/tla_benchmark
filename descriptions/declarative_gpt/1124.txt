This module provides a TLAPS proof of the type-correctness invariant (PCTypeOK) for the Paxos Commit protocol, as specified in PaxosCommit.tla. The proof establishes that the type invariant holds throughout all reachable states of the protocol, using an inductive invariant (Inv) that strengthens PCTypeOK with additional properties: AccInv (relating acceptor state fields), MsgInv1b (constraining phase1b messages), and the finiteness of the message set. The proof also includes a well-founded induction argument for the Maximum operator over finite sets of integers, ensuring that the leader's value selection in Phase2a is type-correct. The module contains detailed proofs for initialization, consecution, and auxiliary lemmas, and leverages TLAPS proof commands and structure.
