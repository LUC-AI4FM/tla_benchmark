This module is a model-checking wrapper around an underlying one-bit mutual exclusion specification imported via EXTENDS Session9onebit. It introduces concrete model constants for three processes and states safety and liveness properties over the imported state variable pc, namely mutual exclusion, deadlock/livelock freedom in the sense that some process trying to enter eventually reaches the critical section, and starvation-freedom properties for processes 0 and 1. The file itself does not define the operational algorithm; instead it specializes the model with ConstN and ConstProcs and states temporal properties to be checked, including a comment noting that NoStarveProc1 is not satisfied by the algorithm.
