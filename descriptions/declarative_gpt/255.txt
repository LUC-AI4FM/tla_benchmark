This module is a functional test specification for finite-set operators provided by the community module FiniteSetsExt. It introduces a trivial state machine with Init and Next both equal to TRUE, and then defines a collection of state-level test operators that assert expected mathematical behavior of operators such as Quantify, kSubset, FoldSet, ChooseUnique, SymDiff, SumSet, and ProductSet. The specification does not model faults or a distributed algorithm; instead, it serves as a suite of invariant-style checks over sets, subsets, cardinalities, and arithmetic results, with one commented-out test noting unsupported expressions for Apalache.
