This specification models a simplified leader-election style protocol inspired by Raft, using variant-typed messages for RequestVote, Vote, and AppendEntries. Servers can become candidates by incrementing their current term and sending RequestVote messages, can vote upon receiving a higher-term RequestVote, and can become leaders once they observe votes from a quorum, after which they send AppendEntries. The spec defines an initial state, a next-state relation with stuttering, a temporal specification `Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars`, a safety property asserting at most one AppendEntries sender per term, and an invariant combining typing constraints with consistency conditions about votes, terms, and uniqueness of leaders.
