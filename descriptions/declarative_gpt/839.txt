This module models an early, now-outdated version of the Nano blockchain protocol. It represents signed blocks, account chains, balances, and per-node ledgers, along with actions for creating and processing genesis, open, send, receive, and change-representative blocks. The specification assumes abstract cryptographic primitives for hashing and signatures, models a distributed setting where each node maintains its own ledger and a set of received-but-unprocessed blocks, and verifies a safety theorem showing that the temporal specification implies the type invariant and a cryptographic safety invariant.
