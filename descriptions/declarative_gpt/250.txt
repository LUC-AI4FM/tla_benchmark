This module is a small test specification for bag-related helper operators provided by the extended bag libraries. It checks algebraic relationships for adding to a bag, removing from a bag, removing all copies of an element, summing weighted bag contents, and taking the product of weighted bag contents. The spec does not model a transition system with meaningful state evolution or a fault model; instead, it defines a trivial Init and Next and gathers the individual test predicates into AllTests as state-level assertions about the imported bag operators.
