This module augments the DiskSynod specification with additional history variables and inductive invariants for a disk-based Synod/Paxos-style consensus protocol. It introduces `allInput` to track every input value ever seen and `chosen` to record the first non-default output produced by any process, then states a series of higher-level invariants relating ballots, disk blocks, reads, majority sets, and the chosen value. The file does not define a full temporal specification of its own, but instead builds on the imported `Init` and `Next` actions from `DiskSynod` and adds safety-oriented predicates that characterize correctness of the protocol state.
