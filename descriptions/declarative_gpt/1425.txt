This specification models a very small transition system with three state variables x, y, and z. It defines two actions: A1 increments x, while A2 assigns x, y, and z all to 1 in a single step. The temporal property Spec starts from the initial state x=0, y=0, z=0 and requires that every step satisfies the boxed next-state condition [][A1 \/ A2]_<<x,y,z>>, meaning each step is either an A1 step, an A2 step, or a stuttering step over the tuple <<x,y,z>>.
