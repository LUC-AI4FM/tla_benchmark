This module defines an inductive invariant for a Tendermint accountability and agreement model, extending a base Tendermint specification. It characterizes both forked and non-forked behaviors by constraining message sets, process states, locking behavior, and quorum relationships for proposals, prevotes, and precommits. The spec focuses on type correctness and safety-style invariants, and states theorems that the invariant is inductive, that agreement holds when fewer than one third of processes are faulty, and that otherwise accountability captures fork scenarios under a weaker fault bound.
