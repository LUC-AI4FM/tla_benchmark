This module, 'test51a', is a test specification to check the definability of certain operator aliases in TLA+. It defines several infix operators (such as =<, >=, \circ, \oplus, \ominus, \otimes, \oslash, \odot) as syntactic aliases for set enumeration of their two arguments. There are no variables, constants, temporal properties, or algorithmic content; the module is purely structural and intended for testing operator definitions.
