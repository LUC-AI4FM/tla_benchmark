This module specifies Peterson's N-process mutual exclusion algorithm, written as a PlusCal algorithm and accompanied by its TLA+ translation. The algorithm models a set of processes in Procs that repeatedly cycle through noncritical section, entry protocol, critical section, and exit, using shared arrays level and last_to_enter to coordinate access to the critical section. The specification assumes a positive number of processes with Procs = 0..(N-1), and it verifies behavior under weak fairness of each process action once the process has left the "ncs" control location.
