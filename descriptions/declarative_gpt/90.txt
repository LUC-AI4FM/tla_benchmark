This specification models the cigarette smokers problem. There is a set of ingredients, a dealer that offers all but one ingredient, and smokers indexed by the ingredient they possess in infinite supply; a smoker is represented by a record with a boolean smoking flag. The spec defines initialization, a step where the dealer places an offer and the matching smoker starts smoking, a step where a currently smoking smoker stops and the dealer chooses a new offer, and it states both a basic temporal specification and a weakly fair version, along with a safety property asserting that at most one smoker is smoking at a time.
