This TLA+ specification provides a TLAPS proof for the Missionaries and Cannibals problem, focusing on type correctness. The proof shows that the system, as defined by the Init and Next operators (from the MissionariesAndCannibals module), maintains the TypeOK invariant throughout all behaviors. The property Spec => []TypeOK is proven, where Spec is defined as Init /\ [][Next]_vars. The proof is structured hierarchically and demonstrates that all reachable states satisfy the type invariant, though the comments note that this is not a real safety property but rather a proof skeleton for illustration.
