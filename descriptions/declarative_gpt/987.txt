This module models an asynchronous Byzantine consensus protocol based on Bracha, Gabriel, and Toueg's 1985 protocol, using a message-counting abstraction instead of explicit message buffers. Processes begin by proposing either 0 or 1, may become Byzantine up to a bound F, can receive ECHO and READY messages, send ECHO and READY messages when guards are satisfied, and eventually decide by moving to an accepting state. The specification includes initialization predicates for arbitrary initial proposals as well as all-0 and all-1 initial states, a weak-fairness-based temporal specification for liveness checking, type correctness, and temporal properties for validity, correctness, and agreement under the stated resilience assumption N > 3 * T with T >= F >= 0.
