This module provides a TLAPS proof for the temporal property Spec => [](TypeInvariant /\ TxLifecycle) of a key-value store system, as specified in KeyValueStore.tla. The proof demonstrates that the system maintains both type invariants and correct transaction lifecycles throughout its execution. The proof is structured hierarchically, showing that the invariants hold initially and are preserved by all possible transitions, including transaction operations and stuttering steps. The design leverages previously defined operators and actions from the KeyValueStore module, and uses TLAPS proof commands and structure to establish the result.
