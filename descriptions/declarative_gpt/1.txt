This module models a simple cloud storage system with one cloud process and a set of client processes, originally written in PlusCal and then translated to TLA+. Clients repeatedly perform a write followed by a read, and the cloud replies according to a selected consistency model: Eventual, Consistent_Prefix, Session, Bounded_Staleness, or Strong. The specification includes the translated operational semantics, a standard safety-style temporal specification Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars, and state predicates expressing the intended consistency condition for the single-client case under the assumption NumClients = 1 and MaxNumOp < 10.
