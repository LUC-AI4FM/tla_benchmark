This module states a large collection of theorems about sequence operators provided by SequencesExt and related modules. It formalizes algebraic, structural, and inductive properties of constructors and transformers such as Cons, InsertAt, RemoveAt, Front, Last, Reverse, prefix and suffix relations, and fold operators, and it also connects these sequence orderings to well-founded induction and recursive function definitions. The file is theorem-oriented rather than operational: it defines a few helper operators used to express recursive-definition principles, contains no state variables or actions, and includes no temporal behavior or fault model.
