This specification models the classic prisoner light-switch puzzle with a designated counter prisoner and a single shared lamp in a special cell. The model includes both the standard variant where the initial light state is known to be off and a harder variant where the initial light state is unknown; in the unknown case, non-counter prisoners may signal twice and the counter waits for a threshold of 2N - 1 instead of N. The system verifies that under weak fairness of the warden's prisoner selection, the prisoners eventually announce victory, and it also asserts a safety property that any victory announcement implies every prisoner has actually visited the cell.
