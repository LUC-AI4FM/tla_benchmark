Specify a formal model of multisets (bags) and their algebraic operations and properties. The model should treat a bag as a finite collection of elements each with a positive integer multiplicity. It must provide operations to convert between finite sets and singleton-count bags, to test element membership and to query the multiplicity of an element (returning zero for absent elements). Include bag union (elementwise addition of multiplicities), bag difference (subtracting multiplicities but removing elements whose resulting multiplicity is zero or negative), and construction of a bag from a collection of bags by taking for each element the sum of multiplicities across the collection. Provide a way to map elements through a function into a new bag, combining multiplicities of elements that map to the same target. Include an operation that enumerates all sub-bags of a given bag (all bags whose multiplicities are pointwise less-or-equal and that only contain elements from the original bag). Define a bag cardinality operator that returns the total number of items counting multiplicities.

State precise invariants and correctness properties: closure of all operations within the type of finite bags; commutativity and associativity of bag union; monotonicity of union and mapping operations w.r.t. pointwise ordering; correctness of difference relative to multiplicity subtraction and element removal; that converting a set to a singleton-count bag and back preserves the set; and that mapping then taking cardinality equals summing multiplicities of preimages. Ensure all definitions handle empty bags cleanly and that all domains and sums are well-defined and finite.
