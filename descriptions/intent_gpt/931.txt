Specify a simple digital hour clock that maintains an integer hour value in the range 0..23 and advances time in discrete steps. The model should represent the state of the clock and a nondeterministic step that increments the hour by one modulo 24. Include an initialization that sets the hour to some valid integer in 0..23. The specification must allow repeated advancement steps forever.

State the obligations the model must verify:
- Safety: the hour is always an integer in 0..23.
- Determinacy of the wrap-around behavior: whenever the clock advances from 23, the next hour must be 0; for any other hour h in 0..22 the next hour must be h+1.
- Stuttering tolerance: allowing steps that do not change the hour (stuttering) is permitted, but such steps cannot violate safety or the wrap-around property.

Also require liveness properties:
- Progress: from any reachable state, the system must inevitably reach every hour value (i.e., each hour recurs infinitely often).
- Fairness: the advance step should not be indefinitely postponed (strong fairness ensuring eventual advancement whenever enabled).

Do not prescribe particular variable names or implementation structure; describe only the abstract state, transitions, invariants, and liveness requirements to be proved.
