Model a voucher redemption system that uses a coordinator-based two‑phase commit to move vouchers from valid to redeemed or to abort the redemption consistently across participants. The system involves a set of vouchers, a set of voucher holders, and a set of voucher collectors, plus a single coordinator that drives the commit/abort decision. Specify the allowed behaviors (not an implementation): holders and collectors may independently decide to prepare or to abort; preparing means they signal readiness to commit; aborting means they decide not to participate. The coordinator collects Prepared signals from every holder and collector; if it has received Prepared from all participants it may decide to commit a specific voucher, causing that voucher’s lifecycle to complete and the coordinator to broadcast a Commit (Redeem) notification; alternatively the coordinator may spontaneously decide to abort and broadcast an Abort notification. After a commit or abort broadcast, all participants must be able to observe the broadcast and enter the corresponding final state (redeemed or aborted). Require invariants: type correctness (states and message formats remain within their allowed domains), and global consistency (no holder ends up marked redeemed while any collector is marked aborted, and vice versa). Also require that the overall voucher lifecycle invariant is satisfied (the high‑level voucher lifecycle specification is implemented by this protocol).
