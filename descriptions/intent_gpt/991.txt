Specify a formal TLA+ model of a fault-tolerant atomic commit protocol for a fixed set of participants coordinated by a coordinator. The model should capture messages exchanged (prepare, vote, commit, abort, and coordinator decisions), participant state transitions (initial, prepared, committed, aborted), and possible failures including coordinator crash and participant crash and recovery. The protocol must guarantee safety properties: agreement (no two non-faulty participants decide differently), validity (if any participant votes to abort then the final outcome is abort; if all vote to commit then the final outcome can be commit), and integrity (once a participant decides commit or abort it never reverts). Liveness should be stated as a weak guarantee under fair delivery and a bounded failure model: if the coordinator does not crash permanently and all non-faulty participants respond, then the protocol eventually reaches a decision. Model nondeterministic scheduling and message loss/duplication but allow assumptions (e.g., reliable retransmission or eventual delivery) needed for the liveness claim. Include symmetry reduction considerations for identical participants to keep state-space manageable. Specify invariants to prove and temporal properties to check with TLC, without prescribing the algorithmic steps for participants or coordinator.
