Specify a TLA+ model of a simple deterministic mapping from a fixed finite set of input pairs to integer outputs. The system must initialize a function whose domain is exactly two specific ordered pairs of integers and whose value at each pair is the sum of the two integers in the pair. After initialization, the function must remain constant: no transition may change its domain or any mapped value. The model should make the domain and mapping explicit so that both the domain membership and the computed values are checkable as invariants. Key correctness properties to assert are: (1) the domain of the mapping is exactly the two required ordered pairs and never changes; (2) for each domain pair, the stored output equals the arithmetic sum of the two components; and (3) all reachable states satisfy that the mapping is total on that domain and that no other pairs are mapped. Also assert that the system has at least one reachable state (i.e., initialization succeeds) and that all invariants hold on all steps. Do not include any extraneous nondeterminism or additional actions that could alter the function.
