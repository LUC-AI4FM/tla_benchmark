Specify a system that constructs and validates directed graphs used for testing graph algorithms. The model should allow creation of small “smoke” graphs that exercise common structural cases: empty graphs, single-node graphs, simple chains, simple cycles, graphs with parallel edges, graphs with self-loops, and small disconnected graphs. The system must support adding and removing nodes and edges, and querying basic graph properties such as node set membership, edge existence, adjacency lists, in-degree and out-degree counts, and reachability between nodes.

Define operations that generate these small test graphs deterministically and operations that mutate graphs to produce variations. Ensure that legal graph invariants are always maintained: nodes referenced by edges must exist in the node set, edge identifiers are unique (if applicable), and graph representations remain well-formed after every operation. Specify properties to check: preservation of node/edge invariants, correctness of adjacency and degree queries, and consistency of reachability computations under additions and removals. Include liveness goals that guarantee that test graph generators can produce each smoke case, and safety assertions that no invalid graph state can be reached. Provide concise, machine-checkable correctness properties suitable for automatic model checking.
