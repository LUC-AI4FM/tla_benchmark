Specify a system that models the bitwise AND operation on nonnegative integers and verifies its expected algebraic and bit-level properties. The model should treat integers as sequences of bits (infinite or sufficiently large finite width) and define bitwise AND as combining corresponding bit positions with logical conjunction. It must accept arbitrary nonnegative integers as inputs and produce an output integer whose binary representation has a 1 in a given position exactly when both inputs have a 1 in that position.

The specification must state and verify these correctness requirements:
- Bit correspondence: for every bit index i, the i-th bit of the result equals the logical AND of the i-th bits of the two inputs.
- Commutativity: a & b = b & a.
- Associativity: (a & b) & c = a & (b & c).
- Identity element for all-ones mask of sufficient width: a & allOnes = a, when allOnes has ones in every bit position considered.
- Monotonicity with respect to bitwise inclusion: if bits(a) ⊆ bits(b) then a & c ⊆ b & c (interpreted per-bit).
- Bound and zero behavior: a & 0 = 0; result ≤ min(a, b).
- Decomposition: the value equals the sum over bit positions of 2^i times the bitwise conjunction at i.

Additionally, include properties to ensure the model handles finite-width representations consistently (specify width or semantics for higher bits) and supports reasoning about division, modulo, and shifting operations used to extract bits.
