Specify a refinement relationship between two resource-allocation systems: a high-level simple allocator and a lower-level scheduling allocator implementation. The model should describe requests from clients for exclusive access to a finite set of identical resources (or slots), how requests are granted and released, and how the scheduler maps requests onto resources over time. The simple allocator must be characterized by its abstract behavior: clients can request and later release resources, at most one client holds a given resource at a time, and every granted allocation corresponds to a previously issued request. The scheduling allocator must implement the same externally observable behavior while possibly using additional internal scheduling steps, queues, or preemption decisions. Require that every trace of the scheduling allocator, when observed only in terms of client requests, grants, and releases, is a valid trace of the simple allocator (refinement / trace inclusion). Safety properties: mutual exclusion on resources, no allocation without request, and invariant preservation of resource counts. Liveness properties: every pending request is eventually either granted or explicitly refused according to policy, and resources are eventually released if clients complete. Specify the refinement relation between observable actions and require a formal proof obligation showing the implementation implies the specification.
