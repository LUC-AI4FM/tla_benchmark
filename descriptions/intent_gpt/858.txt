Specify a concurrent readers–writers control system coordinating a fixed finite set of actors that may request either read or write access to a single shared resource. The model should represent actors issuing access requests, waiting if necessary, and acquiring and releasing the resource. It must allow multiple readers to hold the resource simultaneously, but ensure at most one writer holds it and that writers have exclusive access (no readers while a writer holds the resource). The system should preserve fairness: every request that remains pending long enough should eventually be granted (no indefinite starvation for readers or writers). Support the possibility that actors may reissue requests over time and that waiting requests are queued; correctness should not depend on a particular queue discipline beyond the fairness requirement. Specify initialization conditions, legal transitions for submitting requests, granting and releasing access, and the evolution of the waiting set. Formalize key safety properties: mutual exclusion for writers, writer exclusivity with readers, and that granted holders are a subset of current requests. Formalize liveness properties: progress (if the resource is free and outstanding requests exist, some request is eventually granted) and fairness (every enabled request is eventually served). Also specify invariants asserting counts of holders are consistent with access modes and that only known actors may participate.
