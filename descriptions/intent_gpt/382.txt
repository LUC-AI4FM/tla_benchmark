Specify a small formal system that models reasoning about constant parameters and assumptions in a verification context. The system should represent a set of declared constants and allow the verifier to assert global equalities or membership facts about those constants as assumptions. It must permit introducing additional assumptions (hypotheses) whose truth is taken for the purpose of proving subsequent goals, and it must allow trivial goals that are always true to be discharged regardless of the assumptions. The model should capture the distinction between declared constants and assumed facts: declared constants form the signature of the problem and assumed facts constrain their possible values for a proof attempt. The specification must ensure that checking a proof obligation correctly uses only the declared constants and the currently active assumptions and that an unprovable goal is detectable. It must also express the property that adding an assumption inconsistent with declared equalities can render some goals provable or unprovable depending on consistency, and that a tautological goal is provable in every context. Include correctness properties asserting soundness (no false theorems are derivable from inconsistent handling), proper scoping of assumptions (assumptions do not leak outside their intended proof), and the ability to detect trivial truths.
