Specify a formal TLA+ model of an abstract integer arithmetic module built atop a more general real-number foundation. The model should treat integers as a subset of a richer numeric domain and expose the integer operations and relations needed by higher-level algorithms. Ensure the model defines integer values, negation, subtraction, addition, multiplication, ordering, and conversion to and from the underlying real-type values, and precisely specify their semantics in terms of the real-number foundation.

Require that all integer operations are closed: applying any defined integer operation to integers yields an integer. Require that integer arithmetic obeys the standard ring axioms (associativity, commutativity where appropriate, distributivity), negation is the additive inverse, and subtraction is defined consistently with addition and negation. Require that equality and ordering on integers coincide with equality and ordering restricted from the underlying real domain, and that integer-to-real embedding preserves arithmetic and ordering. Specify totality of comparison operators and that no operation produces non-integer or undefined results.

State invariants expressing these properties and include theorems to show consistency of the integer subset with the real foundation (e.g., embedding is injective and homomorphic). Describe expected proof obligations and correctness properties the formal model must guarantee without prescribing implementation.
