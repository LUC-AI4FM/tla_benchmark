Specify a TLA+ model of a blockchain light client that verifies block headers using a set of validators and occasional full-node probes. The model should capture a client that incrementally advances the highest verified block height by fetching and validating light blocks at increasing heights until a target height is reached. The client may be configured with a trusted starting height, a trusting period, assumptions about clock drift, and a threshold on the fraction of validators that may be Byzantine. The system should model a fixed set of validators, some of which may be faulty, and a notion of primary and secondary peers for fetching light blocks. The client must choose which blocks to fetch, request and receive signatures or commitments from validators, and decide whether to accept a block as verified based on quorum rules and time/clock constraints.

The specification must state safety properties: the client never accepts conflicting headers for the same height, never advances past an invalid branch when a supermajority of honest validators attest to a different header, and never accepts a header after its trusting period has expired. It must state liveness goals: if a sufficient fraction of validators are honest, and the primary peer responds, the client eventually verifies up to the target height. Also include monitoring properties that detect and flag equivocation or timeout-based failures, and ensure the client does not progress when critical conditions (insufficient quorum, expired trust, or clock inconsistencies) are present.
