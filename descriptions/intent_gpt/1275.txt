Specify a simple state-variable system that initially nondeterministically chooses a binary value (0 or 1) for a single variable, subject to the constraint that the initial value must be strictly less than 1. After initialization, the state must remain unchanged forever (no further transitions). The model should capture the nondeterministic choice at initialization and then a stuttering (inert) behavior for all subsequent steps. Require an invariant property stating that the variable is always strictly less than 1 throughout execution, and require verification that this invariant holds for every reachable state under the given initialization and inert transition model. Also require that the initialization is explicitly existential over the two allowed values, and that there are no actions that modify the variable after initialization. Finally, include the liveness-level expectation that no other progress is possible (i.e., the system remains stable), and that model checking should confirm both the safety invariant and the absence of state changes after initialization.
