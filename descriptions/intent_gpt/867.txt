Specify a formal TLA+ model of the classic single-decree Paxos consensus algorithm among a fixed set of acceptors and proposers. The model should describe the protocol at the message-passing level (Prepare/Promise, Accept/Accepted, and decision) without encoding particular implementation details such as leader election, timers, or network delivery order. The system must allow proposers to initiate a round identified by a unique ballot number, solicit promises from a quorum of acceptors, choose a value consistent with any previously accepted higher-ballot value reported by a quorum, and send Accept requests. Acceptors must promise to ignore lower-numbered ballots once they promise, must record the highest ballot they have promised and the highest accepted ballot/value they have accepted, and must accept an Accept request only if it does not violate a prior promise. The model must include quorums with majority overlap and support asynchronous, unordered message delivery (messages may be delayed, duplicated, or reordered). Key correctness properties to guarantee: safety (consistency) — at most one value can ever be chosen/decided; validity (nontriviality) — any decided value must have been proposed by some proposer; and monotonicity of acceptor state (promised ballot and accepted ballot/value evolve correctly). Do not require liveness (termination) guarantees. Include invariants asserting quorum intersection, type-correctness of messages and state, and a mechanism that a decision can be derived when a quorum of acceptors have sent matching accepted messages.
