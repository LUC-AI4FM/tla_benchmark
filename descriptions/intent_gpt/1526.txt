Specify a formal model of a single-lane bridge crossing protocol for multiple autonomous cars approaching from two opposite directions. The model should represent cars located on discrete positions along a linear road that includes a contiguous bridge segment. Cars may move forward one position at a time, enter the bridge only when safe, and exit after crossing. The protocol must prevent head-on collisions: at most one direction’s cars may occupy the bridge at any time, and no two cars may occupy the same position simultaneously. Liveness requirements: every car that wants to cross eventually reaches the far side (no starvation), and cars that repeatedly attempt to cross are guaranteed to visit the bridge infinitely often. Safety requirements: crossing must preserve ordering for cars from the same direction (no overtaking), and deadlocks where cars from both sides block each other must be impossible. The model should allow nondeterministic scheduling of cars’ move attempts and parameterization of the number of cars, road length, and bridge segment. Specify invariants and temporal properties to check the above safety and liveness goals, and provide assertions to detect violations like collisions, deadlock, starvation, or position conflicts.
