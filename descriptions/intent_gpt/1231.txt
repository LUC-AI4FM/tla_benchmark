Specify a formal model of Cartesian product and tuple-indexed function application over finite and infinite integer domains, and verify basic set-theoretic and functional properties. The model should represent n-ary Cartesian products of sets (including nested, finite ranges, and standard integer sets), membership of tuples in such products, subset relationships between sets of tuples and Cartesian products, and evaluation of functions defined by mapping a tuple to an arithmetic expression on its components. The system must permit products of varying arity (2, 3, 4...), include products that mix finite sets and integer domains, and support tuple construction and projection semantics.

Require checks that:
- The Cartesian product enumerates precisely the expected tuples for small finite operands.
- Membership tests correctly recognize tuples that do or do not belong to given products.
- Subset and power-set relationships involving sets of tuples behave as set theory dictates.
- Functions defined on tuple domains evaluate correctly at specified tuples (including negative integers) using positional indexing into the tuple.
- Intersections between function-domain mappings and Cartesian product subsets yield the expected results.

Specify invariants and assertions that must hold in all reachable states, and include examples exercising edge cases: mixed-type components, repeated elements, and products involving infinite integer domains. The model should be independent of any particular algorithm for construction and should focus on the correctness properties above.
