Specify a TLA+ model of a light client peer-to-peer detection system for a Byzantine-fault tolerant blockchain network. The system maintains a reference blockchain and a light client that periodically fetches and verifies block headers (light blocks) from peers to detect peers that provide conflicting or invalid state. The model should represent multiple validator nodes with up to a specified fraction faulty; the system must tolerate equivocation and incorrect timestamps from faulty nodes and should model both correct and faulty peer behaviors.

The model must capture the light client’s trusted checkpoint and its goal of advancing trust to a target chain height while respecting a trusting period and clock drift between the client and the reference chain. It must include the process of requesting light blocks from different peers, merging or comparing fetched blocks, and producing provable evidence when a peer supplies signatures or headers that contradict the reference chain or another honest peer.

Specify correctness properties: safety (the client never accepts a header unless it has been validated by a sufficient quorum of non-faulty validators and respects the trusting period), liveness (if a correct chain exists and enough honest peers respond, the client eventually reaches the target height), and accountability (when a peer equivocates or provides invalid data, the system can produce evidence that will identify at least one faulty validator). Also ensure that clock drift and message delays cannot cause false accusations of honesty or fault beyond the tolerated thresholds. Include invariants for the maximum number/fraction of faulty nodes and for monotonic progression of trusted heights.
