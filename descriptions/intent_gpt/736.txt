Model a bounded-version of Lamport's Bakery mutual-exclusion algorithm for N concurrent processes, where each process repeatedly alternates between noncritical and critical sections and uses ticket numbers drawn from a finite range. The specification should treat the number of processes and the maximum ticket value as parameters. It must describe how processes choose and compare tickets to enter the critical section, including tie-breaking for equal tickets, while preventing ticket values from exceeding the specified maximum by treating the ticket domain as a finite cyclic or bounded set.

Require that the algorithm enforces mutual exclusion: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time. Require progress liveness properties: if a process wishes to enter the critical section and other processes do not indefinitely prevent it (fair scheduling), then it eventually enters (starvation freedom under weak fairness assumptions). Include safety properties that ticket assignments and comparisons always remain within the bounded domain, and that ordering and tie-break semantics are well-defined in that domain. Specify invariants to ensure absence of deadlock and that the protocol preserves per-process local states (thinking, choosing, in-critical). Allow verification of safety (mutual exclusion, bounded tickets) and liveness (eventual entry under fairness) as the primary correctness goals.
