Specify a TLA+ model of a system that incrementally builds a set of distinct human participants by repeatedly adding one human at a time until no further additions occur. The model should treat the universe of humans and an unrelated second universe separately; only elements from the human universe may ever be added to the constructed set. The specification must capture an initial empty set and a nondeterministic operation that chooses a single human and adds them to the set (if not already present). The model should allow any order of additions and ensure that duplicates are not introduced. Key correctness properties to state and verify:

- Safety: the maintained set always remains a subset of the human universe (no outsiders may appear).
- Invariance of distinctness: the set always contains no duplicate entries (i.e., behaves as a mathematical set).
- Progress/termination variant: if desired, express that eventually every human will either be in the set or no more additions are possible; alternatively, permit modeling of both finite and infinite progress so liveness properties can be checked.
- Permutation symmetry: the behavior must be invariant under renaming of humans (symmetry of identities), so correctness does not depend on particular labels.

Do not prescribe implementation details; leave nondeterministic choice of which human to add unconstrained except by the requirement that choices come from the human universe.
