Specify a model of drawing a uniformly random fixed-size subset of a large finite function space and exposing it as the initial state for further inspection. The system must represent a universe of total functions from a finite domain of size n to a finite codomain of size m, where n and m are parameters that make the full set of functions extremely large (m^n). The model should support selecting exactly k distinct functions from that universe without enumerating the entire function space, and record that selection as the system's initial collection. The selection must be uniform over all k-element subsets: every k-sized subset of functions must be equally likely. After initialization, the chosen subset is immutable for the remainder of the execution. The specification must allow reasoning about properties of the selected subset (size equals k, elements are valid total functions from domain to codomain, all elements are pairwise distinct). It should also make clear that the random selection is atomic in the initial step and that no further nondeterminism or state changes affect the subset. Express the parameters n, m, and k so they can be varied, and state invariants and temporal properties ensuring immutability and correctness of the selection.
