Model a distributed two-phase commit protocol between a single coordinator (transaction manager) and a fixed set of resource managers. The model should describe how each participant progresses through states (e.g., initial, prepared, committed, aborted) and how the coordinator collects votes and decides to commit or abort. Specify the allowed messages and asynchronous delivery between processes without assuming reliable FIFO channels.

The system must guarantee safety properties: (1) Agreement — no two resource managers may end up in conflicting terminal decisions (one committed while another aborted); (2) Consistency with coordinator decisions — if the coordinator decides to commit, then every resource manager that ultimately decides must commit; (3) No invalid state transitions — resource managers and coordinator may only move according to the protocol’s rules (vote collection, decision broadcast, and local commit/abort upon receipt of a decision or timeout), and once in a terminal state they do not change.

The model should also include liveness assumptions and corresponding progress properties to be checked under those assumptions: for example, eventual delivery of messages or eventual failure detection so that if all non-failed resource managers vote to prepare, the coordinator eventually decides to commit and all non-failed resource managers eventually reach the decision. Allow modeling of failures and recoveries, and specify invariants capturing that messages and sets of prepared voters reflect the history of actions.
