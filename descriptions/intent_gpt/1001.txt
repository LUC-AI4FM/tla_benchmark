Specify a TLA+ model of a reusable barrier synchronization primitive for N concurrent processes. The model should treat N as a parameter and represent arbitrary process identities. Each process repeatedly executes phases where it performs local work, then waits at the barrier, and only proceeds past the barrier when all N processes have arrived. The barrier must be reusable: after all processes pass, they may start a new phase and synchronize again without interference from previous phases.

The model must capture asynchronous interleaving of steps, possible process speeds, and the fact that processes may repeatedly reach the barrier an unbounded number of times. It must not rely on shared-clock assumptions. Represent process-local states (e.g., working, waiting, released) and any shared control needed to enforce the barrier semantics.

Specify safety properties: (1) No process may be released from a barrier until all N processes have reached that same barrier instance (no premature release). (2) No process can be released for one barrier instance based on arrivals of a different instance (no cross-instance mixing). Specify liveness properties: eventually all processes that enter the barrier are released (no deadlock or permanent starvation), assuming fair scheduling of processes. Also include a property ensuring eventual reuse: after a complete release, processes can enter and be released by a subsequent barrier instance. Include parameters and invariants needed to reason about counts of arrivals and phase numbers, but do not fix an implementation strategy.
