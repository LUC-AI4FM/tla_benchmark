Specify a distributed system that manages cooperative access to a shared resource among multiple clients with leader election and fault tolerance. The system should allow processes to propose, agree on, and commit changes to the shared resource while ensuring safety and liveness despite process failures and message loss or reordering.

Model a set of processes that can take on roles (candidate/leader/follower) and exchange messages to elect a single leader. Once a leader is chosen, it must coordinate proposals: clients send proposals to the leader; the leader sequences proposals and distributes them to followers for acknowledgement. The system must ensure that at most one proposal is committed for each sequence position and that committed proposals are durable despite leader changes.

Key correctness properties:
- Safety: Agreement (no two different proposals committed at the same sequence index), Integrity (only proposed values may be committed), and Leader Uniqueness (at most one leader at a time).
- Liveness: Eventual leadership (if a majority of non-faulty processes exist, some leader is eventually elected) and Eventual Commitment (valid proposals submitted by a live client are eventually committed under stable conditions).
- Fault tolerance: The protocol must tolerate crash failures of some processes (up to a configurable threshold) and handle message loss, duplication, and reordering without violating safety.
- Recoverability: A new leader must learn previously committed proposals before committing further values.

Specify the state, messages, transitions for proposals, acknowledgements, elections, leader change, and recovery, and assert invariants and temporal properties capturing the above requirements.
