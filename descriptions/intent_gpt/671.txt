Specify a system that models and verifies the behavior of functions (finite maps) and their interactions in a concurrent setting. The model should support creation, update, lookup, deletion, domain/range queries, and composition of finite partial functions over a finite universe of keys and values. It must allow multiple threads or agents to perform function operations concurrently and capture non-deterministic scheduling of their actions.

The system must ensure atomicity of individual function operations and preserve consistency under concurrent updates: no operation should observe a partial update. It must enforce that lookups return the most recent committed value for a given key (or indicate absence if the key was deleted), and that updates and deletions are serialized in some interleaving that is consistent with atomic actions. Composition and overriding of functions must obey the usual functional semantics (resulting in a function mapping keys to values according to defined precedence rules).

Key correctness properties to assert include preservation of function domain and range invariants, determinism of single-threaded sequences of operations, absence of lost updates (every committed update must be reflected in the eventual state unless overwritten), and absence of illegal states such as keys mapped to multiple values simultaneously. Also assert safety (no invariant violation) and liveness: enabled operations by non-failed agents should eventually complete under fair scheduling.
