Specify a small-state initialization and invariants model of a symbolic generator that is expected to produce distinct symbolic constants for separate calls, even when those calls supply identical argument expressions. The model should capture the semantics of a symbolic "Gen" operation that, on each invocation, yields a fresh symbolic value uniquely associated with that invocation (not merely by the syntactic argument value). The system to model has two independent locations where the generator is invoked; both locations may call the generator with identical concrete arguments or with different arguments. The specification must assert that the two generated values are allowed to be unequal and that the initialization can require them to be unequal without causing inconsistency or deadlock.

Required correctness properties:
- Freshness: each generator invocation produces a distinct symbolic constant unless explicitly constrained equal.
- Argument-independence of identity: identical argument expressions do not force generator outputs to be identical; generator identity is tied to the call occurrence, not solely to argument values.
- Consistency: if an explicit equality or inequality constraint is imposed between generated values at initialization, the model must accept it when satisfiable and must not deadlock spuriously.
- Stuttering invariance: the system may otherwise be inert (no further state change), and this must be allowed.

Also include simple checks or invariants that detect unintended collapsing of distinct generated values into the same constant.
