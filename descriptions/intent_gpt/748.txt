Specify a formal TLA+ model of a replicated-state-machine checkpoint coordination service for a distributed system with leader-based lease ownership. The model should describe a set of nodes that coordinate taking and finalizing checkpoints of a shared log, where checkpoints are associated with increasing log indices. Only one leader at a time may hold a renewal lease that authorizes making progress; leases have a holder identity and validity period. The system must allow the leader to propose a checkpoint at a chosen log index, obtain agreement that that index can be truncated or compacted, and safely transfer responsibility when leadership changes.

The model must guarantee safety: checkpoints must never cause loss of committed updates; no leader may finalize a checkpoint that would discard entries still needed by any active or potentially active replica. It must guarantee that two different leaders cannot both consider the same lease concurrently effective unless membership and timing semantics permit overlap, and that checkpoint decisions respect lease ownership (a node without a valid lease must not finalize a checkpoint). Liveness properties should ensure that if a stable leader holds a valid lease and communication is sufficiently reliable, the system eventually completes checkpointing for proposed indices and advances the safe truncation point. Specify invariants capturing monotonicity of checkpoint indices, exclusivity of effective lease ownership, and preservation of committed log entries across checkpointing and leader changes.
