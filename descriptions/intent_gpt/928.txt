Specify a simple discrete clock that counts hours on a 12-hour dial. The model should treat time as discrete steps and represent the clock’s state as a single integer in the range 1..12. At every step the clock must advance deterministically to the next hour on the dial (so 1→2, 2→3, …, 11→12, 12→1). The initial state may be any valid hour in 1..12. The specification must capture both the safety property that every state always remains a valid hour value in 1..12, and the liveness property that the clock makes progress forever (i.e., it never gets stuck and always eventually visits subsequent hours). Also require an equivalence property: an alternative concise transition description that computes the next hour using modular arithmetic should be provably equivalent to the stepwise “increment-or-wrap” description. Do not constrain scheduling beyond nondeterministic choice of an initial hour; the per-step update is deterministic. Include invariants and temporal properties sufficient to verify correctness and equivalence of the two transition formulations.
