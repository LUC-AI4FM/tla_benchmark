Specify a formal TLA+ model of a multi-elevator building control system with the following components and requirements.

Model a fixed set of floors and a fixed set of elevator cars that move between floors, open and close doors, and carry persons. Model persons with a current location (either a floor or an elevator), a destination floor, and a waiting flag. Model elevator state including current floor, motion direction (Up, Down, or Stationary), door state (open/closed), and the set of floor buttons pressed inside each car. Model external elevator calls as a pair (floor, direction). The system must support user actions: a person selecting a new destination, calling an elevator from a floor, entering and exiting a car, the elevator opening and closing doors, moving, stopping, and a dispatcher assigning a car to an active call.

Require the following correctness properties to be guaranteed by the model:
- Type safety: all state variables always respect their declared domains (e.g., people locations are either floors or elevators, button sets are subsets of floors).
- Safety invariants:
  - No waiting person is located in the elevator at the same time as their destination (waiting implies location ≠ destination).
  - If an elevator’s doors are open, its current floor is not listed among that car’s pressed buttons and the car is not Stationary (doors open implies a motion direction and the floor not in pressed-buttons).
  - When doors are open and the car has a direction, the corresponding call (floor,direction) is not simultaneously active.
  - A car in Stationary state contains no persons.
  - If a person is located in a car, then either their destination is among that car’s pressed buttons or the car’s doors are open at their destination floor.
- Initial conditions: define an initial state with no active calls, all doors closed, cars Stationary, buttons empty, and all persons located on floors and not waiting.
- Progress assumptions may be left unspecified, but the model should state the safety invariants are preserved under all allowed actions.

Do not prescribe an implementation; instead describe the abstract state, the allowed atomic transitions (preconditions and state effects), and the invariants to be proved inductive for all actions including stuttering.
