Model a system that produces and validates a sequence of CSV-formatted records emitted by a data generator and consumed by a CSV parser. The specification should describe a stream of rows, each row composed of a fixed number of fields that may contain plain values, quoted values, embedded separators, and escaped quotes. The model must capture generation rules for valid CSV entries (including use of quotes and escape semantics) and allow occasional malformed records to appear. It must specify the parser’s behavior in terms of tokenization and record assembly, including how it handles separators, quoted fields, escaped quote characters, line breaks, and end-of-input.

Require that for every well-formed generated record, the parser eventually yields a parsed row whose field sequence matches the original values. For malformed inputs, require that the parser either rejects the record deterministically or produces a clearly flagged error state; it must never silently produce a different but well-formed row that misrepresents field contents. Specify liveness properties ensuring progress (the parser consumes input and produces an output per record) and safety properties forbidding data corruption, lost fields, or misinterpreted escape sequences. Include invariants that quoted-field rules preserve separators and line breaks within fields and that escaped quotes are returned as single quote characters in parsed output.
