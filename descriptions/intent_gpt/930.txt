Specify a concise discrete-time model of a 12-hour cyclic clock that advances by one hour at each step. The model should represent the clock's single state (the hour) drawn from the integer set {1, 2, ..., 12} and should describe a step relation that updates the hour by incrementing it, wrapping from 12 back to 1. The specification must capture an initial condition that the hour is always a valid 12-hour value and a transition relation that never leaves that domain.

State the invariants the system must guarantee: (1) the hour is always within the range 1..12 (safety), and (2) every step advances the hour by exactly one modulo 12 (functional correctness). Also require liveness that the clock continues to make steps infinitely often (no deadlock), so that the system models an ongoing clock rather than a single transition.

Formally express the properties to be checked: the initialization implies the invariant holds globally; each transition preserves the invariant; the step relation implements the modulo-12 increment; and under fair execution the hour cycles through all 12 values infinitely often (strong fairness / eventual recurrence). Keep the model minimal and deterministic, suitable for model checking these safety and liveness properties.
