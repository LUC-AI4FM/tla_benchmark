Model an asynchronous FIFO channel that transmits discrete messages from a sender to a receiver and may buffer at most one message at a time. The specification should describe the abstract state of the channel and the observable protocol events: the sender offering a value, the receiver becoming ready to accept a value, acknowledgment of receipt, and removal of the buffered value. The channel must preserve the value content (no corruption), deliver each offered value at most once, and never invent new values. It must permit concurrency between sender and receiver actions, but ensure that handshaking rules prevent message loss or duplication.

Specify the allowed steps for each participant so that the sender can present a datum, the receiver can indicate readiness and accept the datum when present, and acknowledgements complete the transfer clearing the buffer for a new datum. Include fairness assumptions sufficient to prevent livelock (e.g., weak fairness for actions that enable progress). Express safety properties: values in the channel must be unchanged while buffered; acknowledgement occurs only for a previously offered value; and the channel buffer capacity constraint is respected. Express liveness properties: every value offered in the presence of a continuously eager receiver is eventually delivered and acknowledged; and if a participant repeatedly attempts to send or receive, progress will eventually occur under the fairness assumptions.
