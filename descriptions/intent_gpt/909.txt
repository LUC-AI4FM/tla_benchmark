Specify a formally precise model of a composed message-passing pipeline with three logical components: a sender, an intermediate FIFO buffer of unbounded (or parameterized) capacity, and a receiver, connected by two asynchronous handshake channels. The system must represent channels as pairs of handshake endpoints that support atomic send and receive handshake operations (rdy/ack style), so that a send on one side completes only when the corresponding receive occurs on the other side. The sender nondeterministically produces messages drawn from a fixed domain and injects them into the first channel. The buffer accepts incoming messages from the first channel, enqueues them, and dequeues to send to the second channel; the buffer must preserve FIFO ordering and not lose or duplicate messages. The receiver accepts messages from the second channel at its own pace. The model should allow channels and endpoints to be in arbitrary ready/acknowledge boolean states, but enforce that handshakes are matched correctly.

Specify safety properties: (1) message-order preservation between sender output sequence and receiver input sequence (FIFO), (2) no message loss or duplication (every message sent is eventually received at most once), and (3) channel handshake correctness (ack/rdy consistency and no mismatched handshakes). Specify liveness properties: every message submitted by the sender is eventually delivered to the receiver assuming fair execution of the buffer and channels, and the system does not deadlock under fair scheduling. Allow parameterization of the message domain and buffer capacity for verification experiments.
