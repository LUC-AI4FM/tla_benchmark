Specify a formal model of finite-set aggregation operators and basic numeric set operations, together with their expected algebraic laws and invariants. The model should treat folding (a binary associative/commutative operator with a neutral/base element), summation, and product over finite sets, as well as mapping-then-aggregating functions and the min/max of finite integer sets. It must capture the behavior of folding over an empty set (yielding the base), folding a nonempty finite set as combining one element with the fold of the rest, and that folding preserves the operator’s result type. For associative and commutative operators with a neutral element, require that folding respects element addition, disjoint unions, and is invariant under order. For sum and product over finite sets of naturals/integers, require typing (results are in Nat/Int), identity results for empty sets (0 for sum, 1 for product), decomposition by removing/adding an element, distributivity over disjoint union, monotonicity and subset bounds, and characterizations of when the aggregate equals 0 or 1. For mapping-then-sum, require typing, empty-map result 0, elementwise decomposition, distributivity over disjoint unions, subset monotonicity, and strict monotonicity when the mapping strictly increases for at least one element. Finally, capture properties of min and max on integer sets: existence for nonempty finite sets, correctness when given upper/lower bounds, and that interval endpoints are extrema. State all properties abstractly (no implementation decisions), quantify over finite sets and functions, and assert the invariants and equivalences that must hold for correctness.
