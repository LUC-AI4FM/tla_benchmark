Specify a formal model of a single-producer, single-consumer FIFO buffer that transports messages from a sender to a receiver. The model should allow an unbounded (or optionally bounded by a parameter) sequence of messages to be enqueued by the producer and dequeued by the consumer while preserving arrival order. The system must present operations for the producer to send a message and for the consumer to receive a message; these operations may be concurrent but must respect FIFO semantics.

State the safety properties: messages are never duplicated or lost while in the buffer, the relative order of messages delivered to the consumer matches the order they were enqueued by the producer, and the consumer never receives a message that was never sent. If bounded capacity is used, the producer must be blocked or prevented from enqueueing when the buffer is full.

State the liveness properties: every message enqueued by the producer is eventually delivered to the consumer (unless the producer or consumer halts), and if the producer continues to enqueue and the consumer continues to dequeue, progress continues without starvation. Allow specification of fairness constraints to ensure progress. Also include an invariant representing buffer contents and an assertion that the sequence of delivered messages is a subsequence of the sequence of sent messages preserving order.
