Specify a formal model of a replicated state machine service that uses leader-based consensus to replicate an ordered log of client commands across a fixed set of servers. The model should capture servers that can act as followers, candidates, or leaders, and clients that submit commands and open/close sessions. The system must capture unreliable asynchronous message delivery (messages may be delayed, duplicated, or dropped) and servers that may restart or experience timeouts which drive leader election.

The model should require that:
- Commands proposed by clients are appended to a server’s local log and are eventually replicated to a quorum of servers before being considered committed.
- Once a command at a given log index becomes committed on any server, every server’s log entry at that index that is committed must contain the same command (safety: committed entries are consistent across servers).
- Leaders only replicate entries that are consistent with peers’ logs (no divergent committed history).
- Election rules ensure at most one leader at a given term and leaders progress terms via elections triggered by timeouts and votes from a majority.
- Clients receive responses only for commands that become committed and applied in order.

Express the model as a state-machine specification (initial states and a Next relation) and state invariants/assertions encoding the safety properties above; liveness properties (e.g., eventual leader election and command commitment under reliable periods) may be included separately.
