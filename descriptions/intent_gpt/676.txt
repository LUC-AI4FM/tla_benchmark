Specify a concurrent system that generates and manipulates finite sequences of boolean values under the following behavior and correctness goals.

Model a component that repeatedly produces sequences of booleans and makes them available to consumers. Each produced sequence may vary in length and content, but must be a finite sequence of True/False values. Producers may append elements to the current sequence, reset it to an empty sequence, or publish the current sequence to a consumer. Multiple producers and consumers may operate concurrently.

The system must guarantee:
- Safety: Every published sequence is a finite boolean sequence; no publication exposes partial, malformed, or non-boolean data.
- Atomicity of publication: When a sequence is published, the consumer observes the sequence as a single, indivisible value reflecting some complete state of the producer’s sequence at the moment of publication.
- Consistency under concurrency: No two concurrent modifications and a publication can interleave so that a published sequence contains a mix of elements from two logically distinct production steps (no torn updates).
- Liveness: If a producer repeatedly attempts to publish, then eventually some publication succeeds (no indefinite starvation of publishes).
- Boundedness: The length of any stored sequence never grows unbounded unless producers continue to append; the model must be able to express and check invariants about sequence length limits.

Include properties expressing that sequences can be compared, that operations preserve well-formedness, and that consumer-read sequences reflect a history consistent with the producers’ operations. Provide the ability to assert and check these invariants and liveness properties.
