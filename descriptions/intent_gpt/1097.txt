Specify a TLA+ model of a distributed mutual‑exclusion algorithm in which N processes coordinate solely by exchanging timestamped messages over pairwise FIFO channels. The model should describe asynchronous message passing where processes have local logical clocks that advance on sending and on receiving messages, and where messages are of three kinds: request (carrying the sender’s timestamp), acknowledgement, and release. Each process may request access to a single shared critical section, must broadcast its request to all other processes, collect acknowledgements from every peer, and defer entry until it can determine by timestamps (and tie‑breaking by process id) that its request has priority over every other outstanding request. While in the critical section a process must prevent others from entering; on exit it must broadcast a release so others update their view.

Require the following correctness properties:
- Safety (mutual exclusion): at most one process may be in the critical section at any time.
- Progress liveness under fair delivery assumptions: a requesting process that continues to send/receive messages and whose messages are eventually delivered should eventually enter the critical section provided no permanent crashes occur.
- Nonblocking acknowledgements: receiving a request eventually causes an acknowledgement to be sent (subject to fair scheduling).
- Logical clock monotonicity and message causality: clocks increase correctly so timestamps reflect causal order and are used consistently to compare requests.
- Channels are FIFO between each pair and bounded to keep model checking finite.

Do not commit to specific variable names, program text, or a particular coding of broadcast; model the protocol requirements and invariants abstractly.
