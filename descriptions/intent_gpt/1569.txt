Specify a formal model of Lamport’s Bakery algorithm for N concurrent processes contending for a single critical section. The model should represent each process executing the standard non-blocking numbered-lock protocol: choosing a ticket number based on a scan of others’ numbers, announcing the ticket, waiting until no other process with a smaller ticket (or with equal ticket and lower process id) is contending, entering the critical section, and then releasing the ticket. The model must allow arbitrary interleavings and asynchrony, and must include a parameter for the number of processes and a bound on ticket values for model checking.

Require the following correctness properties be specified to verify the algorithm:

- Mutual exclusion: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time.
- Liveness (weak fairness): if a process repeatedly tries to enter the protocol, it should eventually enter the critical section (or state explicitly which fairness assumptions are required).
- Safety of ticket-management: ticket values should remain within the provided bound, and processes should eventually reset their ticket to zero after leaving the critical section.
- Absence of deadlock: the system should not reach a state where no process can make progress though some are trying to enter.

Also require invariants to capture the intended comparison ordering (tickets and process ids) used to break ties, and allow inspection or assertion of intermediate states (choosing flag, read/scan progress) to aid verification.
