Specify a model of a simple parameterized component instance interacting with an environment that can toggle a single binary state. The system should consist of one global binary variable that the environment initializes to 0 and repeatedly flips between 0 and 1 on each step. Separately, model an independent component that is instantiated with the global variable as both of its inputs; the component itself should expose three logical properties: an initial condition, a safety predicate (an invariant over states and traces), and a liveness predicate (a fairness or eventuality requirement). The specification should assert that the environment’s initialization and step relation hold for the global variable, and that the step relation is weakly fair for that variable. The model must check whether the instantiated component’s initial condition, safety predicate, and liveness predicate hold when interpreted in the context of the global environment. In particular, require proofs or assertions that the component’s initial condition is satisfied, that its safety predicate holds on all reachable states, and that its liveness predicate either holds or is refuted under the given environment behavior. Also include an explicit separate assertion that the component’s invariant and its negation of some other global property are maintained, so that both a positive and a negative property about the instance are examined.
