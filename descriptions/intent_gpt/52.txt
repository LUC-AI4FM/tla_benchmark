Specify a model of a system that manages two integer parameters representing a bounded interval [min, max], where the bounds are declared as global constants set before execution. The model should treat these bounds as immutable during execution but must require that they satisfy the invariant min ≤ max at initialization. The system need not perform any state transitions affecting those constants; instead it should allow arbitrary (possibly no-op) behavior while preserving the constraint on the constants throughout execution. The specification must explicitly require that every reachable state respects the relation between the two constants (min ≤ max) and that this relation is entailed by the initialization conditions imposed on the constants. The correctness properties to check are (1) that the invariant min ≤ max holds in all reachable states, and (2) that the initialization constraints on the constants are sufficient to guarantee the invariant without depending on runtime actions. The model should be suitable for verification tools that accept separate initialization of constants and can detect whether constraints propagate from the constant initialization to the global invariant.
