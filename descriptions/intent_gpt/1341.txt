Specify a distributed lock service that grants exclusive access to a shared resource among a set of clients in a message-passing system. Model clients that may request and release the lock, a single authoritative coordinator that grants or denies requests, and unreliable FIFO channels between clients and coordinator where messages may be lost but not duplicated or reordered. The system must ensure mutual exclusion: at most one client holds the lock at any time. It must guarantee safety under all message-loss scenarios and guarantee eventual progress (liveness) under a weak fairness condition: if a client continuously and persistently retries a request and the coordinator remains non-faulty, then that client eventually either acquires the lock or learns that some other client holds it. Support concurrent request attempts and ensure absence of deadlock: if some client holds the lock and eventually releases it, and there are pending requests, then some pending requester will eventually acquire it.

Specify client behaviors for requesting, waiting for grant, using the resource, and releasing the lock. Specify coordinator behavior to serialize requests and issue grants or denials. Specify the semantics of message loss and retry. Stipulate invariants for mutual exclusion and absence of loss-induced safety violations, and state temporal properties expressing eventual acquisition or notification under fairness assumptions.
