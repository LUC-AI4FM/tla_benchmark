Specify a formal model of a language-level expression-manipulation and naming system that tracks positions inside nested expressions and enforces well-formedness of structured expressions. The model should represent composite values (tuples, records, sequences, functions, conditionals, CASE/LET constructs, and lambda/operator abstractions) and support referring to their positional subexpressions by a path of selectors. It must permit querying and comparing such subexpressions, including nested indexes, field names, lambda/operator bodies, and subterms produced by constructs like EXCEPT, WF_/SF_/temporal wrappers, and choice/quantifier bindings.

The system must ensure that selecting a valid positional path yields the correct subexpression and that ill-formed or out-of-range paths are rejected. It must respect scopes: LET/IN and local operator bindings, quantifier/choice variable scopes, and record/tuple field semantics. It must model evaluation contexts for IF/CASE, operator application, and EXCEPT/UNION updates so selection into the resulting expressions is meaningful. Preserve identity relations where different syntactic constructs yield equal values but distinct positional structure. Key correctness properties: selector soundness (select(path, expr) equals expected subterm when path valid), selector safety (no valid selector returns an undefined value), scope preservation (selections cannot escape local bindings), and compositionality (selection distributes over constructed expressions in a predictable way). Provide invariants to check these properties over representative expressions.
