Model a simple leader-election and single-decree consensus protocol for a fixed set of peer processes. The model should capture processes sending and receiving vote requests and votes, accumulating sets of voting peers, becoming leaders when they gather a quorum, and then deciding a single value. The system must allow asynchronous message delivery (messages can be sent and later received), and each process may vote at most once. Quorums are fixed subsets of processes (majorities); a process becomes a leader only after it has evidence of votes from an entire quorum. Once a process becomes leader it may decide a value; decisions are irrevocable for that process. The specification must enforce safety: no two processes may decide different values (i.e., all decided values across processes must be identical). The model should also represent message loss/duplication possibilities consistent with asynchronous networks (sending need not guarantee eventual delivery). Do not fix a particular communication ordering or encoding; instead specify the abstract message events (send request, send vote, receive vote), vote bookkeeping, leader-establishment via quorum, and decision issuance. Provide initial conditions and a transition relation describing these events and the invariants to check, focusing on the correctness property above.
