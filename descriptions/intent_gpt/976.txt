Specify a concurrent system of N identical processes arranged in a ring. Each process executes two steps in sequence: first it writes the value 1 into a local shared register associated with itself; then it reads the register of its left neighbor and stores that value into its own local result register. All processes start with both registers set to 0 and with every process ready to execute its first step. Processes proceed independently and may interleave arbitrarily; once a process completes its second step it is considered terminated and does no further actions. Model nondeterministic scheduling that may run any enabled step of any nonterminated process at any time, and allow the system to stutter after all processes terminate.

Require the specification to guarantee the following safety property: whenever every process has terminated, at least one process’s result register equals 1. Also require a type invariant ensuring registers only contain 0 or 1 and that each process’s control state is one of the three states (before first step, between steps, terminated). Additionally, require a termination liveness condition stating that it is possible for all processes to eventually reach the terminated state under fair scheduling (used for reasoning but not required as part of the safety proof). The model should be suitable for proving the safety property by establishing an inductive invariant that implies the postcondition.
