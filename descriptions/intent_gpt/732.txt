Specify a TLA+ model of a consensus protocol that implements the Fast Paxos algorithm (consensus with fast and classic rounds). The model should describe proposers, coordinators, and acceptors communicating by asynchronous message passing with possible message loss and node failures/repairs. It must allow two kinds of rounds: fast rounds (which permit direct proposals to acceptors) and classic rounds (coordinator-driven). Quorums are given per round and must satisfy that any two quorums intersect, and any classic quorum intersects every pair of fast quorums. Coordinators may be chosen dynamically; some coordinators and acceptors may be repeatedly “good” (non-failed) while others may fail and recover arbitrarily. Proposers may submit proposals of values; learners may learn a value when a quorum of acceptors have accepted it.

The specification must guarantee safety: only values that were proposed may be learned (nontriviality), and at most one value can ever be learned (consistency). It must capture the Fast Paxos value-selection rule used by coordinators when deciding what value to propose in phase 2a based on previous acceptor replies, including the special-case fast-round behavior and the O4 condition for tie-breaking.

The model must also express realistic liveness assumptions and fairness: if a sufficiently stable coordinator and quorum of acceptors remain non-faulty and continue to act, then some proposed value will eventually be learned (progress). Do not prescribe a particular implementation; describe time, messages, and local state abstractly so safety and progress properties can be checked.
