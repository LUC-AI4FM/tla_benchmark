Specify a formal TLA+ model of a multi-elevator building control system that coordinates several cars and multiple people requesting service across a fixed set of floors. The model should describe how people decide destinations, make elevator calls, board and exit cars, and how cars move, open and close doors, and accept button presses. It must capture asynchronous interleaving of actions and allow nondeterministic choices for scheduling, subject to fairness assumptions that ensure progress of enabled actions.

Require the model to guarantee the following correctness properties:

- Type safety: all state components always conform to their intended domains (people locations are either floors or a car, destinations are valid floors, elevator attributes like floor, direction, door state and pressed buttons are well formed, and the set of active calls is a subset of valid call descriptors).
- Safety invariants: no elevator reports a button for a floor unless some passenger inside wants that floor; people inside a car are only carried in a direction consistent with their destination; active elevator calls correspond to actual waiting people (no ghost calls).
- Liveness guarantees: every active call is eventually serviced by some car reaching that floor and opening doors; any person who has pressed a call and is waiting eventually reaches their destination.
- No deadlock: cars that can move, open doors, or respond to calls may eventually do so under reasonable fairness assumptions.

Do not prescribe a particular dispatching or optimization algorithm, but allow expressing a dispatch policy that chooses a car to serve a call (for example, preferring closest stationary or already-approaching cars). The model should separate environment actions (people creating calls and choosing destinations) from elevator actions and include fairness constraints so temporal properties about eventual service can be checked.
