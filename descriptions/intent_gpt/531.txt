Specify a distributed session-management system that allocates and tracks a bounded pool of reusable resources (sessions) among multiple clients. The model should allow a finite set of clients to request, acquire, use, and release sessions drawn from a fixed universe of session identifiers, where each session can be held by at most one client at a time and clients may concurrently attempt to obtain sessions. Include a mechanism for clients to signal completion and for sessions to return to the pool for reuse. The specification must capture nondeterministic request arrival and nondeterministic scheduling of grant, use, and release steps.

Required safety properties:
- Mutual exclusion: no two clients may hold the same session simultaneously.
- Validity: clients may only acquire session identifiers that belong to the allowed set; no allocations outside the fixed universe occur.
- No duplication: the set of held sessions is always a subset of the universe with unique ownership.

Required liveness properties:
- Progress (weak fairness): any client that repeatedly requests a session and remains enabled should eventually acquire one if sessions are available.
- Reusability: sessions released by clients eventually become available for other clients.

Also specify invariants that preserve the cardinality bounds (total allocated sessions never exceeds the universe size) and allow checking of deadlock freedom under fair scheduling.
