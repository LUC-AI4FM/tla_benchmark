Specify a formal TLA+ model of a transaction commit service implemented atop a consensus protocol. The model should describe a distributed system of clients and a set of acceptors (or replicas) cooperating to decide whether to commit or abort individual transactions. It must capture the interaction of a coordinator (or proposer) and learners that propose and learn a single final decision per transaction via a consensus instance; multiple independent transactions may be decided concurrently. The model must require safety properties: (1) Agreement — no two non-faulty learners decide different outcomes for the same transaction; (2) Validity — if every participant initially votes to commit, the final decision may be commit; if any honest participant votes to abort, the outcome can be abort; (3) Integrity — once a learner decides a commit or abort, that decision is final and does not change; and (4) Nontriviality — decisions are only commit or abort (no spurious values). It must also express liveness under suitable assumptions: if a majority of acceptors remain responsive and messages are eventually delivered, then every transaction proposed by a correct coordinator will eventually be decided. The specification should abstract from low-level implementation details and instead state the roles, message types, state transitions, and the invariants/temporal properties that ensure the commit service refines the abstract transaction-commit specification.
