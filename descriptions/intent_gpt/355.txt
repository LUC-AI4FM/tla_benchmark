Specify a formal model of a static verification harness that exercises tuple, Cartesian-product, function, and sequence constructions and checks basic set and membership properties. The model should represent a simple state that does not change over time and include an initialization that establishes the state. The goal is to assert and report the truth of a collection of independent logical tests concerning:

- Cartesian products of finite sets and of Int with function or sequence spaces, and the expected cardinalities of such products.
- Membership and non-membership of specific tuples (including nested tuples) in these Cartesian products.
- Membership of plain tuples in product spaces built from Int and in sequence spaces restricted by comprehension predicates.
- Equality and inequality comparisons between finite functions (records) and tuples obtained from function-value mappings.
- Construction and indexing of sequences and records, and extraction of tuple components for set membership checks.

For each test, the model must deterministically check the condition and provide a clear pass/fail outcome without modifying the state. The correctness properties to guarantee are that all set cardinalities, membership relations, and equality/inequality checks reflect standard Zermelo–Fraenkel set-theoretic semantics for tuples, sequences, functions, and Cartesian products over the integers and finite domains. The model should be suitable for exhaustive model checking of these properties.
