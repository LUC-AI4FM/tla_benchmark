Specify a formal TLA+ model of a simplified ERC-20–like token contract and the environment that interacts with it. The model should capture a fixed small set of accounts, balances, and allowances and should support the usual token operations: transferring tokens from one account to another, approving an allowance for a spender, and transferring tokens on behalf of an owner using an allowance. The model must allow asynchronous submission of transactions (a set or queue of pending transactions) and nondeterministic scheduling of their execution, including the ability to drop, reorder, or duplicate pending transactions to exercise concurrency and network unreliability.

The specification must enforce conservation of total supply (no minting or burning unless explicitly modeled), nonnegativity of all balances and allowances, and that no transfer succeeds if the sender’s balance or the allowance is insufficient. It must ensure that approvals update allowances only in well-defined ways (e.g., overwrite or safe-update policy as chosen) and that transfers-on-behalf decrement allowances appropriately. The model should also express safety properties: absence of unauthorized balance increases, preservation of invariants under concurrent transactions, and eventual consistency properties under fair scheduling (e.g., pending transactions either execute or are forever ignored). Include temperature-check properties such as absence of double-spend and that a successful transfer changes exactly the two involved balances by the same amount. Provide clear correctness properties to be checked by a model checker (invariants and temporal properties), but do not prescribe a particular implementation strategy.
