Specify a concurrent state machine that models a simple two-part counter process whose purpose is to demonstrate and avoid deadlock while performing bounded incremental updates and a toggling flag. The system has a numeric counter that must start at zero and may be incremented repeatedly but never exceed a fixed maximum bound. In parallel, a boolean flag alternates its value on each step. The model should allow only steps that simultaneously advance the counter by exactly one and flip the flag; no other joint transitions are permitted. The specification must capture initial conditions, the permitted transition relation, and the fairness assumptions needed to ensure progress until the counter reaches its maximum. The correctness properties to prove are: (1) safety — the counter is always between its initial value and the given maximum inclusive, and the flag is always a boolean; (2) liveness — from any reachable state where the counter is below the maximum, the system can eventually take a step (no permanent stuttering or deadlock) and will eventually reach the maximum counter value; and (3) invariants — the counter and flag change only according to the allowed transition (counter increases by one and flag flips). Also assert that once the maximum is reached, further transitions are disallowed. Model must be abstract (no implementation details) and suitable for model checking.
