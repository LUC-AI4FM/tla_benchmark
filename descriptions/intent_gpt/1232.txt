Specify a model of a programming-language-level string datatype and its basic sequence operations, suitable for verifying string-manipulating algorithms. The model should treat strings as finite sequences of characters and provide operations for concatenation, length, and equality. It must support representing and reasoning about escape sequences and characters that require escaping (such as backslash, quote, and newline) so that concatenation and length behave as expected when escape characters are present.

The specification should state the expected semantics: concatenating two strings yields a string whose elements are the concatenation of the two character sequences; the length operation returns the number of characters in the sequence; and equality compares sequences elementwise. Include examples of properties that must hold for all strings, for example associativity of concatenation, that concatenating two literal sequences produces the expected combined literal, and that length(composed string) equals the sum of lengths of components even when escape characters are embedded. The model must allow asserting and checking concrete equalities and lengths involving strings that include escape characters, ensuring that escape syntax is treated only as representation-level detail and does not alter the underlying character sequence semantics.
