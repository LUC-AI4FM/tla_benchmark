Model a small language-feature verifier that checks scoping and name-capture safety for local definitions in a functional/specification language supporting nested local bindings and operator abstraction. The model must represent declarations that can introduce local names, nested local definitions, and operator expressions that can be instantiated or inlined into other scopes. The verifier must ensure that whenever a local definition is introduced or an operator is inlined/instantiated into a different syntactic context, no accidental name collisions or variable capture occur; each occurrence of a bound identifier must resolve to the correct binding according to lexical scope. The model should allow multiple independent instantiations of the same operator into the same outer context and show that they remain distinct (no interference) even when they declare identically named locals. The key correctness properties are: (1) preservation of lexical binding—variables reference the intended binding after expansion/instantiation; (2) avoidance of name collisions—distinct bindings with the same identifier in different nested/local scopes do not conflict; and (3) compositionality—inlining or instantiation does not change semantics of the operator or outer context. Also model and check that renaming (alpha-conversion) is applied when necessary to restore these properties.
