Specify a formal model of a caching mechanism for constant subexpressions evaluated during specification elaboration. The model should represent a global environment in which certain expressions are recognized as constant (i.e., their values do not depend on system state or execution), and where evaluating any such distinct constant expression increments a global evaluation counter. The system must guarantee that each distinct constant expression is evaluated at most once during the entire run of the specification (not once per reference), and that separate constant expressions are tracked independently so caching one does not suppress evaluation of another. The model should allow multiple constant expressions that may be syntactically or semantically related (for example, one defined from another) and must ensure correct evaluation counts even when some constants are referenced in assumptions, initial conditions, and transition conditions. Include properties that (1) each constant expression that is referenced anywhere in the spec contributes exactly one increment to the global counter, (2) unrelated constants produce independent increments, and (3) no extra increments occur due to re-referencing cached values. Also require assertions that check membership of particular values in the computed constants to detect accidental value-sharing. The model should be amenable to model checking to validate the counting and caching behavior.
