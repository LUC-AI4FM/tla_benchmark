Specify a formal TLA+ model of basic set-theoretic and functional mathematics focused on finite functions, injections, surjections, bijections, function restriction, range and inverse, and a generic fold operation over finite index sets. The model should describe abstract sets and total functions between them, define what it means for a function to be injective, surjective, or bijective, and express existence predicates for injections, surjections, and bijections. It must capture relationships among these concepts: restriction preserves function typing and values on the restricted domain; range equals the codomain of a surjection; inverse maps range elements back to preimages; injections and surjections imply the usual one-to-one or onto properties; the inverse of a bijection is a bijection; transitivity of composing injections, surjections, and bijections; and that an injection into a subset can yield a bijection under suitable conditions (Cantor–Bernstein–Schroeder). Include properties about existence: reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity of existence predicates, how injections and surjections between finite intervals correspond to integer inequalities, and uniqueness of cardinality for finite sets represented by bijections with initial segments of the naturals. Also specify an abstract fold operator over finite index sets: its base-case behavior, reduction step, typing preservation, equality for equal elementwise functions, and correctness under associative/commutative operators. State all theorems as invariants or temporal properties suitable for mechanized proof in TLA+.
