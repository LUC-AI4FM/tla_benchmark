Model a library of bitwise operations over non‑negative integers (natural numbers including zero). The specification should treat numbers as infinite binary sequences with finitely many 1 bits and define the standard bitwise operators (AND, OR, XOR, NOT) and a logical right shift. The model must accept arbitrary non‑negative integer operands and produce results equivalent to applying the corresponding Boolean function independently to each binary digit: AND returns 1 only where both operands have a 1 bit; OR returns 1 where at least one operand has a 1 bit; XOR returns 1 where operands differ; NOT flips each bit within the fixed finite support of the operand representation used; logical right shift moves bits toward less significant positions, inserting zeros into the most significant positions.

Specify the semantics declaratively (no particular algorithm or recursion is required). Ensure termination and totality for all natural‑number inputs. State and prove the following correctness properties: commutativity and associativity where applicable (AND, OR, XOR), distributive laws between AND/OR and XOR as appropriate, idempotence and absorption laws for OR/AND, involutive property of NOT (applying NOT twice yields the original value within the chosen bit‑width), correctness of shift with respect to division by powers of two (logical right shift equals integer division by 2^k with truncation toward zero), and that bitwise operations correspond to per‑bit Boolean functions (per‑bit truth tables). Include invariants asserting that results remain non‑negative integers and that bit-length bounds are preserved under each operation.
