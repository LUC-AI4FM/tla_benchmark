Specify a concurrent-state system that maintains a set of distinct integer items drawn from the range 1..N (for a fixed natural N). The system must initialize to any subset of 1..N whose element-sum equals exactly N. After initialization, the system may nondeterministically update the set at discrete steps, but only to subsets whose element-sum is strictly smaller than the current set’s sum. Model the state as just the current subset of 1..N.

The specification should assert and prove the invariant that twice the sum of the current set is always at most N*(N+1) (i.e., 2 * sum(currentSet) ≤ N*(N+1)). The model must permit any nondeterministic choices allowed by the initialization and update rules, and it must check that from every allowed initial state and through every allowed transition the invariant is preserved.

Include safety properties: (1) all states are subsets of 1..N, (2) all transitions reduce the numeric sum strictly, and (3) the invariant above holds in the initial states and is preserved by every transition. Do not assume any particular algorithm for choosing subsets; treat choice as nondeterministic. Provide arguments (or machine-checkable proof obligations) ensuring there are no transitions to states violating the constraints.
