Specify a simple stateful system that maintains a single immutable record whose purpose is to identify a system variant. The system must initialize to a record that contains exactly one field, a string-valued tag with a specific required value (for example, the tag value "B"). After initialization, the system must have a transition relation that does not change the record (i.e., the record remains constant forever). The model should permit an arbitrary internal computation or local step to be represented, but that computation must not modify the record. Formally state the initial condition, the allowed steps (including a step that may compute an internal result but leaves the record unchanged), and the state-space closure (stuttering steps that do not modify state are allowed). The correctness properties to guarantee are: (1) the record equals the required tag value in all reachable states, (2) the record never acquires additional fields or loses the tag field, and (3) all transitions preserve the record (no step may change any part of it). Also specify that the system is finite-state and that invariants are to be checked for all reachable states.
