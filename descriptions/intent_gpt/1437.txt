Specify a model of a simple algorithm language construct that checks well-formedness of a program containing a scoped binding with an explicit body. The system should represent a program element that introduces a new scoped variable initialized to an expression, then requires a nonempty sequence of statements as the body of that scope. The model must accept programs that correctly provide an initialization expression and a nonempty body and must reject programs where the scoped binding is missing its body or where the body is syntactically empty. The model should capture the syntactic structure of the construct, the scoping rules for the bound variable, and the requirement that control flow enters and leaves the scope exactly once in correct programs. It must assert invariants that the bound variable is initialized before use inside the scope, and that leaving the scope removes the binding. The specification must also express error states for ill-formed programs (missing body, multiple bodies, or uninitialized use) and provide properties that such error states are unreachable from well-formed starts. Finally, include LTL properties or temporal assertions ensuring that every execution either completes the scope body or is flagged as a syntactic error, and that no execution silently proceeds past a missing body.
