Specify a simple cyclic hour clock that continuously advances by one hour at each step, wrapping from 12 back to 1. The model should treat hours as the integers 1..12 and must allow only states with an hour value in that range. The system should always start in a valid hour state and every state transition should increment the hour by exactly one, with 12 followed by 1. Non-determinism is not required beyond allowing the single deterministic next-hour transition at each step. The specification must capture the notion of infinite execution (the clock runs forever) and must forbid any transition that produces an hour outside 1..12 or that changes the hour by an amount other than +1 modulo 12.

Key correctness properties to assert: (1) Invariance of the hour domain: every reachable state has an hour value in 1..12. (2) Transition correctness: every step increments the hour in the required wrap-around manner. (3) Initialization: all initial states satisfy the hour-domain invariant. Optionally, express that from any state the sequence of future hours cycles through the twelve values repeatedly. Provide the model and properties so they can be discharged by temporal reasoning tools.
