Specify a parameterized distributed counter service used by a set of processes that must always keep local counts below a fixed upper bound. Model a collection of processes that increment and decrement their own nonnegative integer counters and may read other processes' counters. The service must enforce that no counter ever becomes negative and that no counter ever reaches or exceeds a given maximum value (a strict upper bound provided as a parameter). The model must allow arbitrary interleavings of independent increments, decrements, and reads, and must permit fairness for progress: if a process repeatedly attempts a legal increment or decrement, it should eventually succeed unless doing so would violate the bounds.

Capture the initial condition where every process starts with a nonnegative counter strictly below the maximum. Specify actions for attempting an increment, attempting a decrement, and performing a read of another process's counter. Ensure that attempted increments are disabled when they would reach or exceed the maximum, and that decrements are disabled when they would go negative. Express the key correctness properties: safety properties (no counter ever negative, no counter ever >= maximum, all counters remain integers) and liveness properties (enabled increments or decrements are eventually executed under fairness). Also require invariants that relate local operations to the global bound and that the bound is a fixed natural-number parameter.
