Specify a formal model of a simple asynchronous single-writer single-reader interface that transfers values from a producer to a consumer without assuming synchronous handshaking. The model should represent the state of a single data slot that may hold one of a fixed set of data values, and two independent unbounded nonnegative counters used by the producer and the consumer to indicate readiness and acknowledgement. The system should allow the producer to write a datum and advance its ready counter, and allow the consumer to read the datum and advance its ack counter; communications may be delayed arbitrarily and actions may interleave in any order. Require that the producer may always eventually attempt to publish a new value (no permanent blocking by the interface), and that the consumer may always eventually observe and consume a published value when it is delivered. Specify safety properties ensuring that the consumer never observes a datum that was never written, and that the producer and consumer counters remain nonnegative integers and are only changed by their respective agents. Specify liveness properties: eventual delivery (every written value is eventually read unless continuously overridden by newer writes) and progress (neither agent can be permanently prevented from making its next counter increment). Also require that if the consumer acknowledges a write, the system eventually stops treating that write as pending (i.e., handshaking completes). The model should be parameterized by the finite set of possible data values.
