Specify a formal model of a multiset (bag) data structure extension and its test harness. The model should describe an abstract bag ADT that supports creating an empty bag, inserting elements, removing elements (one occurrence), querying the count of an element, and producing the bag's multiset representation. Additionally, include extended operations or invariants that restrict or annotate elements (for example, tagging, timestamps, or auxiliary counters) used by extended tests. Model concurrent clients that perform sequences of these operations nondeterministically to exercise edge cases such as repeated insertions, removals of absent elements, and interleaved operations by multiple actors.

The specification must state the intended semantics of each operation (preconditions, postconditions, and effects on the multiset), and must capture legal behaviors when operations operate on elements not present or when removal requests exceed counts. Define correctness properties to verify: (1) safety — the multiset representation always reflects the results of completed operations consistent with their specified semantics; (2) linearizability — every operation appears to take effect at some atomic point between invocation and response, preserving a sequential history equivalent to a legal sequence of bag operations; (3) absence of lost updates or phantom elements under concurrency; and (4) invariants relating any auxiliary extension metadata to the underlying multiset (e.g., counts nonnegative, tags preserved). Provide liveness goals demonstrating progress for nonblocking clients.
