Specify a formal model of a concurrent mutual-exclusion algorithm for N processes that repeatedly alternate between noncritical work, an entry protocol, a critical section, and an exit protocol. The model should capture each process’s local control state and any shared synchronization state needed to enforce exclusive access. The system must guarantee safety: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time. It must also guarantee progress and liveness properties: every process that continually attempts to enter the critical section should eventually be able to enter it (no starvation), and no execution may deadlock with all contending processes permanently blocked before entering the critical section. The model should allow N to be chosen as a parameter and should represent fairness assumptions clearly (weak/strong fairness for process steps or specific actions) needed to ensure liveness. Include specifications of initial states and legal transitions for individual processes and shared state, but do not prescribe an implementation strategy. Provide invariants and temporal properties to check safety and liveness, and make explicit what fairness assumptions are required for the liveness claims.
