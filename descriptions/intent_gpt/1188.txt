Specify a formal model of reasoning about finite functions, function domains, and dependent function spaces over finite sets and tuples. The model should represent finite index sets, finite ranges, and functions defined by expressions over those indices; it must allow construction of function spaces (sets of all functions from a finite domain to a finite codomain) and evaluation of expressions that build individual functions. The system must support:

- Computing the domain of a function expression and deciding membership of particular values in that domain.
- Determining cardinalities of domains and of finite function spaces, including nested/comprehension-defined spaces (e.g., sets of functions filtered by a predicate on their values).
- Constructing and testing records/tuples and verifying their membership in spaces of fixed-length sequences or maps from a range of integers to naturals.
- Handling tuple and Cartesian-product domains (cross products of index sets) for multi-argument function definitions.
- Reasoning about equality of function spaces and equality of explicitly constructed functions against comprehension-defined sets.
- Producing diagnostics/assertions when expectations about domain membership, cardinality, or inclusion fail.

The specification must state the correctness properties: domain computations must match the formal Cartesian-product semantics; cardinality calculations must equal the product of sizes where appropriate; membership checks must respect index and range types; and function-space enumerations and predicate filters must yield expected counts. The model should be finite-state (only finite sets) so it can be exhaustively checked.
