Model a system for breaking a single integer-weight stone into a fixed number of integer-weight pieces so that, using a two-pan balance scale, every integer target weight from 1 up to the total stone weight can be measured exactly. The model should treat the pieces as indistinguishable except by their weights and must allow placing any subset of pieces on either pan: a target weight is measured when the weight of pieces on the opposite pan equals the target plus the weight of pieces placed with the target. Require that the pieces’ weights sum to the total stone weight and that each piece weight is a positive integer. The specification must accept the total weight and the number of pieces as parameters and characterize all valid multisets of piece weights that enable measuring every integer weight in the full range.

Key correctness properties to guarantee:
- Completeness: for every integer target 1..total, there exist two disjoint subsets of pieces whose weights satisfy the balance equation described.
- Validity: all piece weights are positive integers and sum exactly to the total.
- Uniqueness considerations: equivalent solutions that differ only by reordering of identical-weight pieces should be treated as the same solution (specify canonical ordering or treat solutions as multisets).
- Soundness: any reported solution must in fact satisfy the balance condition for every target.

Additionally request that the specification enable exploration of which parameter pairs (total weight, piece count) are solvable and produce concrete example solutions for small values.
