Model a voucher issuance service coordinated by a transaction coordinator using a two‑phase commit pattern. The system involves a set of voucher issuers, a set of voucher holders, and a coordinator that decides whether to complete or abort an issuance. The model should abstractly represent the lifecycle of one or more voucher objects, the states of holders and issuers with respect to a single issuance round, and the coordinator’s knowledge of which participants have reported readiness.

Specify the allowed behaviors (not an implementation): participants may either signal they are prepared, or spontaneously decide to abort; the coordinator may receive prepared signals from participants; once the coordinator has received prepared signals from all participants it may decide to commit and broadcast a single commit message causing holders to obtain the voucher and issuers to mark it issued; alternatively the coordinator may decide to abort and broadcast an abort message causing participants to abort. Messages may be broadcast and can be received by multiple participants; model message presence and receipt abstractly.

Require these correctness properties: type invariants on all state variables; safety that no holder ends up holding a voucher while any issuer has aborted, and no issuer ends up marked issued while any holder has aborted (i.e., no conflicting holder/issuer endpoint states); once a voucher is marked valid its lifecycle advances consistently; and the two‑phase commit protocol preserves these invariants (i.e., coordinator decisions occur only when appropriate preparedness conditions hold and all participants eventually see the coordinator’s decision).
