Specify a formal model of a simple mutual-exclusion protocol for N identical processes that repeatedly attempt to enter and leave a single critical section. The model should represent each process cycling through a noncritical region, a two-step entry protocol, the critical section, and termination. The system must maintain a shared integer counter that is incremented when a process completes its critical section and decremented or otherwise constrained so that the counter stays within 0..N.

The specification must guarantee safety and liveness properties:
- Safety (mutual exclusion): At most one process may be inside the critical section at any time.
- Safety (counter consistency): When all processes have finished (reached termination), the shared counter equals N; at all times the counter value remains within the allowed range 0..N.
- Type and domain invariants: Every process’s local control state and any per-process local variable must always belong to their declared finite domains.

Liveness requirements:
- Progress: Any nonfaulty process that repeatedly attempts to enter must eventually either enter the critical section or terminate (no permanent starvation for active processes).
- Termination reachability: It must be possible for every process to eventually reach the termination state under fair scheduling.

Model should allow an arbitrary fixed number of processes as a parameter and express properties universally over processes and relevant ordered pairs of distinct processes. Do not prescribe an implementation; specify the interface, state-space, and the invariants and liveness properties to be checked.
