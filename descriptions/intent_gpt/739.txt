Specify a system that enumerates and verifies solutions for a constrained combinatorial puzzle: a finite search space of candidate configurations must be generated and a predicate must test whether a candidate satisfies the puzzle constraints. The model should express the domain of candidates (a finite set built from small integers and simple arithmetic expressions) and a decision procedure that determines which candidates are valid solutions. The system must compute one or more canonical summaries of the solution set (for example, an aggregate numeric result and a small vector of derived quantities) and expose them as constant results of the specification.

The model must guarantee termination of the search (the search space is finite) and must ensure soundness and completeness: every reported solution satisfies the predicate, and every candidate that satisfies the predicate is reported. It must also guarantee that the computed summaries correspond exactly to the set of found solutions (no mismatch between reported solutions and summary values). The specification should support simple printing/logging of the computed constants for inspection, but the correctness properties must not depend on side effects. Include invariants that assert finiteness, correctness of the predicate evaluation, and consistency between solutions and summary outputs.
