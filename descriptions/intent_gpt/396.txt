Specify a formal TLA+ model of a multiset (bag) library and a small suite of unit-style properties that such a library must satisfy. The model should treat a bag as a finite map from elements to positive natural counts, and must support construction, inspection, and algebraic operations on bags without prescribing any particular implementation.

The specification must include operations to:
- construct an empty bag and a bag from a set (one copy of each element);
- test membership (an element appears at least once) and obtain the number of copies of an element;
- compute the union (add multiplicities) and difference (subtract multiplicities, floor at zero) of two bags;
- form the union of a set of bags (pointwise sum) and map a function over the elements of a bag to produce a new bag (multiplicities aggregate by image);
- enumerate or characterize all subbags of a given bag and compute the total cardinality (sum of multiplicities) of a bag;
- compute the maximum multiplicity present in a bag.

State the correctness properties these operations must satisfy for all bags over a finite universe:
- semantics: bags correspond to finite domains mapping to positive integers, membership and copy-count agree with the domain and mapping;
- algebraic laws: union is commutative and associative; empty bag is identity for union; difference followed by union yields expected inclusions; difference removes at most the available copies and yields empty when subtracting a bag from itself;
- ordering: a subbag relation compares domains and multiplicities pointwise and is preserved by union and difference (monotonicity);
- mapping and bag-union operations preserve multiplicities (mapping aggregates counts of preimages; union over a set equals repeated pairwise union);
- cardinality and maximum multiplicity computations equal the sum and max of multiplicities respectively;
- combinatorial counts: the number of subbags equals the product over elements of (1 + multiplicity), and Set-to-Bag and Bag-to-Set conversions are inverses as expected.

Also include a small set of concrete example bags and boolean assertions (unit tests) that exercise the operations and check the above properties. The model should not constrain internal representations beyond the behavioral requirements.
