Specify a formal model of an asynchronous lossy FIFO channel used to transmit messages between a single sender and a single receiver. The model should describe nondeterministic communication over the channel: the sender may enqueue messages to send, the channel may nondeterministically drop messages, and the receiver may dequeue and accept messages. The system should allow unbounded attempts to send and receive, but the channel's buffer is modeled abstractly as a sequence of messages.

Require the following safety and liveness properties to be specified and proved:

- Type safety: every state must satisfy invariants that restrict the types of the sender's outgoing message, the receiver's last-delivered message, and the channel contents (e.g., channel is a sequence of well-formed messages).

- Message preservation property under reliable delivery: if the channel nondeterminism is restricted so that messages are not lost, then every message sent is eventually received in FIFO order.

- Loss-tolerant safety: even when drops are allowed, the receiver must only ever accept messages that were sent (no spurious messages) and must never violate FIFO order among delivered messages.

- Progress: when a message is present in the channel and the receiver is enabled, the receiver can eventually remove it (unless it is dropped first), and the sender can always attempt to send another message.

Also require a proof obligation that the type safety invariant holds initially and is preserved by all transitions of the system. Do not prescribe specific implementation actions; describe only the allowed behaviors and the invariants and liveness guarantees the specification must ensure.
