Specify a formal TLA+ model of a simple reliable asynchronous channel used for sending and receiving messages between two processes. The system should allow a sender to post messages into the channel and a receiver to take messages out. Messages may be of arbitrary values drawn from a fixed universe. The channel must allow at most one in-flight message at a time (i.e., either empty or holding a single message). The sender may attempt to send only when the channel is empty; the receiver may attempt to receive only when the channel holds a message. The model should capture nondeterministic interleaving of send and receive actions and allow send or receive attempts to be enabled or disabled independently. Specify an initial state with an empty channel. State the safety properties: the channel content is always either empty or a single value from the message universe, and the receiver never obtains a value that was not previously sent. State the liveness expectations informally: if a send is enabled and remains enabled, eventually the message can be placed into the channel; if a receive is enabled and remains enabled, eventually the receiver can remove the message. Provide an invariant expressing the type/sanity condition on channel contents and a temporal specification combining initialization and the allowed send/receive steps. The model should be suitable for mechanical verification of the invariant.
