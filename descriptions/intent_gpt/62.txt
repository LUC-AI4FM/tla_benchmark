Model a simple bounded counting process that repeatedly increments a counter until it reaches a preset positive bound. The specification should treat the bound as an external constant within a known integer range and the counter as state initialized to zero. The system must allow only a single atomic operation: if the current counter value is strictly less than the bound, it may be incremented by one; otherwise no further increments are possible. Require that the counter never decreases and never exceeds the bound. Require also that starting from the initial state, every reachable state satisfies that the counter is an integer between 0 and the bound inclusive. Additionally, specify liveness guarantees: from any state where the counter is less than the bound, it is possible (and under fairness assumptions it will eventually) reach a state where the counter has increased; and the process eventually stabilizes at the bound (i.e., it cannot increment past the bound and once equal to the bound it remains unchanged). Capture safety properties (nonnegativity, monotonicity, upper bound) and progress properties (possibility of increments while below bound, eventual convergence to bound under weak fairness). Keep the model minimal and abstract, avoiding implementation details.
