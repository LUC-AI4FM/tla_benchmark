Model a system that produces and records human-readable diagnostic messages during initialization and execution. The specification should describe processes that can emit two kinds of messages: unconditional notices that must always be produced when the system starts, and conditional trace messages that may be produced depending on internal or environmental boolean conditions. The system must guarantee that the unconditional notices are present in the recorded log for every execution trace (they are liveness/initialization obligations), while the conditional trace messages are optional but, when their enabling condition holds, they must appear at least once in the trace. The model should allow nondeterministic ordering of message emission relative to other startup activities, but must not lose or duplicate the unconditional notices. The recorded log should be a sequence that preserves message order as emitted. Specify invariants asserting that every behavior begins with the unconditional notice(s) and that the trace never contains duplicate instances of any unconditional notice. Specify liveness properties requiring eventual presence of the unconditional notice(s) and eventual presence of the conditional message whenever its guard becomes permanently true. Allow abstraction of message content to distinguish message types without fixing exact text. Include the possibility of concurrent components generating messages.
