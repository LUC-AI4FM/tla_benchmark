Specify an abstract mutual-exclusion lock for a system with a fixed small set of concurrent processes (at least two). Model processes that repeatedly alternate between a non‑critical section, acquiring the lock, executing a critical section, and releasing the lock, forever. The lock is binary (locked/unlocked). The model must allow an acquiring attempt to block until the lock becomes available and must allow a process that holds the lock to later release it.

The specification should state the allowed states and transitions at an abstract level (no code structure or implementation details). It must permit interleaving of steps of different processes and express fairness only insofar as it does not violate safety properties (liveness may be optional). The key correctness properties to guarantee are:

- Mutual exclusion: at no time may two distinct processes be simultaneously in the critical section.
- Lock consistency: whenever some process is in the critical section (or in the immediate state after acquiring but before release), the lock must be in the locked state; when no process holds the critical section the lock must be unlocked.
- Type and state invariants: the lock value must always be one of the two allowed states, and each process must always be in one of the specified control states (non‑critical, trying/acquiring, critical, releasing).

Also specify an initial condition: the lock is initially unlocked and all processes start in the non‑critical state. Optionally include a proof obligation or invariant schema that can be used to verify mutual exclusion and preservation of the type invariants under all transitions.
