Specify a simple type-checked record system and operations on records for a small statically-checked language. The model should treat records as mappings from field names to values with declared field domains, and it must distinguish between two kinds of record types: closed records that permit only the listed fields, and open/legacy records that may carry additional fields beyond those declared. The system must support construction of record instances, membership/type-checking of a record against a record type, and queries that test whether a given record conforms to a declared schema.

The specification should require that:
- A record conforms to a declared record type iff every declared field is present and its value lies in the declared domain; for closed records, it must also have no extra fields; for open/legacy records, extra fields are allowed.
- Field domains are arbitrary sets (including ranges or enumerations), and the model must permit heterogeneous value types across fields.
- Type-checking is decidable given finite domains and finite records.
- The model must expose counterexamples where a record fails to conform because of missing fields, wrong-type field values, or unexpected extra fields when the type is closed.
- The model must allow expressing tests that confirm allowed flexibility of legacy records (i.e., open records can have undeclared fields) and the stricter semantics of closed/new records.

State correctness properties declaratively: conformance, closure constraints, and preservation of declared domains under record construction and validation.
