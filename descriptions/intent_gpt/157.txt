Specify a formal model of the two-phase commit protocol (single coordinator, many participants) suitable for model checking. The model should describe an asynchronous message-passing system with one transaction coordinator and a fixed set of resource managers (participants). Participants can independently decide to vote Prepare or Abort; the coordinator collects votes and then decides to Commit only if all participants voted Prepare, otherwise it decides Abort. Communication is unreliable but delivered without duplication (messages may be delayed and reordered). The model must include explicit message channels and represent sent messages until delivered.

Require the model to capture these behaviors:
- Participants start in an initial state and may transition to a Prepared or Aborted local state by sending a vote to the coordinator.
- The coordinator collects votes, and upon deciding, broadcasts a global decision (Commit or Abort) to all participants.
- Participants receiving the decision transition to a final Committed or Aborted state accordingly.
- Either party may time out and take appropriate aborts consistent with the protocol.

State the key correctness properties to verify:
- Safety (agreement): No two participants may end in different final outcomes (one Committed and another Aborted).
- Commit only if unanimous Prepared: The coordinator may decide Commit only when it has received Prepare votes from every participant.
- Termination liveness assumptions may be specified separately (e.g., eventual delivery or reliable coordinator) but are not required for the safety properties.

Do not constrain implementation details (e.g., specific message labels, variable names, or a particular algorithmic encoding). Provide the protocol, allowed message types, participant and coordinator behaviors, and the invariants above that must hold in all reachable states.
