Specify a model of a simple 12-hour clock that advances its hour once on each tick. The model should represent the clock’s state as an hour value drawn from the set {1,2,...,12}. It must allow an initial state where the hour is any value in that set and repeatedly perform a deterministic transition that increments the hour by one, wrapping from 12 back to 1. The system should permit an infinite sequence of ticks and must maintain that every reachable state satisfies the invariant “hour is in 1..12”.

Formalize the transition relation and an initial condition so the specification captures: (1) legal starting hours are exactly the twelve values; (2) every transition moves to the next hour modulo 12; and (3) no other transitions are possible. State and prove (or make provable) the safety property that the hour remains within 1..12 at all times. Also express and justify the liveness property that every hour value will reoccur infinitely often (i.e., the system cycles through all twelve hour values forever). Do not constrain scheduling beyond these requirements; leave nondeterminism only where necessary to express arbitrary initial choice among the twelve hours.
