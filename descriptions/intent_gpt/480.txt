Specify a TLA+ model of the Paxos consensus algorithm (basic single-decree Paxos) for a system of proposers and acceptors communicating by message passing. The model should describe how proposers propose ballot numbers and values, how acceptors respond in phase‑1 and phase‑2, and how acceptors record and communicate votes. The model must allow arbitrary interleavings of message sends and receives (messages may be delayed or never delivered), and must permit multiple concurrent ballot attempts by different proposers.

Require the model to guarantee safety: at most one value may ever be chosen (i.e., no two different values can both become chosen by quorums of acceptors). Define "chosen" in terms of acceptors’ vote messages such that a value is chosen when some quorum has cast votes for that value in the same ballot. The model must ensure that once a value is chosen it remains the unique chosen value across all future states.

Require liveness assumptions and state what progress liveness properties depend on: specify the minimal assumptions about reliable delivery or timely retransmission (for example, that some proposer can eventually send phase‑1 and its messages reach a quorum) that suffice to guarantee that some value is eventually chosen. Also require invariants capturing acceptor behavior: acceptors must not accept (vote for) a value in a ballot for which they have promised not to (promises must be monotonic); when responding to phase‑1 a acceptor must report its highest prior accepted ballot and value. Do not constrain leader election or learning mechanisms; focus on core proposer/acceptor message flows, safety, and the liveness assumptions needed for termination.
