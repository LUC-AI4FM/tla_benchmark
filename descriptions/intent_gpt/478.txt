Specify a parameterized asynchronous, non-blocking atomic commitment protocol for N processes with crash failures and a failure detector that only ever suspects crashed processes and eventually permanently suspects every crashed process. The model should capture processes proposing an initial binary vote (YES or NO), exchanging one round of broadcast messages, possible crashes (including crashes that occur before sending a vote and may increase bounds on messages from faulty processes), unreliable message delivery with messages from faulty processes bounded by known upper bounds, and local decision actions (commit or abort) based on received messages and suspicion. The specification must state allowed behaviors (sending a vote if correct, receiving individual YES/NO messages up to the known bounds, crashing, being suspected, deciding to commit or abort) but need not prescribe a particular implementation.

Require the following correctness properties:
- Safety (Agreement): No two non-faulty processes decide differently (no correct pair where one commits and the other aborts).
- Abort validity: If any process initially votes NO, then no process may commit.
- Commit validity: If all processes initially vote YES and no process is suspected, then no process aborts or crashes (i.e., progress toward commit is enabled).
- Termination (liveness under stability): If no process ever crashes and no process is ever suspected thereafter, then every non-faulty process eventually decides (either commit or abort), and in the all-YES, no-suspicions case every correct process eventually commits.

Parameterize by N and allow checking special initial configurations (all-YES, all-NO) separately. Include a simple type-invariant for message counters and process states.
