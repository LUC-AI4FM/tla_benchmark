Specify a formal TLA+ model of a mutual exclusion algorithm for N concurrent processes (parameter N ≥ 2) that uses a symmetric two-level protocol generalised for N processes (i.e., a tournament or N-process Peterson-style lock). The model should describe processes repeatedly cycling through noncritical work, an entry protocol to acquire the lock, a critical section, and an exit protocol that releases the lock. It must allow asynchronous interleaving of enabled steps and nondeterministic scheduling.

The specification must require safety: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time (mutual exclusion). It must require liveness: every process that attempts to enter the critical section eventually succeeds (starvation freedom / progress), assuming weak fairness of each process’s enabled actions. The model should include a clear notion of process states (e.g., outside, trying/entering, in critical section), shared variables used to coordinate entry/exit, and initial conditions.

Also require invariants that capture intended relationships between the shared coordination variables and process states to facilitate proving safety. Do not prescribe a specific implementation structure; leave the algorithmic coordination expressed abstractly enough to allow encoding of known N-process Peterson-style or tournament locking mechanisms, but ensure the correctness properties above are the primary requirements.
