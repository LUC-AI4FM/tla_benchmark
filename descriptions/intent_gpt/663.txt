Specify a system that validates and processes CSV files containing structured test records, ensuring reliable detection of malformed input and correct extraction of data for downstream consumers. The model should accept streams of CSV lines that represent test entries, where each entry must conform to a predefined schema (fields present, types and formats correct, required fields non-empty). It must parse records into structured internal representations, reject or flag records that violate schema rules, and produce a deterministic sequence of parsed outputs for valid inputs.

The model must capture error classification: syntactic errors (invalid CSV quoting, unescaped delimiters), semantic errors (type mismatches, invalid field values, missing required fields), and recoverable vs non-recoverable errors. For recoverable errors, the system may skip or sanitize entries according to policy; for non-recoverable errors it must halt processing that stream. The spec should ensure that parsing is order-preserving, that no valid record is lost or duplicated, and that error flags correlate precisely with offending records. Include properties that guarantee termination on finite input, consistency of parsed records with original fields, and that recovery policies preserve downstream invariants (e.g., uniqueness or referential integrity constraints).
