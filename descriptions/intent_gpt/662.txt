Model a concurrent test harness that verifies bitwise operations across multiple independent worker processes interacting with a shared test oracle. The system should generate pairs of random input values, apply a suite of bitwise functions (including but not limited to AND, OR, XOR, NOT, left and right shifts, and combinations thereof) to each pair, and record both expected results (computed by a reference oracle) and actual results produced by worker computations. Ensure workers may run concurrently, may fail transiently, and may deliver results out of order. The specification must capture non-deterministic scheduling, message delivery delays, and possible duplication or loss of worker outputs, while assuming the oracle’s computations are reliable.

Specify correctness properties: for every completed test case where a worker result is accepted, the recorded actual value must equal the oracle’s expected value; if a mismatch is detected it must be flagged and can be used to drive a failing trace. Guarantee eventual completion: under fair scheduling and reliable persistence of accepted results, every submitted test case will either be verified as correct or reported as failed. Include invariants to ensure no test case is silently lost (either verified or failed) and temporal properties expressing that mismatches lead to a visible error state and that correct computations are eventually acknowledged.
