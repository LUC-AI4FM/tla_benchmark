Model a simple concurrent program with a fixed set of identical processes that repeatedly perform an action until they terminate. The system must initialize with each process in a defined starting state and with global data satisfying given type and range constraints. Each process may non-deterministically perform a local step that updates only its own local state and possibly some shared variables; steps must preserve type and range invariants and may change a per-process termination flag. Processes do not create or destroy other processes.

Specify a safety invariant that is always preserved: a global predicate asserting well-typedness and bounds of all variables and a per-process invariant that constrains the relationship between a process’s local state, its termination status, and any shared variables. The specification must prove that (1) the initialization implies the per-process invariant, (2) any single step by any process, or stuttering of the system, preserves that invariant, and (3) the invariant implies the global type-and-range property.

Also specify liveness: every non-terminated process that is enabled infinitely often eventually takes a step (weak fairness). Ensure the model captures stuttering steps and uses a standard next-state relation for either a single-process step or stuttering. The correctness properties to guarantee are safety (the per-process invariant and global type invariants hold always) and the stated fairness liveness condition.
