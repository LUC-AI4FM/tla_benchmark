Model a simple state machine that maintains a fixed finite array-like mapping and a separate scalar counter, and explore behaviors that involve performing no change to parts of the state while optionally updating the counter. The specification should:

- Represent a system with a fixed domain of three indices and an initial mapping that associates each index with an initial integer value (for example, 1..3 → 1..3). Also include an initial scalar counter with a given initial value.
- Allow transitions that either leave the entire state unchanged, or increment the counter while guaranteeing that the mapping remains unchanged during that transition.
- Permit assertions and observable test predicates that check whether various expressions built from the mapping and its entries remain unchanged across a transition. These expressions include: individual entries of the mapping, sets defined from entries, the domain of the mapping, and compound expressions that pair or package entries.
- Require that any transition that claims certain subexpressions are unchanged must indeed preserve their values; when such a claim is false the model should flag an error (or a failed assertion).
- Ensure that invariants about the mapping’s domain and the preservation of mapping values across counter increments hold globally.
- Support emitting simple observable messages or test outcomes when particular unchanged conditions are satisfied, to facilitate checking of the unchanged-ness semantics.
