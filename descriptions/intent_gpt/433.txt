Specify a formal model of basic algebraic operations over finite multisets (bags), finite sequences, and natural-number comparisons, intended to validate that different notations and equivalent constructions produce identical abstract results. The model should include:

- A representation of finite multisets and operations that combine and subtract them; it must allow constructing a multiset from a finite set and support multiset union and multiset difference. The system must guarantee that two different expressions that are intended to denote the same multiset produce equal multisets.

- A representation of finite sequences and a concatenation operation; the system must guarantee that two syntactically different concatenation notations yield the same sequence value.

- Natural-number comparison operators for ≤ and ≥; the system must validate that the alternate notations for these comparisons are consistent (i.e., produce the same Boolean outcomes for sample comparisons).

- An invariant that asserts the equalities above and fails explicitly if any equality does not hold, so counterexamples are returned when notations or operations are inconsistent.

Model should be executable for small finite examples so that the equivalences among alternate notational forms are checked automatically. The goal is to confirm semantic equivalence of alternate operator forms and to detect any inconsistencies in the underlying operator definitions.
