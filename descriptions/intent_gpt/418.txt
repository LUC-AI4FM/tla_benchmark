Specify a simple system that exercises and validates arithmetic on natural numbers and integer operations used by a verification tool. The model should represent a state in which numeric expressions can be evaluated and must ensure that a fixed initial state remains unchanged while a suite of arithmetic facts always holds. Require checks that include: equality and inequality comparisons, power-of-two identities, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division-with-remainder correctness (quotient and remainder satisfy dividend = divisor*quotient + remainder), modulo properties (including modulo by 1 and zero dividend cases), integer range construction semantics (empty when lower bound > upper bound; inclusive enumeration when lower ≤ upper), and two's complement-like behavior for negative multiplied values and remainders (e.g., (-1)*(-3) = 3 and (-1) mod 3 = 2 under the chosen convention). The specification must treat out-of-range or non-natural results appropriately (e.g., detect that certain expressions are not natural numbers). The model must assert that all these arithmetic properties are invariants of the system; any violation should be flagged as an error. Do not prescribe how the checks are implemented, only require that the model can evaluate these arithmetic expressions and verify their truth in every reachable state.
