Specify a TLA+ model of a bounded, multi-replica consensus and ledger-replication system that supports view changes and limited state growth. The model should describe multiple independent nodes that maintain per-node ledgers and histories, communicate via message passing, and may perform local actions such as proposing and committing ledger entries, responding to status requests, and truncating local ledgers as part of recovery or garbage collection. The system must allow view changes (leader changes), but enforce a global upper bound on the total number of view changes that can occur during any execution. It must also bound the length of per-node history structures so responses that produce status-invalid outcomes cannot grow without limit.

Specify the safety and liveness properties to verify: safety must enforce that committed ledger prefixes are consistent across non-faulty nodes (no two nodes permanently disagree on committed entries), and that truncation or recovery never leads to loss of commitment: once an entry is committed, it remains present in every later ledger state of non-faulty nodes. Liveness must ensure progress under eventual synchrony: if a majority of nodes are non-faulty and messages are eventually delivered, then new proposals are eventually committed. Include invariants that express the bounds on view-change count and history length, and model message and failure behaviors needed to check these properties.
