Specify a formal TLA+ model of a two-phase commit protocol between a single coordinator and multiple participants (resource managers). The system must capture asynchronous message passing of three message kinds: prepare notifications, commit decisions, and abort decisions. Model participant behavior that can vote to prepare or fail to prepare, and the coordinator behavior that decides commit only when all participants have prepared; otherwise it must decide abort. Include possible message loss, duplication, and reordering, but assume eventual delivery if retried (or model unreliable channels with explicit resend actions). Allow participants and the coordinator to crash and later recover, retaining durable logs for their locally persisted decisions and votes.

Specify safety properties: agreement (no two non-failed processes decide differently), validity (a commit decision occurs only if every participant had previously prepared), and atomicity (either all non-faulty participants ultimately commit or all abort). Specify liveness properties under suitable assumptions (e.g., eventual reliable communication and bounded crashes): eventual termination (every non-faulty participant eventually decides) and coordinator progress (if all participants prepare and no further failures occur, the system eventually commits). Also require that durable logging ensures decisions survive crashes and that recovery respects logged state. Provide the abstract state space, message semantics, and the invariants to verify these properties.
