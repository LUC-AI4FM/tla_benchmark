Specify a TLA+ model of a Tendermint-style round-based Byzantine-fault-tolerant consensus protocol for a fixed small network. The model should capture multiple processes executing asynchronous rounds; in each round a designated proposer broadcasts a proposal, processes vote in two phases (prevote and precommit), and may lock on values or update a “valid” candidate for future rounds. Model must allow a subset of processes to be Byzantine (faulty) and allow proposers to vary per round. Include message passing with unreliable delivery and possible duplication; messages are persistent in the modeled channels until processed. Include an option to replay a fixed sequence of external events (a trace) to exercise particular scenarios.

The specification must ensure safety properties: (1) Agreement — no two honest processes ever decide different values; (2) Validity — any decided value must come from a predefined set of valid values (invalid proposals must not be decided by honest processes). Liveness-style guarantees are optional but, if modeled, should state that under eventual synchrony and at most T faulty processes, honest processes eventually decide (termination). Capture the lock and valid-value mechanisms that prevent conflicting decisions across rounds, and the evidence accumulation needed to justify decision or lock transitions. Make the model parametrizable by number of processes N, fault bound T, set of valid and invalid values, maximum rounds to explore, and a proposer assignment function (possibly nondeterministic). Provide invariants for safety that can be model-checked for the given parameters and trace.
