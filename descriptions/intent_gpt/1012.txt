Specify a model of a distributed voucher-cancellation protocol that coordinates multiple voucher issuers and multiple voucher holders to cancel vouchers using a two-phase-commit style interaction mediated by a coordinator. The model should describe participants (one coordinator, any number of issuers, any number of holders) and the vouchers whose lifecycle is being affected. It must capture that holders and issuers can independently decide to prepare to cancel or to abort, that they can send “prepared” notifications to the coordinator, and that the coordinator may, based on received prepared notifications, either decide to commit the cancellation for a given voucher or decide to abort the transaction. The communication substrate may be abstract (messages appended to a set) and may allow broadcasts from the coordinator to all participants.

Require the model to guarantee these correctness properties:
- Type-safety: all state variables range only over their intended finite domains.
- Agreement/consistency: no holder and issuer can reach conflicting terminal decisions about the same voucher (i.e., one finalized to cancelled while another finalized to aborted).
- Safety of cancellation: a voucher is marked cancelled only if the coordinator decided to commit after receiving prepared notifications from all relevant issuers and holders.
- Liveness constraints should be expressible (e.g., progress to a coordinator decision when all required participants have prepared), although fairness assumptions may be left explicit.

Describe the global initial conditions (all vouchers valid and working, participants initially idle), and allow nondeterministic actions for participants preparing, deciding to abort, and for the coordinator receiving prepares and issuing commit or abort broadcasts.
