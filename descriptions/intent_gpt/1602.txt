Specify a simple system that models two independent nonnegative integer sources whose values are fixed constants, and a derived value that is the sum of those two sources. The model should treat the two source values as given, immutable inputs and compute the combined total as a deterministic function of them. The system must expose the inputs and the computed sum as observable quantities.

The specification should require that the sum always equals the arithmetic sum of the two input sources and that it is nonnegative. It must also state that neither input changes over time (they are constant parameters), and hence the sum remains constant throughout any execution. Include well-formedness constraints asserting that the inputs are integers (or naturals) and that their sum does not overflow the arithmetic domain assumed by the model.

Describe properties to check: the invariant asserting the sum equals the first input plus the second input, an invariant asserting nonnegativity, and a temporal property expressing that in every state of any behavior the sum equals that arithmetic sum (i.e., always). Do not prescribe implementation details; focus on the constants, the derived quantity, and the correctness properties above.
