Specify a concurrent interaction protocol for M identical agents that repeatedly meet pairwise at a single rendezvous point until a global meeting budget of N pairs has occurred. Each agent starts with one of a fixed set of initial states (three distinct colors) and is active until it becomes inactive (faded). When an agent arrives at the empty rendezvous it waits; when a second agent arrives, the two meet, both update their state according to a symmetric rule that depends only on the two previous states, both record one additional meeting, and the rendezvous becomes empty. Once the global counter of completed meetings reaches N, any agent that would otherwise still wait becomes permanently inactive (faded) and no further meetings count. The model must allow arbitrary scheduling of agents’ attempts to enter the rendezvous (concurrency and nondeterministic interleaving) but must ensure the rendezvous holds at most one waiting agent at a time.

Require the formal model to guarantee safety and liveness properties:
- Safety: state updates on meeting are symmetric and local to the two participants; the rendezvous is never occupied by more than one waiting agent; the global meeting counter only increases when two agents finish a meeting.
- Termination/consistency: once the global meeting counter reaches N, all agents are either inactive or cannot further increase the counter.
- Accounting invariant: when the global meeting counter equals N, the sum of all agents’ recorded meeting counts equals exactly 2*N (each completed pair adds two to the sum).
Include parameters M and N as positive integers and abstract the specific color-transition function as a deterministic, symmetric function of the two participants’ prior states.
