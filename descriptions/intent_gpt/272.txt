Specify a formal model of a finite-set library and small set-manipulation scenarios to validate core set operators and predicates. The model should describe collections of integers and operations on them (union, intersection, set difference, Cartesian product, subset, membership, powerset) and support construction of sets that may include repeated or computed expressions that denote the same element. It must expose examples of empty, singleton, and multi-element sets, and derived sets formed by comprehension and union. Include operations that compute cardinality, map a function over a family of sets (e.g., cardinality-of and filter-by-predicate), and form the powerset of a finite set.

The specification must state precise correctness properties to check: equality and inequality of sets constructed in different ways; correct membership and non-membership for elements; subset and non-subset relations; correctness of union, intersection, and difference results; correctness and size of Cartesian products; correct cardinalities for sets and powersets; that quantifiers over sets (forall, exists) behave as expected; and that choose-like and nondeterministic selection operators can pick elements or sets satisfying given predicates (including behavior on singletons and empty sets). Also require a predicate indicating finiteness and show it holds for finite examples and fails for infinite domains. Model these as invariants or assertions suitable for exhaustive checking.
