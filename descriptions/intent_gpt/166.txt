Specify a formal model of the classic Missionaries and Cannibals river‑crossing problem. Model a system in which two groups of people (missionaries and cannibals) occupy two river banks and may be transported by a single boat that can carry at most two people and never crosses empty. A state must record which bank the boat is on and the distribution of each person between the banks. Transitions correspond to loading one or two people from the boat’s current bank, moving them to the other bank, and unloading them there. Safety constraints: at every bank, whenever at least one missionary is present, missionaries must never be outnumbered by cannibals; no move may violate this on either bank during or after the crossing. The initial state has all people and the boat on one bank; the goal states have all people moved to the opposite bank. Specify the invariant properties to check: safety (no bank ever has missionaries outnumbered) and progress (there exists an execution reaching a goal state). Also require that the boat never moves without carrying at least one person and that each transition respects the boat capacity. Provide the model so a model checker can find a valid sequence of moves achieving the goal or prove none exists.
