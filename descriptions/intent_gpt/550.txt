Specify a concurrent round-robin mutual-exclusion system for N processes that share a single token indicating whose turn it is to enter the critical section. The model should treat N as a positive integer parameter and processes as the set {0,1,...,N-1}. The system must implement the following behavior and guarantees:

- Exactly one process at a time may be in its critical section; no two processes may be simultaneously in the critical section.
- A single shared "turn" value ranges over the process identifiers and identifies which process is currently permitted to enter the critical section. A process may enter its critical section only when the turn value equals that process's identifier.
- After a process leaves its critical section, it hands the token to the next process by advancing the turn value deterministically to the successor identifier in modulo-N order, thereby enabling the next process to enter.
- Each process repeatedly cycles through noncritical work, waiting for its turn, entering the critical section, and then advancing the turn; thus every process continuously attempts to make progress.
- The system must guarantee safety (mutual exclusion and that turn is always a valid process id) and liveness under weak fairness for each process: if a process continually enables the action that enters the critical section when the token equals its id, then under weak fairness it will eventually execute and enter the critical section; moreover, the handoff mechanism should ensure that every process that is perpetually trying to enter will eventually be given the token infinitely often (no starvation under round-robin progression).
- Also specify an invariant that the turn is always in the valid range and that process control states form the expected cycle: noncritical -> waiting -> critical -> exit -> noncritical.

Do not prescribe a particular implementation language beyond TLA+; express states, transitions, and the required safety and liveness properties abstractly.
