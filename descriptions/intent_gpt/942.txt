Specify a formal model of a 12-hour digital clock that tracks the current hour and advances time in discrete steps. The model should represent the logical behavior of the clock (not its user interface) and must expose the possible states and transitions that reflect advancing time by one hour, including wrapping from 12 back to 1.

Requirements:
- The state must include an hour component whose domain is the integers 1..12 inclusive.
- Initialization must place the clock in a valid hour state (any value in 1..12 is allowed).
- The only step allowed is an advancement of the hour by one: from h it must move to h+1 when h < 12, and from 12 it must move to 1.
- The model must permit stuttering (no-op) steps so that temporal properties about invariants can be stated in the usual way.

Correctness properties to guarantee:
- Safety: the hour always stays within 1..12 in every reachable state.
- Progress: from any state there is a finite sequence of steps that reaches every other hour value (i.e., the system is strongly connected modulo stuttering).
- Invariance: a predicate asserting that the hour is within 1..12 is an invariant (holds initially and is preserved by all steps).
- Liveness: the clock eventually visits hour 12 infinitely often (fairness condition), or, alternatively, that every hour recurs infinitely often under a fairness assumption.

Do not prescribe internal variable names or a particular specification language structure; focus on the abstract state, transitions, initialization, and the properties above.
