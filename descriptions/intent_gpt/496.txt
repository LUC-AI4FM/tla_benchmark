Specify a formal model of a replicated state machine cluster that implements a leader-based consensus protocol (Raft-style) supporting client sessions and linearizable command execution. The model should describe a fixed set of servers that may crash and restart, exchange RPCs, and maintain persistent state across restarts (at least current term, vote choice, and log). The system must elect a single leader via term-numbered elections with pre-voting and RequestVote semantics, replicate log entries from leaders to followers via AppendEntries, and advance commit indexes only when a majority of servers have stored the entry and the entry’s term matches the leader’s current term. Leaders must maintain per-follower next and match indices to drive replication and recover from conflicts by backing up. Followers must reject AppendEntries that do not match their log prefix, allow truncation and appending of entries, and apply committed entries to their local state machine in order. The protocol must support client operations to open/close sessions and submit commands, which are converted into log entries by leaders and produce responses to clients only after commitment and application. The model must capture message loss, duplication, reordering, and server timeouts, and state transitions on receiving messages with higher term numbers. Key correctness properties: leader election safety (at most one leader per term), log matching and leader completeness (committed entries are preserved and leaders that commit entries have those entries present in a majority), and linearizability of client commands (responses reflect a total order consistent with committed log order). Also specify liveness assumptions under eventual message delivery and a non-faulty majority (leader election and command commitment eventually progress).
