Specify a formal model of a replicated state machine using a leader-based consensus protocol that supports dynamic membership (reconfiguration) and node retirement. The model should capture multiple servers that may be in roles such as leader, follower, or candidate, and must represent election rounds (terms), voting, leader election, and log replication with indices, match/sent tracking, and commit decisions. The system must allow nodes to join and leave the cluster, including a controlled retirement process that preserves safety during membership changes.

The model should include an asynchronous unreliable network that may drop, delay, duplicate, or reorder messages; it should allow crashes (stops) and recoveries of nodes. Liveness guarantees should be stated under reasonable assumptions (e.g., eventual message delivery and a stable majority of non-crashed nodes): eventually some correct node becomes leader and client commands are eventually replicated and committed. Safety properties must hold under all behaviors: at most one leader per term; no two leaders can commit conflicting logs; committed entries are durable and cannot be lost or overwritten by a later leader; once an entry is committed in a term, any later leader must contain that entry (log matching).

Additionally, specify debugging/observability support: snapshots or derived aggregates that record counts of leader elections, per-node granted votes, message-delivery counts, per-node state-transition histories, and current cluster membership for inspection. Do not prescribe internal encoding; focus on the external behaviors and correctness properties above.
