Specify a model of a small leader-election protocol for a fixed set of deterministic servers that communicate by sending typed messages (RequestVote, Vote, AppendEntries). The model should abstract only message-passing and local term counters; it should not include logs or client requests. Servers maintain a monotonically nondecreasing integer term, may send RequestVote messages to seek higher terms, reply with Vote messages, and, once obtaining votes from a majority (quorum), may act as leader by sending AppendEntries messages for their current term.

The specification must capture the following behavior and requirements:

- Servers can increment their local term to become candidates and broadcast a RequestVote message carrying the new term.
- A server may cast a Vote message for a candidate only in response to a RequestVote message whose term is strictly greater than the voter’s current term; when observing such a higher-term message, the voter updates its local term to that message’s term and emits a Vote.
- A server may declare itself leader for term t only if it has received (or has recorded the presence of) votes from every member of at least one explicit quorum (majority) for term t; upon becoming leader it emits an AppendEntries message stamped with its term.
- Message sets should be finite, typed, and contain only the three message kinds with their associated fields (sender, optionally destination, and term).

State the safety properties required:

- Term monotonicity: each server’s term never decreases and all messages’ term fields are bounded within the declared term range.
- Vote consistency: a given server cannot cast two different votes for the same term (i.e., for two different candidates in the same term).
- Leader uniqueness per term: at most one distinct server may be considered leader in the same term (no two different servers can both have quorums of votes for the same term).
- AppendEntries uniqueness per term: all AppendEntries messages carrying the same term must originate from the same server (leaders are unique per term).
- Type-correctness of state and messages.

Specify initial conditions (all servers start at term zero and no messages in flight), an asynchronous interleaving of the allowed actions above (including the option to do nothing), and a liveness-agnostic safety specification (no need to prove eventual leadership or liveness). Include invariants that enforce the type constraints and the per-server proof obligation that a server will not accept or emit messages with a term higher than its current term without first updating its term accordingly.
