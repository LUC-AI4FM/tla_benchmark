Specify a formal model of a single-lane bridge crossing problem with multiple cars approaching from two opposite directions. The model should represent an environment in which cars arrive, wait, enter, traverse, and leave a constrained bridge that can accommodate traffic in only one direction at a time and has limited discrete positions. The system must allow cars to queue before the bridge, to occupy bridge positions while crossing, and to rejoin the road on the far side when finished.

Require safety properties:
- Mutual exclusion on the bridge: at any time, all cars that occupy bridge positions must be traveling in the same direction; no two cars from opposite directions may be on the bridge simultaneously.
- Exclusive occupancy: each bridge position may be occupied by at most one car.
- Position consistency: cars progress along adjacent bridge positions without teleportation; a car may only move to the next physical position if that position is free.

Require liveness properties:
- No deadlock: if cars are waiting on one or both sides and the bridge is free or will become free, some waiting car will eventually enter and cross.
- Fairness between directions: the model should avoid permanent starvation of one direction when there is continuous arrival from the other; provide a mechanism or fairness condition that guarantees eventual crossing for all waiting cars.

Also require invariants describing conservation of cars (cars are never lost or duplicated) and that each car’s state is always one of: waiting before bridge, occupying a specific bridge position, or having left the bridge.
