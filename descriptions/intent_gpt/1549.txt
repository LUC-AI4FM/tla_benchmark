Specify a parameterized distributed system of N processes that implements an eventually perfect failure detector in a partially synchronous network with crash failures (up to T crashes). The model should capture message sending, delivery delays, and process steps with an explicit notion of global “environment” ticks that advance message ages and bound relative process speeds.

Requirements:
- Processes repeatedly send heartbeats and periodically make failure-suspicion decisions. A process must not both send heartbeats and make a prediction in the same local step; receiving messages may occur in steps when neither sending nor predicting happens.
- Messages are placed into a communication channel with an age that increases on each global tick; delivery is nondeterministic but must respect upper bounds on age determined by the partial synchrony constraints.
- The model must include per-process local clocks (with finite domains) and per-pair timeout estimates used to decide suspicion. Local clocks wrap or reset so the state space is finite for fixed parameters.
- The environment enforces two global constraints: (1) a bound on how long a correct process may remain inactive during any PHI consecutive global ticks (relative-speed constraint), and (2) a delivery-timing constraint ensuring that if a receiver delays at least DELTA ticks since a send, then any message eligible for delivery must have age ≤ DELTA when the receiver steps (message-delay constraint).
- Crashes are permanent and may occur nondeterministically up to T processes; crashed processes do not take steps but messages to them may still be delivered up to a bounded age.

Correctness properties to guarantee:
- Strong completeness: every crashed process is eventually permanently suspected by every correct process.
- Eventual strong accuracy: there exists a time after which no correct process is ever suspected by any correct process.

Parameterize the model by N, T, DELTA, PHI, SendPoint, PredictPoint, and message-delay bounds. Ensure the model is finite for fixed parameters so it can be checked by model checking tools.
