Specify a simple state machine that maintains a single mutable record-valued state and evolves either stutteringly or by updating a field of that record. The system should:

- Represent a state that is always a record with at least one boolean field.
- Allow two kinds of steps: a stuttering step that leaves the state unchanged, and an update step that sets a particular existing boolean field to true.
- Permit nondeterministic choice between the two step kinds at each transition.

Require the specification to guarantee safety and liveness properties:

- Safety: The state is always a record containing the required boolean field, and that field always has a boolean value; no transition may produce a state that lacks the field or assigns a non-boolean value.
- Invariance: Once the required boolean field is true, it remains true in all subsequent states (the update is idempotent).
- Liveness: From any reachable state, it must be possible to eventually perform either a stuttering step or an update step (i.e., the system does not deadlock).
- Termination/Quiescence check: Characterize executions that eventually only stutter, and prove that if an infinite execution performs infinitely many non-stuttering updates, the boolean field is true from some finite point onward.

Do not commit to specific variable names or concrete syntactic encodings; describe the state, allowed transitions, and the above correctness properties abstractly.
