Specify a formal model of the classic two-jug water-pouring puzzle with two containers of fixed capacities. The model should represent a system where one container has capacity 3 units and the other has capacity 5 units. The environment supplies an unlimited source of water and an unlimited sink (discard), and only the following atomic operations are allowed at any step: fill either container to its capacity from the source; empty either container entirely to the sink; pour water from one container to the other until either the source container is empty or the destination container is full. The system should start with both containers empty.

Require the model to explore all reachable states under nondeterministic choice of the allowed operations. Specify a safety property that identifies when the larger container contains exactly 4 units. Specify an observable action that counts any transition that changes the contents of either container (a “pour or change” step). Require instrumentation to count how many reachable states are generated, how many are distinct, and the diameter of the reachable-state graph (max shortest-path length between initial and any state). Also require a simple accounting of total water drawn from the source across the exploration and an average water-drawn-per-distinct-state metric. Finally, state that the model should assert the expected numeric values for: number of generated states, number of distinct states, diameter, total water drawn, and the average drawn per distinct state.
