Specify a formal model of subset relation reasoning over finite sets and ranges of integers. The model should represent sets drawn from integer ranges and arbitrary finite sets, and it should evaluate and enforce subset and non-subset relationships between those collections. The system must be able to express facts such as: any set of the form {1,...,n} being a subset of {1,...,m} when n ≤ m; that empty set is a subset of any set including itself; that a singleton set is not a subset of the empty set; and that finite sets of integers are subsets of the sets of all integers and of natural numbers. It must also allow direct assertions that certain subset relationships do not hold (e.g., a larger upper bound range is not a subset of a smaller one). The correctness properties to guarantee are logical consistency of all asserted subset/non-subset relations and preservation of those truths over transitions (i.e., once the asserted relations hold they remain true). Additionally, require that a boolean flag representing global consistency remains a boolean and stays true throughout execution, serving as an invariant witness to the maintained correctness of the subset assertions.
