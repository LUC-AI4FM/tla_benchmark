Specify a formal model of a numerical combinatorics library that exposes two core functions: factorial(n) and binomial(n,k). The model should describe the domain of valid inputs, the intended deterministic results for those inputs, and the algebraic relationships that must always hold. Requirements:

- Domain: define nonnegative integer inputs up to a chosen safe bound (e.g., n and k limited to prevent overflow in the model). Inputs outside the domain may be considered undefined or error.
- Factorial behavior: for all valid n, factorial(0) = 1 and factorial(n) = n * factorial(n-1) for n>0; the model should enforce equality with a mathematically pure recursive definition on the allowed domain.
- Binomial behavior: for all valid n,k with 0 ≤ k ≤ n, binomial(n,k) equals n! / (k! (n-k)!) and satisfies base identities binomial(n,0)=1, binomial(n,n)=1, and binomial(n,1)=n.
- Consistency properties: binomial must be symmetric (binomial(n,k) = binomial(n,n-k)), and obey Pascal’s identity binomial(n,k) = binomial(n-1,k-1) + binomial(n-1,k) where indices make sense.
- Safety and bounds: specify explicit bounds used in the model and ensure all arithmetic is within those bounds; show that definitions are total on the declared domain.
- Specify invariants and assertions that capture these correctness properties for exhaustive checking by a model checker.
