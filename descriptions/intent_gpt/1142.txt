Specify a formal model of a tool that maps a highlighted region of a source document to the corresponding syntactic units in a related target representation, and that computes structural nesting information for those units. The model should accept as inputs: a linear source document annotated with a sequence of marked elements that are either tokens (each covering a contiguous source-region), left/right delimiters for nested syntactic units, or explicit break markers; and a contiguous highlighted region within the source document. It must determine which marked tokens correspond to the highlighted region (a token pair that bounds the selection), following rules that handle selections that lie entirely inside whitespace, that touch token boundaries, or that lie between tokens — choosing the most appropriate adjacent token(s) in ambiguous cases. After selecting the token pair, the model must compute, by scanning the marked sequence between those tokens, (a) the net change in nesting depth from the left token to the right token, (b) the minimum nesting depth reached within that interval relative to the starting depth, and (c) the matching delimiter positions that bracket the syntactic unit(s) containing the selection. The specification must assert correctness properties: the chosen tokens indeed cover the selection according to the ordering of regions; the computed net depth equals the difference of delimiter nesting levels at the two token positions; and the reported minimum depth equals the minimum nesting level attained at any position in the scanned interval. Also require termination and absence of deadlock.
