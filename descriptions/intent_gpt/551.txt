Specify a formal TLA+ model of a symmetric mutual exclusion protocol for N concurrent processes using a single shared one-bit token or flag to coordinate access to a critical section. The model should describe process behaviors abstractly (request, wait, enter critical section, and exit) and the shared state used for coordination, but must not commit to a specific program text or implementation details. The specification must capture how processes obtain exclusive access, release it, and how the shared coordination variable can be read and updated.

State the safety and liveness requirements the protocol must guarantee:
- Safety (mutual exclusion): at most one process may be in the critical section at any time.
- Progress (deadlock and livelock freedom): whenever some process attempts to enter the critical section, the system should eventually allow some process to enter the critical section (i.e., the protocol cannot get permanently stuck with no progress).
- Starvation-freedom for individual processes should be expressed as separate eventuality properties: if a given process repeatedly tries to enter the critical section (or is in the waiting state), it should eventually be granted the critical section. Allow that certain weaker variants (e.g., starve-freedom for some processes but not all) may be investigated and reported.

Also request specifications to parametrize the number of processes N and to prove or check which of the above properties hold under asynchronous interleaving semantics. Include the expectation of model-checking or invariant proofs to validate safety and temporal properties.
