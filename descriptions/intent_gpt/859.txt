Specify a concurrent readers–writers system with N processes that may act as readers or writers. The model should capture that at any time a process can request to read, request to write, start and finish its critical section as a reader, or start and finish its critical section as a writer. The algorithm must allow multiple readers to be in the critical section concurrently but must ensure that writers have exclusive access. Readers and writers may queue when they cannot immediately enter the critical section; the model should represent waiting sets for readers and for writers.

State the safety requirements: (1) No writer may be in the critical section concurrently with any other reader or writer. (2) Any number of readers may be in the critical section simultaneously provided no writer is present. (3) The sizes of the active reader set and of the writer-waiting set must remain bounded by a given capacity N. (4) The model must prevent deadlocks between readers and writers: if there are waiting readers and no waiting writers, readers must eventually enter; if there are waiting writers and no waiting readers, writers must eventually enter. Liveness properties should ensure progress for waiting requests under fair scheduling assumptions. Provide invariants and temporal properties that express these safety and liveness requirements.
