Model a simple configurable-state system that initializes a single integer state and evolves it over time according to a chosen transition behavior. The model must allow selecting one of several alternative initial conditions and one of several alternative transition rules; selection may come from a configuration source or be overridden by runtime/command-line choices. The system should permit a default initialization and transition when no overrides are provided, and alternative initializations and transitions when a configuration or override is supplied.

Specify that the state is an integer and that transitions may be either stuttering (no change) or arithmetic updates that add a fixed increment to the state. Support a fairness option for the chosen transition (both weak and strong fairness should be expressible for the transition relation). Allow invariants to be specified separately (a default invariant and alternatives provided by configuration or override).

Require checking of safety: the chosen invariant must hold in all reachable states under the selected init and transition choices. Require checking of liveness: the model should be able to express and verify a liveness goal stating that eventually the state exceeds a given threshold, and that this liveness goal can be evaluated under different fairness settings. Ensure the specification framework cleanly separates selection of initial condition, transition relation, fairness, and invariant so that combinations can be analyzed independently.
