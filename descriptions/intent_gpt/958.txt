Specify a formal TLA+ model of the real numbers as a complete ordered field extended with two symbols representing positive and negative infinity, and of the usual arithmetic and order operations on them. The model should define a carrier set for the real numbers and provide operations for addition, multiplication, additive inverse, multiplicative inverse (on nonzero reals), subtraction, division, and exponentiation by integers and by nonnegative reals where defined. It must also define a total linear order on the extended line (reals plus ±infinity) that extends the usual ordering on reals and treats MinusInfinity as strictly less than every real and Infinity as strictly greater than every real. The model must enforce the field axioms for reals (associativity, commutativity, identities, inverses, distributivity), the Archimedean/Nat embedding property (natural numbers are represented and behave appropriately under successor), and the completeness axiom: every nonempty subset of reals that is bounded above has a least upper bound in the reals. Specify the behavior of arithmetic with infinities (e.g., comparisons, subtraction and division when operands include infinities) in a way that does not violate the field axioms on the real subset. Require existence of at least one structure satisfying these properties (a witness) and expose selectors to use the chosen structure in further specifications. State correctness properties to check: closure of operations on reals, preservation of order by addition and multiplication by nonnegative reals, existence and uniqueness conditions for inverses, and the completeness property for suprema.
