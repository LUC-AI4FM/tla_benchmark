Specify a model of the following weighing-piece design problem. Given a positive integer total weight W and a positive integer number of pieces N, cut a single weight of W into N positive integer-weight pieces so that any integer target weight from 1 to W can be balanced exactly on a two-pan balance by placing some subset of the pieces on either pan. The model should treat the pieces as indistinguishable except for their weights, and should consider only partitions of W into N positive integers (order of pieces does not matter).

The specification must capture the requirement that for every target t in 1..W there exists a choice for each piece of one of three placements: left pan, right pan, or unused, such that the balance equation (sum of weights on one pan equals the sum on the other pan plus t, viewed appropriately) holds. Express this in terms of existence of coefficients in {-1,0,1} for the pieces that produce each target weight.

State invariants and properties to check: (1) the pieces form a multiset summing to W; (2) for every target t in 1..W there exists a placement of pieces achieving t; and (3) pieces' weights are positive integers. Allow W and N to be parameters so the model can be explored for different values; the tool should produce a valid partition when one exists and report nonexistence otherwise. Do not prescribe an algorithm for searching partitions—describe correctness requirements only.
