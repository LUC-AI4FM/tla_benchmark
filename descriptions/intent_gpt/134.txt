Specify a model of a simple concurrent/stateful system that maintains, for each element of a fixed finite control set, an associated infinite (unbounded) subset of integers, and that repeatedly updates one of those subsets in a way that preserves the type and basic invariants.

The model must represent:
- A fixed finite set of identifiers (control points or processes).
- For each identifier, an associated subset of integers that may be infinite and can change over time.
- An initial state where every identifier is mapped to a particular nonempty subset (for example, containing 0).
- A nondeterministic step relation that chooses one identifier and replaces its current subset with some other subset of integers (subject to any constraints below), leaving other identifiers’ subsets unchanged.

Required correctness properties:
- Type safety: at all times every identifier is mapped to a subset of integers.
- Invariance of nonemptiness: each identifier’s subset remains nonempty in every reachable state.
- Optional liveness requirement: every identifier is eventually chosen infinitely often (fairness) or at least chosen infinitely often under a specified fairness assumption.
- Safety under replacement: any replacement must produce a subset that respects any given constraints (e.g., boundedness, parity, or inclusion properties) if such constraints are specified.

The specification should not prescribe how choices are made beyond nondeterminism and fairness assumptions; it should focus on the abstract state, allowed transitions, and the safety and liveness properties to be verified.
