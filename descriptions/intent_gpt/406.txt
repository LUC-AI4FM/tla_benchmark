Specify a formal model of a small language of expressions and user-defined binary operators, intended to validate that custom operator definitions and overrides behave consistently with their intended semantics when used in expressions, conditionals, and pattern-like constructs. The model should represent an evaluator for expressions that can include: arithmetic, sequence construction and length, set construction, concatenation, and any user-declared infix operator names mapped to user-specified binary functions. The system must allow overriding or shadowing built-in operator names with user definitions and must support declaring operators with arbitrary symbolic names (including multi-character and unusual symbols) that can be used infix. It must evaluate conditional tests and assertions that depend on these operators and sequences, and must support printing or reporting pass/fail outcomes of individual checks. Key correctness properties: (1) Each expression evaluates to the value determined by the current operator bindings and standard semantics for arithmetic, sequences, and sets. (2) Overriding a built-in operator with a user definition changes evaluation site-locally and consistently across expressions. (3) Declared infix operators with unusual symbols parse and apply as binary functions identical to their declared semantics. (4) Sequence construction, concatenation, and length operations yield standard results. (5) Assertions about evaluated expressions hold exactly when their underlying semantic equality holds. Also ensure the evaluator itself does not change unrelated state.
