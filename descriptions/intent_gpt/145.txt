Model a distributed mutual‑exclusion service based on Lamport’s logical‑clock request/reply algorithm. The system consists of a fixed set of processes that communicate by pairwise FIFO message channels. Each process has a monotonically increasing logical clock and may issue a request to enter a critical section by broadcasting a timestamped request. Other processes receive requests, update their clocks, record the timestamps of requests they have seen, and immediately send acknowledgements. A requesting process may enter the critical section only after receiving acknowledgements from every process and when, according to its view of timestamps, its request has higher priority than every other outstanding request (ties broken by process id). When leaving the critical section the process broadcasts a release message; recipients clear that process’s outstanding request.

Specify the state space, message types (request with timestamp, ack, release), and asynchronous delivery over FIFO per‑pair channels. Include actions for sending a request, receiving a request (and replying), receiving an acknowledgement, receiving a release, entering, and exiting. Require safety: mutual exclusion (at most one process in the critical section). Require progress/liveness properties appropriate to the algorithm: if a process issues a request and messages are eventually delivered, then it eventually receives all acknowledgements and eventually enters the critical section (no deadlock/starvation under reliable delivery). Also state constraints needed for finite‑state model checking (e.g., bounded clocks or channel lengths).
