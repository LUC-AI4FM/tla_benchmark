Model a synchronous barrier synchronization for N identical processes. The system should allow each process to repeatedly execute two phases: "arrive" and "release". On arrival a process declares it has reached the barrier; no process may transition from the barrier’s released state back into the post-barrier phase until all processes have arrived. Once all N processes have arrived, the barrier should atomically transition to the released state and allow all processes to proceed, after which they may later re-enter the barrier for another round.

Specify the abstract states and transitions without committing to a concrete implementation strategy. The model must capture repeated barrier rounds, fairness of process progress (so individual processes are not permanently starved), and support arbitrary N > 0. Correctness properties to guarantee:

- Safety: no process may be released from the barrier until every process has signaled arrival for that round.
- Liveness: whenever all processes eventually arrive for some round, they will all eventually be released for that round.
- Reusability: after a release, the barrier resets so processes can participate in subsequent rounds without confusion between rounds.
- Type invariants: the set of process states and round bookkeeping must remain well-formed (e.g., each process’s local phase belongs to the defined phase set and any shared round counter or token is within expected bounds).

Also state any assumptions (e.g., reliable steps, finite N) and whether fairness or weak/strong fairness constraints are required to ensure liveness.
