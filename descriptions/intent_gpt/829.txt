Specify a model of a search game played on a line of N ≥ 2 discrete locations (boxes) where an adversary (the cat) occupies one location and must move each time step to an adjacent location (no staying still, no wrap-around). Each time step the searcher may inspect exactly one location to see whether the adversary is there. The model should allow the adversary to choose either adjacent move nondeterministically, and the searcher to choose which single box to inspect each step according to a deterministic strategy. Do not assume any simultaneous teleportation or skipping of boxes.

Require a search strategy that is generated from a simple repeated sweep pattern which only inspects interior boxes (i.e., excludes the two endpoints) and alternates direction at the interior endpoints, producing a periodic back-and-forth inspection sequence. The specification must state the allowed state space, the transition relation for the adversary moves, and the transition relation for the searcher’s inspection choice (including reversal at sweep endpoints). Impose fairness that the adversary always moves each step.

The key correctness property to guarantee is eventual discovery: starting from any initial positions and any phase of the sweep, the searcher’s strategy must ensure that eventually a time step occurs where the inspected location equals the adversary’s location. Also require invariants preserving bounds (positions remain within 1..N and inspection indices remain within the interior range) and state that the property holds for both even and odd N ≥ 2.
