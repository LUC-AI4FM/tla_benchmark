The model should include the domain of natural numbers and a formal definition of "even" and "odd" based purely on remainder modulo 2. It must express the property that for every natural number n, the sum n + n is even. The specification should state this as a universal correctness property and provide the logical facts needed to prove it: (a) every natural number is either even or odd, (b) parity arithmetic rules for addition modulo 2, and (c) closure of the operations within the naturals. The proof obligations should be structured so an automated SMT or decision procedure can discharge the main theorem, but the specification must also support a hand-checkable case-splitting proof distinguishing even and odd cases. Do not prescribe a particular proof tool or algorithm; instead require the model to be amenable both to automated solvers and to manual case reasoning. Finally, include lemmas that relate remainder properties to sums (for example, how remainders of summands compose under addition modulo 2) and state as the main invariant that doubling preserves evenness for all naturals.
