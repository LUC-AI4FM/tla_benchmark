Specify a TLA+ model of a concurrent mutual-exclusion protocol for N processes competing to enter a critical section. The model should describe the abstract behavior of processes that alternate between noncritical work, a protocol to request access, and the critical section. The specification must allow arbitrary interleaving of process steps but should not prescribe an implementation style; describe only the states and transitions each process may take (e.g., idle, requesting, waiting, in-critical, exiting).

Require that the protocol enforces mutual exclusion: at most one process may be in the critical section at any time. Require progress properties: (1) global deadlock and livelock freedom—whenever some process begins the entry protocol, the system eventually reaches a state where some process is in the critical section; and (2) starvation freedom—whenever a particular process begins the entry protocol, that process eventually enters the critical section. Allow fairness assumptions needed to state these progress properties (e.g., weak fairness for enabled steps). Also include the ability to vary N as a constant and to reason about safety and liveness for any fixed N. Specify invariants to check safety and temporal formulas to express the liveness properties.
