Specify a concurrent mutual-exclusion protocol for N processes that implements Lamport’s Bakery-style ticket algorithm. Model processes that repeatedly alternate between noncritical and critical sections. When a process wants to enter the critical section it must obtain a ticket (a natural number) that orders contenders lexicographically by (ticket, process-id), wait until it is the smallest among competing tickets, then enter the critical section, and on exit release its ticket.

The model must allow arbitrary interleaving and nondeterministic choices for how tickets are chosen (including the possibility of picking any natural number at certain points), and must permit processes to read each other’s current tickets and an auxiliary boolean “flag” used during ticket selection. It should preserve type-correctness for all state components and allow an unbounded number of ticket values. The specification must express and verify the following properties:

- Mutual exclusion: no two distinct processes may be in the critical section simultaneously.
- Safety of ticket ordering: if a process holds a positive ticket, any competing process that will contend must either have a larger lexicographic pair or will eventually choose one that is larger.
- Liveness variants: deadlock-freedom (some trying process eventually enters a critical section) and starvation-freedom (every trying process eventually enters) should be expressible as temporal properties to be checked or proved.

Do not constrain the design to a particular implementation structure; instead require the abstract behaviors above (ticket allocation, wait-for-smaller-tickets, release) and the correctness invariants they must guarantee.
