Specify a model of two independent concurrent workers that each perform a single, identical, non-atomic increment on a shared integer counter. The system should start with the counter equal to zero and with both workers ready to execute their single increment. Each worker may execute at most once and after executing becomes finished; after both have finished the system may idle forever. The model must allow any interleaving of the two increments, including one worker executing before the other, the other before the one, or any interleaving where the increments interleave at the level of atomic step execution (i.e., the counter update should be modeled as an indivisible state transition for each worker, but scheduling may choose either worker at each step).

Specify safety requirements:
- The counter must never decrease and must always be an integer.
- Every state transition executed by a worker must increase the counter by exactly one.
- The only state changes are the workers performing their increment and the eventual quiescent idle behavior; no other spontaneous changes to the counter are allowed.

Specify liveness requirements:
- Eventually both workers reach their finished state (termination), i.e., the model should guarantee that it is possible for both increments to be performed and that no execution unfairly prevents a worker from executing forever (the property to check is eventual termination of both workers, under a weak fairness assumption for their enabled steps).

Finally, require that the final counter value equals the number of workers that performed their increment (so in any terminating execution the counter equals two).
