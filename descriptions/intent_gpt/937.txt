Model a simple discrete hour clock that repeatedly cycles through the numbers 1..12, advancing the hour in steps that occur indefinitely. The model should represent an ongoing reactive system whose states include a current hour value and any minimal state needed to describe a single-step clock transition. The system must guarantee safety properties: the hour value is always an integer within the set {1,...,12}, and each single-step transition updates the hour by advancing it to the next value modulo 12 (so that after 12 comes 1). The system must also guarantee liveness: transitions that advance the hour occur infinitely often in every execution (the clock cannot stop advancing), and as a consequence every hour value in 1..12 occurs infinitely often over time. Specify the initial condition(s) for the hour and any constraints needed to ensure the type and bounds of variables, but do not depend on external scheduling assumptions. Formulate the behavior as a temporal specification combining the allowed step relation with a fairness condition that enforces perpetual progress. State and require proofs (or model-checkable assertions) that the specification implies (1) invariance of the hour's type and bounds, (2) that infinitely many advancing steps occur, and (3) that each hour value appears infinitely often.
