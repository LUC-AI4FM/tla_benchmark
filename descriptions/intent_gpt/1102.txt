Model a system that augments an existing state-machine specification with controllable stuttering steps that may be inserted either before or after individual transitions. The model should treat the original system as a black box described by its state variables, an initial condition, and a next-state relation made up of alternative subactions. For any chosen subaction, the stuttering mechanism must be able to do the following: when enabled, optionally interpose a finite nonzero number of internal stuttering transitions before the subaction, or perform the subaction and then optionally interpose a finite nonzero number of internal stuttering transitions afterward. Each stuttering run must be parametrized by a stutter counter drawn from a well-founded, totally ordered domain with a specified minimum; each stuttering transition decrements the counter according to a specified function and stops when the minimum is reached. The stuttering augmentation must preserve the original observable behavior when the stuttering counter is at its sentinel value (i.e., not stuttering), must not change original state variables except during the concrete subaction or during stuttering steps that are explicitly allowed to leave them unchanged, and must ensure that concurrent execution of other subactions cannot corrupt the intended stuttering run (the stuttering context must be matched). Specify the invariant ensuring every counter sequence reaches the minimum (no infinite stuttering), and require that the augmented system refines the original specification (trace inclusion of observable variables) and maintains safety and liveness properties of the original when stuttering is ignored.
