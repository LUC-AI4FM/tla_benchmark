Specify a formal model of a test harness that exhaustively validates combinatorial generators used to produce subsets, permutations, combinations, and Cartesian products. The model should describe a system that, given a finite input universe and generator parameters (such as subset sizes, permutation length, or product arities), enumerates all outputs produced by the generator and checks them against the mathematical specification for that combinatorial family.

The model must capture the generator's inputs, its nondeterministic choice of outputs, and a deterministic verification procedure that accepts exactly those outputs that satisfy the combinatorial predicate (e.g., each output is a subset of the universe of the specified size, or a permutation of the given elements, or a tuple whose components come from the specified operand sets). The harness must also check completeness: every mathematically valid object of the requested family must be produced at least once by the generator. It must detect duplicates and report whether the generator yields each valid object exactly once, or at least once, depending on the test mode.

Formalize correctness properties: (1) Soundness — every generated item satisfies the combinatorial specification; (2) Completeness — the set of generated items equals the mathematical set of valid items; (3) Uniqueness (optional mode) — generated items are pairwise distinct. Include invariants to bound the enumeration over finite domains so that exhaustive checking is decidable.
