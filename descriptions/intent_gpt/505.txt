Specify a formal TLA+ model of the single-decree Paxos consensus algorithm (leader election omitted — assume a single fixed proposer) that captures proposers, acceptors, ballots, and quorums, and that reasons about messages exchanged to agree on a single chosen value. The model should represent asynchronous message passing (messages may be delayed, duplicated, or reordered but not corrupted) and a fixed finite set of acceptors and quorums such that any two quorums intersect. Ballots are totally ordered; values are drawn from a finite nonempty set.

The model must express the two-phase protocol: proposers may initiate a prepare (phase 1) with a ballot and then, upon collecting appropriate responses from a quorum, propose a value (phase 2) chosen according to the Paxos rule (if any acceptor in the quorum previously accepted a value with highest accepted ballot, propose that value; otherwise propose any value). Acceptors respond to prepares with their highest seen ballot and any previously accepted value and accept proposals that carry a ballot at least as large as their promise. Messages carry message-type, ballot, and value or acceptor state as needed.

State the correctness properties to be verified:
- Safety (Agreement): at most one value may ever be chosen; no two acceptors may accept different values in the same or different ballots such that they could lead to conflicting decisions.
- Consistency with Paxos choice rule: any proposal that becomes chosen must be a value that was either previously accepted by some acceptor or was proposed in accordance with the highest prior accepted ballots.
- Integrity: only values from the given value set can be chosen, and acceptors only accept proposals when the ballot conditions hold.
- Invariance of quorum intersection: model assumptions ensure any two quorums intersect.

Do not encode leader election; model liveness only optionally (e.g., eventual delivery and non-failure assumptions) — but primarily require safety properties be provable under asynchronous delivery and arbitrary scheduling.
