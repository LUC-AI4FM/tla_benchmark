Specify a concurrent system of three independent worker threads that cooperatively compute and then print a global sum. The system has three workers: one special worker A and two symmetric workers B1 and B2. Each worker holds a private input value and may call a common two-step subroutine that uses a private local temporary to update a shared accumulator. The call to the subroutine must consist of: (1) initializing the callee's local temporary from constants and the call argument, and (2) adding that temporary to the shared accumulator and returning. Calls may be nested only in the sense of a simple call/return handshake per worker (no reentrancy across workers). Each worker should execute its own sequence: compute the argument for the subroutine from its private input and a small private local, call the subroutine, and then mark itself as finished. Worker A, after finishing, should wait until all three workers are finished and then print the final accumulator value. The model must capture asynchronous interleaving of steps of different workers and of subroutine steps.

Specify the following correctness properties to verify:
- Safety: the shared accumulator is updated only by the specified subroutine step, and no other interleaving can violate the atomic two-step call semantics for a single worker (local temporaries remain private).
- Liveness: every worker eventually completes and returns from any call it makes (no deadlock or livelock), and eventually worker A prints the accumulator.
- Functional correctness: the final printed accumulator equals the sum of each worker’s initial private local plus the value passed as the argument they computed (i.e., the intended mathematical sum of all contributions).
- Symmetry: the two B workers are interchangeable; the specification should allow proving properties for both by symmetry.
