This TLA+ module specifies Lamport's Bakery algorithm for mutual exclusion among N concurrent processes. The algorithm uses a ticket-based approach where processes choose numbered tickets and enter the critical section in ticket order, with process identifiers breaking ties.

The constant N represents the number of processes, assumed to be a natural number, with processes identified as integers from 1 to N. The specification uses six variables: `num` (each process's ticket number), `flag` (indicating a process is choosing a ticket), `pc` (program counter tracking each process's location), `unchecked` (set of processes not yet checked), `max` (maximum ticket seen while choosing), and `nxt` (next process to check during waiting).

Each process cycles through phases: non-critical section, entry protocol (setting flag, computing maximum ticket, choosing a number greater than the maximum, clearing flag), waiting phase (checking other processes have lower priority), critical section, and exit. The entry and exit phases include nondeterministic stuttering steps that can temporarily set variables to arbitrary values before settling on correct ones.

The specification employs weak fairness for each process when not in its non-critical section. The lexicographic ordering on pairs (ticket number, process id) determines priority.

The module defines and proves mutual exclusion—no two processes occupy the critical section simultaneously. It includes a complete inductive invariant combining type correctness with conditions about ticket values and the Before relation. Liveness properties for deadlock freedom and starvation freedom are defined but not proven in this module.
