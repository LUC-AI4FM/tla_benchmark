This TLA+ module provides a formal TLAPS proof that the CoffeeCan specification maintains its type invariant throughout all executions.

The module extends a base CoffeeCan specification, which models a classic puzzle involving a can containing black and white beans. The system allows three operations: picking two black beans and returning one black bean, picking two white beans and returning one black bean, or picking one of each color and returning the white bean. Each operation reduces the total bean count by exactly one.

The proof establishes that the specification implies the type invariant always holds. To accomplish this, it introduces a strengthened inductive invariant called Inv, which combines the original TypeInvariant with an additional bound BeanBound stating that the total bean count remains within zero to MaxBeanCount.

The proof proceeds in three main steps. First, it shows the initial state satisfies the strengthened invariant. Second, it demonstrates that the invariant is preserved by every possible transition, analyzing each action separately: PickSameColorBlack, PickSameColorWhite, PickDifferentColor, Termination, and stuttering steps. The PickSameColorWhite case requires careful reasoning because it increases the black bean count while decreasing white beans by two, but the bound is preserved since total beans decrease. Third, the proof shows the strengthened invariant implies the original type invariant, completing the argument using temporal logic.
