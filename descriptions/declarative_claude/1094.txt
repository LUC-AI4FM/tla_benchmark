The module models the Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) process, extending a simpler prior specification by introducing a `stage` variable that enforces a strict ordering of thermal steps and a `cycle` counter that tracks completed PCR cycles.

The system is initialized with fixed stocks of double-stranded DNA (`DNA`) and primers (`PRIMER`), with temperature at `"Hot"`, no single-stranded templates or hybrids, and zero completed cycles. Four actions define the next-state relation. `heat` transitions from `"Hot"` to `"TooHot"`, denaturing all double-stranded DNA and hybrids into single-stranded templates while recovering primers from hybrids; it is only enabled in stages `"init"` or `"extended"`, and sets stage to `"denatured"`. `cool` drops temperature back to `"Hot"` and advances stage to `"ready"`. `anneal` lowers temperature to `"Warm"`, nondeterministically pairing some number of primers with templates to form hybrids, and advances stage to `"annealed"`. `extend` raises temperature back to `"Hot"`, converts all hybrids into new double-stranded DNA, increments the cycle counter, and sets stage to `"extended"`. The temporal specification requires weak fairness on all four actions, ensuring the PCR cycle progresses continuously. The model is checked with `DNA = PRIMER = 5`, enforcing `TypeOK` as a safety invariant, and verifying two properties imported from an instantiated `clean` module: its behavioral specification and a liveness property asserting that primers are eventually depleted.
