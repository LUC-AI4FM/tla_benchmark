This TLA+ module specifies a simple 12-hour clock that cycles through hour values from 1 to 12.

The specification defines a single variable **hr** representing the current hour. The initial condition **HCini** states that the hour must be a natural number between 1 and 12 inclusive.

The next-state relation **HCnxt** describes how the clock advances: if the current hour is not 12, it increments by one; otherwise, it wraps around to 1. This models the cyclic behavior of a standard 12-hour clock face.

The temporal specification **HC** combines the initial condition with a temporal formula stating that every step either satisfies the next-state relation or leaves the hour unchanged (a stuttering step). This allows for behaviors where the clock may sometimes not advance, which is standard practice in TLA+ to enable composition with other specifications.

The module includes a theorem asserting that if the specification HC holds, then the invariant HCini holds in all states—meaning the hour always remains within the valid range of 1 to 12. This is a safety property ensuring the clock never displays an invalid hour value.

No fairness conditions or liveness properties are specified; the clock is not required to eventually advance.
