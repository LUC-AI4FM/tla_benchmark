This TLA+ module specifies a PlusCal algorithm demonstrating nested procedure calls with parameter passing and local variable preservation across a call stack.

The algorithm models three procedures—Proc1, Proc2, and Proc3—that call each other in sequence. The main program calls Proc1 with argument 4. Proc1 sets its local variable u to 2, then calls Proc2 with argument 4 (computed as 2 times u). After Proc2 returns, Proc1 verifies that u remains 2 and arg1 remains 4, then calls Proc2 again with argument 5. Proc2 verifies its argument is either 4 or 5, initializes local variable v to 42, and calls Proc3 with the sum of v and its argument (yielding 46 or 47). Proc3 verifies its argument is in the expected set and returns.

The VARIABLES include pc (program counter), stack (call stack for procedure returns), and procedure-specific parameters and locals: arg1 and u for Proc1, arg2 and v for Proc2, and arg3 for Proc3.

The specification uses weak fairness on all transitions, ensuring progress when actions are continuously enabled. The Termination property asserts that the program eventually reaches the Done state. The embedded assertions serve as runtime safety checks, verifying that arguments and local variables maintain expected values throughout the nested call sequence, testing proper stack-based variable preservation during procedure calls and returns.
