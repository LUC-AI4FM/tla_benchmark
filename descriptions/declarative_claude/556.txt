This TLA+ module specifies a semaphore-based mutual exclusion algorithm for N concurrent processes.

The system models N processes (numbered 0 through N-1) competing to enter a critical section, coordinated by a binary semaphore. The constant N defines the number of processes, and Procs represents the set of process identifiers.

Two variables track the system state: sem is the semaphore (initialized to 1, indicating availability), and pc maps each process to its current program location.

Each process cycles through four phases: a non-critical section (ncs), attempting to enter (enter), the critical section (cs), and exiting (exit). In the non-critical section, processes may proceed freely. To enter the critical section, a process must wait until the semaphore equals 1, then atomically set it to 0. After completing its critical section work, the process releases the semaphore by setting it back to 1.

The specification includes fairness conditions: weak fairness applies to process steps when not in the non-critical section, and strong fairness applies specifically to the enter action. This ensures that processes genuinely attempting to enter will eventually succeed, while allowing processes to remain indefinitely in their non-critical sections.

The module defines the specification Spec but does not explicitly state safety or liveness properties to check, though the semaphore mechanism is intended to guarantee mutual exclusion.
