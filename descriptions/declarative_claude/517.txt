This module provides a model-checking configuration for a specification defined in Session11b, which appears to model the Alternating Bit Protocol for reliable data transmission over unreliable channels.

The module instantiates the Data constant with a concrete set containing three names: "Fred", "Mary", and "Ted". A state constraint limits the lengths of both communication channels (AtoB and BtoA) to at most three messages, bounding the state space for model checking.

The specification uses four variables: AVar and BVar represent the current message state at sender A and receiver B respectively, while AtoB and BtoA are sequence-typed channels carrying messages between the parties.

Three invariants are checked. AB_TypeOK ensures type correctness: both AVar and BVar are messages, AtoB is a sequence of messages, and BtoA is a sequence of acknowledgment bits. AB_Inv captures a key safety property: when the acknowledgment channel is non-empty and its head matches the sender's current bit, the sender and receiver agree on the current message. AB_TwoValsSeq constrains both channels to contain at most two distinct consecutive values, reflecting the alternating bit structure.

The module also verifies that the implementation refines a higher-level specification ABSpec from Session11a, checking this as a temporal property.
