This TLA+ module is a model-checking configuration for a single-lane bridge crossing problem, extending a base module called SingleLaneBridge.

The system models vehicles traveling across a one-lane bridge from two directions. The constants define two sets of cars: CarsRight contains vehicles "r1" and "r2" approaching from the right, while CarsLeft contains "l1" and "l2" approaching from the left. The bridge occupies positions 4 and 5 within a linear space of eight positions (1 through 8), representing the roadway including approaches on both sides.

The specification tracks car locations as they move through positions, with the bridge being the critical shared resource where only cars traveling in one direction may occupy it at any time to prevent collisions.

The model checks several properties. The Invariants predicate verifies safety conditions, likely ensuring no head-on collisions occur on the bridge. Three temporal properties are checked: CarsInBridgeExitBridge likely ensures cars eventually leave the bridge; CarsEnterBridge likely ensures cars waiting to cross eventually enter; and CarsInfOftenOnBridge, defined in this module, asserts that every car from the right side infinitely often appears on the bridge, a strong liveness requirement indicating repeated bridge crossings.

The specification Spec from the base module governs the system's temporal behavior, presumably including fairness conditions enabling the liveness properties to hold.
