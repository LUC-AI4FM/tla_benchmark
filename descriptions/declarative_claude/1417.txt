This TLA+ specification models a simple three-state sequential process and demonstrates refinement mapping to a more abstract two-state specification.

The concrete system has a single variable x that progresses through three states: "init", "in-progress", and "done". The initial state sets x to "init", and the next-state relation allows transitions from "init" to "in-progress" and from "in-progress" to "done". The specification includes weak fairness on the Next action, ensuring progress when enabled.

The abstract specification (defined across modules Abstract and Abstract2) models a simpler two-state system where x begins as "in-progress" and transitions to "done". The refinement mapping AbstractX collapses the concrete states by mapping both "init" and "in-progress" to "in-progress", while "done" maps to itself.

The module defines several properties for testing purposes. Refinement asserts that the concrete specification refines the abstract one through the refinement mapping. Additional parameterized properties explore different expression levels (constant, state, and temporal) and operator applications, including a recursive operator that causes stack overflow when checked.

The model configuration specifies the concrete Spec as the system specification, disables deadlock checking (since the system terminates at "done"), and verifies the Refinement property along with PropParam.
