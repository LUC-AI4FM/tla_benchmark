The module PickPerf2 specifies a small synthetic system combining two independently growing integer sets and a finite-state counter, likely designed as a performance or stress test for symbolic model checkers (particularly those using set-based or "pick" operations).

The state consists of four variables: `S` and `T` are sets of integers, `R` is their union, and `state` is an integer. Initially, `S = {1}`, `T = {4}`, `R = {1, 4}`, and `state = 0`. On each step, exactly one of six set-growth rules fires: elements 1, 2, and 3 form a cycle of additions into `S` (each element's presence triggers adding the next), while elements 4, 5, and 6 form an analogous cycle of additions into `T`. After any set update, `R` is kept equal to `S ∪ T`. Simultaneously, `state` transitions according to a fixed finite directed graph over values 0–4, allowing moves such as 0→1, 0→2, 1→3, 2→1, 2→4, 1→0, or self-loops. All three conjuncts of `Next` must hold simultaneously, so set growth and state transitions are coupled in each step. The sole invariant `Inv` asserts that `S` and `T` are never equal. No fairness conditions or liveness properties are specified.
