This module provides machine-checked proofs about a multi-car elevator system specification defined in a separate Elevator module. The proofs establish two safety theorems: that the specification preserves a type invariant and a safety invariant throughout all executions.

The specification models elevator systems with multiple cars serving multiple floors. People have locations (either on a floor or inside an elevator), destinations, and waiting status. Elevators have current floors, directions (up, down, or stationary), door states, and sets of pressed buttons. The system tracks active elevator calls.

The proof strategy introduces a strengthened invariant combining the type invariant with an auxiliary property stating that non-waiting persons must be located on floors. For the safety invariant proof, seven additional auxiliary invariants are introduced covering properties like: waiting persons have distinct locations and destinations, open doors imply the current floor is not in pressed buttons, stationary elevators have no passengers, and passengers' destinations are reflected in button presses.

The module explicitly assumes elevators and floors are disjoint sets and provides lemmas for evaluating multi-argument function applications that TLAPS cannot automatically unfold.

The type correctness theorem is fully proved through initialization and inductive step lemmas covering all actions (picking destinations, calling elevators, opening/closing doors, entering/exiting, moving, stopping, and dispatching). The safety theorem proof structure is established but the detailed inductive step is omitted, noted as requiring substantial case analysis across approximately ninety sub-cases.
