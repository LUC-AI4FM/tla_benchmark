This TLA+ module specifies a refinement mapping between two channel implementations: a high-level channel H carrying values from 1 to 12, and a low-level channel L carrying single bits. The specification demonstrates how the bit-level channel can implement the higher-level channel through serialization.

The system uses two variables: h represents the high-level channel state, and l represents the low-level channel state. Both channels follow the same underlying Channel specification but with different data types. An auxiliary variable bitsSent (hidden through existential quantification) tracks accumulated bits during transmission.

The key actions are SendBit, RcvBit, and Error. SendBit transmits individual bits on the low-level channel while accumulating them; after four bits are collected, they are converted to a natural number (via little-endian bit sequence conversion) and sent on the high-level channel. RcvBit receives bits from the low-level channel, triggering a high-level receive only when no partial transmission is pending. The Error action handles unexpected low-level channel behavior by transitioning the high-level channel to a designated error value.

The temporal specification IR uses existential quantification over bitsSent to hide the implementation detail, expressing that some internal state exists making the refinement work. This demonstrates that the low-level bit channel correctly implements the high-level data channel through the serialization protocol.
