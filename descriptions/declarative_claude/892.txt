This TLA+ module provides a machine-checked proof of type correctness for a communication channel specification defined in a separate Channel module.

The module extends two components: the Channel module containing the actual system specification, and TLAPS (the TLA+ Proof System) which provides the proof infrastructure.

The single theorem, TypeCorrect, establishes that the specification Spec implies the TypeInvariant holds in all reachable states (expressed as []TypeInvariant, meaning "always TypeInvariant"). This is a safety property asserting that the channel never violates its type constraints.

The proof proceeds in two steps following the standard invariance proof pattern. Step 1 shows that the initial condition Init establishes the TypeInvariant. Step 2 shows that the TypeInvariant is preserved by any transition allowed by the next-state relation, including both actual Next steps and stuttering steps (expressed as [Next]_chan). The proof of step 2 relies on the definitions of TypeInvariant, Next, Send, and Rcv actions from the Channel module. The final QED step combines these results using PTL (propositional temporal logic) reasoning to conclude the theorem.

This module contains no executable specification itself—it exists solely to mechanically verify that the Channel specification maintains type safety.
