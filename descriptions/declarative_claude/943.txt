This TLA+ module specifies a simple 12-hour clock system.

The specification models a clock that cycles through hours from 1 to 12 and wraps back to 1 after reaching 12. There is a single variable, `hr`, representing the current hour displayed on the clock.

The initial condition `HCini` states that the hour must be a value between 1 and 12, inclusive. The next-state relation `HCnxt` defines how the clock advances: if the current hour is not 12, it increments by one; otherwise, it resets to 1.

The temporal specification `HC` combines the initial condition with a temporal formula stating that every step either satisfies the next-state relation or leaves `hr` unchanged (a stuttering step). This allows for steps where the clock does not advance while still permitting normal clock progression.

The module includes a theorem asserting that if the system satisfies the specification `HC`, then the initial condition `HCini` is always satisfied—meaning the hour value remains within the valid range of 1 to 12 throughout all behaviors. This serves as a safety invariant, ensuring the clock never displays an invalid hour.

No fairness conditions or liveness properties are specified in this module.
