This TLA+ module specifies a simple twelve-hour clock that cycles through hours one through twelve.

The specification defines a single variable, `hr`, representing the current hour displayed on the clock. The initial condition `HCini` constrains the hour to be any value from one to twelve inclusive.

The next-state relation `HCnxt` describes how the clock advances: if the current hour is not twelve, it increments by one; otherwise, it wraps around to one. This models the cyclic behavior of a standard twelve-hour clock face.

The temporal specification `HC` combines the initial condition with a temporal formula stating that every step either satisfies the next-state relation `HCnxt` or leaves the hour unchanged (a stuttering step). This allows for behaviors where the clock may remain at the same hour indefinitely, which is standard in TLA+ to permit refinement.

The module includes a theorem asserting that the specification `HC` implies the invariant `[]HCini` always holds—meaning the hour variable remains within the valid range of one to twelve throughout any execution. No fairness conditions are specified, so the clock is not required to eventually advance.
