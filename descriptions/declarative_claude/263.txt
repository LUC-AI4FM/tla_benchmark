This is a test or example module illustrating the use of labels within TLA+ expressions, particularly in the context of invariants and action definitions. It models a trivial integer system with a single variable `x` of type `Int`. The `Init` predicate allows `x` to start at either 0 or 1 (labeled `Init0` and `Init1`), and the `Next` relation allows `x` to transition to either 2 or 3 (labeled `Next2` and `Next3`). The invariant `Inv` asserts three labeled conjuncts: that `x` is never equal to 2 (`Inv2`), never equal to 3 (`Inv3`), and never equal to 4 (`Inv4`). Since `Next` unconditionally sets `x` to 2 or 3, the invariants `Inv2` and `Inv3` are immediately violated after any step, making this a specification designed to exercise or test how a model checker reports labeled invariant violations rather than to model a correct system. The predicate `V` asserts `x > 0`, which would hold after any `Next` step but not necessarily in the initial state where `x` may be 0.
