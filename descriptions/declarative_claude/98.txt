This TLA+ module specifies a simple predicate over two sets and a user-defined binary relation. It serves as a template or abstract specification for checking whether a particular relationship holds between elements of two collections.

The module declares three constants: two sets of integers, A and B, and a higher-order constant operator AreGood that takes two integers and returns a boolean. The AreGood operator is left abstract—its concrete definition must be supplied at model-checking time—allowing the module to be instantiated with different notions of what makes a pair of values "good together."

The sole definition in the module, GOOD_PAIR_EXISTS, is a state predicate asserting the existence of at least one pair consisting of an element from A and an element from B such that AreGood holds for that pair.

The module contains no variables, no actions, no next-state relation, and no temporal formulas or fairness conditions. It defines no invariants or liveness properties to be checked. It is a purely constant-level specification that could be used as a building block or imported into a larger specification where the existence of such a good pair is relevant to system behavior.
