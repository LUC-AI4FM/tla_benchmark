This TLA+ module specifies the classic "Die Hard" water jug puzzle, where the goal is to measure exactly 4 gallons using two jugs of different capacities.

The system models two water jugs: a small jug with capacity 3 gallons and a big jug with capacity 5 gallons. The two variables, `small` and `big`, track the current water level in each jug.

The initial state has both jugs empty. The next-state relation permits six possible actions: filling either jug to its full capacity, emptying either jug completely, or pouring water from one jug to the other. When pouring between jugs, the specification handles two cases: if the receiving jug can hold all the water being poured, the source jug empties completely; otherwise, the receiving jug fills to capacity and the remainder stays in the source jug.

The type invariant `TypeOK` constrains the small jug to values 0 through 3 and the big jug to values 0 through 5, reflecting their respective capacities.

The temporal specification uses standard TLA+ form with an initial condition and a next-state relation under stuttering. The configuration file specifies this as the system specification to check. No liveness properties or fairness conditions are included; only the basic safety specification is defined.
