This TLA+ module explores and tests TLC model checker behavior regarding duplicate state generation when evaluating different syntactic forms of next-state actions.

The system has a single variable x that starts at 42 and can transition to various integer values. The specification defines several next-state actions (N1a through N5) that demonstrate how TLC handles conjunctions and disjunctions in different orders, particularly when combined with trivially true or false predicates.

Actions N1a, N1b, and N1c all involve disjunctions of tautologies (like 1=1) combined with assignments, but differ in their syntactic structure to test whether TLC generates duplicate states. N1a and N1c place the assignment first or use double negation to avoid duplicates, while N1b may generate duplicates. Actions N2 and N3 contain impossible conditions (like 1=2) making them always disabled. Action N4 allows transitions to either 5 or 6, and N5 simply sets x to 7.

The safety invariant Inv asserts x must always be in the set {42, 0, 1, 2, 5, 6, 7}, confirming which states are reachable.

The postcondition PossibleCounts verifies TLC's internal counting of how many times certain state predicates (HasFive and LeaveInit) could potentially be satisfied during exploration, checking specific expected counts to validate TLC's state generation behavior.
