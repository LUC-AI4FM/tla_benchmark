The module specifies a simple 12-hour clock, drawn from the "Specifying Systems" examples. It has a single variable `hr`, typed as an integer, representing the current hour on the clock face.

The initial condition `HCini` requires that `hr` is a value in the range 1 through 12. The next-state action `HCnxt` advances the hour by 1, wrapping from 12 back to 1, modeling the cyclic progression of a 12-hour clock. The overall specification `HC` is the conjunction of the initial condition and the always-enabled stuttering-tolerant box action `[][HCnxt]_hr`, meaning the clock either ticks forward or stutters (leaves `hr` unchanged). The invariant `TypeOK` asserts that `hr` remains in the set 1..12 throughout all reachable states, which is equivalent to `HCini`. The module also states the theorem `HC => []HCini`, asserting that if the specification holds then the initial condition is maintained as an invariant for all time. No fairness conditions are imposed, so the spec permits infinite stuttering and does not guarantee that the clock ever actually advances.
