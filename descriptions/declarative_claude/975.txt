This TLA+ module serves as a proof wrapper that establishes two key invariance theorems for a specification defined in an imported module called Simple.

The module extends Simple, inheriting all its definitions including the specification Spec, the type invariant TypeOK, a stronger invariant Inv, and various components such as Init, Next, vars, and process-related definitions.

Two theorems are proven using hierarchical proof structures with temporal logic reasoning via PTL (Propositional Temporal Logic):

The first theorem, TypeCorrect, proves that the specification Spec implies that TypeOK holds in all reachable states. The proof proceeds by showing that Inv is an inductive invariant (established by Init and preserved by Next), and that Inv implies TypeOK.

The second theorem, InvInvariant, proves that Spec implies Inv holds globally. This follows the standard inductive invariance proof pattern: demonstrating that Init establishes Inv, and that Inv combined with any Next step (or stuttering) preserves Inv.

Both proofs rely on an assumption NAssump and utilize definitions from the underlying Simple module including process sets, actions a and b, and termination conditions. The module essentially provides formal machine-checked proofs that the Simple specification maintains its declared invariants throughout all behaviors.
