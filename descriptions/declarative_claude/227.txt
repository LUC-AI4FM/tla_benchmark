The module Slicer4 is a small test or utility specification designed to exercise symbolic transition slicing in a model-checking context, rather than modeling a real system.

It declares a single string-typed variable `state`, initialized to `"Init"`. The next-state relation `Next` is the disjunction of two actions: `A`, which unconditionally sets `state` to `"A"`, and `B`, which existentially quantifies over elements of the set `{1, 2, 3}` and, within a locally defined set `C = {4, 5, 6}`, further quantifies over `{4, 5}`, ultimately setting `state` to `"B"`. The nested quantifiers and the local `LET` definition in action `B` serve as the subject of the slicing test, even though neither bound variable nor the set `C` appears in the state assignment. The invariant `Inv` asserts that `state` is never equal to `"B"`, which is violated by action `B` and is therefore expected to be falsified during model checking. No temporal specification or fairness conditions are stated.
