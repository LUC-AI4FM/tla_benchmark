The module specifies Huang's algorithm for distributed termination detection. The system consists of a fixed set of processes (`Procs`) with one distinguished `Leader`. The algorithm uses dyadic rational weights to track whether all computation has ceased: the leader begins holding the entire weight (`One`), and whenever it activates another process by sending a message, it splits its current weight in half and includes the half in the message. A non-leader process that finishes its work returns its weight to the leader via a message; the leader accumulates returned weights and declares termination when it holds `One` again while itself being idle.

The state comprises `active` (a per-process boolean), `weight` (a dyadic rational held by each process), and `msgs` (a per-process sequence of in-transit dyadic rational weights). The `TypeOK` invariant enforces that the total weight across all processes and in-flight messages always sums to `One`. Key actions are `SendMsg` (an active process halves its weight and sends the half to any process), `RcvMsg` and `Idle` for non-leaders (receiving a message activates the process and adds its weight; going idle sends weight back to the leader), and `RcvLdr` and `IdleLdr` for the leader (accumulating returned weights or becoming inactive). The temporal specification `Spec` adds weak fairness on all receive and idle actions. Two properties are checked: `Safe` asserts that once termination is detected (`TerminationDetected`) all processes are and remain truly idle (`Terminated`), and `Live` asserts that actual termination eventually leads to its detection.
