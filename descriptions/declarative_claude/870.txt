This TLA+ specification models the **single-lane bridge problem**, where a narrow bridge permits traffic in only one direction at a time. Cars approaching from opposite sides must coordinate to avoid collisions.

**Constants and Variables:** The specification uses constants `CarsRight` and `CarsLeft` (disjoint sets of cars traveling in opposite directions), `Bridge` (positions on the bridge), and `Positions` (all road positions). Variables track each car's `Location` and a queue `WaitingBeforeBridge` of cars waiting to enter.

**Initial State:** Cars start at opposite ends of the road—right-bound cars at the maximum position, left-bound at the minimum. The waiting queue is initialized with cars adjacent to the bridge.

**Actions:** Cars can move outside the bridge freely, move inside the bridge if no collision occurs, or enter the bridge. Bridge entry is controlled: a car may enter only if the bridge is empty or all current occupants travel the same direction, and no position conflict exists. The waiting queue enforces ordering.

**Fairness:** Weak fairness applies to all movement and entry actions, ensuring enabled actions eventually occur.

**Safety Properties (Invariants):** No two cars occupy the same bridge position; bridge capacity is respected; cars traveling opposite directions never occupy the bridge simultaneously.

**Liveness Properties:** All cars eventually exit the bridge after entering, and all cars eventually enter the bridge.
