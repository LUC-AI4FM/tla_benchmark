This TLA+ module specifies a turn-based mutual exclusion algorithm for N processes, implementing a simple round-robin approach to critical section access.

The system models N processes, numbered 0 through N-1, that repeatedly cycle through four phases: a non-critical section, an entry protocol, a critical section, and an exit protocol. A single shared variable called turn determines which process may currently enter the critical section.

Each process begins in its non-critical section and may proceed to the entry phase at any time. At the entry phase, a process must wait until the turn variable equals its own process identifier before advancing to the critical section. After completing the critical section, the process increments the turn variable modulo N, passing permission to the next process in circular order, then returns to its non-critical section.

The specification includes weak fairness for each process, ensuring that every process will eventually make progress when its actions are continuously enabled.

The initial state allows turn to be any valid process identifier, and all processes start in their non-critical sections. The next-state relation permits any single process to take one step according to its current phase.

No explicit safety or liveness properties are defined in the module for model checking, though the algorithm inherently provides mutual exclusion since only one process can have turn equal to its identifier at any time.
