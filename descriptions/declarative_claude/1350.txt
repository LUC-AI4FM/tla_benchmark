The module Github1037 is a minimal TLA+ specification modeling a single counter variable that increments from 1 to 5 and then remains fixed.

The sole variable `x` is initialized to 1 by `Init`. The `Next` action increments `x` by 1 if it has not yet reached 5, and leaves `x` unchanged once it equals 5. The temporal specification `Spec` combines the initial condition, the always-enabled box-next constraint `[][Next]_x`, and a weak-fairness condition on `Next`, ensuring that the system cannot stutter indefinitely when `Next` is continuously enabled. The liveness property `Liveness` asserts that if `x` starts at 1, it must eventually reach the value 5 and remain there permanently — expressed as `(x = 1) => <>[](x = 5)`. The model-checking configuration designates `Spec` as the temporal specification and `Liveness` as the property to verify. In effect, the module checks that the counter, starting at 1, is guaranteed under weak fairness to reach and stabilize at 5.
