This TLA+ module specifies a parameterized model of the non-blocking atomic commitment protocol with failure detectors, based on Guerraoui's 2001 paper on failure-sensitive agreement problems.

The system models N processes attempting to reach agreement on whether to commit or abort a transaction. The constant N defines the number of processes. The variables track message counts: nSntNo and nSntYes count messages sent by correct processes, while nSntNoF and nSntYesF bound messages from faulty processes. Each process maintains local counts of received YES and NO messages (nRcvdYes, nRcvdNo), a failure detector indication (someFail), and a program counter (pc) indicating its status among YES, NO, SENT, ABORT, COMMIT, or BYZ (crashed).

Initially, processes vote YES or NO with arbitrary failure detector values. The next-state relation allows processes to crash (transitioning to BYZ), send their vote (moving to SENT), receive messages, abort if suspicious or upon receiving a NO, or commit after receiving N YES messages with no NO messages.

The specification includes weak fairness on all actions except crashes, ensuring progress when enabled actions exist. Three variants exist: general voting, all-YES voting, and all-NO voting.

The checked properties include type correctness, agreement (no process commits while another aborts), abort validity (NO vote prevents commits), commit validity (unanimous YES without suspicion prevents aborts), termination (failure-free runs decide), and universal commitment.
