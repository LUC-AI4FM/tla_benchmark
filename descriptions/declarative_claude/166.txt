The module specifies the classic Missionaries and Cannibals river-crossing puzzle, annotated with Apalache type aliases. Three missionaries and three cannibals must cross a river using a boat that carries at most two people, subject to the constraint that missionaries on either bank must never be outnumbered by cannibals on that bank.

The two constants `Missionaries` and `Cannibals` represent the sets of people of each kind, both typed as sets of persons. The two variables are `bank_of_boat`, a string ("E" or "W") indicating which bank the boat is currently docked at, and `who_is_on_bank`, a function mapping each bank label to the set of people currently on that bank. The initial state places the boat and all people on the east bank ("E"), with the west bank empty. Each step is defined by the `Move(S, b)` action, which moves a non-empty subset S of one or two people from bank b to the opposite bank, provided both banks remain safe afterward — meaning no bank has missionaries present and outnumbered by cannibals. The `Next` relation existentially quantifies over all such subsets of the people on the current boat bank. The `TypeOK` predicate serves as a type invariant. The intended use is model checking: TLC is run with the negation of `who_is_on_bank["E"] /= {}` to find a counterexample trace, which constitutes a valid solution to the puzzle.
