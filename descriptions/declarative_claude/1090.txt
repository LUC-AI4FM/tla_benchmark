This module provides TLAPS proofs for safety properties of a specification called "clean" that models a DNA replication or PCR-like process involving primers, templates, hybrids, and DNA molecules.

The module extends the base "clean" specification and the TLAPS proof system. It introduces an assumption that the constants DNA and PRIMER are natural numbers, which is necessary for the arithmetic reasoning in the proofs.

Three main theorems are proven:

**TypeCorrect** establishes that the type invariant TypeOK holds throughout all executions. The proof proceeds by showing TypeOK holds initially and is preserved by all actions: heat, cool, anneal, extend, and stuttering steps. The anneal case requires the most detailed reasoning about the natMin function and bounds on the parameter k.

**PrimerPositive** proves that the primer count remains non-negative, derived as a consequence of the type correctness theorem since natural numbers are non-negative.

**Preservation** proves a conservation invariant stating that the quantity "template + primer + 2*(dna + hybrid)" remains constant. This represents conservation of genetic material across all transitions. The proof shows this sum is unchanged by heating (which converts hybrids and dna back to single strands), cooling, annealing (which pairs primers with templates), extending (which converts hybrids to complete dna), and stuttering.

A helper lemma confirms that natMin of two naturals yields a natural number.
