It defines a single operator called ChiSquare that takes three parameters: expected counts (representing a theoretical frequency distribution), actual observed counts, and a significance level alpha. The operator is intended to evaluate whether observed data conforms to an expected frequency distribution, which is a standard statistical hypothesis test.
