This specification models a multi-car elevator system serving a building with a configurable number of floors. The system consists of people who travel between floors and elevators that service their requests.

The constants define the set of people, the set of elevators, and the number of floors. Three variables track system state: PersonState records each person's current location (a floor or inside an elevator), their destination floor, and whether they are waiting; ActiveElevatorCalls tracks pending hall calls with floor and direction; ElevatorState tracks each elevator's current floor, movement direction (up, down, or stationary), door status, and pressed floor buttons.

The next-state relation encompasses nine possible actions: a person picks a new destination, a person calls an elevator, an elevator opens or closes its doors, people enter or exit an elevator, an elevator moves to an adjacent floor, an elevator stops at boundary floors, and the dispatch action assigns elevators to calls. The dispatch algorithm selects the closest stationary or approaching elevator to handle each call.

The specification enforces weak fairness on most actions and strong fairness on door closing, elevator movement, and dispatching, ensuring the system makes progress.

Safety invariants verify type correctness, that pressed buttons correspond to actual passengers, that elevators move toward their passengers' destinations, and that active calls have people actually waiting. The temporal invariant guarantees that every call is eventually serviced and every waiting person eventually reaches their destination.
