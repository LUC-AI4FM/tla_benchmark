This TLA+ module models the classic "Die Hard" water jug puzzle, where the goal is to measure a specific quantity of water using two jugs of different capacities without any measuring marks.

The specification defines two constants, SmallCap and BigCap, representing the capacities of the small and large jugs respectively. Two variables track the current water levels: small for the smaller jug and big for the larger jug.

The system begins with both jugs empty. Six possible actions define all legal operations: filling either jug completely from a tap (FillSmall, FillBig), emptying either jug entirely (EmptySmall, EmptyBig), or pouring water from one jug to the other (SmallToBig, BigToSmall). The pouring actions handle two cases: if the receiving jug can hold all the water, it receives everything and the source becomes empty; otherwise, the receiving jug fills to capacity and the source retains the remainder.

The next-state relation allows any of these six actions to occur at each step. The temporal specification permits stuttering steps where neither variable changes.

No explicit safety or liveness properties are defined in this module. Typically, model checking would verify whether a target water amount (such as exactly 4 gallons in the movie scenario) can be achieved, expressed as an invariant violation to find a counterexample trace.
