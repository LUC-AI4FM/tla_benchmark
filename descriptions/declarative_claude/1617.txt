This TLA+ module specifies a simple sequential algorithm called NoLoop that performs arithmetic operations through three labeled steps without any looping.

The algorithm uses three variables: a program counter `pc` tracking execution progress, and two integers `x` and `y` both initialized to zero.

The execution proceeds through three steps. Step `a` assigns the constant 3 to `x`. Step `b` nondeterministically chooses a value from the set {1, 2}, assigns it to `y`, and updates `x` to be the sum of the current `x` and the newly chosen `y`. Step `c` conditionally increments `x`: if `y` equals 1, it adds 1 to `x`; otherwise, it adds `y` to `x`. Step `c` also prints the final value of `x`.

The temporal specification combines the initial state, the next-state relation (which allows any of the three steps or stuttering after termination), and weak fairness on the next-state relation to ensure progress.

The module defines a termination property asserting that the program counter eventually reaches "Done". It also defines auxiliary state predicates: `AtStepC` indicates when execution is at step `c`, and `PickTwo` captures transitions where `y` becomes 2. The `PossibleCounts` predicate checks TLC statistics about how many states satisfy these predicates during model checking.
