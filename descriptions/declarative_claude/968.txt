This TLA+ module specifies a non-deterministic state-graph exploration algorithm that searches for safety violations or deadlocks without enforcing breadth-first search ordering.

The system models a graph explorer operating on a directed state graph with designated initial states and violation states. The graph is represented using an adjacency function mapping states to their successors.

**Constants and Variables:** The specification uses constants `StateGraph` (the graph to explore) and `ViolationStates` (states representing safety violations). Variables include `S` (frontier set of unexplored states), `C` (visited states), `T` (spanning tree as predecessor-successor pairs), `L` (BFS level assignments), `successors` (pending successor triples to process), `done` (completion flag), and `counterexample` (path to violation or deadlock).

**Actions:** `InitCheck` verifies initial states for violations. `Explore` non-deterministically selects a frontier state, computes its unvisited successors, updates the spanning tree, and detects deadlocks (states with no successors). `Each` processes individual successors by marking them visited, adding them to the frontier, and checking for violations. `Trc` reconstructs the counterexample path by walking backward through the spanning tree once a violation or deadlock is found.

**Temporal Properties:** The specification includes weak fairness on the next-state relation. It checks `Termination` (exploration eventually completes) and `Live` (if violations exist, a valid counterexample path from an initial state to a violation state is eventually constructed).
