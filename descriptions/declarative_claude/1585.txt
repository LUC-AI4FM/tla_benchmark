This TLA+ module specifies a concurrent algorithm with two processes (identifiers 1 and 2) that each execute independently with their own local variables.

Each process maintains three variables: x, y, and z, all initially zero. The algorithm proceeds through labeled steps with nondeterministic choices. At step "a", each process chooses one of three branches: set x to 1 and continue to step "b", set y to 1 and continue to step "c", or set z to 100 and skip directly to step "d". Steps "b" and "c" increment x or y respectively before proceeding to "d".

At step "d", another nondeterministic choice occurs based on guards: if x equals 0, increment z by 1; if x equals 2, increment z by 3. Two assertions then verify invariants about the final state: either the sum x+y equals 2, or z equals 101 with x+y equaling 0; and if z is less than 100, then z must equal x+1.

The specification includes weak fairness for each process, ensuring continuous progress. The temporal formula specifies standard safety (initial state and valid transitions) combined with these fairness conditions. The module checks a termination liveness property ensuring all processes eventually reach the "Done" state.
