This TLA+ module specifies Euclid's algorithm for computing the greatest common divisor (GCD) of two natural numbers. The algorithm is expressed in PlusCal and translated to TLA+.

The specification models the classic subtraction-based GCD algorithm operating on two numbers: a fixed value 24 and a variable v chosen from 1 to N, where N is defined as 500. The variable v_ini preserves the initial value of v for verification purposes.

The algorithm uses four variables: u (initialized to 24), v (ranging over 1 to N), v_ini (storing v's initial value), and pc (the program counter tracking execution location). The main loop at label "lp" continues while u is non-zero. Each iteration first swaps u and v if u is smaller than v, then at label "a" subtracts v from u. When u reaches zero, v contains the GCD.

The next-state relation combines the loop actions and a terminating stuttering step. The temporal specification includes initial conditions, the next-state relation under stuttering, and weak fairness on all actions to ensure progress.

The specification checks two properties: an assertion that upon termination v equals the mathematically-defined GCD of 24 and the initial v, and a liveness property (Termination) ensuring the algorithm eventually completes.
