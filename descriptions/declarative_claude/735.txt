This TLA+ module specifies the Boulangerie Algorithm, a variant of Lamport's Bakery Algorithm for mutual exclusion among concurrent processes, developed by Moses and Patkin.

The algorithm models N processes (numbered 1 to N) competing for entry into a critical section. The key variables include: `num`, an array of ticket numbers where each process eventually chooses a number to establish priority; `flag`, indicating when a process is in its doorway section; `unchecked`, tracking which other processes remain to be checked; `max`, storing the maximum ticket number seen; `nxt`, the next process to examine; and `previous`, used to detect changes in other processes' ticket numbers.

The algorithm proceeds through phases: a process enters the doorway (e1-e4), choosing a ticket number larger than any observed, then waits (w1-w2) until it has priority over all other processes before entering the critical section. Safe register semantics are modeled through nondeterministic intermediate writes to `num` and `flag`.

The specification includes weak fairness for non-critical-section actions. The primary safety property verified is mutual exclusion—no two processes occupy the critical section simultaneously. This is established through an inductive invariant combining type correctness and a `Before` relation that captures priority ordering between processes.

The module also defines liveness properties (deadlock-freedom and starvation-freedom) but does not include their proofs.
