This TLA+ module specifies a simplified Tendermint consensus protocol extended with proposer-based timestamps (PBTS). The specification models Byzantine fault-tolerant consensus where processes progress through rounds, exchanging proposal, prevote, and precommit messages to reach agreement on a value and timestamp.

**Constants** define the system configuration: sets of correct and faulty processes, thresholds for Byzantine tolerance (N total processes, at most T Byzantine), valid and invalid values, maximum rounds and timestamps, timing parameters (Delay, Precision, Accuracy), a proposer function mapping rounds to proposers, and a clock drift flag.

**Variables** track per-process state including current round, local clock, step (PROPOSE/PREVOTE/PRECOMMIT/DECIDED), decision, locked and valid values/rounds. Additional variables maintain message pools for each message type per round, received timely proposals, evidence for accountability, and timing measurements (real time, consensus start/end times, proposal times).

**Key actions** model the consensus phases: proposers broadcast timestamped proposals; processes receive and filter proposals based on timeliness constraints involving clock precision and message delay; processes vote through prevote and precommit phases based on quorum thresholds (2T+1); decisions occur upon receiving sufficient precommits. Round advancement happens on timeouts or catching up to faster processes.

**Safety invariants** verify agreement on values and timestamps, validity of consensus times relative to local clocks and real time, and bounded message delivery. A liveness property checks that timely proposals from correct proposers are eventually received.
