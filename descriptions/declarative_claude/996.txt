This TLA+ module specifies a refinement relationship between two allocator specifications. It establishes that the scheduling allocator is a correct refinement of a simpler allocator design.

The module extends the SchedulingAllocator module, thereby inheriting all of its constants, variables, and behavioral specification. It then instantiates the SimpleAllocator module, creating a mapping between the scheduling allocator's state and the simple allocator's state.

The core claim is expressed as a theorem stating that the Allocator specification (from SchedulingAllocator) implies the SimpleAllocator specification. This means every behavior permitted by the scheduling allocator is also a valid behavior of the simple allocator—the scheduling version refines the simple version by potentially restricting behaviors while preserving all safety and liveness guarantees.

The model-checking configuration defines three clients and two resources as the concrete values for the system constants. It instructs the model checker to verify the Allocator specification and check that the SimpleAllocator property holds as a temporal property.

This module serves as a verification bridge, confirming that the more sophisticated scheduling allocator correctly implements the abstract contract defined by the simple allocator, ensuring that any system relying on the simple allocator's guarantees can safely substitute the scheduling implementation.
