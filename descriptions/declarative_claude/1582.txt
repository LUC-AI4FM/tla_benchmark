This TLA+ module specifies a simple sequential algorithm demonstrating nondeterministic choice using PlusCal's `either-or` construct.

The algorithm models a single process that begins with two variables, x and y, both initialized to zero. At label "a", the process makes a nondeterministic choice between two branches: either it sets x to 1 and proceeds to label "b", or it sets y to 1 and proceeds to label "c". In the first branch, at "b", it increments x by 1. In the second branch, at "c", it increments y by 1. Both branches then converge at label "d", where an assertion checks that x plus y equals 2. This assertion holds regardless of which branch was taken, since one variable reaches 2 while the other remains 0.

The specification uses a program counter variable pc to track execution progress through labels "a", "b", "c", "d", and "Done". The temporal specification combines the initial state and next-state relation with weak fairness on the Next action, ensuring progress. The Termination property asserts that the process eventually reaches the "Done" state.

Additional state predicates XBranch and ChooseX identify when the x-branch is taken, and PossibleCounts appears to verify TLC coverage statistics for these predicates.
