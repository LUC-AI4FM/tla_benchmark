This TLA+ module defines operators for working with vector clocks, a mechanism used in distributed systems to track causality between events.

The module specifies two fundamental relations on vector clocks: the happened-before relation and concurrency. Two vector clocks are concurrent if neither happened before the other, detected when at least two components show opposite orderings. One clock happened before another if all its components are less than or equal to the corresponding components of the other, with at least one being strictly less.

The main operator `IsCausalOrder` checks whether a log of events respects causal ordering according to their vector clocks. For every pair of entries where one appears earlier in the log, it verifies that either the earlier entry's clock happened before the later one, or they are concurrent. The operator handles vector clocks with different domains by filling missing entries with zero.

The `CausalOrder` operator produces a causally-ordered permutation of a given log. It selects a reordering of the log entries such that the resulting sequence satisfies `IsCausalOrder`. The operator accepts lambda functions to extract the vector clock, originating node, and clock domain from each log entry, allowing flexibility in log structure.

No variables, temporal properties, or model-checking configuration are defined; this module provides reusable library operators for vector clock reasoning.
