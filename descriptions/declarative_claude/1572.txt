This TLA+ module specifies a simple sequential algorithm demonstrating non-deterministic choice using PlusCal's "either-or" construct.

The algorithm models a single process that initializes two variables, x and y, both to zero. At the labeled step "a", the process makes a non-deterministic choice between two branches: either it sets x to 1 and proceeds to step "b", or it sets y to 1 and proceeds to step "c". In step "b", x is incremented by 1. In step "c", y is incremented by 1. Both branches converge at step "d", where an assertion checks that the sum of x and y equals 2.

The module contains three variables: pc (the program counter tracking execution state), x, and y. The initial state sets both x and y to zero with pc at "a".

The temporal specification combines the initial condition with the next-state relation under stuttering, plus weak fairness on all actions, ensuring the algorithm eventually makes progress rather than stuttering indefinitely.

The key property checked is an assertion-based safety property: at step "d", the sum x+y must equal 2. A liveness property, Termination, asserts that the program counter eventually reaches "Done". The algorithm terminates after either branch completes and passes the assertion.
