This TLA+ module serves as a test suite for the `Statistics` module, specifically validating the behavior of a `ChiSquare` operator that performs chi-square statistical tests.

The module defines a single constant function `A` that maps integers 1 through 6 to their corresponding values (creating an array-like structure representing observed frequencies or a distribution).

The specification consists entirely of `ASSUME` statements that verify the `ChiSquare` operator behaves correctly under various conditions. The `ChiSquare` operator takes three arguments: two frequency distributions and a significance level string.

The first three assumptions verify that comparing the distribution `A` against itself passes the chi-square test at significance levels 0.001, 0.01, and 0.5—as expected, since identical distributions should not show statistically significant differences.

The final two assumptions verify that comparing a uniform distribution (all values equal to 6) against the non-uniform distribution `A` correctly fails the chi-square test at significance levels 0.45 and 0.5, indicating these distributions are statistically different.

This module contains no variables, no temporal logic, no state transitions, and no invariants or liveness properties. It functions purely as a compile-time validation that the `ChiSquare` statistical operator from the extended `Statistics` module produces expected results.
