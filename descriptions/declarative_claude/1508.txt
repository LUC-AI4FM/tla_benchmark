Module H is a small TLA+ specification illustrating a system with a single natural-number variable `x` ranging over the set `MyNat` (0 through 30).

The specification `Spec` is defined as the conjunction of the invariant `Inv` and the stuttering-allowed box-next formula `[][Next]_x`. The invariant `Inv` asserts both that `x` belongs to `MyNat` and that `x` equals 0, making it simultaneously the initial condition and the safety property. The next-state relation `Next` is the disjunction of three action schemas: `A` sets `x` unconditionally to 23; `BandC` either moves `x` to some value in 23..25 (when `x` is in `MyNat`) or sets `x` to 26 (when `x < 25`); and `DandE` performs one of two specific transitions, setting `x` to 42 when it equals 23, or to 4711 when it equals 123. Because `Spec` is the conjunction of `Inv` (which requires `x = 0` at all times) with the box-next formula, any transition that changes `x` away from 0 would violate `Spec`. The model checker is directed to check `Spec` as the temporal specification, effectively verifying that no enabled next-state action is ever taken — since all non-stuttering steps would immediately violate the `x = 0` requirement. No separate `PROPERTY` or liveness condition is declared.
