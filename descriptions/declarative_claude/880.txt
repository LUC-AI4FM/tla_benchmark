This TLA+ module provides a mathematical specification for solving ordinary differential equations through integration. It defines the foundational concepts needed to express differential equations and their solutions in a purely declarative, set-theoretic manner.

The module locally imports the Reals and Sequences standard modules and defines several auxiliary concepts: positive reals, open intervals, and epsilon-neighborhoods around real numbers.

The core mathematical machinery consists of two key definitions. The first, IsFirstDeriv, specifies when one function is the first derivative of another using the epsilon-delta definition of limits—a difference quotient must approach the derivative value within any desired tolerance. The second, IsDeriv, generalizes this to nth-order derivatives through recursive application.

The main operator, Integrate, takes four parameters: a differential equation D (expressed as a relation that equals zero), interval endpoints a and b, and a sequence of initial values. It uses CHOOSE to select functions satisfying the differential equation with given initial conditions, returning the solution values at endpoint b. The specification requires existence of a function family where each successive function is the derivative of the previous, initial conditions hold at point a, and the differential equation D is satisfied throughout an extended open interval.

This module serves as a purely mathematical formalization rather than an executable algorithm, expressing differential equation solutions through their defining properties.
