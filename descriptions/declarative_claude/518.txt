This TLA+ module specifies the Alternating Bit Protocol, a simple reliable data transfer protocol operating over unreliable channels that may lose messages.

The system models communication between a sender (process A) and a receiver (process B) connected by two unidirectional channels, with a separate process modeling message loss.

**Constants and Variables:** The constant `Data` represents the set of possible data values. The variables are `AVar` (sender's current message with data and alternating bit), `BVar` (receiver's last accepted message), `AtoB` (channel from sender to receiver as a sequence), and `BtoA` (acknowledgment channel from receiver to sender as a sequence).

**Process Behaviors:** Process A either sends its current message by appending to `AtoB`, or receives an acknowledgment from `BtoA`—if the acknowledgment bit matches, A advances to new data with a flipped bit. Process B either sends an acknowledgment of its current bit, or receives from `AtoB`—accepting the message only if the bit differs from its current bit. Process LoseMsgs nondeterministically removes any message from either channel, modeling unreliable transmission.

**Temporal Specification:** The specification `Spec` requires the initial condition and that every step either follows the `Next` relation or leaves all variables unchanged. No fairness conditions are included, meaning liveness properties are explicitly omitted as noted in the module comments.
