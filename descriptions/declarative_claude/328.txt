The module models a highly simplified version of the Schrödinger's cat thought experiment. It has a single string variable `state`, which is initialized to `"ALIVE"`. The next-state relation is written as a disjunction: either `state` remains unchanged (`state' = state`) or it transitions to `"DEAD"`. This means the cat can stay alive indefinitely or transition to the dead state at any point, and once dead it remains dead (since `state' = state` would preserve `"DEAD"` and `state' = "DEAD"` also preserves it). The invariant `Inv` asserts that `state` must always equal `"ALIVE"`, which will be violated as soon as the system takes the transition to `"DEAD"`. The module thus serves as a minimal demonstration of invariant violation: the safety property `Inv` is not maintained by the specification, because the next-state relation permits a transition that falsifies it. No fairness conditions, liveness properties, or constants are defined.
