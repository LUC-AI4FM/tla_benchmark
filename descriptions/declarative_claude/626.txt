This TLA+ specification models the attacker isolation protocol for Tendermint light clients when a full node receives evidence of conflicting blocks, following the Tendermint specification for isolating attackers.

**System Overview:**
The specification models how a full node identifies Byzantine validators who signed conflicting blocks, violating Tendermint's security model. It assumes each validator has voting power of 1.

**Constants:**
- `AllNodes`: all potential validators
- `COMMON_HEIGHT`: block height where peers agree
- `CONFLICT_HEIGHT`: block height where peers disagree
- `TRUSTING_PERIOD`: validator trust duration
- `FAULTY_RATIO`: upper bound on faulty validator ratio (typically 1/3)

**Variables:**
- `blockchain`: the full node's chain
- `refClock`: reference clock
- `Faulty`: set of Byzantine validators
- `conflictingBlock`: evidence of conflicting blocks
- `state`: isolation machine state (init, Lunatic, Equivocation, or Amnesia)
- `attackers`: identified malicious validators

**Behavior:**
Initialization sets up a blockchain and receives arbitrary conflicting evidence that passes light client verification, with preconditions ensuring more than one-third of validators are faulty. The next-state relation classifies attacks into three types: Lunatic (invalid block fields), Equivocation (same round, different blocks), or Amnesia (different rounds), computing attackers accordingly.

**Invariants:**
- `DetectionCompleteness`: identified attackers control more than one-third of voting power
- `DetectionAccuracy`: only faulty validators are identified as attackers
