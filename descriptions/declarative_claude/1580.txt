This TLA+ module specifies a solution to the Dining Philosophers problem for N philosophers seated in a circle, using an asymmetric fork-pickup strategy to prevent deadlock.

The system models N philosophers sharing N forks represented by the array `sem`, where each semaphore value indicates fork availability (1 means available, 0 means taken). The VARIABLES include `sem` for fork states, `pc` for process control locations, and `stack` for procedure call stacks.

Philosophers 1 through N-1 follow a uniform protocol: acquire their right fork, then their left fork, eat, then release both forks in order. Philosopher 0 (the Leader) breaks symmetry by acquiring forks in the opposite order—left fork first, then right—which prevents the circular wait condition that causes deadlock. The Leader also calls a dummy procedure `foo` during its eating phase.

The specification includes additional dummy processes (DummyProcessSet and DummySingleProc) that perform no meaningful work.

The temporal specification combines initial conditions with the next-state relation under stuttering, along with fairness conditions: weak fairness for most actions and strong fairness for certain critical steps in the eating cycle.

Two properties are defined: an invariant asserting mutual exclusion (no two adjacent philosophers eat simultaneously), and a liveness property `StarvationFree` requiring that every philosopher eventually eats infinitely often.
