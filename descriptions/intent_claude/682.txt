# Specification Request: Dyadic Rational Number Arithmetic

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for an arithmetic system that operates on **dyadic rational numbers** — rational numbers whose denominators are powers of 2 (such as 1/2, 3/4, 7/8, etc.). This number system is useful in various computational contexts where binary subdivision is natural.

## Functional Requirements

The system should provide:

1. **Representation**: A way to represent dyadic rational numbers as fractions (numerator and denominator pairs)

2. **Canonical Form**: A reduction mechanism that simplifies fractions to their lowest terms by removing common factors

3. **Validation**: A predicate to check whether a given rational number is actually dyadic (i.e., its denominator is a power of 2)

4. **Constants**: Representations for fundamental values like zero and one

5. **Arithmetic Operations**:
   - **Addition**: Combine two dyadic rationals into their sum, returning a reduced result
   - **Halving**: Divide a dyadic rational by 2, producing another dyadic rational

6. **Display**: A human-readable string representation for debugging and output purposes

## Key Properties and Correctness Guarantees

The specification must ensure:

1. **Closure under operations**: Adding two dyadic rationals or halving a dyadic rational must produce another dyadic rational

2. **Canonical representation**: Results should be in reduced form (numerator and denominator share no common factors other than 1)

3. **Arithmetic correctness**: The addition operation must compute the mathematically correct sum, properly handling different denominators

4. **Identity elements**: Zero should act as the additive identity (adding zero to any number returns that number unchanged)

## Notes

The implementation can take advantage of the special structure of dyadic rationals — since all denominators are powers of 2, finding common denominators is simplified compared to general rational arithmetic.
