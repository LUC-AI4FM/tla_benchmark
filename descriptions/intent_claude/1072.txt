Specify a TLA+ model of the EWD998 distributed termination-detection algorithm over a ring of named nodes (identified by arbitrary strings or similar tokens rather than natural-number indices). The system consists of a finite, non-empty set of nodes arranged in a directed ring. One arbitrarily chosen node acts as the token initiator. Each node tracks whether it is active, its color (white or black), a message counter, an inbox of in-flight messages, and a vector clock.

Nodes may send payload messages to other nodes (incrementing their counter), receive payload messages (decrementing their counter and turning black), or deactivate themselves. A token circulates the ring carrying an accumulated counter and a color; nodes forward it when idle, updating its fields according to EWD998 rules. The initiator restarts a probe when the returning token indicates the round was inconsistent.

The specification must guarantee two correctness properties: safety, meaning termination is never falsely declared while computation is still ongoing; and liveness, meaning that once the system truly terminates (all nodes inactive and no payload messages in flight), termination is eventually detected. Additionally, the spec must satisfy a bound that termination is detected within at most three token rounds after actual termination occurs. The model must refine a corresponding natural-number-indexed EWD998 channel specification under a straightforward renaming.
