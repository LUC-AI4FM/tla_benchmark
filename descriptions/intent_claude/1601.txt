Specify Fischer's mutual exclusion algorithm for N timed processes sharing a single integer variable used as a lock. Each process repeatedly attempts to enter a critical section by first waiting until the shared variable is zero, then setting a local timer to a delay Delta and writing its own identifier into the shared variable, then resetting the timer to a smaller delay Epsilon and waiting for that timer to expire before re-checking that the shared variable still holds its identifier. A separate global clock process decrements all active timers by one each step, but only when every timer is strictly positive. The key timing parameters are Delta and Epsilon: mutual exclusion is guaranteed only when Delta is strictly less than Epsilon, and a violation should be detectable when Delta is greater than or equal to Epsilon with more than one process. The safety property to verify is mutual exclusion: no two distinct processes may be in the critical section simultaneously. The liveness property to verify is that some process enters the critical section infinitely often. Fairness must be assumed for all processes and the clock.
