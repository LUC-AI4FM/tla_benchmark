# Specification Request: 12-Hour Clock

## System Description

I need a formal specification of a simple 12-hour clock that tracks only the hour value. The clock should model the cyclical progression of hours as seen on a standard analog clock face.

## Behavioral Requirements

1. **Valid Hour Range**: The clock should always display an hour value between 1 and 12, inclusive. There is no hour "0" or hour "13" — it follows the conventional 12-hour format.

2. **Hour Progression**: When the clock advances, it should move to the next hour in sequence. After reaching hour 12, the next advancement should wrap around to hour 1.

3. **Stuttering Allowed**: The system should permit the clock to remain unchanged between steps (modeling that time doesn't always advance in every observation).

## Correctness Properties

The key safety property to verify is that **the hour value always remains within the valid range of 1 through 12**. Once initialized to a valid hour, the clock should never display an invalid hour value, regardless of how many times it advances.
