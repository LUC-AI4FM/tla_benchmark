# Specification Request: Prisoner Switch Puzzle

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for the classic "100 Prisoners and a Light Bulb" puzzle (adapted for a smaller number of prisoners). This is a coordination problem where prisoners must collectively determine when all of them have visited a room, using only a limited communication mechanism.

## Problem Description

A group of prisoners are held in isolation. They are brought one at a time, in an arbitrary order chosen by the warden, to a room containing two switches. The prisoners may be brought to the room multiple times, and the selection is entirely under the warden's control (potentially adversarial).

Before the process begins, the prisoners can agree on a strategy. Once the visits begin, the only communication between prisoners is through the positions of the two switches in the room—they cannot communicate in any other way.

## Requirements

### Basic Mechanics
- There are multiple prisoners, one of whom is designated with a special coordinating role
- The room contains exactly two binary switches (each can be up or down)
- When a prisoner enters the room, they observe both switch positions and may flip zero, one, or both switches before leaving
- The warden controls which prisoner visits the room and when; this selection is non-deterministic and potentially unfair in the short term

### Goal
- At some point, a prisoner must be able to declare with certainty that every prisoner has visited the room at least once
- This declaration must only happen when it is actually true—a false declaration is not allowed

### Strategy Constraints
- The strategy must work regardless of the initial switch positions (which are unknown to the prisoners)
- The strategy must work for any sequence of prisoner visits chosen by the warden, as long as every prisoner is eventually brought to the room infinitely often (weak fairness)

## Correctness Properties

1. **Safety**: A prisoner may only declare "done" when every prisoner has indeed visited the room at least once

2. **Liveness**: If every prisoner is guaranteed to eventually visit the room (repeatedly), then eventually a prisoner will correctly declare that all have visited

## Objective

Model this puzzle and verify that the counting-based strategy (where one designated prisoner counts arrivals signaled through switch manipulation) correctly solves the problem under the stated fairness assumptions.
