Specify the Cigarette Smokers Problem as a TLA+ model. There are three ingredients (matches, paper, tobacco) and three smokers, each of whom possesses an infinite supply of exactly one ingredient. A dealer repeatedly places two of the three ingredients on the table. The smoker who holds the missing ingredient picks up the offered pair, combines them with their own supply, and smokes; when done smoking, the dealer places a new pair on the table. The system should be parameterized by a set of ingredients and a set of offers, where each offer is a subset of ingredients of size one less than the full set. The model must capture the dealer selecting an offer, a smoker recognizing they hold the complementary ingredient and beginning to smoke, and the smoker eventually finishing and signaling the dealer to proceed. The key safety property is mutual exclusion: at most one smoker may be smoking at any given moment. The specification should also support a fairness condition under which every offer eventually leads to a smoker smoking, ensuring the system does not deadlock or stall indefinitely.
