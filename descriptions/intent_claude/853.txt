# Specification Request: The Prisoners and Light Switch Puzzle

## System Overview

I need a formal specification of the classic "100 Prisoners and a Light Switch" puzzle (or a parameterized version with N prisoners). This is a coordination problem where prisoners must devise a strategy to eventually announce that all prisoners have visited a room, using only a light switch as their means of communication.

## Problem Description

A group of prisoners are held in separate cells with no way to communicate directly. They are taken one at a time, in some arbitrary order, to a central room that contains a light switch (or possibly two switches). When a prisoner enters the room, they can observe the current state of the switch(es) and optionally toggle it. At any point, a prisoner may declare that all prisoners have visited the room at least once. If the declaration is correct, all prisoners go free; if incorrect, they all face consequences.

Before the process begins, the prisoners can agree on a strategy. The challenge is to devise a protocol that guarantees eventual correct declaration.

## Key Elements to Model

- A designated "counter" prisoner who has a special role in the protocol
- One or two light switches that serve as the only communication mechanism
- The state tracking needed for the counting strategy (how many times prisoners have signaled)
- Non-deterministic selection of which prisoner visits the room at each step

## Requirements

The specification should model a common solution strategy where:
- Regular prisoners signal their first visit(s) by toggling a switch in a specific direction
- The designated counter prisoner counts these signals and tracks progress toward the total
- The counter eventually knows when all prisoners have visited

## Correctness Properties

1. **Safety**: The counter never declares that all prisoners have visited unless this is actually true—every prisoner must have entered the room at least once before any announcement.

2. **Liveness**: Under fair scheduling (every prisoner eventually gets another turn), the protocol eventually terminates with a correct declaration.

## Parameters

The specification should be parameterized by:
- The set of prisoners
- Which prisoner serves as the designated counter
