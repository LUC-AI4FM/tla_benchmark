The specification should include a type invariant ensuring that every node's state always remains within the bounds of what is considered a legal set value.

**Intent:**
This specification appears designed to explore or demonstrate a scenario where the system's transition relation allows updates that would violate the type constraints—specifically, where an action could assign a value that falls outside the legal domain.
