# Specification Request: Incremental Counter with Evenness Guarantee

## System Description

I need a formal specification for a simple counting system that repeatedly increases a numeric counter by a fixed increment of 2. The counter starts at zero and runs indefinitely, adding 2 to its current value in each step.

## Behavioral Requirements

1. The counter must begin at zero
2. The system must continuously increment the counter by exactly 2 in each step
3. The system runs forever (non-terminating loop)

## Correctness Properties

The specification must formally prove two key invariants:

1. **Type Safety**: The counter value must always remain a natural number (non-negative integer)

2. **Evenness Invariant**: The counter value must always be even (divisible by 2)

## Purpose

This is intended as a basic exercise in formal verification and proof development. The goal is to demonstrate that a simple arithmetic loop maintains a mathematical property throughout its infinite execution—specifically, that starting from an even number (zero) and always adding an even increment (two) preserves evenness at every step.
