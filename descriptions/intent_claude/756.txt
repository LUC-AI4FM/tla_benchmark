# Specification Request: Water Jug Puzzle Solver

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for the classic "Die Hard" water jug puzzle, generalized to work with any number of jugs of varying capacities. The goal is to model the process of measuring out an exact target amount of water using a set of unmarked jugs and an unlimited water supply.

## Problem Description

The system consists of a collection of jugs, each with a known maximum capacity but no measurement markings. The only operations available are the physical actions one could perform with real jugs:

1. **Fill a jug completely** from an unlimited water source
2. **Empty a jug completely** by pouring its contents out
3. **Pour water from one jug into another**, stopping when either the source jug is empty or the destination jug is full (whichever comes first)

## Requirements

The specification should:

- Support an arbitrary set of jugs with configurable capacities
- Track the current water level in each jug
- Model all three types of operations (fill, empty, pour between jugs)
- Ensure water is conserved during pouring operations
- Never allow a jug to contain more water than its capacity or a negative amount

## Correctness Properties

The primary goal is to determine whether it's possible to reach a state where at least one jug contains exactly the target amount of water. The specification should be suitable for model checking to either:

- Find a sequence of actions that achieves the goal amount
- Verify that no such sequence exists for given jug capacities and goal

## Example Configuration

A typical instance would involve two jugs (e.g., a 3-gallon jug and a 5-gallon jug) with a goal of measuring exactly 4 gallons—the classic puzzle from the movie "Die Hard with a Vengeance."
