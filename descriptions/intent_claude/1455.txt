Write a TLA+ specification that demonstrates and verifies the behavior of the subset-of-powerset (`\subseteq` applied to `SUBSET` expressions) operator in TLA+. The module should use a single boolean variable that is set to the conjunction of several subset-equality test cases involving integer ranges and explicit sets, confirming that the operator behaves correctly. The test cases should verify that the powerset of a smaller integer range is a subset of the powerset of a larger range but not vice versa, that removing an element from a range yields a powerset that is a subset of the original but not the reverse, that nested set inclusions work for small explicit sets, that the powerset of the empty set is a subset of itself and of any other powerset but not vice versa, and that the powerset of a singleton is a subset of the powerset of the integers and natural numbers. The invariant should assert that the boolean variable remains true and is a member of BOOLEAN throughout execution.
