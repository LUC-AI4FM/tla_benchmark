Please write a formal specification for a **PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction) thermal cycling process**.

The system should model the stages of a PCR amplification protocol, tracking:
- The temperature state of the reaction
- The quantities of key molecular components: DNA strands, primers, templates, and primer-template hybrids
- The current stage within the thermal cycling protocol
- The number of completed cycles

The specification should capture how the reaction progresses through its characteristic temperature-dependent stages (such as denaturation, annealing, and extension), and how molecular quantities change as the reaction proceeds through multiple amplification cycles.

**Key correctness properties the specification should guarantee:**
1. The system follows a valid sequence of temperature stages according to PCR protocol
2. Molecular quantities remain non-negative and change consistently with the biochemical reactions occurring at each stage
3. The cycle count accurately reflects completed rounds of amplification
4. The relationship between temperature stages and molecular state transitions is properly maintained (e.g., DNA denatures at high temperature, primers anneal at lower temperature)

The specification should be suitable for verification with model checking tools to ensure the protocol logic is correctly captured.
