Specify a simple producer-consumer system where:

- A producer can add new items to a shared collection at any time. Items are drawn from a fixed alphabet of possible values.
- A consumer can only act when the collection is non-empty, and it removes some (possibly all) items from the collection.
- The system tracks whether the collection is currently empty.

The specification should model the interleaving of produce and consume operations on this shared state. Key properties to verify would include that the consumer never attempts to consume from an empty collection, and that the empty/non-empty status accurately reflects the actual state of the collection.
