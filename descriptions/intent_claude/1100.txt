Specify a simple mutual-exclusion lock shared by two processes. The system has a single binary lock variable and two processes that repeatedly execute a non-critical section, acquire the lock, execute a critical section, and then release the lock. Acquiring the lock must atomically wait until the lock is free and then mark it as taken; releasing it marks it as free again. The specification must prove two correctness properties: a type-correctness invariant confirming the lock is always either free or held and each process is always in one of its four control locations, and a mutual-exclusion invariant establishing that no two processes can be in their critical section or post-critical-section location simultaneously, and that whenever any process holds the lock the lock variable reflects that. Both properties should be verified with machine-checked TLAPS proofs, including the inductive invariant argument showing the mutual-exclusion property is preserved by every possible step of the system.
