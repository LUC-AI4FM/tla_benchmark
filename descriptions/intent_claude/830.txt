# Specification Request: Multi-Car Elevator System

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for a multi-car elevator system serving a building with multiple floors. The system manages several elevator cars that transport people between floors in response to hall calls and destination requests.

## System Components

The system should model:

1. **People** who want to travel between floors, each with a current location and a desired destination floor
2. **Elevator cars** that move between floors, each with its own position, movement direction, door state, and set of requested floor buttons
3. **Hall calls** representing requests from people waiting on floors who want to go in a particular direction (up or down)

## Required Behaviors

The system must support the following operations:

1. **Destination selection**: People can choose new destination floors
2. **Calling an elevator**: A person waiting on a floor can request an elevator going in their desired direction
3. **Elevator dispatch**: The system assigns elevators to respond to hall calls
4. **Door operations**: Elevators can open and close their doors when appropriate
5. **Passenger boarding**: People waiting on a floor can enter an elevator that arrives going in their direction
6. **Passenger exiting**: People inside an elevator can exit when it reaches their destination floor
7. **Elevator movement**: Elevators can move up or down between floors
8. **Stopping**: Elevators can become stationary when they have no more requests to serve

## Key Correctness Properties

### Safety Properties

The specification must guarantee:

1. **Type safety**: All system state remains well-formed (people are either on floors or in elevators, elevator positions are valid floors, directions are valid, etc.)

2. **Consistent passenger direction**: When a person is inside an elevator, the elevator must be traveling in the direction that will take them toward their destination (not away from it). Specifically, if someone's destination is above the elevator's current floor, the elevator should be going up, and vice versa.

3. **Proper floor button tracking**: Floor buttons pressed inside an elevator accurately reflect the destinations of passengers currently aboard

4. **Hall call consistency**: Active hall calls reflect people actually waiting to travel in those directions

### Additional Consistency Requirements

The system should maintain coherent relationships between:
- A person's waiting status and their location (people in elevators are not "waiting")
- Elevator door states and movement (elevators shouldn't move with doors open)
- Elevator direction and passenger presence (stationary elevators should be empty)
- The servicing of calls when doors open (calls should be cleared when answered)

## Goal

Create a specification that models realistic elevator behavior where multiple cars coordinate to transport people efficiently while maintaining all safety guarantees. The specification should be suitable for formal verification of the safety properties described above.
