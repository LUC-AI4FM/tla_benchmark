# Specification Request: Single-Lane Bridge Traffic System

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for a single-lane bridge traffic control system. The scenario involves vehicles approaching a narrow bridge from both directions (left and right), where the bridge can only safely accommodate traffic flowing in one direction at a time.

## System Requirements

### Environment Model
- There are two groups of cars: those traveling from the left side and those traveling from the right side
- The roadway consists of a series of positions, with a designated subset of those positions representing the bridge itself
- Cars move along these positions as they travel across the bridge

### Behavioral Requirements
- Cars should be able to enter and traverse the bridge
- The system must manage traffic flow to prevent collisions
- Cars from opposite directions must not occupy the bridge simultaneously (mutual exclusion on the bridge)
- Cars should be able to progress through their journey without permanent blocking

## Key Correctness Properties

### Safety Properties
- **No head-on collisions**: Cars traveling in opposite directions must never be on the bridge at the same time
- **No physical overlap**: Cars should not occupy the same position simultaneously

### Liveness Properties
- **Fair access**: Cars from both directions should eventually be able to cross the bridge
- **Progress**: Every car waiting to cross should eventually get the opportunity to be on the bridge (no starvation)

## Verification Goals

The specification should be checkable with a small number of cars on each side (e.g., 2 cars from each direction) and a reasonably sized position space that includes both approach areas and the bridge section itself. The model should verify that all cars can repeatedly access the bridge fairly over time.
