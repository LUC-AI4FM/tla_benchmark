# Specification Request: Atomic Commitment Protocol

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for an **atomic commitment protocol** — a distributed consensus mechanism where a group of participants must collectively agree on whether to commit or abort a transaction.

## System Description

The system involves multiple distributed participants that must coordinate to reach a unanimous decision. Each participant can vote on whether they are prepared to commit, and based on all votes, the system must reach a global decision that all participants agree upon.

## Key Requirements

### Decision Properties

1. **Agreement**: All participants that reach a decision must reach the *same* decision — either all commit or all abort. No participant should commit while another aborts.

2. **Validity/Justification**: 
   - A commit decision can only happen if all participants were willing to commit
   - An abort decision is always a valid outcome (safety valve)

3. **Termination**: Under appropriate conditions, all participants should eventually reach a decision.

### Irrevocability

4. **Decision Stability**: Once a participant has decided to commit, that decision cannot be changed — it must remain committed forever. Similarly, once a participant has decided to abort, that decision is final and permanent. Decisions must be **irrevocable**.

## Correctness Properties to Verify

The specification should guarantee:

- **Safety**: No inconsistent decisions across participants (one commits while another aborts)
- **Stability**: Decisions, once made, never change
- **Proper commit conditions**: Commits only occur when justified by participant votes

## Model Checking Considerations

The specification should be suitable for exhaustive verification using a model checker, taking advantage of the symmetry among participants (since participants are interchangeable from the protocol's perspective) to reduce the state space that must be explored.

---

This protocol is fundamental to distributed databases and transaction processing systems where atomicity guarantees are required across multiple nodes.
