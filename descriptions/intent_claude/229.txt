# Specification Request: Sliding Block Puzzle Solver

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for a sliding block puzzle game, specifically modeling the classic Klotski puzzle. The system should represent a grid-based puzzle where multiple pieces of various shapes can slide around, with the goal of moving a specific large piece to a target location.

## Problem Description

The puzzle consists of a rectangular board (4 units wide by 5 units tall) containing several pieces of different sizes and shapes. The pieces include:
- One large 2×2 square piece (this is the piece that must reach the goal)
- Several 1×2 vertical rectangular pieces
- Several 2×1 horizontal rectangular pieces  
- Several 1×1 single-square pieces

The board has exactly enough empty space for pieces to slide. Pieces cannot overlap, cannot leave the board boundaries, and can only move in cardinal directions (up, down, left, right).

## Initial Configuration

The puzzle starts in the classic Klotski arrangement with pieces positioned in a specific initial layout, with the large 2×2 piece starting near the top of the board.

## Goal Condition

The puzzle is solved when the large 2×2 piece occupies the bottom-center position of the board (specifically, covering the four positions that form a 2×2 square at the bottom middle of the grid).

## Movement Rules

1. A piece can only move if there is empty space adjacent to it in the direction of movement
2. The entire piece must be able to fit in its new position (all squares of the piece must land on valid board positions)
3. The piece's new position cannot overlap with any other piece
4. Only one piece moves at a time, sliding by exactly one unit in one of the four cardinal directions

## Correctness Properties

The specification should enable verification of:
1. **Type Safety**: The board state always consists of a valid collection of pieces, where each piece is a set of grid positions
2. **Reachability**: Whether the goal state (large piece at bottom-center) is reachable from the initial configuration
3. **State Space Exploration**: The model should generate all valid puzzle states reachable through legal moves, enabling analysis of solution paths

## Purpose

This specification is intended to model the state space of the Klotski puzzle to verify that a solution exists and to understand the structure of reachable configurations through formal methods.
