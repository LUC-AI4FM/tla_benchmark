Specification Request: Fair Sequential Counter with Termination

**System to Model:**
A simple sequential counting process that increments a counter from zero up to a fixed target value and then terminates.

**Behavioral Requirements:**
- The system maintains a single counter that starts at zero.
- The process repeatedly increments the counter by one as long as it remains below the target value (10).
- Once the counter reaches the target, the process stops executing.

**Fairness Requirements:**
- The system must guarantee fair execution, meaning the counting process cannot be indefinitely postponed. If the counter has not yet reached its target, progress must eventually be made.

**Key Correctness Property:**
- **Termination:** The system must eventually complete its execution. Under fair scheduling, the process is guaranteed to reach its terminal state where the counter equals the target value.
