# Specification Request: Single-Lane Bridge Traffic Control

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for a **single-lane bridge traffic control system**. The bridge connects two sides and can only accommodate traffic flowing in one direction at a time. Cars approach the bridge from both directions and must be coordinated to cross safely.

## System Description

The scenario involves:
- Two groups of cars: those traveling from the right side and those traveling from the left side
- A road with discrete positions, where a subset of those positions constitutes the bridge itself
- Cars that move along the road and must cross the bridge to reach the other side

## Behavioral Requirements

1. **Car Movement**: Cars should be able to move along the road, progressing through positions toward their destination on the opposite side.

2. **Bridge Access Control**: Since the bridge is single-lane, cars traveling in opposite directions cannot be on the bridge simultaneously. There must be a mechanism to manage which cars are allowed to enter the bridge.

3. **Waiting Mechanism**: When a car cannot immediately enter the bridge (because opposing traffic is present or has priority), it should wait in a queue before the bridge entrance.

4. **Fairness**: Cars should eventually be able to cross; the system should prevent indefinite starvation of either direction.

## Key Correctness Properties

1. **Safety - No Collisions**: At no point should cars traveling in opposite directions occupy the bridge at the same time. This is the fundamental safety invariant.

2. **Safety - No Overtaking on Bridge**: Cars should not occupy the same position simultaneously (no collisions anywhere on the road).

3. **Liveness - Progress**: Every car that wants to cross the bridge should eventually be able to do so (no deadlock or permanent starvation).

## Configuration Parameters

The specification should be parameterized by:
- The set of cars approaching from each direction
- The total set of positions on the road
- Which positions constitute the bridge (a contiguous subset of all positions)

This allows verification with different numbers of cars and bridge lengths to explore the system's behavior under various conditions.
