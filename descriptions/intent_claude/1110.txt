# Specification Request: Even Sum Property Verification

## System Overview

I need a formal specification that verifies a mathematical property about the sum of two even numbers.

## Goal

The specification should model the addition of two even natural numbers and prove that the result is always even.

## Requirements

1. **Domain**: Work with natural numbers, specifically even natural numbers.

2. **Core Property to Verify**: When any two even numbers are added together, their sum must also be an even number.

3. **Model Checking Constraints**: For practical verification, the specification should support bounding the natural numbers to a finite range so that model checking can exhaustively verify the property.

## Correctness Properties

The specification must guarantee:

- **Closure of Even Numbers Under Addition**: For all pairs of even natural numbers, their sum is even. This is the fundamental theorem that needs to be formally stated and verified.

## Additional Notes

The specification should clearly define what it means for a number to be "even" and express the universal property as a theorem that can be checked by the TLA+ toolbox.
