Please write a formal specification for a **DNA amplification process** (such as PCR - Polymerase Chain Reaction).

## System Description

The system should model the core biochemical steps of DNA amplification, where short DNA primers bind to template DNA strands to enable replication. The process involves tracking the quantities of key molecular components as they interact and transform through the amplification cycle.

## Key Components to Model

- **DNA strands**: The target genetic material to be amplified
- **Primers**: Short synthetic DNA sequences that initiate replication
- **Templates**: DNA strands available for primer binding
- **Primer-template hybrids**: The intermediate molecular complexes formed when primers bind to templates

## Required Behavior

The specification should capture:

1. The cycling nature of the amplification process (temperature-dependent phases)
2. Primer annealing (binding) to available template strands
3. Extension/synthesis of new DNA strands from primer-template complexes
4. Denaturation (separation) of double-stranded DNA into single strands
5. Conservation of molecular quantities through each transformation step

## Correctness Properties

The specification should guarantee:

1. **Conservation of mass**: The total amount of genetic material (accounting for primers, templates, DNA, and hybrids) remains consistent with what was added to the system
2. **Non-negativity**: Quantities of all molecular species remain non-negative throughout the process
3. **Proper sequencing**: The biochemical reactions occur in the correct order according to the thermal cycling phases
4. **Amplification progress**: Under appropriate conditions, the amount of target DNA increases with each cycle
