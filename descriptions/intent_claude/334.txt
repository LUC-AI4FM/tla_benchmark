Please write a formal specification for a **driving license eligibility verification system** that must correctly handle Year 2000 (Y2K) date representation issues.

**System Description:**
The system determines whether a person is old enough to obtain a driving license based on their birth year. The core challenge is that years may be represented using only two digits (e.g., "80" for 1980), which creates ambiguity around the year 2000 boundary.

**Requirements:**
1. The system must track a person's birth year and compare it against the current year to determine age
2. There must be a configurable minimum age requirement for obtaining a license (e.g., 18 years)
3. The birth year should be configurable as a parameter (e.g., someone born in 1980, represented as "80")

**Key Correctness Properties:**
1. The system must correctly calculate a person's age even when years wrap around from 99 to 00
2. A person should only be granted license eligibility when they have actually reached the minimum required age
3. The age calculation must handle the Y2K transition correctly—someone born in 1980 turning 18 in 1998 should be handled the same as edge cases near the century boundary

**Goal:**
Demonstrate how two-digit year representations can lead to incorrect age calculations during the Y2K transition, and verify that any proposed solution correctly determines license eligibility across this boundary.
