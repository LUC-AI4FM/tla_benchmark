# Specification Request: Missionaries and Cannibals River Crossing Puzzle

## System Overview

I need a formal specification that models the classic "Missionaries and Cannibals" river crossing puzzle and can be used to find a valid solution.

## Problem Description

Three missionaries and three cannibals need to cross a river using a single boat. The boat can carry at most two people and cannot cross the river empty—at least one person must operate it for each crossing.

## Safety Constraint

The critical constraint is that missionaries must never be outnumbered by cannibals on either riverbank. If cannibals outnumber missionaries on any bank where missionaries are present, the cannibals will eat the missionaries. This safety condition must hold at all times during the crossing—both before and after each boat trip.

## What the Specification Should Model

- Two riverbanks (east and west)
- The current location of the boat
- The current location of all people (which bank each missionary and cannibal is on)
- Boat trips as atomic transitions: each step represents moving one or two people from the boat's current bank to the opposite bank

## Initial and Goal States

- **Initial state**: Everyone (all missionaries and all cannibals) starts on the east bank, along with the boat.
- **Goal state**: Everyone has successfully crossed to the west bank.

## Correctness Properties

1. **Type correctness**: The boat is always at one of the two banks, and each person is always on exactly one bank.

2. **Safety invariant**: At every reachable state, if there are any missionaries on a bank, they must not be outnumbered by cannibals on that same bank.

3. **Solution finding**: The specification should be structured so that a model checker can find a sequence of moves (a counterexample to "someone is still on the east bank") that transports everyone safely to the west bank.

## Purpose

The primary goal is to use model checking to discover a valid solution to the puzzle—a sequence of boat crossings that safely moves all six people from east to west without ever violating the safety constraint.
