# Specification Request: Integer Division Algorithm

## System Overview
I need a formal specification of an integer division algorithm that computes the quotient and remainder when dividing two integers. The algorithm should handle both positive and negative dividends while working with a positive divisor.

## Functional Requirements

1. **Input**: The system takes two integers:
   - A dividend (which can be positive, negative, or zero)
   - A divisor (which must be non-zero)

2. **Output**: The system must compute:
   - The integer quotient of the division
   - The remainder after division

3. **Algorithm Behavior**: The algorithm should iteratively compute the quotient by repeatedly subtracting (or adding, for negative dividends) the divisor from the dividend while tracking how many times this operation is performed.

## Correctness Properties

1. **Division Correctness**: Upon termination, the computed quotient and remainder must satisfy the fundamental division equation: `dividend = quotient × divisor + remainder`

2. **Remainder Bounds**: The remainder must be properly bounded (non-negative and less than the absolute value of the divisor)

3. **Sign Handling**: The algorithm must correctly handle negative dividends, producing appropriately signed quotients

4. **Termination**: The algorithm must always terminate for valid inputs

## Testing Parameters
- The dividend should be tested across a range of negative and positive integers (excluding zero from the divisor set)
- The divisor should be tested across a reasonable range of non-negative integers
