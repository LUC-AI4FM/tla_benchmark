# Specification Request: Water Jug Puzzle

## System to Model

I need a formal specification of the classic "Die Hard" water jug puzzle. The puzzle involves two jugs of different capacities that must be manipulated to measure out a specific quantity of water.

## Problem Description

There are two water jugs:
- A small jug that holds exactly 3 gallons
- A large jug that holds exactly 5 gallons

Both jugs start empty. There is an unlimited water supply and a drain. The goal is to measure out exactly 4 gallons of water (which must end up in the large jug, since the small one can't hold that much).

## Allowed Operations

The following operations can be performed at any time:
1. **Fill either jug completely** from the water supply
2. **Empty either jug completely** into the drain
3. **Pour water from one jug to the other** until either the source jug is empty or the destination jug is full (whichever happens first)

## Requirements

The specification should:
- Track the current water level in each jug
- Model all six possible operations (fill small, fill big, empty small, empty big, pour small into big, pour big into small)
- Ensure water is conserved during pouring operations
- Ensure jugs never contain negative water or exceed their capacity

## Correctness Properties

1. **Type Safety**: The small jug should always contain between 0 and 3 gallons, and the large jug should always contain between 0 and 5 gallons.

2. **Goal Reachability** (for analysis): The specification should allow exploring whether it's possible to reach a state where exactly 4 gallons are measured out.

The purpose is to use model checking to find a sequence of operations that solves the puzzle, or to verify properties about the reachable states.
