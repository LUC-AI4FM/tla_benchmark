Please write a formal specification for **Dijkstra's mutual exclusion algorithm**.

The system should model multiple concurrent processes that compete to enter a critical section. The algorithm must coordinate these processes so that they can safely access a shared resource without conflicts.

**Key requirements:**

1. **Mutual Exclusion**: At any point in time, at most one process may be in the critical section. No two processes should ever be in their critical sections simultaneously.

2. **Deadlock Freedom**: The system must guarantee progress—if one or more processes are trying to enter the critical section, eventually some process will succeed in entering it. The algorithm should never reach a state where processes are waiting indefinitely with none able to proceed.

The specification should capture Dijkstra's classic solution to this problem, modeling the protocol that processes follow to request entry, wait their turn, enter the critical section, and then exit.

For verification purposes, the model should be testable with a small finite set of processes (e.g., three processes) to check that the mutual exclusion invariant always holds and that the deadlock freedom property is satisfied across all possible executions.
