# Specification Request: Balance Scale Weighing Puzzle

## System Overview

I need a formal specification to solve a classic puzzle: Given a stone of a certain total weight, find how to break it into a fixed number of pieces such that any integer weight from 1 up to the total can be measured using a two-pan balance scale.

## Problem Description

The scenario involves:
- A stone with a known total weight (e.g., 40 pounds)
- A balance scale with two pans
- The ability to break the stone into a specified number of pieces (e.g., 4 pieces)

The key insight is that when using a balance scale, weights can be placed on *either* side. This means pieces can go on the same side as the item being weighed (effectively subtracting from the target) or on the opposite side (adding to the target).

## Requirements

1. **Valid Decomposition**: The pieces must have positive integer weights that sum to the total stone weight.

2. **Complete Coverage**: For every integer weight from 1 through the total weight, there must exist a way to partition some subset of the pieces between the two scale pans such that equilibrium is achieved when that weight of material is on one side.

3. **Representation Constraint**: When balancing a target weight, the pieces placed with the target plus the target weight itself must equal the total weight of pieces placed on the opposite pan. Pieces used on one side cannot simultaneously be used on the other.

4. **Generality**: The specification should be parameterized by the total weight and the number of pieces, allowing exploration of different problem sizes.

## Correctness Properties

The specification should identify all valid solutions—that is, all ways to break the stone such that every required weight can be balanced. A decomposition is a solution if and only if:

- The piece weights are positive integers summing to the total
- For each target weight from 1 to the total, there exist two disjoint subsets of pieces where the target plus one subset's total weight equals the other subset's total weight

## Expected Output

The specification should enable computing the set of all solutions. For verification purposes, it should also be possible to expand any solution to show, for each target weight, which pieces go on which side of the scale.
