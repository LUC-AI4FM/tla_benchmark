# Specification Request: DNA Amplification Process Model

## System Overview

I need a formal specification of a simplified DNA amplification process, similar to the Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR). The model should capture the key biochemical phases of DNA replication and track the quantities of molecular components as they transform through heating and cooling cycles.

## System Description

The system models a reaction mixture containing:
- **DNA strands** (double-stranded, complete molecules)
- **Template strands** (single strands available for replication)
- **Primer molecules** (short sequences that initiate replication)
- **Hybrid complexes** (primer-template pairs ready for extension)

The process consists of distinct phases that occur during thermal cycling:

1. **Heating phase**: Double-stranded DNA and hybrid complexes denature (separate), releasing their component strands back into the pool of available templates and primers.

2. **Cooling phase**: The system transitions to a state where annealing can occur.

3. **Annealing phase**: Free primers bind to complementary template strands, forming hybrid complexes. Some number of primers and templates pair up simultaneously.

4. **Extension phase**: Hybrid complexes are extended by polymerase, completing replication and producing new double-stranded DNA molecules.

## Configuration Parameters

The system should be parameterized by:
- An initial quantity of DNA molecules
- An initial quantity of primer molecules

Both parameters must be non-negative integers.

## Required Correctness Properties

The specification must guarantee the following safety properties:

1. **Type Safety**: All molecular counts remain non-negative integers throughout execution.

2. **Non-negative Primers**: The primer count never becomes negative (primers are never over-consumed).

3. **Conservation of Genetic Material**: The total amount of genetic material in the system is conserved. Specifically, there should be an invariant relationship between the counts of templates, primers, DNA, and hybrids that holds in every reachable state—representing that nucleotides are neither created nor destroyed, only rearranged between molecular forms.

## Goal

Provide a formal model that captures the essential dynamics of DNA amplification while proving that the conservation law and type invariants hold across all possible execution sequences.
