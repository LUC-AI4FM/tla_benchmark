# Specification Request: Round-Robin Mutual Exclusion Protocol

## System Overview

I need a formal specification of a mutual exclusion protocol for a fixed number of concurrent processes that take turns accessing a critical section in strict round-robin order.

## System Description

The system consists of N processes (numbered 0 through N-1) that repeatedly cycle through phases of non-critical work, waiting to enter, executing in a critical section, and then exiting. Access to the critical section is governed by a simple turn-based mechanism where processes must wait until it is their designated turn before proceeding.

When a process finishes its critical section, it passes the turn to the next process in circular order (wrapping from the last process back to the first).

## Requirements

1. **Process Lifecycle**: Each process continuously cycles through: performing non-critical work, waiting for permission to enter the critical section, executing in the critical section, and then releasing access.

2. **Turn-Based Entry**: A process may only enter the critical section when a shared turn indicator shows it is that process's turn.

3. **Circular Handoff**: Upon exiting the critical section, a process must advance the turn to the next process in sequence, cycling back to process 0 after the highest-numbered process.

4. **Initial State**: The system may start with any process holding the initial turn.

## Correctness Properties to Verify

1. **Mutual Exclusion**: At most one process may be in the critical section at any time.

2. **Fairness Considerations**: The round-robin structure should ensure that if all processes are participating, each process eventually gets its turn (though this depends on fairness assumptions about process scheduling).

## Parameters

- N: The total number of processes (must be a positive integer)
- Processes are identified by integers from 0 to N-1
