# Specification Request: Differential Equation Solver

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for a mathematical system that solves ordinary differential equations (ODEs) through integration. The system should model the mathematical semantics of finding a function that satisfies a given differential equation, along with specified initial conditions, and then evaluating that solution at a target point.

## Core Functionality

The system should:

1. **Accept a differential equation** of arbitrary order, expressed as a relationship that must equal zero when evaluated with the independent variable and the function's derivatives up to some order.

2. **Accept initial conditions** specifying the values of the function and its derivatives at a starting point.

3. **Compute the solution** by finding a sufficiently smooth function (defined on an open interval containing the region of interest) that simultaneously:
   - Satisfies the differential equation at every point in its domain
   - Matches all provided initial conditions at the starting point

4. **Return the evaluated results** of the solution function and its derivatives at a specified endpoint.

## Mathematical Requirements

The specification must formally capture:

- **Derivative definition**: The classical epsilon-delta definition of derivatives, where a derivative at a point is the limit of difference quotients as the interval shrinks to zero.

- **Higher-order derivatives**: The ability to define and work with derivatives of any order, built inductively from the first derivative definition.

- **Smoothness requirements**: The solution function must be differentiable enough times (matching the order of the equation) on an open neighborhood extending beyond the interval of interest.

## Key Correctness Properties

1. **Well-definedness**: The chosen solution must genuinely satisfy the differential equation at all points in its domain.

2. **Initial condition satisfaction**: At the starting point, the function and each of its derivatives must exactly match the corresponding initial values provided.

3. **Continuity of solution**: The solution should exist on a slightly larger domain than strictly required, ensuring the solution is well-behaved at the boundary points.
