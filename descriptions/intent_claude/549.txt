# Specification Request: Mutual Exclusion Protocol

## System to Model

I need a formal specification of a mutual exclusion protocol for concurrent processes. The system should model multiple processes (configurable, but typically around 3 for verification purposes) that compete for access to a shared critical section.

## Required Behavior

The system should model processes that:
- Operate concurrently and may attempt to enter a critical section at any time
- Follow some protocol to coordinate access to the critical section
- Eventually enter and exit the critical section

## Key Correctness Properties

### Safety Property
**Mutual Exclusion**: At most one process may be in the critical section at any given time. No two distinct processes should ever be in the critical section simultaneously.

### Liveness Property
**Starvation Freedom**: Every process should eventually be able to enter the critical section. No process should be permanently blocked from accessing the critical section while other processes continue to make progress.

## Verification Scope

The specification should be parameterized to allow checking with a small number of processes (e.g., 3) to make model checking feasible while still catching concurrency bugs that arise from process interactions.
