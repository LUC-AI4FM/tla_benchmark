# Specification Request: 12-Hour Clock

## System Description

I need a formal specification of a simple 12-hour clock that tracks only the hour.

## Behavioral Requirements

The clock should:
- Display hours in the range 1 through 12 (standard 12-hour format)
- Start at any valid hour within that range
- Advance by incrementing the hour, wrapping from 12 back to 1

## Correctness Properties

The specification must guarantee that the hour value always remains valid—that is, the clock can never display an hour outside the 1-to-12 range. This invariant should hold in the initial state and be preserved by every possible transition of the system.
