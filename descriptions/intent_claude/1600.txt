Write a TLA+ specification for Lamport's Fast Mutex algorithm for N processes competing for a critical section. Each process repeatedly attempts to acquire the lock using two shared registers and an array of per-process flags. The entry protocol works in two phases: a process first announces its interest and writes its identity to a shared variable, then checks a second shared variable to decide whether to proceed directly or fall back to a waiting loop. If the fast path is taken but another process may have interfered, the process clears its flag, waits until every other process's flag is clear, then checks whether it still holds the second shared variable; if not, it waits for the second variable to become zero before retrying. Only one process may occupy the critical section at a time. After completing the critical section, the process clears the shared coordination state and its flag before restarting. The key safety property is mutual exclusion: no two processes may be in the critical section simultaneously. The liveness property is that some process must infinitely often succeed in entering the critical section. The model should be checked with weak fairness for each process and instantiated with a small concrete value of N.
