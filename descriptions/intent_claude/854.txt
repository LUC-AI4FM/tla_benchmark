# Specification Request: Prisoner Light Switch Puzzle

## System to Model

I need a formal specification of the classic "100 Prisoners and a Light Switch" puzzle. In this puzzle, a group of prisoners must collectively determine when all of them have visited a special room, using only a single light switch as their means of communication.

## Problem Description

A fixed number of prisoners are held in separate cells with no way to communicate directly. There is a central room containing a single light switch. The warden will repeatedly select prisoners (possibly the same prisoner multiple times) to visit this room one at a time. When a prisoner enters the room, they can observe the current state of the light switch and may choose to toggle it or leave it unchanged.

The prisoners are allowed to agree on a strategy beforehand, but once the process begins, the light switch is their only means of conveying information. At any point, a prisoner may declare that all prisoners have visited the room at least once. If correct, they all go free; if wrong, they face consequences.

## Requirements

The specification should model:

1. **The room visits**: The warden's arbitrary selection of which prisoner visits next
2. **The light switch**: A binary state that prisoners can observe and optionally toggle
3. **The counting strategy**: A designated "counter" prisoner who tracks visits, while other prisoners signal their first visit by manipulating the switch according to an agreed protocol
4. **The declaration**: The counter's eventual assertion that everyone has visited

## Correctness Properties

The specification must guarantee:

1. **Safety**: The counter never declares "all prisoners have visited" until every prisoner has actually visited the room at least once

2. **Liveness** (under fairness): If every prisoner is eventually selected infinitely often, the counter will eventually be able to correctly declare that all have visited

The key insight to verify is that the counting mechanism correctly tracks prisoner visits despite the limited communication channel, ensuring the count never exceeds the actual number of distinct prisoners who have visited.
