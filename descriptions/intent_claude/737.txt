Please write a formal specification for a bakery-style mutual exclusion algorithm (Lamport's Bakery Algorithm).

**System Description:**
The system consists of multiple concurrent processes that need to coordinate access to a critical section. Each process should be able to request entry to the critical section, and the algorithm must ensure that only one process can be in the critical section at any given time.

The algorithm works by having processes "take a number" (like customers at a bakery), where each process chooses a ticket number and then waits until its number is the lowest among all processes trying to enter. Processes with lower numbers have priority, with ties broken by process identifier.

**Key Requirements:**

1. **Mutual Exclusion:** At most one process may be in the critical section at any time.

2. **Deadlock Freedom:** If one or more processes are trying to enter the critical section, eventually some process will succeed in entering.

3. **Starvation Freedom (Fairness):** Every process that requests entry to the critical section will eventually be granted access.

**Modeling Constraints for Verification:**
Since ticket numbers can grow unboundedly in the real algorithm, the specification should support bounded model checking by constraining the maximum ticket number that any process can hold. This allows exhaustive state exploration while still verifying the core correctness properties.
