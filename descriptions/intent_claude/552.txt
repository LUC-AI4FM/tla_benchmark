# Specification Request: One-Bit Mutual Exclusion Protocol

## System Overview

I need a formal specification of a mutual exclusion protocol for N concurrent processes where each process uses only a single shared boolean flag to coordinate access to a critical section. This is a classic "one-bit" mutual exclusion algorithm.

## System Description

The system consists of N processes (numbered 0 through N-1) that repeatedly cycle through three phases:
1. A non-critical section where they perform regular work
2. An entry protocol where they attempt to gain exclusive access
3. A critical section followed by an exit protocol

Each process has access to a shared array of boolean flags—one flag per process—that it uses to signal intent and coordinate with other processes. The entry protocol involves checking other processes' flags in a specific order, backing off when conflicts are detected, and ensuring no higher-numbered processes are also trying to enter.

## Key Requirements

### Functional Behavior
- Processes must be able to repeatedly enter and exit the critical section
- The entry protocol should use a systematic scan of other processes' flags
- When a process detects a conflict with a lower-numbered process, it should back off (clear its flag, wait, then restart)
- Before entering the critical section, a process must verify that no higher-numbered processes have their flags set
- Upon exiting, a process clears its flag

### Fairness Assumptions
- The system should assume weak fairness for processes that are actively trying to enter or exit (not while in the non-critical section)
- Processes may remain in the non-critical section indefinitely

## Correctness Properties to Verify

### Safety: Mutual Exclusion
At most one process may be in the critical section at any given time.

### Liveness: Starvation Freedom
Under the fairness assumptions, any process that wishes to enter the critical section will eventually do so (no process starves indefinitely).

## Parameters

- N: The number of processes (must be a positive integer)
- The set of process identifiers: 0 through N-1
