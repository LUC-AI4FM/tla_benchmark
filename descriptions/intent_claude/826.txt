# Specification Request: Missionaries and Cannibals River Crossing Puzzle

## System to Model

Model the classic **Missionaries and Cannibals river crossing puzzle**. In this puzzle, a group of missionaries and a group of cannibals must cross a river using a single boat. The boat can carry a limited number of people and requires at least one person to operate it.

## Problem Description

Three missionaries and three cannibals stand on one bank of a river and need to reach the opposite bank. A boat is available that can transport one or two people at a time. The boat cannot cross the river empty—someone must row it.

## Key Constraints

1. **Boat capacity**: The boat can carry at most two people per trip
2. **Boat operation**: At least one person must be in the boat to cross
3. **Safety constraint**: On either bank of the river (including the boat when it's at that bank), cannibals must never outnumber missionaries. If cannibals outnumber missionaries at any location where missionaries are present, the missionaries will be eaten.

## Goal

Find a sequence of crossings that transports all six people from the starting bank to the destination bank without ever violating the safety constraint.

## Correctness Properties

1. **Type correctness**: The boat is always at one of the two banks, and each bank contains only valid combinations of missionaries and cannibals (subsets of the original groups).

2. **Safety invariant**: At no point should cannibals outnumber missionaries on any bank where at least one missionary is present.

3. **Solution finding**: The specification should be structured so that a model checker can discover a valid sequence of moves that achieves the goal state (all people on the destination bank).

## Notes

The primary purpose is to use a model checker to explore the state space and find a valid solution path. The "solution found" condition is typically expressed as something the model checker will report as a counterexample, revealing the successful crossing sequence.
