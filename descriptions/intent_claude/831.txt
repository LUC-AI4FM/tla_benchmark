# Specification Request: Multi-Car Elevator System

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for a multi-car elevator system serving a building with multiple floors. The system should model the interaction between people who want to travel between floors and a fleet of elevators that service their requests.

## System Components

The system involves:
- A set of people who use the elevators
- Multiple elevator cars that can move between floors
- A fixed number of floors in the building

## Required Behaviors

### People Behavior
- People start on some floor and can decide they want to travel to a different floor
- When a person wants to travel, they call an elevator by indicating their current floor and desired direction of travel
- People board an elevator when it arrives at their floor, is going in their direction, and has its doors open
- People exit the elevator when it reaches their destination floor

### Elevator Behavior
- Elevators move up and down between floors or remain stationary
- Elevators open their doors when they arrive at a floor where someone is waiting (in the appropriate direction) or where a passenger wants to exit
- Elevators close their doors after all entering and exiting is complete
- Elevators stop when they reach the top or bottom floor
- Inside each elevator, passengers press buttons for their destination floors

### Dispatch Logic
- When a call is made, the system should dispatch an appropriate elevator
- The dispatch algorithm should select from elevators that are either stationary or already moving toward the call in the matching direction
- Among eligible elevators, prefer the one closest to the call

## Correctness Properties

### Safety Requirements
1. Floor buttons inside an elevator are pressed only if someone inside that elevator actually wants to go to that floor
2. If a person is riding in an elevator that hasn't yet reached their destination, the elevator must be moving in the direction of that destination
3. There should be no "ghost calls" — every active call in the system must have at least one person actually waiting for it

### Liveness Requirements
1. Every elevator call is eventually serviced — an elevator will eventually arrive at the calling floor moving in the requested direction
2. Every person who calls an elevator will eventually reach their destination floor

## Fairness Assumptions

The system should assume that:
- People will eventually call an elevator once they've decided on a destination
- Elevators will eventually open doors, let people on and off, close doors, and move when appropriate
- The dispatch mechanism will eventually assign elevators to pending calls
- Certain operations (closing doors, moving, dispatching) should happen with strong fairness to ensure progress
