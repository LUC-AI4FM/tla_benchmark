# Specification Request: Prisoners and Switches Puzzle

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for a classic coordination puzzle involving prisoners who must collectively determine when all of them have visited a specific room, using only a pair of switches as their shared communication mechanism.

## Problem Description

A group of prisoners must solve the following challenge:

- There is a room containing two switches (call them A and B), each of which can be in one of two positions (up or down)
- The warden repeatedly selects prisoners at random and brings them into the room one at a time
- When a prisoner enters the room, they must flip exactly one switch (they cannot leave both unchanged)
- The initial positions of the switches are unknown to the prisoners
- The prisoners can agree on a strategy beforehand but cannot communicate once the process begins
- At any point, a prisoner may declare that all prisoners have visited the room at least once
- If the declaration is correct, all prisoners go free; if incorrect, all are executed
- The warden guarantees that every prisoner will be brought to the room infinitely often (no prisoner is permanently excluded)

## Strategy to Model

The specification should model the standard solution where:

1. One prisoner is designated as a special "counter" who tracks progress
2. The remaining prisoners follow a different protocol than the counter
3. Each non-counter prisoner is allowed to signal their presence a limited number of times (specifically, twice each)
4. The counter accumulates these signals until reaching a threshold that guarantees all prisoners have visited

## Correctness Properties

The specification must verify two critical properties:

### Safety
When the counter determines that the threshold has been reached and declares success, it must actually be true that every prisoner has visited the room at least once. The counter must never make a premature declaration.

### Liveness
Given the fairness assumption that every prisoner visits the room infinitely often, the system must eventually reach a state where the counter can safely declare that all prisoners have visited. The prisoners must eventually go free.

## Additional Requirements

- The specification should work for any number of prisoners greater than one
- The initial switch positions should be arbitrary (modeling the prisoners' uncertainty)
- The fairness condition must capture that the warden eventually brings each prisoner to the room repeatedly
