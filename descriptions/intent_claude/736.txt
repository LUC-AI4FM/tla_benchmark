Please write a formal specification for Lamport's Bakery Algorithm, a classic mutual exclusion protocol that allows multiple concurrent processes to coordinate access to a shared critical section.

The algorithm should model how processes "take a number" (like customers at a bakery) to determine their turn to enter the critical section. Each process should be able to:

1. Request entry to the critical section by choosing a ticket number higher than any currently held ticket
2. Wait until it has the lowest ticket number among all waiting processes (with ties broken by process ID)
3. Enter and execute within the critical section
4. Exit the critical section and release its ticket

The specification must guarantee the following correctness properties:

- **Mutual Exclusion**: At most one process may be in the critical section at any given time
- **Deadlock Freedom**: If any process is trying to enter the critical section, eventually some process will succeed in entering
- **Starvation Freedom** (ideally): Every process that requests entry to the critical section will eventually be granted access

For model checking purposes, the specification should support bounding the natural numbers to a finite range, since ticket numbers can grow unboundedly in the pure algorithm. This allows verification with finite-state model checkers while still capturing the essential behavior of the protocol.
