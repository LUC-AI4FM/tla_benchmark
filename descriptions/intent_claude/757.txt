# Specification Request: Die Hard Water Jug Puzzle

## System to Model

I need a formal specification of the classic "Die Hard" water jug puzzle. This is the puzzle where you have two jugs of different capacities and must measure out an exact target amount of water using only basic operations, with no measuring marks on the jugs.

## Problem Setup

The puzzle involves:
- A large jug that can hold exactly 5 gallons
- A small jug that can hold exactly 3 gallons
- An unlimited water supply
- The goal is to measure exactly 4 gallons of water

## Required Operations

The model should capture all the legal moves a person can make with the two jugs:

1. **Fill either jug completely** from the water supply
2. **Empty either jug completely** (pour out all water)
3. **Pour water from one jug to the other**, stopping when either the source jug is empty or the destination jug is full (whichever happens first)

## Correctness Properties

### Type Safety
The specification must guarantee that the water levels always remain valid:
- The large jug always contains between 0 and 5 gallons (inclusive)
- The small jug always contains between 0 and 3 gallons (inclusive)

### Solution Search
The specification should support checking whether a state exists where exactly 4 gallons have been measured (typically in the large jug). This is framed as searching for a violation of "the puzzle is not yet solved" — finding a counterexample demonstrates a solution path.

## Purpose

This specification serves two purposes:
1. Model the puzzle precisely enough to find a solution sequence through model checking
2. Prove formally that the type safety property (valid water levels) holds in all reachable states
