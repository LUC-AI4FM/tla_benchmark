Specify a system that computes factorial values using a recursive definition.

**System Description:**
The system maintains a single integer state representing a factorial result. It initializes by computing the factorial of a small number, then transitions to hold the factorial of a larger number.

**Requirements:**
- Define a recursive factorial function that returns 1 for inputs of 1 or less, and otherwise multiplies the input by the factorial of one less than the input
- The system starts with the factorial of 4 stored in its state
- The system can transition to store the factorial of 7
- The factorial computation must use explicit recursion

**Correctness Property:**
- The stored value must always be at least 1 (factorials are always positive)
