# Specification Request: 12-Hour Clock

## System Description

I need a formal specification of a simple 12-hour clock that tracks only the hour value. The clock should model the cyclical nature of time as displayed on a standard analog clock face.

## Behavioral Requirements

1. The clock should display hour values from 1 through 12 (not 0 through 11)
2. The hour should advance sequentially: 1 → 2 → 3 → ... → 11 → 12 → 1 → 2 → ...
3. When the clock reaches 12, the next hour should wrap around to 1
4. The clock may also remain unchanged (stuttering is allowed)

## Correctness Properties

The specification must guarantee that the hour value is always valid—that is, the hour must remain within the range of 1 to 12 at all times. This invariant should hold in every reachable state of the system.

## Purpose

This serves as a foundational example of modeling a simple cyclical counter with bounded values and wraparound behavior.
