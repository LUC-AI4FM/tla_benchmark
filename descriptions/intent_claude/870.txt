# Specification Request: Single-Lane Bridge Traffic Control

## System Description

I need a formal specification for a traffic control system managing a single-lane bridge. The bridge is too narrow to allow two-way traffic simultaneously, so vehicles can only cross concurrently if they are all traveling in the same direction.

## System Elements

- A road with discrete positions, including a bridge segment in the middle
- Two groups of cars: one group traveling in each direction (e.g., eastbound and westbound)
- Cars circulate continuously along the road, crossing the bridge repeatedly

## Required Behaviors

1. **Movement**: Cars move along the road in their designated direction, wrapping around when they reach the end (circular traffic pattern)

2. **Bridge Entry Protocol**: Before entering the bridge, cars must wait in a queue. A car may only enter the bridge if:
   - The bridge is empty, OR
   - All cars currently on the bridge are traveling in the same direction as the waiting car
   - The position the car wants to move into is unoccupied

3. **Queuing**: Cars approaching the bridge join a waiting queue and are served in order

4. **Continuous Progress**: Cars should keep moving when it is safe to do so

## Safety Properties

1. **No collisions**: Two cars cannot occupy the same position on the bridge simultaneously

2. **No head-on conflicts**: Cars traveling in opposite directions must never be on the bridge at the same time

3. **Capacity limits**: The number of cars on the bridge cannot exceed the bridge's length

## Liveness Properties

1. **Bridge exit**: Every car that enters the bridge eventually exits it

2. **Bridge entry**: Every car eventually gets a turn to cross the bridge (no starvation)

## Goal

The specification should demonstrate that the bridge control protocol correctly prevents dangerous situations while ensuring all cars make progress and eventually cross the bridge, regardless of their direction of travel.
