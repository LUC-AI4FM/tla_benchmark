Write a TLA+ specification for a simple concurrent algorithm in which N processes, numbered 0 through N−1 and arranged conceptually in a circle, each perform two sequential steps. In the first step a process writes 1 to its own slot in a shared array. In the second step it reads its left neighbor's slot (using modular indexing) and stores that value in a second shared array. Both arrays are initialized to all zeros. The key correctness property to verify is that once every process has finished, at least one entry in the second array equals 1. This postcondition must be proved as a safety invariant: it must hold in every reachable state where all processes have terminated. The specification should also establish that all processes eventually terminate. Provide a suitable inductive invariant that implies the postcondition and can be verified by a theorem prover such as TLAPS, capturing both type correctness and the structural reason the postcondition holds.
