# Specification Request: Proof of Even Sum Property

## Overview

I need a formal proof in TLA+ demonstrating a fundamental property of natural number arithmetic: that doubling any natural number always produces an even result.

## Property to Prove

The core theorem to establish is that for every natural number, adding that number to itself yields an even number. In other words, twice any natural number is always even.

## Definitions Required

The specification should include clear definitions of:
- What it means for a number to be even (divisible by 2 with no remainder)
- What it means for a number to be odd (having remainder 1 when divided by 2)

## Proof Approaches

I would like to see two proof approaches demonstrated:

1. **Direct automated proof**: Show that an SMT solver can establish this property directly without manual guidance.

2. **Structured case-based proof**: Provide a more detailed proof that:
   - Establishes that every natural number is either even or odd
   - Handles the even case: shows that an even number plus itself is even
   - Handles the odd case: shows that an odd number plus itself is even
   - Combines these cases to conclude the general result

## Purpose

This specification serves as an example of using TLA+'s proof system (TLAPS) to verify basic arithmetic properties, demonstrating both automated reasoning capabilities and manual proof structuring techniques.
