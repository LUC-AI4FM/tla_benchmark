# Specification Request: 12-Hour Clock

## System Description

I need a formal specification of a simple 12-hour clock that tracks only the hour value. The clock should model the cyclic behavior of hours as they appear on a standard analog clock face.

## Behavioral Requirements

1. The clock displays an hour value that is always between 1 and 12 (inclusive)
2. When the clock advances, the hour increments by one
3. When the hour reaches 12 and advances, it wraps around to 1 (not 0)
4. The clock may stutter (remain unchanged) at any point

## Correctness Properties

The key safety property that must be guaranteed:

- **Hour Range Invariant**: The hour value must always remain within the valid range of 1 through 12. At no point should the clock ever display a value outside this range.

## Notes

This is a simple temporal model focusing on the hour value only—no minutes, seconds, or AM/PM distinction needed. The specification should capture the essential cyclic nature of a 12-hour clock face.
