# Specification Request: Single-Lane Bridge Traffic Control

## System Overview

I need a formal specification for a single-lane bridge traffic control system. The bridge can only accommodate traffic flowing in one direction at a time, and cars from opposite sides must coordinate to cross safely without collisions.

## System Description

There are two groups of cars: those approaching from the left side and those approaching from the right side. The environment consists of a series of positions that cars can occupy, with a designated subset of these positions representing the bridge itself. Cars move through these positions, and the bridge section is the critical shared resource.

## Functional Requirements

1. Cars from either side should be able to enter and cross the bridge
2. Cars progress through positions as they travel across the system
3. Multiple cars may be on the bridge simultaneously, but only if they are traveling in the same direction
4. Cars should eventually be able to complete their crossing

## Safety Properties

The specification must guarantee:

1. **No head-on collisions**: Cars traveling in opposite directions must never occupy the bridge at the same time
2. **No position conflicts**: Two cars cannot occupy the same position simultaneously
3. **Directional exclusivity**: If any car from one side is on the bridge, no car from the opposite side may enter the bridge

## Liveness Properties

1. **Progress**: Cars waiting to cross should eventually be able to do so (no indefinite starvation)
2. **Fairness**: Both directions should get opportunities to use the bridge

## Configuration Parameters

The specification should be parameterizable with:
- The set of cars approaching from the left
- The set of cars approaching from the right
- The positions that constitute the bridge
- The complete set of positions in the system
