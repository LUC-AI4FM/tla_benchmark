Write a TLA+ specification for a small model-checking harness that instantiates a Percolator-style distributed transaction protocol (the CollapseRollbacks variant) over a minimal configuration: one table with two keys and two concurrent clients, where each client has a distinct primary key. The harness should fix the key set to {1, 2}, the client set to two named clients, and assign each client its own primary key via a function. The specification should verify the full suite of correctness invariants exported by the underlying protocol module, including type correctness, write consistency, lock consistency, consistency of committed and aborted transaction records, rollback consistency, uniqueness of writes, and snapshot isolation. The goal is to confirm that the CollapseRollbacks optimisation preserves all safety guarantees of the Percolator protocol under concurrent execution with two clients competing over two shared keys.
