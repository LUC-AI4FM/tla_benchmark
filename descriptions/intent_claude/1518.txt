Please write a formal specification for a simple bounded counter system.

**System Description:**
The system models a counter that starts at zero and increments by one until it reaches a configurable upper bound, at which point it stops changing.

**Behavior Requirements:**
- The counter begins at zero
- While the counter is below the upper bound, it increments by one on each step
- Once the counter reaches the upper bound, it remains unchanged (the system stutters)
- The upper bound should be a configurable natural number parameter

**Correctness Properties:**
- The counter value must always be a natural number
- This natural number property should hold universally across a range of auxiliary checks (the specification should include a parameterized invariant that verifies the counter remains within the natural numbers through universal quantification over a derived range)

The specification should be suitable for testing expression breakpoint functionality in a model checker, where the invariant involves nested quantification that can be evaluated at different points during state exploration.
