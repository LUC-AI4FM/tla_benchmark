Write a TLA+ specification that solves the following puzzle: given a balance scale and a single stone of weight W pounds, cut it into exactly N pieces such that any integer weight from 1 to W pounds can be measured. Because pieces may be placed on either side of the balance, each piece effectively contributes a positive, negative, or zero amount to the measured weight. The task is to find an ordered partition of W into N positive integer parts such that every integer weight between 1 and W can be expressed as a signed sum of those parts, where each coefficient is drawn from {-1, 0, 1}. The specification should enumerate candidate partitions efficiently by considering only non-decreasing sequences of positive integers summing to W, and use TLC to evaluate an assumption that either prints a valid partition when one exists or prints "No solution" otherwise. W and N should be provided as constants, with the motivating case being W = 40 and N = 4.
