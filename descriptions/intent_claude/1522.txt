Specify Huang's termination detection algorithm for a distributed system with a fixed set of processes and a designated leader. The system starts with only the leader active, holding the entire unit of weight, while all other processes are idle with zero weight. Active processes may send messages to any other process by splitting their weight in half and including one half in the message. A process receiving a message becomes active and accumulates the message's weight. When a non-leader process becomes idle, it returns its weight to the leader via a message. The leader has a distinct idle transition that does not return weight, and a distinct receive transition for collecting returned weight.

The key correctness properties are: safety — termination is never falsely declared, meaning that if the leader is idle and holds the entire unit of weight then all processes are truly idle with no messages in transit, and this condition is stable once reached; and liveness — if the system genuinely terminates (all processes idle, all message queues empty), then termination is eventually detected by the leader. Throughout all executions, the total weight distributed across process local weights and in-flight messages must always sum to exactly one.
