Write a TLA+ specification for the classic Die Hard water jug puzzle, in which two jugs — one holding up to 3 gallons and one holding up to 5 gallons — can be filled from a fountain, emptied, or have water poured between them. Both jugs start empty. The allowed operations are: fill either jug to capacity, empty either jug completely, or pour water from one jug into the other (stopping when the source is empty or the destination is full). The specification should track the state of both jugs and enforce the type invariant that each jug's contents stay within its capacity at all times. Additionally, instrument the model to track total gallons drawn from the fountain across all explored behaviors. Use TLC's reachability checking to verify that the big jug can reach exactly 4 gallons, and confirm that the state space has the expected shape: 16 distinct states, a diameter of 8, and 97 total generated states. The specification should also verify how many states satisfy the "big jug contains four gallons" predicate and how many transitions involve simultaneously changing both jugs.
